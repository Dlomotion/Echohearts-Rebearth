# DARK BEASTS + ANCIENT BEASTS — ECO-KIN LINEUP

**Project:** Echohearts: Rebearth / Echohearts: Resonance Arena

**Status:** CANON classification rule; individual creatures remain subject to their own registry/art status.

## Purpose

Dark Beasts and Ancient Beasts are official Eco-Kin lineup classifications inside the existing Eco-Kin registry. They do **not** create a second creature system, second element chart, second evolution system, second card game, or separate workflow.

They use the same permanent `EcoKinID` / `EchoprintID`, Kindling, V/D/H/P, elemental, combat, growth, art, animation, world, card, save and QA pipelines as all other Eco-Kin.

## Dark Beasts

Dark Beasts cover Eco-Kin whose ecology, origin, behavior or world-state expression is associated with one or more of the following:

- nocturnal or deep-night ecosystems;
- shadowed / light-starved habitats;
- abyssal, subterranean or Zero-Veil environments;
- Spirit-, Lunar- or Chaos-heavy resonance patterns;
- spectral, anomaly-born or rift-exposed lineages;
- cursed or fractured historical conditions;
- Blight-touched individuals or populations requiring treatment/purification.

### Dark Beast correction

`Dark Beast` is **not** a moral alignment and does not mean inherently evil. A naturally nocturnal, spectral or abyssal Eco-Kin may be healthy. Blight corruption remains a **condition/state**, not a new species merely because an individual is wounded or infected.

Dark Beasts can therefore be wary, territorial, protective, curious, frightened, social, predatory, restorative or guardian-class depending on species.

## Ancient Beasts

Ancient Beasts are primordial or heritage Eco-Kin connected to Rebearth's deep history, including:

- pre-Collapse / pre-war lineages;
- extinct or near-extinct biological DNA;
- old migration routes and lost ecosystems;
- Meridian / pre-Meridian ruins and relic sites;
- deep geological strata, Paleon regions and sealed habitats;
- ancient ecological memory;
- living archaeological adaptations;
- old Guardian, Sovereign or relic-bound bloodlines where canon supports it.

`Ancient Beast` does not automatically mean Legendary, Titan, Guardian, boss, or bondable partner. Rarity, encounter class, Kindling eligibility and narrative role are authored independently.

## Shared gameplay rules

Both classifications follow current Echohearts canon:

1. **Field relationship:** Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer.
2. **No capture mechanic:** no balls, spheres, cages, weakening-to-capture, ownership transfer or sentient storage.
3. **Growth:** evolution / awakening / adaptation must preserve identity and agency. Forced mutation/evolution is non-canon.
4. **Elements:** only Solar, Lunar, Hydro, Magma, Aero, Flora, Volt, Terra, Iron/Tech, Spirit, Chaos and Radiant are core elements.
5. **Public stats:** Vibrance, Density, Harmony and Purity.
6. **Combat:** campaign behavior is real-time third-person partner combat; tactical/turn-based rules are confined to approved Arena/simulation contexts.
7. **Cards:** cards may represent Eco-Kin and unlock approved opportunities/content, but may not sell or transfer ownership of living Eco-Kin.
8. **Sanctuary:** participation is voluntary through Sanctuary Aptitudes / Partner Assist.
9. **Corruption:** Blight / Data Virus is treatable world-state pressure, not a prestige power farm.
10. **Art:** approved creature identity, anatomy, DNA, silhouette and source art must be preserved unless a redesign is explicitly ordered.

## Registry fields

Every Dark Beast / Ancient Beast entry must declare:

- `EcoKinID`
- `EchoprintID`
- display name + aliases
- classification tags (`DarkBeast`, `AncientBeast`, or both where justified)
- biological / ecological DNA and body plan
- canonical element(s)
- Vibrance / Density / Harmony / Purity
- biome / habitat / spawn rules
- personality / temperament / behavior state
- Kindling route and refusal behavior
- combat role + signature ability
- Sanctuary Aptitudes if voluntary and appropriate
- story / quest connection
- corruption / purification state if applicable
- evolution / awakening / adaptation rules
- art source / approved visual anchor
- rig / animation / VFX / audio requirements
- card / EcoDex / save metadata
- QA status: CANON / APPROVED-PENDING / MERGED-RENAMED / RETIRED-REFERENCE-ONLY / REJECTED

## Overlap rule

A creature may be both **Ancient Beast** and **Dark Beast** when its approved lore genuinely supports both. This is an overlap of lineage/history and ecological/resonance condition, not a new element or separate species system.

## Roster control

Do not regenerate or replace already-approved Dark/Ancient creature art simply to make the category look uniform. Reuse approved production references and only correct anatomy, text, artifacts, continuity or explicitly requested design changes.
