# Piplin — Canon Identity Record

Status: CANON IDENTITY / DEVELOPMENT DETAILS PENDING

## Locked identity
- Name: Piplin
- Franchise role: established early/Foundation Eco-Kin and starter-facing character.
- Core visual anchor: cute lime-colored seedling creature.
- Existing approved identity takes priority over later generated variants.

## Canon safeguards
Piplin must remain biologically and visually related across any growth, mutation, seasonal or ascension form. New artwork may not replace Piplin with an unrelated armored humanoid or generic fantasy creature.

The following remain NOT YET VERIFIED until the permanent registry/Game Bible confirms them:
- permanent EcoKinID/Echoprint mapping
- exact class/body-plan label
- primary/secondary element
- final growth/evolution line
- combat role and numeric tuning
- Sanctuary Aptitudes

Public core gameplay stats remain Vibrance, Density, Harmony and Purity. Any older HP/ATK/etc. sheets are historical/reference-only unless internally mapped by an approved systems contract.

## Historical Divine/STARZ* proposal
A historical art brief proposed a late 'Divine' transformation using ornate gold, sapphire and diamond Ancient-Tech/relic materials, luminous STARZ* projections, a supernova-like aura and a Sky-island guardian presentation.

Disposition: APPROVED-PENDING AS A VISUAL/FORM CANDIDATE ONLY.

Rules before promotion:
1. It cannot overwrite Piplin's established identity or normal growth path.
2. STARZ* remains visual/sub-affinity/presentation language, not a new core element.
3. Extra wings, horns, tails or similar appendages should default to temporary energy projections/VFX unless an approved biological body-plan change explicitly requires physical anatomy.
4. 'Cherub-stone' remains a historical descriptor pending originality, naming and cultural/religious review; it is not automatically publication canon.
5. The form must have a clear story trigger, Kindling/consent condition, gameplay purpose and reversible/non-coercive transformation logic where appropriate.
6. High-end 8K/ray-traced wording is a key-art target, not a runtime performance specification.

## Story integration target
Any ascension should emerge from Piplin's relationship to restoration, the living world and the player's choices, not from raw power escalation alone. The form must create a visible Rebearth consequence and preserve Eco-Kin agency.
