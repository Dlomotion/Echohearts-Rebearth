## Ecology / world role direction
Glow Worm should express bioluminescence as ecology, communication and restoration rather than generic magical light.

Working gameplay/ecology functions:
- illuminate dark root tunnels, humid caves, forest hollows and night restoration spaces without replacing the player's normal navigation systems;
- use living light as a calm/communication cue during Kindling encounters;
- help reveal healthy moisture, spores, fungal/root pathways or Blight-disturbed habitat changes;
- provide low-intensity environmental guidance rather than becoming a flashlight inventory item;
- retain autonomous behavior and the right to refuse unsafe actions.

Final habitat, signature ability, Growth Rite/forms, Sanctuary Aptitude, story placement and V/D/H/P values remain pending Dex review.
