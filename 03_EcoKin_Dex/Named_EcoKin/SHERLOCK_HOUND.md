# Sherlock Hound

Status: **APPROVED-PENDING**

## Identity

- Name: **Sherlock Hound**
- Relationship: bonded field partner of **Kaelen “Kale,” Frontier Scout**
- Class: Animal Eco-Kin
- Body plan: true quadruped canine/hound
- Primary role: tracking, scent work, rescue search, perimeter warning, route-finding
- Secondary role: Sanctuary scouting and safe-path verification

## Canon guardrails

Sherlock Hound is a partner, not equipment. He may refuse unsafe orders, rest when injured, react to Kaelen’s stress, and develop trust with the player independently.

His tracking fantasy should rely on believable canine behaviors translated through Resonance: scent discrimination, ground-sign reading, air-scenting, directional listening, hesitation at contaminated routes, and learned recognition of Eco-Kin/NPC trails.

## Gameplay hooks

- Reveals recent trails after A.E.G.I.S. identifies a viable track zone.
- Distinguishes panicked flight from ordinary migration.
- Can warn about Blight-contaminated water, unstable ground, hostile machines, or injured Eco-Kin nearby.
- Improves rescue quests by identifying safer approach routes rather than exposing exact enemy positions through omniscient UI.
- Can participate in Kaelen’s Sanctuary-building tutorial by locating clean soil, water, and safe camp boundaries.

## Art / animation requirements

- Preserve believable canine locomotion and four-legged anatomy.
- Unique readable silhouette without turning the hound into a humanoid armor character.
- Tracking animation set: nose-to-ground, air-scent, paw-stop, alert freeze, circling, cautious approach, recall, rest, friendly bond gestures.
- Equipment, if used, must be light, non-restrictive field gear.
