## Critical Anatomy Lock

Skyraxis has **exactly two legs**.

The historical three-legged rendering is an AI anatomy error and is not valid character anatomy.

Production anatomy rules:

- exactly 2 legs
- wings, if present, remain visually and structurally distinct from the legs
- no duplicated feet, hips, claws, wing joints, tails, heads, horns, or floating pseudo-limbs
- no accidental humanoid torso unless a later classification explicitly approves Humanoid-Kin anatomy
- gait, takeoff, landing and combat motion must be physically readable
- silhouette must remain recognizable when VFX and armor-like surfaces are removed

## Franchise Identity Rule

Skyraxis must not read as a generic fantasy dragon/bird/griffin with crystals glued onto the body.

Its Echohearts identity must come from the combination of:

1. unique biological body plan
2. specific environmental adaptation to high-altitude riftwind zones
3. distinctive movement language
4. behavior and ecology
5. A.E.G.I.S./Resonance interactions
6. materials and markings that grow from anatomy rather than cover it
7. a story/world function that changes Rebearth

## Visual Direction

Target: **raw, authentic, strange, beautiful, dangerous, and unmistakably Echohearts**.

Avoid:

- generic armored dragon language
- random crystal spikes
- ornamental over-detail that hides anatomy
- copied fantasy-monster silhouettes
- extra limbs
- symmetrical detail with no ecological reason

Prefer:

- wind-sculpted surfaces
- asymmetry caused by habitat exposure, age, or healed damage
- aerodynamic anatomy
- membranes, feathers, quills, fins, or skin structures only when biologically coherent
- visible riftwind wear patterns
- resonance markings that respond to pressure, storms, altitude, and player proximity

## Gameplay Fantasy

Skyraxis should feel like a creature that **owns the airspace because it understands it**, not because it is simply large.

Potential field roles pending balance review:

- aerial route reading
- stormfront warning
- rift-current stabilization
- high-altitude traversal support
- protection of migrating flying Eco-Kin
- controlled downdraft / pressure manipulation
- rescue during broken sky-route events

## Behavior

Skyraxis is not automatically aggressive.

Possible encounter states:

- distant circling observation
- territorial warning passes
- storm-herding behavior that pushes smaller Eco-Kin away from dangerous currents
- defensive dive only when nests, migration routes, or wounded Kin are threatened
- retreat when injured rather than fighting to death

The player's first meaningful encounter should require reading wind, posture, distance and environmental stress before combat or Kindling becomes possible.

## Kindling Direction

Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer

Possible trust requirements:

- repair a damaged sky route
- help a stranded juvenile or migration group
- stop a rift-current extractor
- survive a storm without attacking Skyraxis unnecessarily
- restore a high-altitude landmark

No forced capture, no containment sphere, no weakening-to-own.

## Story Connection

Skyraxis should connect to the existing sky progression instead of creating a parallel region.

Candidate placements to reconcile:

- Solis Reach
- Veridian Isles / sky-island routes
- Starvault Spires proposal material
- Riftway / Great Fracture legacy concepts after canon review

Its story purpose should reinforce the theme that restoring the sky means restoring **migration, weather balance, freedom of movement, and safe passage**, not controlling the climate.
