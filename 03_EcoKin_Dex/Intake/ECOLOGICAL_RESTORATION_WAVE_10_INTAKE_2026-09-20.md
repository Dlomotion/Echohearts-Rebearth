## AI Mistake Patch / Continuity QA

The source references use several useful ecology labels that are not part of the locked combat-element roster. Normalize them as follows:

- Water → **Hydro**
- Earth → **Terra**
- Air / Wind → **Aero**
- Life / Nature / Verdant → **Flora**
- Ice → primarily **Hydro** with **Aero** where the species' alpine/wind ecology requires it; Frost/Chill remains a status/biome tag, not a new core element
- Harmony → public attribute / behavioral or resonance tag, not a combat element
- Sand / Coastal / Sentinel / Guardian / Tracker / Explorer / Support / Excavator / Purifier → ecology, behavior, role or family tags, not core elements

The locked core roster remains: **Solar, Lunar, Hydro, Magma, Aero, Flora, Volt, Terra, Iron/Tech, Spirit, Chaos, Radiant**.

No EcoKinID, rarity, final stats, growth line, ability numbers or card-game values are assigned by this intake. Those require Master Dex normalization and production proof.

## Individual integration notes

### Azurithor — The Tidal Oracle
- Preserve the whale-shark body plan and large, calm explorer silhouette.
- `Harmony` is not a second element. It should influence temperament, support kit, Kindling behavior and/or the Harmony public attribute.
- Candidate world placement: Azuredeep / Neon Sea / Sunken Shelf region family, exact spawn and landmark pending.
- Story use: reveals ocean health through migration, current and reef-state changes rather than exposition-only dialogue.

### Vuyamba — The Echo Seeker
- Preserve exaggerated auditory/sensory anatomy only if it remains biologically readable and animation-safe.
- Candidate world placement: Shifting Dunes / Blackfell region family.
- Story use: teaches non-combat tracking, safe-route discovery and environmental listening.

### Mubariki — The Rainwarden
- Armor should read as ecological/Ancient-Tech adaptation, not generic plated monster armor.
- Candidate world placement: humid forest/rainforest sub-biome within an approved region such as Veridian Isles, exact location pending.
- Story use: demonstrates how canopy, rainfall and soil health connect.

### Kelparc — The Tideweaver
- Kelp armor remains soft/flexible where anatomy requires movement; do not turn it into rigid humanoid plate.
- Candidate world placement: Azuredeep/coastal shelf/kelp-forest sub-biome.
- Story use: restores juvenile-fish habitat and damaged coastal vegetation.

### Duneskimmer — The Subsurface Harvester
- `Harvester` means ecological excavator/forager role, not forced labor or resource-extraction property.
- Candidate world placement: Shifting Dunes and buried Meridian strata where appropriate.
- Story use: exposes underground water, seed banks or damaged Living Crust routes without converting the whole world into voxel terrain.

### Riftspine — The Highland Sentinel
- Quilled defensive morphology should support believable mountain movement.
- Candidate world placement: Skyforge Reach / mountain-ridge sub-biomes; Glacial Spires only if final anatomy/climate review supports it.
- Story use: warns settlements of slope failure, severe winds and unstable passes.

### Nzalaqua — The Floodweaver
- `Floodweaver` is ecological function, not magical unlimited terraforming.
- Candidate world placement: wetland/floodplain sub-biome within an existing region; exact world slot pending.
- Story use: player learns that controlled seasonal flooding can restore habitat rather than every flood being treated as damage.

### Siltrex — The Sediment Keeper
- Terraforming ability is bounded and authored: riverbanks, bars, deltas and restoration zones only.
- Candidate world placement: river-mouth/delta system connected to approved coastal regions.
- Story use: visible long-term world delta through repaired banks, reed beds and nursery habitat.

### Virelily — The Floating Reclaimer
- Flora remains the primary core element; Hydro may be considered later only if move kit and physiology justify it.
- Candidate world placement: marsh, pond and slow-water restoration sites.
- Story use: pollution cleanup, water-quality quests and Sanctuary wetland recovery.

### Frostwind Stagel — The Skywind Herald
- Preserve the exact working name for now; naming review remains open.
- `Ice` is not a core element. Use **Hydro / Aero** with Frost/Chill/Alpine tags.
- Candidate world placement: Glacial Spires / alpine ridges.
- Story use: climate warning, snowpack routes, avalanche risk and fragile alpine habitat restoration.

## Shared Kindling / agency rule

All ten follow the existing relationship loop:

**Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer**

Their ecological utility does not make them workers or property. Sanctuary roles are voluntary, and an Eco-Kin may refuse a task, leave an unsafe area, or remain a wild ally instead of becoming a permanent partner.

## Public stat rule

When card/Dex-facing stats are assigned, use only the four established public attributes:

- Vibrance
- Density
- Harmony
- Purity

No legacy HP/ATK/DEF/SPD-style identity block overrides this schema. Separate EchoDeck/Harmony Circuit combat values may exist later if clearly labeled as simulation/card-game data.

## Story and game integration targets

This wave should function as an **ecological restoration network**, not ten disconnected combat collectibles. Together they cover:

- ocean/current health;
- desert route and soil stabilization;
- rainforest/canopy regeneration;
- kelp/coastal recovery;
- burrowing and subsurface restoration;
- mountain/highland safety;
- wetland/floodplain repair;
- river-delta sediment recovery;
- pollution/phytoremediation cleanup;
- alpine climate and snowpack monitoring.

Each final species entry must receive at least one authored world-state delta, one non-combat field interaction, one behavior/agency pattern, one Kindling path, and one story or restoration mission connection before promotion from intake.
