## AI Mistake Patch / Continuity QA

This intake was originally written against the retired 12-element front-facing chart. It is now migrated to the active **Rebearth Essence** canon:

**Flora, Torrent, Pyre, Terra, Aero, Glaze, Voltic, Aura, Shade**.

Source-card labels normalize as follows:

- Water → **Torrent**
- Earth → **Terra**
- Air / Wind → **Aero**
- Life / Nature / Verdant → **Flora**
- Ice / Frost → **Glaze** when it is a true physiological/field Essence expression; Frost, Chill, Alpine and Snowpack may also remain ecology/status tags
- Harmony → public attribute / relationship or behavior tag, never a second combat Essence
- Sand, Coastal, Sentinel, Guardian, Tracker, Explorer, Support, Excavator, Purifier, Terraformer and Herald → ecology, behavior or gameplay-role tags, not Essences

Legacy Hydro/Magma/Volt/Spirit/Radiant/Solar/Lunar/Iron-Tech/Chaos labels do not re-open a second type chart. They follow the migration rules in `00_Canon_Lock/REBEARTH_ESSENCE_TAMER_VARIANT_CANON_2026-09-20.md`.

No EcoKinID, rarity, final V/D/H/P values, growth line, ability numbers, card values or spawn rates are invented here. Those require Master Dex normalization and playtest/production evidence.

## Individual integration notes

### Azurithor — The Tidal Oracle
- Preserve whale-shark anatomy and a broad, calm pelagic silhouette.
- `Harmony` affects temperament, Kindling/support behavior and/or the Harmony public attribute, not Essence typing.
- Candidate placement: Azuredeep / Neon Sea / Sunken Shelf region family, exact landmark pending.
- Story use: communicates ocean health through current, migration and reef-state changes rather than exposition-only dialogue.

### Vuyamba — The Echo Seeker
- Preserve large sensory ears only where they remain biologically readable and animation-safe.
- Candidate placement: Shifting Dunes / Blackfell region family.
- Story use: teaches non-combat tracking, safe-route discovery and environmental listening.
- “Echo” in the title means perception/return signal; it does not require a music-themed ability kit.

### Mubariki — The Rainwarden
- Armor should read as natural/ecological or Ancient-Tech-compatible adaptation, not generic humanoid plate.
- Candidate placement: rainforest/humid-forest sub-biome within an approved Rebearth region.
- Story use: demonstrates the relationship between canopy, rainfall, roots and soil health.

### Kelparc — The Tideweaver
- Kelp covering remains flexible and anatomically compatible with locomotion.
- Candidate placement: Azuredeep/coastal shelf/kelp-forest sub-biome.
- Story use: restores nursery habitat and damaged coastal vegetation.

### Duneskimmer — The Subsurface Harvester
- `Harvester` describes ecological excavation/foraging, never forced labor or ownership.
- Candidate placement: Shifting Dunes and selected buried Meridian strata.
- Story use: reveals underground water, seed banks or damaged Living Crust routes without turning the whole world into voxel terrain.

### Riftspine — The Highland Sentinel
- Quilled defensive morphology must remain compatible with believable climbing and mountain traversal.
- Candidate placement: Skyforge Reach / mountain-ridge sub-biomes; Glacial Spires only after climate/anatomy review.
- Story use: warns settlements of slope failure, severe winds and unstable passes.

### Nzalaqua — The Floodweaver
- `Floodweaver` is a bounded ecological function, not unlimited magical terraforming.
- Candidate placement: wetland/floodplain sub-biome within the approved map.
- Story use: demonstrates that seasonal flooding can be restorative when channels, soils and migration routes are healthy.

### Siltrex — The Sediment Keeper
- Terrain influence is authored and localized to banks, bars, deltas and restoration zones.
- Candidate placement: river-mouth/delta system connected to approved coastal regions.
- Story use: creates visible long-term world deltas through repaired banks, reed beds and nursery habitat.

### Virelily — The Floating Reclaimer
- **Flora** is primary. Torrent is not added automatically simply because the species lives in water.
- Candidate placement: marsh, pond and slow-water restoration sites.
- Story use: pollution cleanup, water-quality quests and Sanctuary wetland recovery.

### Frostwind Stagel — The Skywind Herald
- Preserve the working name pending naming/originality review.
- **Glaze / Aero** replaces the old Ice/Wind shorthand.
- Candidate placement: Glacial Spires / alpine ridges.
- Story use: climate warning, snowpack routes, avalanche risk and fragile highland-habitat restoration.

## Shared Kindling / agency rule

All ten use the existing relationship loop:

**Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer**

Their ecological utility does not make them workers, tools or property. Sanctuary participation is voluntary; an Eco-Kin can refuse, rest, leave unsafe conditions or remain a wild ally.

## Public stat rule

When card/Dex-facing attributes are assigned, use only:

- Vibrance
- Density
- Harmony
- Purity

Internal combat/runtime values may exist where needed, but they do not replace the four public attributes.

## Story and game integration targets

This wave functions as an **ecological restoration network**, not ten disconnected collectibles. Together they support:

- ocean/current health;
- desert route and soil stabilization;
- rainforest/canopy regeneration;
- kelp/coastal recovery;
- burrowing and subsurface restoration;
- mountain/highland safety;
- wetland/floodplain repair;
- river-delta sediment recovery;
- pollution/phytoremediation cleanup;
- alpine climate and snowpack monitoring.

Each promoted species entry must receive at least one authored world-state delta, one non-combat field interaction, one behavior/agency pattern, one Kindling path, and one story/restoration mission connection.

## Tree of Life connection

Where ecology supports it, these Eco-Kin may become witnesses, helpers or indicators during **Sovereign Heartroot / Tree of Life** root-restoration missions. They do not exist only to repair the Tree; their own habitats, populations and choices remain important.
