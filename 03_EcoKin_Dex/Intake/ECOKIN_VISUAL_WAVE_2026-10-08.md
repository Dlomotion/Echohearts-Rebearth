## Visual and anatomy QA contract

For every new or repaired Eco-Kin image:

- preserve the approved identity once an identity is locked;
- produce a unique Echohearts-native silhouette rather than a recognizable outside character;
- use biologically coherent limb counts and joints for the intended body plan;
- no extra limbs, duplicated legs, fused appendages, malformed paws/claws/digits, missing limbs, or impossible joint placement;
- hands are only required on truly hand-bearing humanoid or primate-like body plans; non-humanoid Eco-Kin should use anatomically appropriate paws, claws, talons, fins, hooves, or manipulators rather than forced five-finger hands;
- maintain coherent scale, contact points, weight distribution, locomotion mechanics, and natural posture;
- keep lighting direction, material response, lens/perspective, and environment physically coherent;
- where a 35 mm cinematic reference is requested, treat it as camera/lens language rather than forcing a human-photography anatomy template onto non-humanoid creatures;
- generated text on artwork is presentation-only until spelling, identity, Essence mapping, role, and permanent ID are QA-approved;
- avoid generic fantasy shorthand and repeated creature templates; each Eco-Kin needs a distinct ecological adaptation, silhouette, locomotion profile, resonance behavior, and gameplay purpose.
