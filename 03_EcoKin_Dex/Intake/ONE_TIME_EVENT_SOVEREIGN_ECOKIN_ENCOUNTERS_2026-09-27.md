## 1. One-time event encounter contract

These encounters are **Event Sovereigns**: exceptional Eco-Kin, Guardian states, anomaly-bound forms or ecology-scale beings whose first canonical encounter occurs only after a specific authored world event.

### Canonical spawn rule

- one canonical first-spawn per save/profile/world state;
- event gate must be explicit and recoverable from save data;
- encounter cannot be farmed by reload/duplication exploits;
- outcome commits to the persistent world ledger;
- the living Sovereign is not converted into loot;
- victory may end through cleansing, protection, trust, migration, sealing, alliance or story-specific combat resolution;
- if a future roster mapping allows a bond relationship, that relationship is authored and unique rather than a generic capture roll;
- replay after resolution occurs only through **A.E.G.I.S. Archive / Chrono Simulation** and cannot duplicate the canonical reward or world-state transaction.

### No real-world FOMO rule

A 'one-time event' means one canonical world event inside the save, not a permanently missable real-world calendar window by default.

Seasonal presentation may rotate, but essential story access must return through archive/reissue rules.

# 2. EVENT SOVEREIGN 01 — MORROWMIRE COLOSSUS

**Replaces/reference-detaches:** `Amorphous Core` progression-wall concept.  
**Working classification:** ecology-scale mutable Eco-Kin / anomaly-fed Sovereign candidate.  
**Primary fantasy:** a living wetland mass that accumulated contaminated restoration matter until an entire marsh began moving as one organism.

### Event trigger — **The Mire That Learned to Move**

Unlocked after:

- first major Mawrot containment chain;
- restoration of two wetland flow channels;
- A.E.G.I.S. detects an impossible migration signature from the marsh itself.

### Encounter identity

The Colossus is not a generic slime mountain and does not split into copied elemental mini-bosses.

Its body is a shifting colony of semi-translucent mud membranes, root fibers, mineral plates and symbiotic micro-life.

### Phase structure

**Phase 1 — Mass Drift**  
The Colossus reroutes water and physically moves the arena's channels. Players must use pumps/gates and Eco-Kin traversal roles to stop it from burying restoration sites.

**Phase 2 — Density Inversion**  
It alternates between a dense armored state and a dispersed flood state. Different roles are useful in each state; raw DPS alone is inefficient.

**Phase 3 — Heart-Marsh Exposure**  
The objective changes from damage to stabilizing three ecological organs while protecting nearby wildlife from contaminated overflow.

### Persistent payoff

The restored marsh becomes a new migration nursery and unlocks a **Mire Renewal Route** for future seasonal Eco-Kin.

No global stat bonus.

# 3. EVENT SOVEREIGN 02 — VESPERWING PARALLAX

**Replaces/reference-detaches:** `Ocular-Vortex Nova` giant-eye/sky-boss concept.  
**Working classification:** aerial Guardian-scale Eco-Kin bonded to a damaged Ancient Tech navigation lattice.  
**Primary fantasy:** a vast manta/raptor-like sky organism whose wing membranes refract navigation light into false horizons.

### Event trigger — **Black-Sky Meridian**

Unlocked when:

- three sky-route beacons are restored;
- a rare upper-atmosphere eclipse/ion storm occurs in story state;
- the damaged lattice mistakes all moving objects for invasion targets.

### Phase structure

**Phase 1 — False Horizon**  
The arena fills with mirrored flight corridors. A.E.G.I.S. reads pressure and navigation signatures rather than 'targeting the correct eye.'

**Phase 2 — Shearwake**  
The Sovereign creates moving air-pressure walls that reshape traversal routes. Ground and aerial partners cooperate rather than requiring one exact species.

**Phase 3 — Parallax Accord**  
Players attach non-invasive stabilization beacons to damaged lattice points while defending the creature from automated Aurelian interceptors.

### Persistent payoff

Restores a permanent **High Meridian Skyway** and unlocks new upper-atmosphere scenery, migrations and exploration events.

# 4. EVENT SOVEREIGN 03 — CROWNSPORE REGENT

**Replaces/reference-detaches:** `Hive-Queen Vespa` bee-queen analogue.  
**Working classification:** fungal/pollinator symbiotic Sovereign candidate.  
**Primary fantasy:** a mobile crown-shaped fungal organism carried by a cloud of tiny pollinator symbionts; it rules no hive and is not a bee queen.

### Event trigger — **The Great Bloom Misfire**

Unlocked after:

- restoring a major canopy pollination network;
- an old Meridian growth accelerator reactivates incorrectly;
- the Regent emerges to contain runaway plant growth but becomes overloaded by Blight residue.

### Phase structure

**Phase 1 — Pollen Geometry**  
Wind currents carry safe and hazardous spore lanes. Players redirect airflow and protect pollinator swarms.

**Phase 2 — Root-Crown Lock**  
The Regent anchors to the forest floor. The goal is to sever corrupted machine roots without damaging healthy mycelial connections.

**Phase 3 — Bloom Decision**  
Players choose between rapid purge, controlled cleansing or full ecological stabilization. The choice affects how quickly the biome recovers and which species return first.

### Persistent payoff

Unlocks a **Canopy Renewal State** and new flora/fungi/pollinator ecology encounters.

# 5. EVENT SOVEREIGN 04 — RIFTBACK LEVIATHAN

**Replaces/reference-detaches:** `Aether-Vortex Boundary` moving-wall/Hardmode gate concept.  
**Working classification:** colossal fracture-adapted migratory Eco-Kin / dimensional Sovereign candidate.  
**Primary fantasy:** a serpent/whale-like being that swims along unstable gravity seams, dragging a moving fracture front behind its body.

### Event trigger — **Horizon Tear**

Unlocked after a major **Fracture Escalation** story milestone when multiple Riftway anchors desynchronize.

### Phase structure

**Phase 1 — Moving Faultline**  
The battlefield moves because the Leviathan migrates through a chain of floating terrain shelves. Players must traverse, glide, climb and stabilize anchors ahead of it.

**Phase 2 — Riftback Breach**  
Temporary dimensional openings appear along its body where trapped debris/constructs emerge. The player targets unstable external anchor growths, not body organs.

**Phase 3 — Migration Choice**  
The objective becomes guiding the Leviathan toward a safe dimensional corridor instead of killing it. Failing to stabilize enough anchors produces heavier regional damage; success opens a new Rift route.

### Persistent payoff

Unlocks the **Riftback Passage**, a postgame/late-game traversal corridor and regional restoration branch.

No `Hardmode` terminology or global irreversible server escalation is used.

# 6. EVENT SOVEREIGN 05 — HUSHGLASS WARDEN

**Source:** original rewrite of **The Static Orchard of Looming Frequencies** concept.  
**Working classification:** Echo/Harmony-specialist Guardian-scale Eco-Kin / suppression-field survivor.  
**Event zone:** **Static Orchard of Looming Frequencies** may remain as an authored specialist region name pending level-registry review.

### Story purpose

Challenge the Core-Binder's reliance on familiar partner timing by entering a zone where a damaged phase-cancellation system suppresses selected Resonance channels.

This is a specialist Echo/Harmony anomaly. It does not redefine all Resonance as music or acoustics.

### Event trigger — **The Orchard Falls Silent**

Unlocked after:

- a Chrono record identifies an Era-4 containment array;
- the player restores the route into the Silent Fractures;
- A.E.G.I.S. detects the array reactivating around a trapped Guardian signature.

### Scene correction

Use **Core-Binder**, not Frequency Tamer.

Use **A.E.G.I.S.**, not Tuning Gauntlet.

The landscape is a dark glass-fiber root lattice with calcified signal spires. The danger is an engineered phase-cancellation field left by Great Collapse containment infrastructure.

### Gameplay

**Suppression Windows**  
Specific ability channels are temporarily dampened. The player reads the field and changes stance/role timing instead of losing all Vibrance automatically.

**Visible Interference Nodes**  
Pigment, spores, dust, scanning or other authored tools can reveal otherwise hard-to-see suppression emitters. Do not require Pigcasso specifically.

**Resonance Bridge Puzzle**  
Players tune multiple environmental anchors into a bounded compatibility range. Do not require mathematically exact equal Harmony and Density values from the party; support multiple valid team compositions and accessibility assists.

**Pause/UI safety correction**  
The pause/settings/accessibility menu never creates an enemy buff, punishment or combat state. In single-player, pause behaves according to platform/game rules. In multiplayer, non-pausing menus may show diegetic signal distortion without affecting combat stats.

### Hushglass Warden fight

The Warden appears fragmented because the containment field is repeatedly phase-canceling parts of its Resonance silhouette.

**Phase 1 — Lost Signal**  
Protect the Warden while breaking external suppression pylons.

**Phase 2 — Feedback Spiral**  
The Warden lashes out as stored cancellation energy rebounds. Players create safe resonance windows by alternating environmental emitters.

**Phase 3 — Return the Pulse**  
The win condition is synchronized restoration: stabilize the central array, keep the Warden above a recovery threshold, and reconnect the Orchard to the Heartroot network.

### Persistent payoff

- Static Orchard changes from `Suppressed` to `Recovering` world state;
- a rainforest/Heartroot travel corridor opens;
- a **Harmony Window** field technique or Growth Rite permission may unlock;
- no universal +15% maximum Harmony bonus is granted.

# 7. EVENT SOVEREIGN 06 — BASALITH WARDEN

**Replaces/reference-detaches:** `Obsidian Harmonizer (Marrow-09)` concept.  
**Working classification:** basalt/glass Guardian-scale Eco-Kin candidate.  
**Primary fantasy:** a massive quadrupedal geological lifeform that stabilizes fractured terrain by distributing heat, pressure and Density through its mineral lattice.

### Name correction

`Marrow-09` is retired as the public species name because it reads like a manufactured serial designation.

If an old Aurelian archive later labels the individual `M-09`, that may survive only as historical metadata, not the creature's identity.

### Anatomy

- **Basalt Lattice Core** — mineral-organic pressure organ, not a detachable collectible heart;
- **Vent Crests** — hollow stone structures that regulate heat and pressure rather than universal sound horns;
- **Siltwake** — temporary mineral/Resonance trail useful for environmental scanning and limited field recharge, not an endlessly farmable battery-tripling resource.

### Event trigger — **Anchorfall**

A floating land shelf begins drifting toward a lethal atmospheric layer after an old stabilization network fails.

The Basalith Warden anchors itself and becomes dangerously overloaded while trying to hold the region together.

### Phase structure

**Phase 1 — Pressure Lock**  
Players match environmental stabilization windows through movement/defense timing.

**Phase 2 — Gravity Ripple**  
The Warden creates visible expanding gravity waves; accessibility settings provide non-audio telegraphs and timing assists.

**Phase 3 — Living Anchor**  
Instead of 'critical-health world rewrite,' the Warden begins sacrificing its own mineral lattice to keep the region stable. The player must repair external anchors fast enough that the creature can disengage safely.

### Persistent payoff

The land shelf stabilizes and becomes a new explorable biome layer. The Warden may migrate or become a story ally depending on authored outcome.

# 8. Event Sovereign reward philosophy

One-time Sovereign events should reward **access, story, ecology and identity**, not missable permanent combat superiority.

Allowed reward families:

- new region/traversal route;
- restored biome state;
- A.E.G.I.S. archive entry;
- cosmetic mark/title/banner;
- Sanctuary structure permission;
- non-sentient blueprint;
- Growth Rite/Form eligibility;
- relationship/Accord state;
- story ally access;
- future seasonal migration table.

Avoid:

- unique irreplaceable raw-stat advantage unavailable to later players;
- boss body parts from sentient Eco-Kin as routine loot;
- capture rolls;
- infinite farming by rollback;
- global permanent percentage inflation;
- paid access to the one canonical story resolution.

# 9. Replay and accessibility

After the canonical event is resolved:

- world spawn flag remains consumed;
- canonical reward transaction remains consumed;
- A.E.G.I.S. Archive may unlock a simulation replay;
- replay can support difficulty modifiers, speedrun timing, cooperative practice and boss-rush modes;
- replay rewards are bounded simulation rewards/cosmetics and never duplicate the original world-state unlock.

Event combat must support:

- visual alternatives for audio telegraphs;
- audio alternatives for color-dependent telegraphs where practical;
- motion/flash intensity controls;
- timing-assist options where compatible with difficulty design;
- multiplayer reconnection policy;
- failure recovery without corrupting the one-time event ledger.
