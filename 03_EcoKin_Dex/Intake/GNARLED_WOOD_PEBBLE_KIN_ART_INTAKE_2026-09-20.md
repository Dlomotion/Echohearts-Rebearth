# Forest Guardian Pair — Eco-Kin Art Intake

Status: **VISUAL-DEVELOPMENT REFERENCE / NAMES NOT YET CANON-LOCKED**

Source: user-provided reference image in the Echohearts: Eco-Kins project conversation, showing two realistic forest Eco-Kin together in a dense woodland environment.

## Visual read

### Concept A — working label: Gnarled-Wood

- upright/bipedal tree-and-stone guardian silhouette;
- bark/wood facial structure;
- moss, ferns, small plants, and luminous cyan fungi growing from the upper body/head;
- green internal glow through torso and limb seams;
- long heavy arms and large articulated hands;
- ancient-forest guardian impression;
- realistic, high-detail material treatment suitable for current Echohearts visual-development direction.

### Concept B — working label: Pebble-Kin

- heavy quadruped/gorilla-like stone guardian silhouette;
- rounded rock plating with carved/organic seams;
- moss, flowers, ferns, and luminous cyan fungi across the back;
- warm glowing eyes;
- very low center of gravity and high-mass locomotion read;
- strong fit for Heavy Grounded Beast animation family.

## Canon normalization

The image itself is useful visual development, but the sign text is not sufficient to lock species names.

- `Gnarled-Wood` is descriptive but generic and should be treated as a **working art label** until naming/originality/Dex duplication review.
- `Pebble-Kin` is also a **working art label**. Because `-Kin` is already the franchise collective term, final naming should avoid repetitive naming unless deliberately chosen for a specific species identity.
- Both concepts should use official Rebearth Essences rather than generic external element labels.
- Provisional ecological fit: **Flora / Terra** presentation, with exact primary/secondary Essence assignment pending Dex design.
- Neither concept is a “Conductor Lattice,” “Organic Hardware unit,” or inventory creature.

## Gameplay role candidates

### Concept A

Possible roles:

- forest restoration guardian;
- canopy/soil health sentinel;
- support/controller in combat;
- Ancient Grove or Sovereign Heartroot-linked quest presence;
- voluntary Sanctuary aptitude: Garden Care, Root Repair, Scout Watch, Comfort/Protection depending on final species behavior.

### Concept B

Possible roles:

- terrain stabilizer;
- boulder/landslide clearing support;
- defensive frontliner;
- soil/moisture retention ecology role;
- voluntary Sanctuary aptitude: Stone Work, Garden Care, Defense, Water Channel support depending on final species behavior.

## Animation routing

- Concept A: `Bipedal Animal / Humanoid-Kin` or a dedicated Flora Guardian rig depending on final anatomy.
- Concept B: `Heavy Grounded Beast` rig family.
- They should not share identical animations merely because they appear together.
- Each needs unique personality idles, ecology interaction, bond response, combat language, and restoration behavior.

## Story integration candidates

Best fit is a forest-restoration encounter connected to a damaged Heartroot root corridor. Their relationship should be ecological rather than ownership-based: one may tend vertical/canopy growth while the other stabilizes soil/stone/water flow. A quest can visibly improve the surrounding woodland after the player resolves the local Blight pressure.

## Art QC checklist before promotion

- final species names and stable EcoKinIDs;
- silhouette distinction from existing roster;
- hand/foot anatomy and joint readability;
- moss/fungus growth attached cleanly to body geometry;
- no accidental human-costume look unless Humanoid-Kin is intentional;
- Essence VFX limited so the creatures remain readable;
- realistic scale sheet;
- front/side/back turnaround;
- facial expression sheet;
- material breakup for bark, stone, moss, fungi, eyes, glow seams;
- rig-family confirmation;
- locomotion test pose;
- story/ecology connection;
- Dex duplicate/name audit.

## Current decision

**Keep the creature concepts. Do not lock the sign wording as final species names yet.**
