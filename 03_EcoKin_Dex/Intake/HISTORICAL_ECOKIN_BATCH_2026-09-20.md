# Historical Eco-Kin Intake — 2026-09-20

Status: SOURCE / PROPOSAL UNTIL RECONCILED

This intake consolidates reposted historical Eco-Kin concepts and generated-art names without allowing older chat claims to overwrite current canon.

## Canon naming rule
Publication-facing spelling is **Eco-Kin** / **ECO-KIN’S**. Older `Echo-Kin`, `Eko-Kin`, and similar variants are historical errors unless quoted for provenance.
