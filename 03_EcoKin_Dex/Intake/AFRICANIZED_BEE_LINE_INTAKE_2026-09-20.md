## Shared line mechanics
- **Bond theme:** trust through habitat protection, patience and care.
- **Kindling path:** Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer.
- **Bond requirement:** player must avoid damaging the hive, repair water/flower access and resolve Blight disturbance around the colony.
- **Sanctuary Aptitudes:** Garden Care, Scout Watch, Defense, Habitat Care.
- **Reclamation function:** reconnect pollinator corridors and improve flowering-plant recovery.
- **Combat style:** evasive swarm positioning, short-range stings, defensive interception and support, not suicidal swarm attacks.
- **Personality direction:** vigilant, communal, energetic, intensely protective of young and habitat.

4. **Crown Swarm** — high-Bond coordinated finisher/restoration field.

## Art anatomy lock
A production design must show:
- exactly six insect legs;
- one pair of antennae;
- two pairs of wings unless a specific form intentionally reduces visibility through pose;
- readable head, thorax and abdomen segmentation;
- one coherent stinger structure;
- bee-derived mouthparts/eyes rather than a human face;
- no humanoid hands or feet;
- no random armor plates that erase the insect silhouette;
- no extra wings, tails, limbs or fused anatomy.

## Art direction
Use the historical generated bee image as a visual reference only.
