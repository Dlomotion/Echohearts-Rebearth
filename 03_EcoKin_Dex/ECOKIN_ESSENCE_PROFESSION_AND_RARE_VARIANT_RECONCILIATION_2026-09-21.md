## AI Mistake Patch / Continuity QA

This document prevents four systems from fragmenting the project:

1. Do **not** run a new nine-type Essence chart beside the established twelve-type combat taxonomy.
2. Do **not** replace the player identity **Frequency Tamer / Veridian Keeper** with a second protagonist title.
3. Do **not** turn ethical Kindling into cage/snare capture gameplay.
4. Do **not** make every Eco-Kin revolve around Harmony, music, sound, or resonance motifs. Those are specialized traits for selected species only.

## 1. Tree of Life main mission

The **Tree of Life / Sovereign Heartroot** healing arc is a primary campaign mission spine.

The player is not completing a detached checklist. Regional restoration, Eco-Kin trust, Sanctuary work, faction choices, and major story missions gradually restore the Tree of Life's living network.

Each major region contributes a distinct ecological repair objective such as water recovery, soil stabilization, canopy renewal, Blight purification, pollinator return, migration-route restoration, or atmospheric recovery.

The Tree of Life visibly changes over the campaign and never resets to an untouched perfect state. Healing preserves scars and historical memory.

### Composite ecology tags, not extra core elements

The following are **not** new elements:

- **Glaze** = Hydro/Aero frost, hail, snowpack, or ice expression.
- Sand = Terra ecology tag.
- Coastal = habitat tag.
- Harmony = one of the four public Resonance attributes and may also describe behavior; it is not an element.
- Guardian, Sentinel, Tracker, Warden, Explorer, Harvester, Reclaimer = role/title tags.

This prevents a second type chart from developing beside the established system.

## 3. Eco-Kin body categories

Body-plan categories may be used for readable field taxonomy without replacing the broader canonical class system.

- **Feral-Kin:** primarily quadrupedal mammalian body plans.
- **Sky-Kin:** winged avian, flying insect, gliding, or true-flight body plans.
- **Saur-Kin:** reptilian and amphibious crawler body plans.
- **Burrow-Kin:** digging mammals and other specialized subterranean animal body plans.

These are morphology/body-plan descriptors, not combat elements and not ownership classes.

## 4. Biomimetic and rare Morphologies

### Biomimetic Shift

A persistent regional adaptation caused by long-term habitat conditions. Examples include tundra, stormfront, mire, reef, volcanic, alpine, or desert shifts. A Biomimetic Shift must preserve species identity and believable anatomical continuity.

### Rare Variant rule

Rare variants are intentionally uncommon. They are **not** required for every species and are **not** automatically stronger than standard Eco-Kin.

#### Resonant Morph

A rare planetary-frequency variant. Only selected species can express it. Visual signs may include unusual iridescence, patterned luminescence, changed pulse behavior, or localized resonance effects.

**Important:** Resonant Morph does not mean every Eco-Kin sings, uses music, emits sound waves, or revolves around Harmony. Acoustic or musical abilities belong only to species whose biology and story justify them.

#### Heartroot-Blessed Variant

An exceptionally rare restoration-linked state found near healed Heartroot networks. It emphasizes ecological recovery traits rather than raw combat power.

#### Ancient-Tech Attuned Variant

A naturally willing Eco-Kin that has adapted to coexist with safe Ancient Tech infrastructure. Technology supports the creature's biology and never replaces its identity or agency.

#### Restored Scar Variant

A previously Blight-Touched Eco-Kin that has recovered while retaining visible, non-glorified scars. This represents healing and memory, not a prestige corruption skin.

#### STARZ* Variant

A rare approved visual/cosmic family where already established by canon. STARZ* does not become a new universal element.

## 6. Tamer of Beasts profession track

The protagonist remains the **Frequency Tamer / Veridian Keeper**. **Tamer of Beasts** is a professional frontier credential/reputation track recognized by settlements and restoration organizations.

Progression is based on **successful ethical field resolutions**, not ownership counts or forced captures.

Suggested thresholds:

- Bronze Tamer of Beasts: 100 verified field resolutions
- Silver Master Tamer: 250 verified field resolutions
- Gold Grand Warden: 500 verified field resolutions
- Legendary Apex Tamer: 1,000 verified field resolutions

A field resolution may include Bond, Release, Defer, Treat, Relocate, rescue, habitat restoration, or successful nonviolent conflict resolution. Repeatedly farming the same individual does not count.

Higher reputation can unlock harder restoration contracts, specialist rescue gear, expert NPCs, hazardous-region permits, and advanced ecological missions. It does **not** unlock stronger cages or coercive capture equipment.

## 7. Real-world interaction correction

Eco-Kin remain physically present living beings.

Allowed:

- voluntary Follow Pack behavior;
- free-roaming bonded companions;
- scent tracking and environmental warning behavior;
- Sanctuary grazing, play, feeding, grooming, rest, medical care, and habitat enrichment;
- species familiarity reducing fear when behaviorally plausible;
- voluntary task participation based on aptitude, trust, health, mood, and habitat conditions.

Not allowed as player progression:

- leashing sentient partners as default control;
- snares, cages, harpoons, pitfall capture, forced restraint, or suppressive containment;
- forced work contracts;
- ownership-based creature storage.

Emergency stabilization, rescue transport, quarantine, and relocation equipment must be safety-focused and temporary.

## 8. Contract Board correction

The Guild/Frontier Contract Board is approved as a professional reputation system, with ethical objectives.

### Threat response

Track a distressed or Blight-Touched Eco-Kin, protect civilians, identify the cause, stabilize the situation, and resolve through treatment, relocation, habitat repair, release, or voluntary Kindling.

### Apex Guardian response

Rare regional guardians require investigation, evacuation planning, ecological diagnosis, and coordinated restoration. They are not trophy hunts.

### Poacher interception

Rescue illegally restrained Eco-Kin, disable coercive devices, treat injuries/stress, document evidence, and return individuals to appropriate habitat or Sanctuary care.

### Agricultural restoration

A willing Flora-compatible Eco-Kin may assist farms through its natural abilities. Participation is temporary, trust-based, and voluntary.

### Civil restoration

Terra, Ferrum, Voltic, Hydro, or Aero Eco-Kin may voluntarily assist with blocked routes, damaged infrastructure, water systems, power recovery, or hazard mitigation when the task matches their ecology and temperament.

### Reintroduction contracts

Restore habitat first, then reintroduce appropriate species only when food, shelter, population balance, disease risk, and migration conditions support it.

## 9. Consistency rule for future Eco-Kin

Every new Eco-Kin must answer these questions before canon promotion:

1. What is its unique body plan and biological inspiration?
2. Which of the twelve canonical Essence identities does it use?
3. What habitat does it actually need?
4. What does it do besides battle?
5. What behavior earns or loses trust?
6. Does it have a rare variant? If yes, why is that variant ecologically or narratively justified?
7. Is music/sound/resonance genuinely part of this species, or was it added generically? If generic, remove it.
8. How does the species connect to the Tree of Life restoration campaign, a biome, NPC, faction, landmark, or regional problem?
