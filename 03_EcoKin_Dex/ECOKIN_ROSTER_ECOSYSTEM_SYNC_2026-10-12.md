## 2. Hidden Eco-Kin research loop

Hidden Eco-Kin are discovered through environmental investigation rather than automatic map markers.

Discovery sequence:

**Environmental clue → A.E.G.I.S. field scan → habitat stabilization → observation window → optional Kindling opportunity → Eco-Device research sync → laboratory record → Eco-Dex completion credit**

Rules:

- discovery does not equal ownership;
- scanning does not automatically bond the Eco-Kin;
- research completion can occur through observation, protection, treatment, relocation, safe release, or voluntary bond;
- laboratory data stores field observations, morphology, behavior, habitat conditions, Essence readings, and restoration impact;
- hidden Eco-Kin may refuse contact and still count toward research progression when the required non-invasive evidence is collected;
- no harmful capture spheres, ownership pods, forced cages, or sentient storage mechanics.

## 3. Ecosystem model

Every Eco-Kin roster record must connect to a living ecosystem through five required fields:

1. **Native habitat** — where the species naturally belongs.
2. **Ecological function** — what the species changes, maintains, transports, regulates, or protects.
3. **Stressors** — Blight, habitat loss, invasive pressure, pollution, weather instability, resource collapse, or faction interference.
4. **Restoration response** — how the species reacts when the environment improves.
5. **Player consequence** — what changes in traversal, resources, encounters, research, settlement safety, or story state when the ecology is restored.

Do not design Eco-Kin as isolated combat units with no environmental purpose.

## 6. Ecology differentiation rule

To prevent roster repetition and cliché creature design, every candidate must differ in at least four of these dimensions from nearby roster entries:

- body plan;
- locomotion;
- habitat;
- feeding / energy strategy;
- ecological function;
- active Essence interaction;
- social behavior;
- Kindling trigger;
- restoration response;
- traversal utility;
- combat role;
- silhouette/material language.

A visual recolor is not enough to justify a separate permanent species.
