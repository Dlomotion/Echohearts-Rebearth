## Story Function

Answer one production question clearly: **Why must this Eco-Kin exist in Echohearts?**

It should teach, reveal, restore, complicate, or emotionally change something. Battle usefulness alone is not enough to justify a permanent roster identity.

## Art / Anatomy QA

- Correct limb, digit, wing, tail, eye, gait, and body-plan counts.
- Animal Eco-Kin retain authentic body plans and locomotion across permanent stages.
- Silhouette remains readable at gameplay distance.
- Ancient Tech supports anatomy instead of replacing it.
- Permanent forms preserve biological continuity.
- Corruption is a wound/state, not a prestige cosmetic.
- No outside-franchise visual identity, name, silhouette, costume, or signature design is copied.

## Story / World QA

- Habitat supports the creature's biology and traversal.
- Abilities connect to ecology, not only combat.
- Kindling method matches behavior and agency.
- Growth does not require forced fusion, breeding exploitation, sacrifice, or sentient consumption.
- Sanctuary activity is voluntary, not forced labor.
- The Eco-Kin has at least one meaningful relationship with a biome, NPC, faction, landmark, quest, or restoration state.
