# 08_Audio

Official audio folder for the existing Echohearts: Rebearth workflow.

Store and route:

- regional and biome music direction
- Eco-Kin vocal/cry identity sheets
- A.E.G.I.S. and D.A.H.L.I.A. interface audio
- Kindling, restoration, Blight, Gloom, Ancient Tech, STARZ*, and Rift sound language
- dialogue/voice direction and recording notes
- accessibility audio cues
- combat, traversal, weather, Sanctuary, and environmental ambience specifications

Audio must reinforce story state. Restored regions should sound measurably different from wounded regions, and individual Eco-Kin should keep recognizable vocal identity across forms.

Do not import recognizable protected music, samples, character voices, or outside-franchise sound identity.
