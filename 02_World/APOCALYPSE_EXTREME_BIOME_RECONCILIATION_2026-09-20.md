## Core concept: Wounded Lands

Rebearth's harshest regions are not theme-park biomes. They are ecosystems where natural extremes, Meridian damage, Blight pressure, climate instability and ancient infrastructure failures overlap.

The player reads each region through four layers:

1. **Natural extreme** — cold, drought, pressure, altitude, wind, darkness, acidity or low oxygen.
2. **Historic wound** — war damage, Meridian machinery, failed infrastructure, abandoned settlement or ecological manipulation.
3. **Present crisis** — Blight outbreak, migration collapse, poisoned water, unstable weather, faction extraction or distressed Eco-Kin.
4. **Restoration path** — repair the cause enough for life to recover without erasing the region's natural danger or identity.

## Tree of Life integration

The **Sovereign Heartroot / Tree of Life** root network is the unifying main-mission spine for the Rebearth-side extreme domains.

Each approved Root-Wound crisis follows:

**Locate Root Wound → Read Local Ecology → Identify Human/Blight/Natural Cause → Resolve Community + Eco-Kin Conflict → Stabilize/Purify Root Node → Restore Local Flow → Persistent World Delta**

A Root-Wound mission never turns an extreme biome into a mild one. A tundra remains cold. A desert remains dry. Restoration means the system becomes viable, connected and resilient again.

## Candidate extreme domains

### 1. Crimson Corruption / working label: Sanguine Blight

**World function:** severe Blight expression across damaged Flora ecology.  
**Essence context:** **Flora / Shade**, with Blight-Touched state.  
**Hazards:** iron-rich runoff, aggressive parasitic growth, sensory/memory distortion, unsafe soil and water.

Do not literalize every old 'blood-fed land' statement. Keep the visual red/crimson identity while grounding it in corrupted pigments, mineral runoff, fungal/vascular-looking Blight growth and psychological Gloom effects where authored.

**Candidate sovereign:** Hemovore Colossus → Flora/Shade, Blight-distressed guardian concept. Name/design pending originality and anatomy review.

### 2. Polar cold desert / working label: White Silence

**World function:** driest/coldest surface survival region.  
**Essence context:** **Glaze**, with Torrent/Aero where locally justified.  
**Hazards:** whiteout, katabatic wind, ice fracture, low humidity, glare, thermal loss and isolated coastal life corridors.

Fantasy effects must remain readable against real survival logic. 'Sound freezing' may become an authored Glaze anomaly, not a universal scientific rule.

**Candidate sovereign:** Glaciarch Leviathan → Glaze/Torrent; gravity-scale legend is visual/story exaggeration pending encounter design.

### 3. Polar night/permafrost / working label: Permafrost Veil

**World function:** long darkness, frozen ground, aurora and buried pre-Collapse history.  
**Essence context:** **Glaze / Shade**.  
**Tags:** Celestial, Aurora, Permafrost, Low-Light; gravity anomalies only at authored sites.

**Candidate sovereign:** Noctyrr the Pale Stag → Glaze/Shade with celestial visual tag. It does not create a Celestial Essence.

### 4. Hyper-arid hot desert / working label: Ashen Expanse

**World function:** drought, heat, glassed scars and scarce-water survival.  
**Essence context:** **Pyre / Terra**.  
**Hazards:** heat load, dehydration, dust/sand abrasion, glare, thermal mirage, cracked Meridian solar infrastructure.

**Candidate sovereign:** Solar Basilisk → Pyre/Voltic with Solar as celestial/cycle trait, not Essence.

### 5. High alpine fracture zone / working label: Skyreach Wounds

**World function:** altitude, low oxygen, wind shear, intense UV and unstable mountain routes.  
**Essence context:** **Aero / Voltic / Terra** depending encounter.  
**Hazards:** hypoxia, lightning, rockfall, avalanche, exposed climbing and localized gravity faults.

**Candidate sovereign:** Aeralith Prime → Aero/Voltic; 'storm given feathers' is a visual metaphor, not automatic species biology.

### 6. Boreal / taiga crisis forest / working label: Blackneedle Expanse

**World function:** long winters, acidic/low-nutrient soils, conifer ecology and subterranean fungal networks.  
**Essence context:** **Flora / Shade**.  
**Hazards:** poor soils, wildfire/frost cycles, fungal Blight mimicry, limited daylight and damaged migration corridors.

**Candidate sovereign:** Mycorr King → Flora/Shade fungal-network guardian concept. Avoid mind-control as a default fungal trope; behavior must be authored ecologically.

### 7. Deep-sea extreme / working label: Abyss Unlit

**World function:** pressure, darkness, chemosynthetic life and trench restoration.  
**Essence context:** **Torrent / Shade**.  
**Hazards:** crushing pressure, thermal/chemical vents, navigation loss, darkness, Blight plumes and damaged undersea Meridian structures.

**Candidate sovereign:** Thalassyx the Unfathomed → Torrent/Shade. It is not automatically evil because it inhabits darkness.

## Offworld proposals

### Gravity Globe

**Status:** CONCEPT / future cosmic-expansion candidate.

An artificial or engineered world with authored gravity regions can support wall/ceiling traversal, orbital settlements and spatial puzzles. It must use an explicit gravity framework and readable player orientation. It cannot be treated as implemented until UE5.8 gravity, networking, camera, AI navigation and save/travel behavior are proven.

### Celestial Starboard

**Status:** CONCEPT / future cosmic-expansion candidate.

A living or ancient transit-world / starship-world may become a late-game navigation hub tied to Star Rewrite. It must not replace the existing Echo-Warp/Riftway world structure or become a deus-ex-machina weapon/map/judge without story limits.

### Prismatic Lacewing

**Status:** APPROVED-PENDING alien/celestial Eco-Kin candidate.

- Working anatomy: lacewing/insect-inspired, requiring correct six-leg invertebrate anatomy if retained.
- Potential role: rare navigator / refraction / probability-visualization ecology.
- It does not literally guarantee knowledge of future outcomes.
- Final Essences, class, EcoKinID, rarity and story role remain pending Dex review.

## Floating player territory / metropolis concept

Historical **Aetherhold Ascendant** is not promoted as a replacement city. The useful idea is a late-game **player-developed floating Sanctuary district/estate or sky settlement** connected to the established world.

Requirements before promotion:
- clear relationship to Echohearts City / Sky Haven;
- no contradiction with true Eco-Kin flight/travel rules;
- building limits and persistent-state ownership rules;
- multiplayer permissions if shared;
- World Partition/streaming/save proof;
- original final name.

## Magical creatures rule

'Fantasy' does not mean every Eco-Kin receives the same mystical treatment. Creature identity should begin with anatomy, habitat, behavior and Essence, then add authored supernatural traits. Rare cosmic, Primal, Resonant or magical phenomena remain special enough to feel rare.

## Gameplay consequences

Every extreme-domain mission should provide:
- one survival/traversal challenge;
- one Eco-Kin behavioral/ecological problem;
- one human/faction pressure or historical cause when appropriate;
- one non-combat resolution path where fiction supports it;
- one persistent before/after world-state change;
- one Tree of Life/root-network consequence for campaign-connected domains;
- one reason to revisit after restoration.
