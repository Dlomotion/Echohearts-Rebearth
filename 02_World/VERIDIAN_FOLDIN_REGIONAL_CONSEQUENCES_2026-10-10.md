# Echohearts: Rebearth — Veridian Fold-In Regional Consequence Matrix

**Status:** CONCEPT / WORLD ROUTING  
**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Story source:** `01_Story/VERIDIAN_FOLDIN_MISSION_CHAINS_2026-10-10.md`  
**Canon effect:** none until explicit review.

## Purpose

This document isolates the persistent world-state consequences from the Veridian Fold-In mission chains.

It does **not** create:
- a new morality meter;
- a second Kindling system;
- a Star-specific percentage track;
- a new Essence;
- a replacement world map;
- a new faction definition.

All consequences are authored world/NPC/mission states.

## Global rules

- Planet name is **Rebearth**.
- Eco-Kin remain autonomous partners.
- Shadow-altered is a condition descriptor, not a faction and not a replacement for Shade Essence.
- Kindling remains the only relationship percentage.
- Resonance Alignment is a story-state flag gated by Kindling and authored mission conditions.
- Anima-Link strain remains bidirectional.
- Every region keeps a visible scar after completion.
- Restoration can improve conditions but does not reset a region to an untouched state.

## Required visible scars

### Verdantia Fields
At least one grove must remain visibly altered after VF-01.

### Driftmist Peaks
Blackened fire corridors must remain visible beside regrowth. The extent is branch-dependent.

### Moonlit Marsh
The water-state layout must visibly encode the player's allocation decision:
- one strongly restored channel plus one blighted channel; or
- two contained/managed channels.

### Crystalgrove Hollow
A subset of crystal formations remains permanently dim. The number/extent depends on relay-study duration.

### Evershade Wilds
The sealed rift becomes a monitored dead zone that alters local traversal/ecology.

### Ashfall Dominion
Collapsed terrain remains buried and the settlement's resettlement footprint changes.

### Convergence foundry zone
Purification and destruction produce different permanent environmental aftermaths. Neither restores the location perfectly.

## NPC/world memory

These are discrete remembered states unless a separately approved reputation system later owns them.

- Verdantia settlement remembers whether immediate safety or deeper purification was prioritized.
- Driftmist shepherds remember whether herds/ecology or technical evidence carried the greater cost.
- Moonlit communities remember resource allocation separately.
- Crystalgrove wardens remember how much light was sacrificed for data.
- Evershade trackers remember the operative decision.
- Ashfall survivors remember the evacuation/device decision.
- Professor Jackson remembers which evidence survived across all chains.

D.A.H.L.I.A. may summarize these facts from authoritative mission/world state. It does not invent or rewrite them.

## Star presentation rule

Nova, Eclipse, Nebula, Black Hole, and Shooting Star remain **unclassified Star entities** in this concept package.

A.E.G.I.S. may initially render them as:
`UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE`

Until production/Dex review resolves classification:
- do not assign Permanent Dex IDs;
- do not call them confirmed Eco-Kin in data authority;
- do not create new Essence categories for them;
- do not create a separate Star Bond numerical progression system.

## Level-design handoff

Each mission level should expose:
1. the immediate crisis;
2. the readable ecological/systemic cause;
3. at least one non-destructive intervention path;
4. Anima-Link pressure where an active Eco-Kin partner is involved;
5. a persistent visual consequence;
6. an explicit mission/world-state flag;
7. a later callback in VF-07 or post-chain world state.

No world-state branch may erase the user's earlier choice during convergence.
