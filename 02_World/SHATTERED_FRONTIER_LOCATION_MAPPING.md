## World-state rule

Every restored location must have at least one persistent visible delta: cleaner water, returned wildlife, reopened trails, repaired infrastructure, changed NPC schedules, safer migration, restored vegetation, altered Blight pressure, new archive access, or Sanctuary support.

## Shattered Frontier tone

The name **Shattered Frontier** survives as a storyline/quest-arc label describing the boundary between restored settlements and unstable wilderness. It is not a replacement continent, era, or parallel world map.
