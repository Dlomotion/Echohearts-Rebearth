## 3. What is kept, with working names

All names are placeholders. None is canon. Each needs the exact/fuzzy name and originality review before use.

- **Restoration spine:** the player restores a wounded region by healing its Eco-Scars, not by importing life. Maps to the existing Sovereign Heartroot / Tree of Life campaign spine. No duplicate tree.
- **Heartseed Vaults** (was "Echoheart Nodes"): hidden pre-Collapse vaults holding living-system memory. Maps to Meridian Nodes and archives. Reward: lore, region unlocks, Resonance Trait unlocks (non-destructive).
- **Bloomworks** (was "Atmospheric Stabilizers / Bio-Domes"): player-built restoration structures. Maps to Sanctuary.
- **Meridian Wardens** (was "rogue drones"): automated guardians from the Meridian Age that read restoration as intrusion until their Heart-Code is read, calmed and not merely destroyed. Fits the thesis that safety must not mean command over life.
- **Rebearth Compass Archive** (was "Aria's record database"): a working name for an in-world archive voiced through the existing A.E.G.I.S. interface. Do not create a separate AI character without review.
- **Final goal:** a planet-scale restoration climax that ties into Star Rewrite. It must respect "No absolute Purity. No perfect reset. Rebearth survives with memory."

## 4. Eco-Kin Dex: secret Eco-Kin and secret variables

The Dex stays at **125 permanent IDs**. This proposal does not add, renumber or promote any ID. Existing Dex governance applies to anything below.

**Secret Eco-Kin (proposal):**
- A secret Eco-Kin is a **hidden state of an existing permanent identity** (a hidden form or discovery state) **or** an unrevealed entry already counted in the 125. It must not create a 126th permanent species.
- Any new candidate goes to `03_EcoKin_Dex/Intake` as `PROPOSAL` with the full review: name, species-role, anatomy, ecology, originality, source image, ownership, roster capacity.
- Dex presentation: undiscovered entries show as silhouette and ID placeholder only. Names, stats and forms are not exposed before discovery.

**Secret variables (proposal):**
- Hidden flags may include: discovery conditions, hidden form unlocks, region/season/weather gates, and relationship or Eco-Scar thresholds.
- The Master Game Bible reserves **five secret ending variants**. Their names and condition bundles are not locked, and this proposal does not name them.
- Requirements for implementation, to be placed in the build repo: server-authoritative evaluation, deterministic validation, versioned save migration, reversible failure handling, no client-trusted unlocks. The data contract and schema must be approved before any enum or DataAsset exists.
