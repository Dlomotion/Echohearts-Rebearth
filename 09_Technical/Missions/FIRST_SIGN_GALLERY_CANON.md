## First Sign — authored tutorial beats
1. Receive the field briefing at Deepwood; identify natural traces without disturbing habitats.
2. Observe track spacing, feeding marks and a naturally shed trace; never remove material from an Eco-Kin.
3. Match environmental observations to A.E.G.I.S. scan evidence and the approved Dex record.
4. Approach a safe observation point; remain outside the Eco-Kin's comfort boundary.
5. Capture a **photograph**, not the Eco-Kin; submit a verified observation to D.A.H.L.I.A.
6. Return to the Chronicler; unlock research-gallery submission after provenance checks.

## Gallery validation

A print never grants Binding rights, Kindling, materials or a new species. Preserve Echoprint identity. If an individual declines interaction, keep distance. No speculative Dex IDs.
