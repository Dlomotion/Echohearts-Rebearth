## AI Mistake Patch / Continuity QA

The new material is accepted with these corrections:

1. The nine **Rebearth Essences** replace the previous front-facing 12-element chart.
2. **Tamer of Beasts** becomes the player profession; **Veridian Keeper** remains the restoration/ethics tradition.
3. Tamer rank is based on documented field resolutions, not sentient ownership counts.
4. Trapping/caging language is not a normal player ownership loop. Rescue, relocation, calming, treatment, and voluntary Kindling remain valid.
5. Eco-Kin assistance on farms, construction, restoration, or settlement work is voluntary and governed by preference, health, Kindling, fatigue, and aptitude.
6. Resonance is broader than music. Most Eco-Kin are not song/harmony creatures.
7. Rare variants exist, but are special. They do not become the normal presentation of the entire roster.
8. The wounded **Sovereign Heartroot** is the **Tree of Life** and is a main campaign restoration objective.

# 1. Rebearth Essence Chart

The planet's natural flows are called **Essences**.

| Essence | Core identity | Primary environmental fantasy | Strong against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flora** | growth, roots, canopy, regeneration | forests, crops, overgrowth, restoration | Terra, Torrent |
| **Torrent** | rivers, tides, rain, deep water | oceans, wetlands, currents, flood control | Pyre, Terra |
| **Pyre** | heat, ash, ember, magma | volcanic zones, wildfire, thermal pressure | Glaze, Flora |
| **Terra** | stone, soil, dust, mountain roots | cliffs, caves, bedrock, erosion control | Glaze, Voltic |
| **Aero** | wind, pressure, lift, atmosphere | cliffs, storms, flight lanes, seed dispersal | Flora, Shade |
| **Glaze** | frost, hail, snow, sheet ice | alpine regions, cryogenic effects, snowpack | Voltic, Torrent |
| **Voltic** | electricity, storms, conductive current | lightning zones, power transfer, charged weather | Aero, Torrent |
| **Aura** | spirit, life-force, illumination, clarity | restoration fields, spirit phenomena, protective projection | Shade, Terra |
| **Shade** | deep night, decay, entropy, void pressure | caves, eclipse zones, fear-space, destabilized ecosystems | Aura, Pyre, Flora |

### Balance rule

The matchup directions above are **canon design relationships**, but final damage multipliers, resistance values, Break modifiers, PvP coefficients, and status interactions remain subject to gameplay testing.

### Legacy migration map

- Hydro → Torrent
- Magma → Pyre
- Volt → Voltic
- Spirit → Aura
- Radiant → Aura or rare visual/affinity tag depending on species
- Solar / Lunar → celestial or cycle tags, not primary Essences
- Iron/Tech → Ancient Tech / synthetic trait, not an Essence
- Chaos → instability/Blight/behavior state, not an Essence
- Flora / Terra / Aero remain directly compatible

No second combat-element taxonomy should be created beside the Essence chart.

# 2. Harmony and Resonance are not universal music themes

**Harmony** remains a public attribute measuring relational/ecological stability.

**Resonance** is a general physical and biological principle. It can be expressed through:

- electrical discharge
- bioluminescence
- pressure waves
- temperature changes
- magnetic orientation
- root communication
- scent and pheromone response
- water movement
- vibration
- body posture
- spirit projection
- sound

Only certain Eco-Kin should have explicit musical, acoustic, rhythm, choir, song, or instrument-like mechanics.

## Sonic-specialist rule

A species receives a sonic/music identity only when at least one of the following is true:

- its biology naturally depends on acoustic signaling;
- its ecology uses echolocation, calls, drumming, vibration, or resonance chambers;
- its story role is tied to a song, choir, ritual, sound landmark, or acoustic ruin;
- it has a rare variant specifically authored around a sonic phenomenon.

Do not retrofit music motifs onto unrelated species just because the setting uses Resonance terminology.

# 3. Kin field categories

The following are **body/ecology tags**, not Essences:

- **Feral-Kin** — terrestrial mammalian predators, grazers, canines, felines, and similar quadrupeds.
- **Sky-Kin** — winged or sustained-flight animals.
- **Saur-Kin** — scaled reptilian or amphibious body plans where appropriate.
- **Burrow-Kin** — subterranean or digging specialists.

These tags describe anatomy and encounter behavior. They do not replace the broader Eco-Kin class structure and must never be used as elemental damage types.

# 4. Eco-Kin morphologies

## Biomimetic Shifts

Environment-driven adaptations tied to long-term habitat pressure.

Approved examples:
- High Crags
- Stormfront
- Tundra
- Sun-Meadow
- Deep Mire
- Coral Shallows
- Twilight Canopy

A Biomimetic Shift can change coloration, coat/scale texture, locomotion support, resistances, field skill, or secondary Essence while preserving recognizable species identity.

## Resonant Morphs

**Rare variant class.** A Resonant Morph occurs when an individual develops unusually strong alignment with Rebearth's living systems.

Rules:
- rare by default;
- species-specific visual treatment;
- not automatically stronger in every stat;
- may alter one field behavior, passive, or signature interaction;
- does not automatically change Essence;
- does not require music or sound;
- cannot erase anatomy or identity;
- rarity values remain a balance-data decision, not a lore promise.

Possible expressions include iridescent keratin, unusual eye-glow, translucent tissue, crystalline patterning, atmospheric particles, heat shimmer, static arcs, shifting leaf pigment, or altered shadow behavior.

## Primal Morphs

Ancient, guardian-scale, or deeply matured expressions associated with long-lived habitats and major ecological responsibilities.

Rules:
- not merely 'bigger Alpha' skins;
- may function as sovereign/Titan encounters;
- often tied to biome history or major story objectives;
- cannot be farmed as ordinary capture rewards;
- Bond, alliance, release, treatment, relocation, or non-roster outcomes are all valid.

## Sonic rare subset

A small subset of Resonant Morphs may become **Chorus Morphs** as an internal working label for species with genuine acoustic biology. This is a specialist rare-variant family, not a global evolution tier.

# 5. Example Eco-Kin line correction

Working example: fox/fawn-like Eco-Kin with **Flora / Aura**.

### Base Morph
- body identity: animal Eco-Kin, not humanoidized;
- ecology: restores small patches of damaged vegetation;
- heartbeat may influence nearby root growth, but this does not automatically make every ability musical.

### Stormfront Shift

- darkened jagged foliage
- conductive antler/crest structures
- electrical charge redirected into plant restoration or defensive pulses

### Tundra Shift
- Flora / Glaze
- frozen vine/crystal structures
- cooling mist and snowpack interaction
- calming effect may be thermal/scent/behavioral rather than song-based

### Twilight Shift
- Flora / Shade

# 6. Tamer of Beasts profession

**Tamer of Beasts** is the recognized field profession.

**Veridian Keeper** is the ethical/restoration discipline that defines how a responsible Tamer works with living beings.

The profession supports:
- wildlife tracking
- behavioral reading
- calming
- rescue
- relocation
- treatment
- Kindling
- habitat restoration
- contract response
- dangerous-area certification
- Primal/Titan incident response

## Rank ladder

The user's four-rank concept is retained, but the counter is **successful certified field resolutions**, not owned Eco-Kin.

| Rank | Working threshold | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| **Tamer of Beasts — Bronze** | 100 resolutions | proven field safety and basic behavioral competency |
| **Master Tamer — Silver** | 250 resolutions | trusted with high-risk predators, migrations, and complex rescues |
| **Grand Warden — Gold** | 500 resolutions | authorized for regional ecological crises and Primal/Titan response |
| **Apex Tamer — Legendary** | 1,000+ resolutions | world-renowned record; some wild populations may begin encounters with lower fear/aggression when reputation conditions are met |

A resolution may be:
- Bond
- Release
- Defer
- Treat
- Relocate
- Escort to Havenlink/Sanctuary
- restore the habitat so intervention is no longer needed

Exact thresholds are currently a **design target** and remain subject to pacing tests.

# 7. Field interactions

## Follow Pack

The player can travel with a small visible set of active partners while the larger field roster follows existing battle rules.

- default is voluntary free-roaming behavior;
- safety lead-lines may exist only for individuals that accept or require them during rescue/travel;
- partners can detect spoor, hazards, hidden resources, weather shifts, or predators according to species ability;
- a same-species companion may reduce fear or aggression only if behavior, territory, social rank, season, and context support it.

## Warden Homestead / Sanctuary

Eco-Kin live physically in appropriate spaces rather than disappearing into item storage.

Care may include:
- feeding
- brushing/grooming where species-appropriate
- play/enrichment
- medical care
- rest
- habitat maintenance
- social grouping
- voluntary work/task participation

# 8. Guild Board and contracts

Keep the strong open-world professional fantasy, but route it through non-coercive outcomes.

### Wildlife Resolution Contracts
A dangerous Eco-Kin threatens a settlement or ecosystem. Track the cause, stabilize the situation, and choose the correct outcome.

### Primal Warden Contracts
High-danger regional incidents involving Primal/Titan beings. These focus on survival, environmental counterplay, calming, purification, evacuation, or restoring the source of distress.

### Poacher Interception
Locate illegal traps/cages, rescue captured Eco-Kin, secure evidence, calm trauma responses, and return survivors to Havenlink or habitat.

### Agricultural Support
A partner with a compatible aptitude may voluntarily assist with soil restoration, irrigation, pollination, seed dispersal, or crop recovery.

### Civil / Infrastructure Support
Terra, Voltic, Aero, Torrent, or other appropriate partners may assist with safe road clearing, emergency power, water control, debris removal, or storm response when the individual is willing and the activity is species-safe.

### Eco-Restoration Contracts
Rebuild habitat, restore migration corridors, reintroduce appropriate populations, purify water/soil, or stabilize invasive/Blight-driven ecological imbalance.

No contract treats Eco-Kin as rented machinery.

# 9. Tree of Life main campaign mission

The **Sovereign Heartroot**, commonly called the **Tree of Life**, is one of the central living systems of Rebearth.

The campaign repeatedly returns to its damaged root network.

Core progression:

**Find Root Wound → Diagnose Cause → Restore Local Biome → Resolve Eco-Kin/NPC Conflict → Purify or Stabilize Root Node → Return Flow to Heartroot → Unlock Persistent World Change**

Each restored root should affect at least two systems such as:
- biome health
- migration
- farming
- water quality
- Sanctuary capacity
- travel route
- Eco-Kin spawn behavior
- settlement safety
- story memory
- faction pressure
- endgame state

The final Tree of Life state reflects the player's actual restoration history. It never becomes an untouched perfect reset.
