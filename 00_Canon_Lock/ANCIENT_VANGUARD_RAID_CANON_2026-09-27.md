The **Ancient Vanguard** is preserved as a five-member historical defense formation encountered through Chrono-Echo records tied to the Great Collapse / Spacetime Conflict material and later Iron Assembly interference.

The five names are:

1. **Aegis-Core — The Indomitable Bastion**
2. **Forge-Titan — The Kinetic Arsenal**
3. **Voltic-Sovereign — The Storm-Bringer**
4. **Behemoth-Goliath — The Jade Monolith**
5. **Shadow-Stalker — The Void Specter / Marksman**

These are approved as **named Ancient Vanguard encounter identities**. They are not automatically promoted into the 125-ID Permanent Eco-Kin Dex merely because this story packet exists.

## 2. Relationship to the Chrono-Structural Sandbox

The Vanguard appears through **Chrono-Echo Anchoring**, not possession or body replacement.

The player remains physically present as the modern **Core-Binder** with A.E.G.I.S. and bonded autonomous Eco-Kin. The old concept in which the player remains hidden in a sleep pod while directly possessing Eco-Kin bodies remains retired from main-campaign canon.

Vanguard missions use bounded Chrono-Rift records. They may expose ancient battlefields, damaged archive states and future projections without rewriting established history or duplicating persistent inventory.

## 3. Iron Assembly corruption event

The Iron Assembly can attack preserved historical data by injecting a hostile **Necro-Strain / Blight resonance signal** into Chrono-Rift archive layers.

This creates corrupted **Vanguard Echo Boss manifestations**.

Important identity rule:

- the corrupted manifestation is a damaged temporal/resonance state, not proof that the original historical Vanguard willingly became evil;
- corruption is a state, not a new species;
- defeating the manifestation means **cleansing and stabilizing the record**, not harvesting a sentient body;
- raid rewards must come from purified anomaly residue, shed corruption structures, Ancient Tech hardware, restored-biome growth, or recovered non-sentient equipment.

## 6. The First Invasion historical sequence

Working sequence title: **The First Invasion — Vanguard Record**.

**Causality:** C1 Immutable Anchor or C3 Contested Record depending final story placement.

The player views or enters a bounded archive of an Iron Assembly assault. D.A.H.L.I.A. manages archive telemetry, gravity warnings, threat markers and A.E.G.I.S. integrity.

The scene preserves these beats:

1. orbital anchors tear open the battle sky;
2. Aegis-Core creates a defensive lattice against the first barrage;
3. Forge-Titan breaks a lead Assembly warship's protected firing line;
4. Voltic-Sovereign collapses the storm grid onto automated attackers;
5. Behemoth-Goliath destroys a landing vector through mass and terrain force;
6. Shadow-Stalker identifies the exposed command-energy network;
7. the Vanguard synchronizes around an **Echoheart Sync** climax;
8. the archive ends with the sector stabilized, not a literal universal “data wipe” that deletes historical continuity.

The modern player cannot change the locked outcome of a C1 event. The gameplay objective is to protect the record from corruption and learn the truth.

## 7. D.A.H.L.I.A. continuity

D.A.H.L.I.A. remains the ethical/systems intelligence associated with the player's technology stack where already established.

- no infinite off-screen generation;
- no duplication through rewind, disconnect, travel or replay.
