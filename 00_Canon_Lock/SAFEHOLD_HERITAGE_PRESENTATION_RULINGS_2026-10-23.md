# Safehold, Heritage and Presentation rulings

Status: CANON design ruling by Tay, supplied and reaffirmed 2026-10-25. Implementation status is separate. This addendum refines existing locks; it does not replace the Master Game Bible, Master Project Index, Permanent Dex or art manifest.

## Safehold

Safehold is temporary, non-damaging rescue containment, permitted only while an Eco-Kin in Rage, Alpha frenzy, Blight or Hollow is actively violent. Never deploy it on a calm Eco-Kin or use it for collection. It becomes escapable immediately upon calm.

The sequence is **Safehold → Purity → the Ask**. Purity clears confusion; the free choice, ceremony and dignity timer remain. Trust is earned afterward through missions, actions and Growth Rites, never granted by restraint. This is a crisis entry into the existing voluntary bonding loop, not a second ownership system.

Approved family: Heart Fruit Lure Cradle, Softfield Corral, Grounding Anchor Set, Haven Mesh, Quiet Shade Screen and Multi-Anchor Safehold for Alpha/Omega-class events. Player rescue opens toward the creature; villains' cages close upon it. Both purpose and visible behavior must express this difference.

## Heritage Pairing

Two partners who both choose may reproduce in Sanctuary or the wild according to their species' life cycle. The player is an honored witness and sometimes a chosen raiser. Offspring are new individuals with forming Echoprints, not new Dex species.

Player-operated breeding, selection for optimized traits, IV production, offspring as commodities, nursery factories and hybrids for power remain excluded. There is no universal death-to-egg rule. This supersedes blanket statements that all reproduction is forbidden.

## Nine Essences, eighteen expressions

The Link, ceremony and existing essence enum retain nine Essences. A.E.G.I.S. field labels and move families use two expressions per Essence. These are not eighteen new essence enum values. Chaos is an untuned state, not an Essence or type.

## Dimorphic expressions

One species Echoprint may have female and male expressions. Signature marks and identity persist through every Ecoform and Shimmer Form. Species ecology can vary silhouette, ornamentation, marking pattern and size within its range. No global size, color, costume or personality stereotype applies. Temperament, role and strength remain individual.

Kindrel children have no adult expression display. Singular beings such as Undalume/Nature-class do not acquire a pair template. Proto/Monera morphs and Synth-Origin lab lines are filed per entry; Humanoid-Kin remain persons. Bramblecub flowering/thorn crowns, Prismara waterfall mane/crystal crest and Chromapaw filigree/bold patterns are approved directions to reconcile with existing portraits, not evidence that missing sheets were inspected. Expression never changes Dex identity or creates a breeding optimization system.

## Presentation

Judge each mechanic by what the player sees and what the Eco-Kin experiences. Rescue, chosen family and kindness extended first express divine grace. Labels alone cannot turn coercive behavior into care.
