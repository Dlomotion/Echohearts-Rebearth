## Story progression
Guest → Trusted → Keeper → Witness → Chosen Savior.
Act I M1–M4: First Sign / Deepwood; Act II M5–M30: Corrupted Mainland; Act III M31–M33: syndicates Gilt Hand, Cinderline, Quiet Ledger; Act IV M34–M35: forged Sky Archive Timeline page and unforced verdict.
Field School methodology: Observe → Study → Assist → Understand → Restore. The Timeline is studied, never wielded.
Boss-fight classification of M15, M21, M27 does not supply a new named boss identity. M2 is the suffering colossal's vent-clearing encounter, not a kill encounter.
Wild EcoKin yield, withdraw or recover rather than being killed by the player; Binding is by choice and never a mission reward. Neither Binding, Kindling nor Growth Rites may be gated by mission/milestone completion. Anima-Link strain and partner refusal are honored.
