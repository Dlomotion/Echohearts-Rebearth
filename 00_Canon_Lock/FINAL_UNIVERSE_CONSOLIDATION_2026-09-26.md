## 2. Final creature governance

The final creature-production rule is:

1. **125 Permanent EcoKinIDs** = shippable identity authority.
2. **Forms Registry** = approved forms, mutations, reversible states, seasonal states, Shimmer overlays, purified/deviant states, and other identity-preserving transformations.
3. **Historical Naming Pool** = preserved recovered names and concepts; not automatic canon.
4. **Art Manifest** = visual source/provenance/status; an image never creates a new species by itself.
5. **Production gate** = mesh + rig + animation + physics + gameplay + habitat + audio/VFX + save/network + QA evidence before a creature is called production-complete.

No new chat-generated creature may silently become `DEX-126` or displace an existing permanent identity.

## 5. Great Somatic Fracture / Aurelian history reconciliation

The **Aurelian Hierarchy** and **Great Somatic Fracture** are retained as **far-cosmic antiquity** discovered through later Star Rewrite / Rift history. They do not overwrite Rebearth's Six Eras.

Canonical placement:

1. **Far-Cosmic Antiquity** — Aurelian civilization pursued biological control, immortality, and dimensional manipulation.
2. **Great Somatic Fracture** — an ancient off-world/dimensional catastrophe destabilized relationships between body, memory, Resonance, and space.
3. **Rebearth's Six Eras** — First Chorus → Meridian Age → War on Humanity → Great Collapse → Rebearth Awakening → Star Rewrite remain the local historical spine.
4. Aurelian ruins, stranded technology, descendants, or remnant factions may be discovered later, but they are not allowed to erase Meridian history or become the retroactive cause of every Eco-Kin.

### Aether-Stitcher correction

`Aether-Stitcher` may survive as an **ancient Aurelian research/program designation** associated with prohibited consciousness-link experiments. It is not the universal modern player profession and does not authorize direct possession of Eco-Kin bodies.

The old idea that the entire playable character is secretly a sleeping child permanently piloting monster bodies is **not main-campaign canon**. Its useful psychological horror is retained as an Aurelian archive/failed-program story possibility. Modern Eco-Kin remain autonomous beings.
