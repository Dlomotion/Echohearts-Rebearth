© 2026 Into Deep Studios and Donta L. Owens. All rights reserved.

Status: CANON WORKFLOW RULE.

## Scope
The official Echohearts character-development workflow applies to the entire character universe, not only characters visible in a specific image, chat, or reference sheet.

Included:
- all established and proposed Eco-Kin
- Animal Eco-Kin
- Humanoid-Kin
- Guardians, Titans, Celestial Echoes
- humans, scientists, civilians and Sanctuary residents
- mentors, rivals and quest NPCs
- villains, faction leaders and bosses
- STARZ* / Saviors presentation characters where canon-compatible
- regional forms, Growth Rites, approved variants and approved mutations
- legacy candidates preserved for review

## One-workflow rule
No second Master Bible, Dex, GDD, roster, character registry, art pipeline, or game-development workflow may be created for these characters. Every character routes through the existing MASTER_PROJECT_INDEX and folders 00_Canon_Lock through 11_Publication, with rejected/superseded work routed to 99_Reference_Retired_Needs_Redesign.

## Required character pipeline
Continuity/canon review
→ classification
→ character registry / EcoDex
→ uniqueness/DNA audit
→ naming
→ biology/anatomy/silhouette

→ gameplay role

→ ecology/social role

→ biome/habitat
→ A.E.G.I.S./Ancient Tech/STARZ* fit
→ Bond/Growth Rite/forms
→ UE5.8 model/materials/rig/animation
→ VFX/audio
→ data implementation
→ PvE/PvP/Arena/world integration
→ balance/accessibility/QA
→ publication

## Canon priority
Current approved Rebearth canon overrides contradictory historical card text, generated labels, old element taxonomies, malformed measurements, duplicate names, forced evolution/fusion language, and unsupported lore.

## Heart Statement gate
No character is publication-ready until it contributes to at least one:
- This world needs me.
- These Eco-Kin are alive.
- My choices changed Rebearth.
