# ECHOHEARTS: REBEARTH — SEASONAL CORE GAMEPLAY RECONCILIATION

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Status:** CANON-CORRECTED SYSTEM DIRECTION / IMPLEMENTATION PENDING

## 1. Party contract

Preserve the current campaign party structure:

- player carries/coordinates an **8-Eco-Kin field roster**;
- **3 active Eco-Kin** may participate in the current real-time combat encounter where the system contract applies;
- remaining roster partners remain available through authored swap/recovery rules;
- each active partner may fill attacker, defender, supporter/control, healer/restoration, traversal, or hybrid tactical roles;
- duplicate-species/identity restrictions remain mode/balance authored rather than assumed universally.

This is not a turn-based main campaign.

## 2. Main campaign combat

Main campaign combat remains **real-time third-person action**.

The Core-Binder fights alongside autonomous Eco-Kin using movement, dodging, timing, A.E.G.I.S. tools, commands, Resonance abilities, environmental interactions, and eligible weapons.

Weapons may include original Echohearts implementations of melee/ranged categories such as:

- swords/blades;
- bows;
- spears/polearms;
- staff/tuner tools;
- nunchaku-like flexible weapons where animation/readability is safe;
- firearms or launchers where story, rating, balance, animation and platform policy support them.

A weapon category existing in design does not mean every weapon is available at launch.

## 3. Tactical / turn-based play remains bounded

Turn-based, timeline, automated-squad, card-driven or tactical-grid play is allowed in approved secondary contexts such as:

- Resonance Arena;
- Harmony Circuit;
- EchoDeck simulations;
- sanctioned competitive/training modes;
- selected Chrono simulations;
- optional strategy/base-defense interfaces.

These modes do not convert exploration and the main story campaign into turn-based play.

## 4. Bonding replaces forced capture

Retire the generic `capture Eco-Kin with success-rate traps` loop as the primary relationship mechanic.

Canonical field relationship loop remains:

**Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer**

A.E.G.I.S. may use nonlethal field tools to stabilize a dangerous area, calm corruption, create a safe approach, identify a compatible frequency, or protect the Eco-Kin/player. The device does not turn a sentient Eco-Kin into owned inventory.

Historical `capture rate`, `capture fragment`, and `failed capture` language routes to reference-only material unless specifically redesigned into non-sentient scanning/research progression.

## 5. Roster access / Sanctuary storage correction

Retire `digital PC storage` language for living Eco-Kin.

Use the **Sanctuary / Havenlink roster network**:

- bonded partners live in appropriate habitats, sanctuaries, expeditions, or story locations;
- A.E.G.I.S. provides roster records and eligible recall/deployment interfaces;
- the UI may look like a roster manager, but the fiction does not compress sentient partners into transferable inventory files by default.

## 6. Growth and evolution

Retain:

- species-authored evolution paths;
- Growth Rites;
- Resonance Branches;
- special catalysts/items;
- Shimmer Form;
- mutation/form branches;
- regional/seasonal variants;
- reversible corrupted/purified states where authored.

`Evolution Stones` may be reinterpreted as **Resonance Catalysts / Growth Rite catalysts** rather than copied franchise terminology.

Forced de-evolution/degeneration stat farming is not a global progression requirement.

## 7. Breeding / heredity / fusion correction

Eligible biological breeding may exist only where an Eco-Kin species design explicitly allows it and current ethics/rarity rules permit it.

Retire as the normal system:

- forced cross-species breeding to optimize stats;
- combining two sentient Eco-Kin into one body;
- consuming one creature as fusion material;
- creating arbitrary hybrids solely from stat recipes;
- breeding Legendary/unique beings where they are locked as non-breedable.

Preserve the useful customization fantasy through:

- inherited biological traits where appropriate;
- Resonance Trait Infusion;
- Bio-Synergy Weaving;
- Growth Rite choices;
- environmental adaptation;
- non-sentient equipment/catalyst systems.

## 8. Critical defeat and Eco-Egg correction

An Eco-Kin reaching zero combat health does **not** automatically die and become an egg with better stats.

Default loop:

- incapacitation / critical condition;
- rescue/extraction;
- Healing Incubator / Sanctuary recovery;
- lasting stress or temporary injury where authored;
- relationship/story consequences for reckless treatment.

Eco-Eggs remain valid only for species/life-cycle/story systems where eggs are biologically or cosmologically appropriate. They cannot become a repeatable death-to-stat-upgrade exploit.

## 9. Sanctuary work becomes voluntary Aptitudes

Preserve farming, watering, mining, hauling, crafting support, healing, exploration dispatch and other base utility as **Sanctuary Aptitudes / Partner Assist**.

Eco-Kin are not forced factory workers.

The system should account for:

- habitat compatibility;
- energy/fatigue;
- stress/sanity where the survival design uses it;
- food/water/care;
- willingness/availability;
- Kindling relationship;
- task suitability;
- safety;
- ecology/resource limits.

## 10. Reclamation Cohorts — support-unit name

The historical request for Eco-Kin-led `troops` is normalized into:

# **Reclamation Cohorts**

Reclamation Cohorts are voluntary NPC/construct/logistics teams organized through the Sanctuary/Alliance network.

They may support:

- defense;
- rescue;
- surveying;
- material transport;
- restoration;
- expedition supply;
- emergency evacuation;
- anti-Blight containment.

They are not disposable armies owned by Eco-Kin, and the Power score does not replace the project's relationship/ecology progression.

## 11. Base / Homeland / Sky Island

Preserve the useful two-hub fantasy:

- **Homeland / Sanctuary land base** — rebuilding, farming, housing, hospitals/healing, hatchery/incubator where appropriate, crafting, community NPC invitations, decoration, habitat care;
- **Sky Island / Meridian-linked information hub** — research, story intelligence, world-state review, Dr./scientist debriefs, Rift/portal information and higher-level expedition planning.

Exact naming/location placement must continue through the current world/level registry.

## 12. Exploration and mounts

Preserve large-world traversal using eligible Eco-Kin partners for:

- riding;
- swimming;
- flight;
- climbing/special traversal;
- puzzle cooperation;
- environmental access.

Mount use requires species-appropriate anatomy, trust/Kindling, safety and animation support.

## 13. Multiplayer targets

Working multiplayer targets may include:

- cooperative exploration;
- raid instances;
- private/social hubs;
- competitive modes;
- dedicated server experiments;
- up to 4-player co-op as a bounded early target;
- larger capacities such as 32-player servers only after profiling/network evidence.

`32 players`, `100-member guilds`, Server-vs-Server and similar values are **capacity/product proposals**, not verified runtime promises.

## 14. Competitive mode correction

Historical `Egg Heist` is retired because sentient/reproductive Eco-Kin assets should not be treated as stealable match loot.

Working original replacement:

# **Resonance Relay**

PvEvP teams recover and secure **non-sentient Resonance Cores / restoration beacons / data relays** while surviving hostile anomalies and opposing teams.

Competitive scoring should favor objectives, restoration, control, assists and tactical success rather than reducing the entire game to kill-count rankings.

## 15. Guild / Shrine play

Guild/alliance systems may support:

- shared Sanctuary projects;
- restoration campaigns;
- regional research;
- guild housing;
- cooperative raid progression;
- seasonal leaderboards;
- defending/repairing Shrines and Sanctums.

Shrine bosses are not `captured` as property. A Sovereign/guardian encounter can end in cleansing, earned trust, alliance, migration, release or story-specific Kindling.

## 16. EchoDeck strategy layer

The historical `Yu-Gi-Oh type` reference routes to the existing **EchoDeck** simulation/card system.

External franchise rules, names, art, card layouts and proprietary mechanics remain reference-only.

EchoDeck may use representations of Eco-Kin/techniques without containing or transferring ownership of the sentient being.

## 17. Elements / Essences

The `18 elemental types` idea is historical design lineage only.

Production authority remains exactly nine Essences:

**Flora, Torrent, Pyre, Terra, Aero, Glaze, Voltic, Aura, Shade.**

Additional concepts such as Solar, Lunar, Echo, Geo, Blood, Spirit, Light, Dark, Iron/Tech, Chaos or Radiant may survive as move, environment, faction, lore or interaction tags where explicitly mapped.

## 18. Economy and seasonal cosmetics

The historical `gold/silver/bronze coin` proposal does not automatically replace the existing economy/currency workstream.

Seasonal cosmetics are compatible with the project if they remain presentation-first and non-pay-to-win.

A free-to-play/gacha model, boss-reward energy system, paid rerolls, paid creatures, or premium stat progression is **not locked** by this intake.

## 19. Large-roster promises

Do not market `over 1,000 playable Eco-Kin` as a current production fact.

The project currently has:

- a 125-ID Permanent Dex authority;
- a 1,120-name Historical Naming Pool containing prototypes/forms/renames/etc.;
- a new rule allowing controlled seasonal and expansion growth.

Future roster expansion is possible, but the shipped count must always match actual approved/implemented content.

## 20. Kindling surge terminology

`Kindlin Mode` / `Kindlin Overdrive` is normalized to a working **Kindling Surge** concept if a temporary high-bond combat state is retained.

Kindling Surge must:

- arise from relationship/gameplay, not coercion;
- preserve readable counterplay;
- have bounded duration/cost/cooldown;
- avoid an always-on flat pay-to-win multiplier;
- use the current combat/balance data rather than the pasted unbounded damage formula.

## 21. Survival / care

Preserve the strong care layer:

- beds/habitat;
- food;
- water;
- energy;
- stress/sanity where applicable;
- medical care;
- recovery/incubation;
- ecosystem cleanup.

Care systems should create meaningful planning without turning the game into punitive micromanagement or endless grind.

## 22. Product scope rule

The game may eventually contain survival, action combat, base restoration, raids, seasonal ecology, EchoDeck, tactical modes, PvP/PvEvP, farming, housing and cosmic DLC—but each feature enters production through a bounded milestone.

A design mention is not evidence that the feature is implemented, network-safe, optimized or release-ready.
