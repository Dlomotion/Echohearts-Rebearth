# ECHOHEARTS: REBEARTH — ECO-KIN GROWTH RITES & EARTHLINE PATH

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Status:** CANON-CORRECTED SYSTEM DIRECTION / IMPLEMENTATION PENDING  
**Purpose:** Replace copied/genre-generic linear evolution ladders with a species-authored Echohearts growth framework connected to ecology, Kindling, restoration, Forms Registry, seasonal content and the 125-ID Permanent Dex.

## 1. Governing rule

Eco-Kin do not share a universal three-stage evolution ladder.

Every Permanent Dex identity receives a **Species Growth Profile** that declares which kinds of growth are biologically, ecologically, spiritually or technologically valid for that identity.

Growth can change anatomy, abilities, traversal, V/D/H/P expression, behavior and ecological role without implying that an individual becomes a different owned object.

The current growth vocabulary remains:

- Growth Rites;
- Resonance Branches;
- authored biological evolution where appropriate;
- Mutation / Form paths;
- Shimmer Form;
- Aurivelle Form where eligible;
- regional/seasonal forms;
- corrupted/purified states;
- Resonance Trait Infusion / Bio-Synergy Weaving where separately approved.

No outside game's stage names, rarity labels, item names or evolution recipes are imported.

## 2. The Earthline Path

### Canon name

# **Earthline Adaptation Path**

The Earthline Path is the Echohearts route inspired by **real biological processes observed on Earth** rather than by another creature-collection game's evolution ladder.

It is not the Terra Essence and does not grant Terra typing by default.

Earthline growth uses species-authored natural-development logic such as:

- maturation from juvenile to adult morphology;
- metamorphosis;
- molting;
- seasonal coat/plumage/exoskeleton change;
- diet-linked coloration or structure where biologically plausible;
- habitat acclimation;
- migration-conditioned endurance;
- symbiotic relationships;
- pollination/seed-cycle roles;
- amphibious or aquatic life-stage change;
- social or reproductive maturity where appropriate and respectfully authored;
- regeneration after non-fatal injury where the species biology supports it.

### Earthline rule

A player does not force Earthline growth by feeding arbitrary materials until a meter fills.

A Growth Rite may require:

- appropriate age/development state;
- healthy habitat;
- restoration milestone;
- food/ecology condition;
- Kindling threshold;
- species-specific behavior;
- season/weather window;
- safe Sanctuary condition;
- story permission;
- non-sentient catalyst where biologically justified.

The A.E.G.I.S. Growth View must explain these requirements clearly before the player commits resources.

## 3. Growth-path families

### A. Earthline Adaptation

Naturalistic biological development.

Best for:

- insects with metamorphosis;
- amphibious lineages;
- flora/fungi-linked species;
- animals with juvenile/adult morphology;
- species whose habitat meaningfully shapes anatomy.

Earthline changes may be permanent maturation or reversible seasonal expression depending on the species.

### B. Resonance Branch

A relationship/ecology-driven branch influenced by:

- Kindling;
- Harmony;
- restored habitat;
- repeated role use;
- meaningful story choices;
- species-authored Resonance preferences.

A Resonance Branch is not a level-only promotion and is never unlocked through abuse/stress farming.

### C. Habitat Form

Identity-preserving regional adaptation.

Examples of triggers:

- long-term glacial habitat;
- coastal/brackish habitat;
- volcanic habitat;
- canopy/high-altitude habitat;
- deep-cave habitat;
- off-world/Yonderworld habitat where canon permits.

Habitat Forms remain mapped to the parent EcoKinID unless a deliberate future Dex migration establishes a true separate species.

### D. Weather Pulse Form

Temporary or semi-persistent morphology/ability expression during authored weather conditions.

Examples:

- lightning storms;
- auroral events;
- drought;
- monsoon;
- solar flare;
- frost bloom.

Weather does not create a new Dex ID.

### E. Mutation Path

Uses the project's established mutation families:

- Resonance;
- Purity;
- Stress;
- Dimensional.

Mutation is authored per species. Stress mutation must not incentivize player cruelty or neglect; where stress is a trigger, it should represent exposure to extraordinary environmental conditions with recovery/consent protections rather than abuse optimization.

### F. Shimmer Form

Universal ascension layer approved across species families.

Rules:

- preserve parent species identity;
- preserve recognizable anatomy/silhouette DNA;
- no humanoid-only template;
- no new Permanent Dex ID by default;
- bounded power budget;
- strong visual/readability requirements.

### G. Aurivelle Form

Rare iridescent/refractive Resonance expression.

Aurivelle is not copied Prismana terminology and does not automatically change primary Essence or create a new species.

Eligibility depends on the parent identity and Forms Registry approval.

### H. Ancestral Echo Rite

A late-game/postgame route where a living Eco-Kin can learn an archived ancestral technique or temporarily express an ancestor-informed form after a valid Chrono/Memory-Lattice quest.

Rules:

- the modern individual remains itself;
- no ancestor possession;
- no duplicate historical individual;
- no timeline rewrite;
- knowledge/form permission comes through a completed Chronicle or lineage archive.

### I. Restoration Bloom Rite

A growth path triggered because the player restored a species' ecological niche.

Examples:

- reopening migration corridors;
- restoring a pollinator network;
- cleaning a spawning wetland;
- regrowing a food plant;
- stabilizing a Sanctuary population.

This directly rewards the game's ecology loop instead of generic grinding.

## 4. Growth stage count is species-specific

Allowed structures include:

- no staged evolution;
- one maturation transition;
- two-stage line;
- three-stage line;
- branching line;
- metamorphic life cycle;
- form-only identity with no permanent evolution;
- seasonal/regional form system;
- late Growth Rite only.

The roster is therefore biologically varied instead of forcing every Eco-Kin into the same template.

## 5. Requirement clarity contract

A.E.G.I.S. must never hide essential Growth Rite requirements behind vague text.

For each known Growth Rite, show:

- Rite name;
- parent EcoKinID;
- resulting identity/form;
- permanent vs reversible;
- exact known story gate;
- exact known Kindling gate;
- habitat/region requirement;
- weather/season requirement;
- required non-sentient catalyst;
- required ecology/restoration milestone;
- current progress;
- map/coordinate guidance when the condition requires a specific place;
- why the Rite is currently unavailable.

Unknown lore secrets may remain hidden, but ordinary progression requirements should not require external guides.

## 6. Training — Main Story Progress connection

**Training (Main Story Progress)** remains the mandatory narrative/tutorial progression track.

Training teaches growth in this order:

1. movement / survival;
2. A.E.G.I.S. operation;
3. Eco-Kin observation/tracking;
4. Kindling;
5. real-time combat and partner coordination;
6. restoration/purification;
7. Sanctuary Aptitudes;
8. regional world-state reading;
9. first natural Earthline maturation example;
10. first Growth Rite / Resonance Branch choice;
11. Forms Registry and reversible states;
12. Shimmer / advanced mutations later in story;
13. Chrono/Ancestral rites only after the relevant story systems unlock.

Training is story-gated and should not become repetitive XP grinding.

## 7. Do not use license-count progression as the growth system

Older Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum 'Tamer of Beasts' license-count framing does not replace Core-Binder story progression.

If social ranks/licensing remain in a regional faction, they are reputation/permission systems only and cannot define Eco-Kin maturity or universal player class.

## 8. Growth reward philosophy

Growth should primarily unlock:

- new roles;
- new move families;
- traversal;
- ecology interactions;
- team synergies;
- new behavior;
- visual development;
- story identity;
- bounded V/D/H/P reallocation.

Avoid simple universal 'next stage = all stats higher' design.

A mature form can gain a role while losing another advantage, preserving meaningful team-building.

## 9. Seasonal and event connection

Future seasons may introduce:

- a new Growth Rite for an existing permanent identity;
- a new Habitat Form;
- a Weather Pulse Form;
- a Shimmer/Aurivelle eligibility quest;
- a Restoration Bloom Rite;
- a new Permanent Dex identity only through deliberate versioned roster expansion.

Limited events must not create permanent competitive stat advantages that late players can never obtain.

## 10. Data contract direction

A production implementation should separate **identity**, **growth option**, and **instance state**.

Suggested conceptual records:

- `EcoKinID` — permanent identity key;
- `GrowthProfileID` — species growth rules;
- `GrowthRiteID` — one authored transition/branch;
- `FormID` — identity-preserving form;
- `RequirementSetID` — story/ecology/Kindling/weather/catalyst gates;
- `AvailabilityState` — production/release availability;
- `InstanceGrowthState` — this bonded individual's completed rites/current form.

Do not encode requirements in free-text names and do not infer Permanent Dex membership from a form row.
