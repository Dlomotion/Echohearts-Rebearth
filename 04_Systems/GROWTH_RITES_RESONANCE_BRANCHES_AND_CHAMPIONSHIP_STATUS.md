# GROWTH RITES, RESONANCE BRANCHES & CHAMPIONSHIP STATUS

**Project:** Echohearts: Rebearth / Echohearts: Resonance Arena

**Status:** CANON system direction; exact numbers remain APPROVED-PENDING balance work.

## Purpose

This document replaces copied or conflicting mutation/evolution frameworks with an original Echohearts growth system built on **Kindling, agency, ecology, Resonance Shards, Growth Rites, Resonance Trait Infusion, Bio-Synergy Weaving and Primal Reversion**.

It does **not** use DNA fusion, consumed donors, forced mutation, capture ownership, copied stage names, or another franchise's evolution tree.

---

## 1. Growth is not forced evolution

Eco-Kin remain autonomous sentient partners. Growth is an earned adaptation process shaped by:

- Kindling and trust;
- habitat and ecological conditions;
- story and restoration milestones;
- voluntary training;
- Resonance Shards and approved catalysts;
- Sanctuary care;
- combat/exploration experience;
- the Eco-Kin's own willingness and identity.

The player may **invite** a Growth Rite. The Eco-Kin may accept, defer, or refuse.

No growth path may be triggered by neglect, abuse, starvation, forced containment, donor sacrifice, or intentional corruption.

---

## 2. Resonance Branches

An Eco-Kin can have one or more authored **Resonance Branches**. Branches are species-specific and do not force every creature into the same tree.

### Example branch families

- **Vanguard Branch** — stronger frontline pressure, pursuit, impact and protective aggression.
- **Bastion Branch** — protection, terrain holding, shielding, counterplay and survival.
- **Chorus Branch** — support, healing, cleansing, status control, team coordination and ecosystem interaction.
- **Wayfinder Branch** — traversal, scouting, tracking, stealth, weather reading and exploration utility.
- **Primal Branch** — rare ancestry-forward adaptation tied to deep ecology or Ancient Beast lineage.
- **Restored Branch** — recovery from Blight or ecological trauma into a healed but scar-remembering form.

These are design families, not new elements and not universal mandatory slots.

### Canon element rule

Every branch still uses only the 12 core elements:

**Solar, Lunar, Hydro, Magma, Aero, Flora, Volt, Terra, Iron/Tech, Spirit, Chaos, Radiant.**

Legacy labels such as Fire, Water, Nature, Earth, Wind, Light, Shadow, Void, Psychic, Aether, Life, Celestial, Bio, Kinetic or Matter may survive only as old art/source language until mapped to canon.

---

## 3. Growth Rite requirements

A Growth Rite can require a combination of:

- **Kindling threshold**;
- **Resonance Milestones** such as protecting allies, restoring a biome, surviving a Guardian encounter, completing a species quest or mastering a movement behavior;
- **Habitat condition** such as moonlit marsh, volcanic basin, coral reef, high-altitude wind corridor or restored Heartroot zone;
- **Resonance Shard** or purified ecological catalyst;
- **Story flag**;
- **Voluntary Sanctuary preparation**;
- **A.E.G.I.S. scan confirmation** that the form is stable and non-coercive.

A Rite never requires defeating a quota of the same species, consuming another Eco-Kin, caging a creature, harvesting a sentient genome, or intentionally causing suffering.

---

## 4. Reharmonization instead of mutation reset serum

Legacy "Amniotic Serum" / respec-serum concepts are retired.

Some temporary adaptations may be reconfigured through a **Reharmonization Rite** at a Sanctuary or approved natural site.

Rules:

- permanent identity/history is never erased;
- scars and relationship memory remain;
- no body rewrite is instant or consequence-free;
- material refund values are a balance variable and are **not yet locked**;
- major authored forms may be one-way story commitments where appropriate.

---

## 5. Resonance Trait Infusion

This is the ethical replacement for DNA trait splicing.

Two bonded Eco-Kin may train together and, where species design allows, one may learn a compatible **Echo Trait** inspired by the other.

The donor Eco-Kin:

- is not consumed;
- loses no identity;
- remains fully independent;
- must be willing to participate;
- cannot transfer species anatomy or overwrite the recipient's DNA.

Echo Traits are curated authored synergies, not unrestricted passive copying.

---

## 6. Bio-Synergy Weaving

Bio-Synergy Weaving allows compatible partners to create **temporary cooperative effects** through coordinated action.

Examples:

- Hydro + Volt partners create a controlled conductive field;
- Flora + Terra partners stabilize collapsing terrain;
- Aero + Solar partners create a thermal lift corridor;
- Spirit + Radiant partners expose hidden Blight memory traces.

The partners remain separate bodies and separate identities at all times.

No player/Eco-Kin fusion and no Eco-Kin/Eco-Kin body fusion.

---

## 7. Resonance Strain

Legacy `Genetic Instability` is replaced by **Resonance Strain** / **Spirit Load**.

Strain can rise when an Eco-Kin:

- chains high-output abilities;
- remains in a hostile biome too long;
- carries unresolved Blight stress;
- attempts an advanced temporary adaptation;
- fights while exhausted or injured.

High Strain is a **risk state**, not a desirable permanent power farm. It may produce unstable output, reduced control, slower recovery or temporary ability distortion.

Recovery comes from rest, care, Sanctuary treatment, appropriate food/water, calm interaction, Purity support and time.

---

## 8. Public stats remain V/D/H/P

The only public Eco-Kin stats are:

- **Vibrance** — vitality, recovery capacity and life-force expression;
- **Density** — physical presence, impact, resistance and structural strength;
- **Harmony** — coordination, technique flow, support, control and Resonance recovery;
- **Purity** — stability, cleansing, resistance to corruption and ecological balance.

Legacy HP / ATK / Physical DEF / Magic DEF / Regen / Break tables are not public stat replacements.

Runtime values such as health, damage, stagger resistance, cooldowns and recovery may be **derived internally** from V/D/H/P, abilities, level, equipment, role and encounter tuning.

---

## 9. Combat role templates

The useful role ideas are retained without copied naming or rigid stat packages.

### Striker
High pressure, pursuit, combo finishers, guard pressure.

### Guardian
Protection, body-blocking, terrain control, counterplay, ally cover.

### Conductor
Status control, elemental setup, support, team sequencing.

### Restorer
Healing, cleansing, environmental restoration, rescue utility.

### Scout
Tracking, stealth, traversal, discovery and warning behavior.

An Eco-Kin may change role emphasis across Growth Rites without becoming a different owned object or losing personality.

# CHAMPIONSHIP STATUS

## 10. Championship wins increase status, not species worth

Winning sanctioned championship fights increases **Resonance Circuit Standing**.

This is separate from Kindling and separate from biological growth.

### Standing ladder

1. **Qualifier**
2. **Challenger**
3. **Crestbearer**
4. **Champion**
5. **Grand Champion**
6. **Harmonic Crown**

Exact point thresholds are **NOT YET BALANCED**.

### Earned by

- sanctioned Resonance Arena victories;
- tournament objectives;
- clean tactical play;
- optional challenge modifiers;
- seasonal championship milestones;
- special rival / faction circuits where story supports them.

### Standing unlocks may include

- titles and profile crests;
- cosmetic-only arena presentation;
- advanced training challenges;
- new Arena rule sets;
- exhibition invitations;
- lore and rival encounters;
- technique trials;
- non-pay-to-win rewards;
- public recognition in Echohearts City.

Standing does **not** grant ownership over other Eco-Kin and does not force evolution.

---

## 11. Campaign vs Arena

- **Campaign:** real-time third-person partner combat, 8 roster / 3 active.
- **Resonance Arena / Harmony Circuit:** sanctioned competitive simulation space with its own rules, matchmaking and championship standing.

No overworld griefing. No permanent injury from simulation matches. No sentient trading or wagering.

Current art such as **Totemflare Rainforest Strider / Galecrest / Stormbringer**, **Psylopath Mindcoil / Mirefiend**, **Marmara Seedling / Verdant / Prime Guardian**, **Flames Emberling / Cinderstride / Blazewarden**, and **Sandveil Burrowkin / Sandshield / Sandshaper** may be interpreted as candidate Growth Rite branches or forms after identity, naming, element and anatomy audit.
