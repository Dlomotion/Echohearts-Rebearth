## AI Mistake Patch / Continuity QA

The following older ideas are retired or transformed:

- Eco-Kin as modular combat frames → retired. Eco-Kin are autonomous sentient partners.
- kill-only Somatic Affinity → retired. Progress comes from meaningful field participation, restoration, rescue, exploration, and cooperation.
- Rank-30 wipe/reset prestige loops → retired.
- Bio / Kinetic / Matter and the former 12-element list → retired from front-facing canon. The active Rebearth Essences are **Flora, Torrent, Pyre, Terra, Aero, Glaze, Voltic, Aura, Shade**.
- capture cages, coercive snares, harpoons, containment boxes, suppression rigs, and ownership-by-force → retired as player progression.
- DNA fusion, donor consumption, genome extraction, forced mutation, or sacrifice → retired.
- sentient storage-as-items → retired. Use Sanctuary/Haven residency and roster assignment.
- industrial breeding of sentient Eco-Kin → retired. Natural lifecycle and Echo-Egg care may exist where established.

## Canon progression stack

### 1. Kindling

**Kindling** measures the relationship between the player and an Eco-Kin.

Raised through:
- mutual protection
- restoration work
- rescue and recovery
- exploration/camping
- species-specific quests
- respecting refusal and retreat decisions
- successful cooperative combat
- appropriate care

Kindling can unlock trust behaviors, Command Sync options, Growth Rite eligibility, Sanctuary preferences, relationship scenes, and signature interactions.

### 2. Field Mastery

Working player-facing name remains **Field Mastery** unless later UX naming replaces it.

Earned from:
- combat participation
- restoration objectives
- tracking/rescue contracts
- exploration discoveries
- Sanctuary/community support
- environmental puzzles
- cooperative squad actions

No kill-only requirement. No reset-to-zero loop.

### 3. Growth Rites / Adaptation

Growth and branching forms may depend on:
- biome attunement
- Kindling threshold
- story milestone
- restored habitat state
- safe catalyst/relic interaction
- weather/celestial condition
- Eco-Kin choice/consent

Permanent forms preserve recognizable identity and use the official Rebearth Essence chart.

## Tamer of Beasts profession progression

The player profession is **Tamer of Beasts**. **Veridian Keeper** remains the ethical/restoration discipline.

Ranks use certified successful field resolutions rather than ownership count.

| Rank | Working threshold | Core authorization |
|---|---:|---|
| Tamer of Beasts — Bronze | 100 resolutions | standard frontier contracts and lower-risk wildlife response |
| Master Tamer — Silver | 250 resolutions | dangerous predators, migration crises, advanced rescue |
| Grand Warden — Gold | 500 resolutions | regional ecological emergencies and Primal/Titan response |
| Apex Tamer — Legendary | 1,000+ resolutions | highest reputation tier; rare reputation-based wildlife reactions and elite contracts |

Valid resolutions include Bond, Release, Defer, Treat, Relocate, Havenlink transfer, escort, or habitat restoration.

Exact thresholds are a **design target** and must be pacing-tested before final implementation.

## Tracking and field encounters

Core loop:

**Track signs → scan habitat → diagnose behavior/cause → prepare safe approach → protect/calm/stabilize → Kindle / Release / Defer / Treat / Relocate**

Useful clues:
- prints
- disturbed soil
- scratched bark
- fur/feathers/shed scales
- scent traces
- bioluminescent residue
- water disturbance
- feeding remains
- damaged vegetation
- Blight residue

Food spoilage and bait placement may influence wildlife behavior, but should not become a coercive sentient-capture exploit.

## Large guardian / Primal / Titan encounters

Allowed structure:
- territorial or distressed guardian
- multi-phase real-time encounter
- biome hazards
- Break/guard windows where appropriate
- visible environmental reaction
- purification, calming, rescue, evacuation, or restoration objective
- nonlethal completion path when fiction supports it
- rewards from restored ecology, safe shed material, purified deposits, archives, contracts, or faction support

Not allowed:
- harpoon-and-cage ownership loops
- forced extraction
- suppressing a sentient core for possession
- killing rare guardians as the only progression route

## Professional contract board

The Guild/settlement board may offer:

### Wildlife Resolution Contracts
Track a dangerous or displaced Eco-Kin, discover the cause, and resolve the situation safely.

### Primal Warden Contracts
Regional high-risk incidents involving Primal/Titan beings and major ecological damage.

### Poacher Interception
Destroy illegal trap networks, free captured Eco-Kin, secure evidence, stabilize trauma, and return survivors to Havenlink or habitat.

### Agricultural Support
Voluntary Eco-Kin partners may assist with irrigation, pollination, seed dispersal, soil restoration, or crop recovery.

### Infrastructure Support
Appropriate willing partners may assist with debris removal, emergency power, storm response, safe route clearing, water control, or rescue operations.

### Eco-Restoration Contracts
Restore migration corridors, water/soil health, habitat structure, population balance, and damaged regional ecosystems.

No contract treats Eco-Kin as rented machinery.

## Rare morphology rule

- **Biomimetic Shifts** = biome adaptations.
- **Resonant Morphs** = rare variants, not a universal default.
- **Primal Morphs** = ancient/guardian-scale expressions.
- Sonic/music-based morphology is reserved for specific species with actual acoustic biology or story justification.

Harmony remains a public attribute and Resonance remains a broad planetary principle. Neither requires every Eco-Kin to sing, pulse to music, or use sound powers.

## Tree of Life connection

The **Sovereign Heartroot / Tree of Life** is a main campaign restoration spine.

Sanctuary growth, contracts, regional restoration, Eco-Kin behavior, farming, travel, and faction state should increasingly connect back to the condition of its root network.

Restoration must create persistent visible changes without erasing all scars.
