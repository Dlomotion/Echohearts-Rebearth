The Python reference models human slots, not NPC spawning. field_training/ghost_trial
have one human record; their opponent must be authored by the eventual engine host.
Each human's eight links remain access to living partners, not consumable inventory.
Injury, withdrawal, ceremony and consent are honored at entry and continuously by
the eventual runtime. Simulation defeat is not permanent injury. Campaign combat
does not become mandatory rhythm play or a different species/stat system.

## Core action duel

Before play, disclose ruleset, objective, partner readiness, accessibility options,
site and end condition. Both humans accept. Each partner also consents through
authoritative relationship state. Preparation precedes a readable countdown.
Player and partner use real-time movement, guarding, sightlines, cover and authored
techniques. Retreat/surrender is valid. A short objective round may end by points,
safe incapacity, time/draw or surrender; actual health/damage formulas stay with BUILD.

Cooperation is shown through the existing Harmony/Anima-Link and Encounter Chronicle,
not a fifth public stat. A proposed temporary coordination window can follow a
well-timed guard, safe partner switch or rescue; it must be bounded and use tested
authoritative technique rules before any damage effect ships. No numerical damage
multiplier or Essence matchup is invented here. Human contribution matters through
positioning and decisions, not using a sentient partner as ammunition.

Optional sonic exhibitions may have rhythm feedback only for authored sonic-capable
participants. Provide equivalent hold/assist/visual input options and identical
competitive opportunity. No microphone, heartbeat sensor or emotion inference required.

## Raid flow and map consequences

Warning signs precede a temporary encounter perimeter: migrating fauna, warped
Ancient-Tech fields, shelter requests and readable escape paths. This perimeter is
a gameplay safety boundary, not a literal Isolation Protocol rewriting reality.

Phase A: assess the hazard, protect civilians and mark a safe approach.
Phase B: split tasks among up to four partners/humans—hold an escape route, interrupt
an extraction rig, clear debris or stabilize a damaged root. Combat is one tool.
Phase C: expose an authored vulnerability without treating corruption as evil.
Phase D: stabilize, release, retreat or complete a rescue; optional combat cannot
grant a bond. The boss remains an existing approved individual/encounter record.

Late join is allowed only at safe host-approved checkpoints with a current encounter
revision; cap four. The reference checks revision/permissions/capacity, not phase
geometry. Rewards review support, rescue and objectives, not damage alone. The actual
world service applies bounded ecology/route changes once with persistence; no raid
client can directly rewrite terrain, root restoration, fast travel or spawn tables.

## Mindscape exhibition

A proposed optional Arena ruleset projects authored weather/terrain motifs chosen
before the match. A storm-sea scene may express grief or a fractured courtyard may
express distrust; these are dramatic metaphors, not diagnosis or extracted memories.
Both sides receive the same published field rules and can decline the modifier.
Balance must not secretly favor an undisclosed Essence. Reduced-flash, camera and
audio alternatives preserve mechanics. It never interrupts an actual Growth Rite,
overwrites history, deletes a loser or changes the campaign environment.

## Competitive progression

Retain Resonance Circuit Standing: Qualifier → Challenger → Crestbearer → Champion
→ Grand Champion → Harmonic Crown. Thresholds, ranking formula and calendars remain
unbalanced. The proposed regional season offers steward matches, team exhibitions
and civic objectives before invitations/qualifiers and an authored championship
bracket. Eight steward profiles are choices, not eight mandatory crests that gate
the main story. No new governing Symphony Council replaces existing factions.

Competition offers titles, cosmetics, replay records and invitations. No exclusive
stat power, forced evolution, sentient wagers or cross-server currency minting.
Ghost wins, private-host results, offline saves and casual victories never become
ranked wins. A cosmetic season title is scoped to the relevant season, not a claim
to own a region or govern inhabitants. Ranking resets do not erase bonds/story.

## Roaming opponents and tactical nodes

- Node stewards: visible challenge surface, declared objective and noncombat access
  alternative. A win recognizes competence; route unlocks still need world authority.
- Traveling challengers: schedule-driven NPCs on approved routes. Invitations are
  opt-in, never triggered into an unavoidable duel by sprinting or proximity alone.
- Storm witnesses: rare weather-conditional story challengers. Encounter identity,
  motives and approved Eco-Kin team are authored; no generic forbidden species tier.
- Tactical nodes: competitive objectives live in Arena replicas or temporary event
  volumes. Ranked occupation cannot confiscate a campaign home or close public routes.

## Human stories

Humans returned to a physical world that continued without them. They disagree over
who carries risk and who has the right to decide. People may battle for practice,
public sport, a livelihood, rescue, defense or domination; these motives are not
morally interchangeable. A tournament proves skill under rules, not universal truth.

Existing story anchors: Kale and Sherlock Hound teach rescue over reckless pursuit;
Liora questions when operating a restored machine is justified; Professor Jackson
demands observation and accountable science. The Grand Crucible chapter can expose
sabotage and exploitation in a competition without replacing its established arc.
Vorlag's control doctrine, Hugo's consent violations and Corporate Muscles' extractive
contracts explain hostile conflict; their coercion is never the player's progression.
De-escalation, defending an escape route and refusing a corrupt sponsor can be wins.

See the companion HUMAN_BATTLE_STORIES proposal for eight new human profiles. Names,
teams, station assignments and dialogue require collision/continuity/art review.
