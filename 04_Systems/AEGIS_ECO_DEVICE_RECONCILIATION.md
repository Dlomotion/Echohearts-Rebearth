# A.E.G.I.S. / Eco-Device Reconciliation

Status: **CANON DIRECTION + LEGACY FEATURE SALVAGE**

The historical `Echo-Link Nexus` / Eco-Device material and recovered Gemini A.E.G.I.S. drafts contain valuable handheld-computer, EcoDex, radar, care, farm-monitoring, customization, navigation, portal, archive-puzzle and communication ideas. They also contain capture/capsule/storage/trading mechanics, forced evolution, direct franchise comparison language and literal time-travel/data-world claims that conflict with current Echohearts canon.

## Canon device hierarchy

### A.E.G.I.S.
A.E.G.I.S. remains the canonical physical bracer carried by the player. It owns field scanning, survival telemetry, EcoDex access, voluntary Kindling support, partner commands, navigation, compatible communication, mission data and approved remote Sanctuary functions.

### D.A.H.L.I.A.
D.A.H.L.I.A. remains an A.E.G.I.S. systems module. It analyzes, interprets, warns, remembers and proposes. It does not replace player judgment or become a separate floating AI companion unless a later canon change explicitly creates a physical projection/avatar state.

### EcoDex
EcoDex is the biological/ecological knowledge system inside the A.E.G.I.S. ecosystem. It records discovered species, behavior, habitat, growth/form observations, cries, ecology, care notes and story evidence.

### Optional handheld / phone-like shell
The useful historical idea of a rugged handheld device may survive as a **Living Glass Field Slate** or **A.E.G.I.S. Companion Slate** used by researchers, NPCs, students and players in menu-heavy contexts. It does not replace the bracer.

## Visual identity salvage from the Gemini draft

The following visual direction is approved as design language, subject to art production review:

- forearm-mounted physical bracer rather than a modern phone;
- pale stone/ceramic **Living Porcelain** shell with tactile wear;
- thin gold Ancient-Tech conductor lines integrated into the shell;
- restrained moss/root growth where the device has remained exposed to Rebearth;
- central **Prism Core** / Resonance Core that shifts light based on mode;
- hard-light / holographic field UI projected above the forearm;
- a tactile tuning dial / bezel may survive as a physical accessibility and interaction affordance;
- feedback through haptics, light, audio and D.A.H.L.I.A. prompts.

Outside-franchise labels such as `Digivice`, `D-Terminal`, `D-Scanner`, `Gate Open` and `Digi-Port` are **reference-only language and must never ship**.

## Bonding correction

Historical Gemini language describes `Echo-Bind`, `capture`, heartbeat capture meters and throwing capture spheres. Those mechanics are retired for ordinary Eco-Kin recruitment.

A.E.G.I.S. may still visualize **Resonance Synchronization** during calming/Kindling interactions, but the ethical field loop remains:

**Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer.**

The player does not lower HP to capture, imprison a sentient Eco-Kin in a sphere, or force a bond through a device.

## Growth / evolution correction

A.E.G.I.S. can detect Growth Rite readiness, explain environmental requirements and help stabilize a voluntary Resonance event. It does **not** fire a beam that forcibly evolves an Eco-Kin, rewrite its genome on command, or turn the Eco-Kin into programmable hardware.

Approved cooperative terms remain:

- Growth Rite
- Resonance Trait Infusion
- Bio-Synergy Weaving
- Primal Reversion
- Command Sync
- Harmonic Overdrive

No forced DNA fusion or identity overwrite.

## Inventory correction

The Gemini draft describes dissolving all items into pixels and storing them inside the bracer. That is not currently canon.

A.E.G.I.S. may provide inventory UI, authentication and indexing for the player's physical Field Rig / storage systems. Item presentation can use projection and tagged-object scanning, but the bracer is not assumed to contain an unlimited matter-conversion pocket dimension.

## Mobility / travel reconciliation

### 1. Fast travel — Pulse / Meridian Monolith network

**Approved direction:** A.E.G.I.S. can authenticate with discovered **Pulse Monoliths / Meridian Monoliths** and request travel between unlocked nodes.

Rules:

- destination must already be discovered/authorized;
- the server validates the destination ID and player state;
- World Partition destination data must be ready before relocation;
- safe-arrival validation is mandatory;
- the server performs authoritative relocation;
- discovery/travel state is replicated and persisted;
- local A.E.G.I.S. presentation is only the interface layer.

Legacy `Warp Totem` / `Ley-Line teleport at light speed` language is reference-only unless a later world-lore pass approves a regional name.

### 2. Temporal archive projection — **Memory Echo Field**

The Gemini `Chrono-Phase Bubble` is **not literal unrestricted time travel**.

Salvaged mechanic: at specific Meridian ruins, A.E.G.I.S. can reconstruct a bounded **Memory Echo Field** from preserved spatial/temporal records. The field overlays a past-state reconstruction of architecture so the player can interpret ruins, reveal hidden routes, replay events or temporarily interact with approved Ancient-Tech mechanisms.

Rules:

- only authored locations with recorded states support it;
- it cannot rewrite history, kill past characters or create paradoxes;
- player actions affect present puzzle state only through explicitly authored mechanisms;
- any temporary traversal geometry requires fail-safe recovery if the projection drops;
- resource drain/cooldown is optional and must be data-driven rather than an arbitrary battery gimmick.

### 3. Echo-Rifts / pocket spaces

A.E.G.I.S. may stabilize and tune approved **Echo Rifts** for dungeon/event access. This is a controlled gateway interaction, not evidence that Rebearth is a computer simulation.

The player scans → stabilizes → validates destination → enters a bounded rift space. Gravity/visual anomalies can support level design where justified.

## Puzzle-system salvage

The Gemini ruin ideas contain strong design material when translated out of literal `hacking the server` fiction:

- **Resonance Locks:** respond to emotional/elemental resonance, but never require forcibly changing an Eco-Kin's identity.
- **Memory Echo Fields:** reveal historical spatial states for authored puzzles.
- **Cipher Walls:** A.E.G.I.S. decodes partial Meridian inscriptions; recovered evidence completes the interpretation.
- **Narrative Keys:** puzzle solutions come from understanding local history, ecology and relationships rather than generic keycards.

Player choices may alter route, danger, reward or interpretation, but cannot arbitrarily rewrite canon text into any result.

## Alert-state reconciliation

A.E.G.I.S. can provide multimodal alerts:

- normal / healthy state: restrained Veridian pulse;
- Blight anomaly: corrupted/flickering warning state;
- severe environmental danger: high-priority haptic + audio + visual alert;
- rare/important life signature: contextual EcoDex pulse after discovery rules permit it;
- incoming communication: holographic/contact indicator.

Warnings must include non-color cues for accessibility.

## Removed / redesigned functions

Historical ideas below are not player-facing canon:

- converting Eco-Kin into energy/capsules for transport;
- trap catch-rate systems based on lowering HP/status;
- 100% Golden Trap success;
- forced storage limits framed as owning creatures;
- Eco-Kin trading network;
- direct stat injection into Eco-Kin from the device;
- forced evolution beams;
- mandatory remote work assignments;
- personality device as a separate canon AI replacing D.A.H.L.I.A.;
- unlimited matter conversion / inventory printing without an approved physical-world system;
- unrestricted time travel or history rewriting;
- treating Rebearth as a literal server because the UI uses digital visual language;
- app/device history copied from outside monster-collecting franchises.

## Party / roster language

The device may display the current field roster and bond registry. It must not describe sentient Eco-Kin as stored inventory. Temporary care/transport uses Kindling Haven, Echo Cradle, Recovery Sled, Recovery Ward or habitat relocation depending on context.

## Remote expedition rule

Historical `send six Eco-Kin on missions` becomes an optional **Partner Expedition Board**. Participation must depend on bond, willingness, health, habitat suitability, danger, story restrictions and player relationships. Legendary/Guardian/Titan/Celestial or restricted Eco-Kin are not generic dispatch labor.

## Medical rule

Medical technology can monitor babies, injured Eco-Kin, eggs and Blight-Touched patients, but must not promise fictionalized real-world neonatal safety. In-world medical claims are fictional unless separately researched and clearly framed.

## Monetization rule

Device skins, themes, charms and cosmetic frames may be cosmetic-only monetization candidates. No pay-to-win capture bonuses, stat power or exclusive bond success.

## UE5 implementation direction

Prefer data-driven services and UI events rather than one giant `EcoDevice` class. A.E.G.I.S. presentation should consume authoritative data from EcoKin registry/Echoprints, inventory, Sanctuary, mission, world-state and save systems. Do not create duplicate runtime ownership.

Travel authority should follow the approved path:

`Local UI/Input → player-owned request component → server validates destination/action ID → authoritative world/restoration service → World Partition readiness → safe-state validation → authoritative relocation/state change → replication/persistence → A.E.G.I.S. presentation event`

**NOT YET VERIFIED** until connected repository classes, Blueprints, data assets, World Partition travel path and UI are inspected, built and tested.
