## Purpose
Reconcile the historical survival, farming, ecosystem, physics, research, rebuilding, multiplayer, and progression ideas into the current Echohearts: Rebearth canon without creating a second gameplay framework.

## Heart Statement
Echohearts is strongest when players feel:
1. This world needs me.
2. These Eco-Kin are alive.
3. My choices changed Rebearth.

The survival layer exists to reinforce those three feelings, not to become a detached hunger-meter simulator.

## Core Loop
SURVIVE → CARE → BUILD → RESEARCH → EXPLORE → SOLVE → FIGHT → RESTORE → EXPAND

### Survive
- Food, clean water, shelter, medicine, temperature, weather, terrain and hazard management.
- Hostile nights, Blightfronts, storms, predator pressure and faction threats.
- Failure teaches route, ecology and preparation knowledge.
- Permadeath is NOT locked. Current recommendation: optional hardcore ruleset, not mandatory campaign loss.

### Care
- Seasonal crops and Living Soil.
- Foraging, fishing, mining and clean resource recovery.
- Eco-Kin health, sickness, injury, fatigue, morale, habitat comfort and recovery.
- Sanctuary Aptitudes are voluntary behaviors, not forced jobs.
- Legendary/Guardian/Titan/Celestial beings are never generic labor units.

### Build
Use the existing Sanctuary and BCT-001 architecture.
- Shelter
- Purity Well
- Recovery Ward
- Healing Incubator
- Gardens and Living Soil plots
- Listening Posts
- Field craft stations
- Clean logistics
- Research stations
- Defenses that protect communities without turning Eco-Kin into weapons or inventory

### Research
Research is a real progression track:
- Biology
- Ecology
- Agriculture
- Engineering
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Energy Science
- Ancient Technology
- Resonance Science
- Xenotechnology only when story-approved

Artifact progression:
Artifact → Scan → Recover evidence/components → Decode → Experiment → Prototype → Field Test → Stabilize → Manufacture

No alien or Ancient Tech device becomes instant usable loot without study.

### Explore
- Surface biomes
- Underwater layers
- Living Crust / Deep Root Tunnels
- Ruins
- Meridian facilities
- Sky routes and islands
- Portal/Riftways
- Late cosmic zones when story-unlocked

### Solve
- Environmental logic
- Physics and momentum puzzles
- Gravity and pressure systems
- Resonance alignment
- Memory reconstruction
- Ancient machinery
- Traversal problem-solving

### Fight
Main campaign combat remains real-time third-person action.
Threats include:
- Blightborn
- hostile wildlife states
- corrupted Eco-Kin that should be restored when possible
- faction forces
- world bosses
- raid-scale threats

### Restore
Restoration must be visible:
Dead soil → living soil
Polluted water → purified water
Broken migration route → restored corridor
Collapsed settlement → inhabited community
Disconnected route → reopened travel
Blight-heavy spawn table → healthier ecology

### Expand
Progression adds harder regions, more complex survival conditions, new research, new restoration problems, new Eco-Kin ecologies, stronger bosses and deeper world consequences.

## Sanctuary Daily Rhythm
A daily Sanctuary Condition Check may review:
- weather
- water and food
- crop state
- resident wellbeing
- Eco-Kin wellbeing
- defenses
- research status
- active world threats

Eco-Kin may choose to assist based on species behavior, bond, habitat comfort, stamina/fatigue, trust, Sanctuary Aptitude and player treatment.

Do NOT use the legacy "worker crew" framing as ownership. Use voluntary Sanctuary Aptitude participation.

## Eco-Kin Welfare Rule
Overwork is a failure state, not a productivity strategy.

Possible chain:
Fatigue → stress → sickness/injury → trust loss → refusal → long-term recovery consequences.

Good food, sleep, shelter, clean habitat, appropriate tasks, Harmony, Purity and player care improve recovery.

## Farming
- Seasonal crops matter.
- Winter or regional seasons can restrict or change crops, but do not need to make farming inactive everywhere.
- Crop outputs support food, medicine, crafting, habitat restoration and community economy.
- No permanent stat-lock consumables that trivialize survival.

## Ecology Simulation
Ecological state should respond to:
- habitat
- water quality
- climate
- predators
- prey
- food availability
- disease
- Purity
- Blight pressure
- restoration work
- player harvesting pressure

The player should solve ecology problems, not simply kill whatever overpopulates.

## Evolution / Adaptation
Historical breeding/genetics ideas are NOT automatic canon.
Current rule:
- no forced breeding
- no forced mutation
- no sentient trait extraction
- no forced evolution

Valid routes include:
- Growth Rites
- environmental adaptation
- inherited traits where biologically and ethically justified
- Shard-Arc Adaptation
- Primal Reversion
- Resonance Trait Infusion where it does not erase identity

## Physics
Physics should support gameplay, but implementation must remain performant and authored.
Candidate gameplay forces:
- gravity
- momentum
- slopes
- knockback
- falling objects
- water currents
- wind
- structural stability
- portal/rift traversal

Do not promise unrestricted fully simulated destruction everywhere.

## Portal / Riftway Physics
Historical Echo-Warp concepts are folded into existing Riftway / portal systems.
Possible rule:
- entering with velocity may preserve authored momentum
- exit orientation can redirect trajectory
- gravity and pressure differ by destination

This must be implemented as curated traversal gameplay, not as an unbounded physics exploit.

## Progression Philosophy
Echohearts progression is parallel, not one treadmill:
- Player capability
- Gear
- Eco-Kin relationship and growth
- Research
- Sanctuary
- World restoration
- Travel network
- Knowledge
- Faction/community trust

## Multiplayer
- Kin-Ships co-op supports shared objectives.
- Personal rewards avoid loot theft.
- Players can trade approved materials, crafted gear, cosmetics, furniture, food and EchoDeck cards.
- Eco-Kin themselves are not trade commodities.

## Horror Tone
Use tension carefully:
- dangerous nights
- unknown cries
- Gloom memory effects
- Blightfronts
- ruined facilities
- weather
- limited safe routes

Do not make the world permanently hostile 24/7. Cozy recovery is necessary so danger still feels dangerous.

## Canon Corrections Applied
Retired or redesigned from the historical draft:
- forced farm labor → voluntary Sanctuary Aptitudes
- Eco-Kin trading → approved item/material exchange only
- selective breeding optimization → ethical growth/adaptation review
- mutation as productivity system → contextual ecology/story system
- compulsory permadeath → optional hardcore candidate
- monster attacks 24/7 → dynamic danger windows and region threat states
- generic factory automation using sentient partners → clean logistics + voluntary partner assistance

## Acceptance Tests
A survival feature is ready to promote only when it can answer:
1. What player decision does it create?
2. What visible world response follows?
3. How does it affect Eco-Kin agency or wellbeing?
4. Does it duplicate an existing system?
5. Can it be implemented without violating current technical gates?
6. Does it support the Heart Statement?
