# Echohearts Economy Reconciliation

Status: CANON-COMPATIBLE ECONOMY PRINCIPLES / NUMERIC BALANCE NOT YET VERIFIED

## Preserve from historical economy work
The historical design established useful economic loops around resource expeditions, farming, gathering, gems/minerals, storage, crafting, merchants, risk/reward and regional supply. These survive as systems principles after ethical/canon cleanup.

Core loop:
Expedition / Restoration
→ transport
→ storage
→ identify/refine/process
→ cook/craft/build/repair
→ player/Sanctuary/settlement use
→ item/resource trade where allowed
→ reinvestment into restoration and infrastructure

## Eco-Kin participation
Eco-Kin are partners, not labor inventory.

Use voluntary Sanctuary Aptitudes and contextual partner assists such as:
- Garden Care
- Water Care
- Repair Help
- Stone Work / safe excavation support
- Scout Watch
- Comfort / Recovery support
- Defense during emergencies
- Archive/Research assistance where species-appropriate

An Eco-Kin may refuse unsafe, exhausting or trust-breaking work. Fatigue, injury, habitat discomfort and Kindling affect willingness. No forced work, creature-as-generator, sentient fuel or permanent assignment ownership.

## Resource economy
Approved direction includes:
- biome/season/weather/Purity-dependent gathering
- Living Soil agriculture
- Living Crust materials
- purified Blight-derived materials only after safe treatment
- finite or ecologically regenerative deposits rather than infinite vending nodes
- value-added processing and crafting
- storage/logistics constraints
- repair/upkeep and other sinks
- settlement demand and scarcity
- route risk, spoilage/contamination and equipment wear
- restoration increasing long-term yield and biodiversity

## Historical currencies/items needing canon review
Gold, generic Runes, Elemental Gems, Ancient Coins, Echo Crystals, ERU, Scrap Tokens, Starchrom and other old currency labels are SOURCE/PROPOSAL unless a current canon ledger explicitly approves them.

Existing established resource names and restoration materials take priority. Resonance Shards are not generic money.

## Forbidden / retired economy patterns
Do not implement:
- buying or selling Eco-Kin
- Eco-Kin egg marketplace
- creature trading as commerce
- dissolving unwanted Eco-Kin into resources
- extracted creature cores as crafting currency
- forced breeding/fusion for profit
- pay-to-win progression
- gacha energy as a core loop
- raw Blight Husk as ordinary crafting currency/material
- infinite passive-income loops based on forced Eco-Kin labor

## Regional market simulation
Settlements may track item/material supply, demand, route safety, ecological health and faction pressure. Restoration can lower scarcity; disasters can raise it. Market changes must have bounded rates and designer caps so simulation cannot destroy progression or create exploits.

## Co-op economy
Co-op composition can improve efficiency and open opportunities, but must not multiply resources without bound. Use server-authoritative contribution rules, personal/shared reward contracts and exploit telemetry when implementation begins.

## Technical direction
Prefer data-driven definitions and transaction contracts. Permanent economic outcomes are server authoritative. Building/crafting must connect to BCT-001 reservation → validation/ecological veto → atomic commit/rollback → authoritative event → persistence.

No economy C++ should be added merely because old brainstorm code exists. First inspect the actual UE5.8 project and satisfy current technical gates.
