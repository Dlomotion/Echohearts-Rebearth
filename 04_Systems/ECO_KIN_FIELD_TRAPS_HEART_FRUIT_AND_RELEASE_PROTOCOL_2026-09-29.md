## 1. Core relationship rule

Echohearts does not use a universal health-reduction + random capture-roll loop.

The authoritative field relationship remains:

**Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer**

The player may use **Eco-Traps** as temporary, nonlethal field tools. In Echohearts, the word *trap* means a reversible environmental device used to:

- stop an Eco-Kin from running into an active hazard;
- redirect movement away from civilians, machinery or unstable terrain;
- establish a safe observation zone;
- create a short calming/Kindling opportunity;
- contain Blight spread long enough to cleanse or treat it;
- support rescue of injured or frightened Eco-Kin;
- protect the Core-Binder while a high-risk interaction is resolved.

An Eco-Trap never converts a sentient Eco-Kin into inventory, a disc, a file, a traded object or owned property.

## 2. Canon interaction state machine

Recommended player-facing state progression:

```text
Unaware / Wary / Distressed
→ Observed
→ Lured or Safely Approached
→ Safehold Window
→ Calmed
→ Kindling Offered
→ Bonded | Released | Deferred | Retreated
```

Additional emergency states may include:

```text
Blight-Distorted
Injured
Rescue Transport
Sanctuary Recovery
Returned to Habitat
```

### Bonded
The Eco-Kin voluntarily accepts an ongoing Kindling relationship and becomes eligible for the player's roster/Havenlink network under authored rules.

### Released
The Core-Binder deliberately opens the field and lets the Eco-Kin return to its habitat. Release is not failure. It may improve local ecology, reputation or future trust.

### Deferred
The relationship remains unresolved. The individual may remember the encounter and be met again later.

### Retreated
The Eco-Kin leaves on its own. The game records what happened without treating escape as a broken capture roll.

## 3. Heart Fruit

### Canon function

**Heart Fruit** is a non-sentient ecological lure and trust offering used to make first contact more accessible.

It is not a mind-control berry and it does not force a Bond.

Heart Fruit works because many Eco-Kin recognize its scent, hydration, mineral profile or nectar as safe food. Species preferences remain authored and ecological rather than universal magic.

### Heart Fruit effects

Depending on the species and context, a valid Heart Fruit offering may:

- lower initial fear/stress;
- slow a retreat timer;
- encourage an Eco-Kin to enter a safe observation area;
- create a longer A.E.G.I.S. scan window;
- make a nonviolent approach possible;
- reduce the need for precision trap placement;
- open a Kindling opportunity earlier;
- improve trust if the player leaves the fruit and backs away;
- support post-rescue recovery.

Heart Fruit **never**:

- guarantees Bonding;
- overrides refusal behavior;
- replaces species-specific food ecology;
- converts into a hidden capture percentage;
- works on every Eco-Kin identically;
- permits starving an Eco-Kin to make it easier to control.

## 4. Heart Fruit accessibility mode

Heart Fruit is the default accessibility bridge for players who enjoy the relationship loop but do not want high-precision interception every time.

Optional assists may include:

- highlighted safe placement zones;
- clear scent/wind direction indicators;
- longer approach windows;
- slower retreat escalation;
- stronger visual/audio cues when an individual is interested;
- automatic identification of already-known food preferences;
- reduced precision requirements for A.E.G.I.S. anchor placement;
- an optional one-button **Offer & Wait** interaction for low-threat Eco-Kin;
- colorblind-safe and non-audio-only feedback.

Accessibility changes execution difficulty, not consent. An Eco-Kin may still Release/Defer rather than Bond.

## 5. Eco-Trap families

### A. Heart Fruit Lure Cradle

A small environmental station that presents Heart Fruit without enclosing the Eco-Kin.

Use cases:

- shy species;
- first observation;
- photography/scanning;
- calming after a weather anomaly;
- nonviolent quest interactions.

The player can place it, move away, and observe from cover.

### B. Softfield Corral

A low-force A.E.G.I.S. perimeter that shapes movement without hard imprisonment.

Rules:

- contains at least one visible/open exit unless an emergency rescue state explicitly requires a closed safety perimeter;
- auto-releases if stress exceeds the authored safe threshold;
- cannot damage a healthy Eco-Kin merely for crossing it;
- duration is bounded;
- used to create a safe approach/cleansing window, not permanent storage.

### C. Grounding Anchor Set

Physical field anchors used against high-Density movement, unstable land, charging behavior or strong environmental forces.

Anchors stabilize the **field geometry**, not the Eco-Kin's ownership state.

Possible uses:

- prevent a large Eco-Kin from accidentally crossing a collapsing shelf edge;
- hold a safe corridor during a tremor;
- stabilize an A.E.G.I.S. boundary during an Event Sovereign objective;
- support the Static Orchard and other environmental puzzles.

### D. Haven Mesh

A multi-target emergency rescue lattice for herds/flocks/groups during wildfire, Blight, flood, Rift activity or structural collapse.

It is not a mass-capture tool.

The system guides multiple individuals toward evacuation or treatment routes and then releases them according to the rescue objective.

### E. Quiet Shade Screen

A non-contact visual/scent barrier used to reduce sensory overload and provide a low-stimulation corridor.

Useful for:

- frightened nocturnal Eco-Kin;
- high-traffic rescue scenes;
- sanctuary intake;
- veterinary/recovery encounters.

## 6. Trap-and-release gameplay loop

A normal field encounter can proceed as follows:

1. **Survey** — A.E.G.I.S. reads terrain, behavior and known preferences.
2. **Choose approach** — direct observation, Heart Fruit, lure cradle, Softfield Corral, anchors, environmental tool, or no tool.
3. **Prepare terrain** — place devices so the Eco-Kin has safe routes and the player is not cornering it unnecessarily.
4. **Invite interaction** — offer food, remove a hazard, calm a Blight source, or perform the species-specific trust action.
5. **Maintain Safehold** — use positioning/timing to prevent panic or environmental failure.
6. **Kindling window** — if the individual becomes receptive, A.E.G.I.S. presents the Bond / Release / Defer interaction.
7. **Resolve** — update relationship memory and local ecology.

No random capture percentage is required.

## 7. Skill without inaccessible precision

The system should reward skill through:

- reading terrain;
- understanding behavior;
- choosing the right field tool;
- correct placement;
- timing;
- keeping exits safe;
- responding to weather/forms;
- protecting the Eco-Kin rather than maximizing damage.

Difficulty must not depend on:

- unreadable sub-second UI tricks;
- deliberately false UI;
- mandatory audio-only cues;
- perfect analog precision;
- one specific Eco-Kin in the player's roster;
- hidden RNG after the player has completed the interaction correctly.

## 8. Weather Pulse connection

Weather Pulse Forms alter the **encounter conditions**, not the individual's ownership rules.

Examples:

- high winds require heavier anchor placement;
- a cold pulse may require shelter or a warming field before approach;
- lightning conditions may temporarily disable exposed electronics;
- rain may spread scent farther and make Heart Fruit useful from a greater distance;
- a Shade-adapted individual may prefer visual cover before accepting an offering.

Weather values are authoritative world state in multiplayer and must not be accepted directly from a client.

## 9. Blight-distorted Eco-Kin

A Blight-distorted Eco-Kin is not an inherently hostile species.

Recommended loop:

**Protect area → isolate Blight source → stabilize movement → cleanse/treat → release pressure → observe individual choice → Bond / Release / Defer.**

A temporary closed safety field is permitted when required to prevent immediate injury, but the field ends when the emergency does.

## 10. Rescue transport exception

An injured Eco-Kin may require physical transport to a Healing Incubator or Sanctuary recovery space.

This is **medical/rescue transport**, not ownership storage.

Required rules:

- transport is temporary;
- the reason is recorded;
- the Eco-Kin is released to a suitable habitat/recovery space afterward unless it later chooses a Bond;
- no resale/trading;
- no permanent confinement as a shortcut around Kindling.

## 12. Heart Fruit economy and ecology

Heart Fruit should remain accessible through normal play.

Sources may include:

- Sanctuary cultivation;
- restored orchards;
- regional foraging after ecological recovery;
- quest gifts;
- ordinary lawful vendors.

It is **not** premium-only, black-market-only or a paid capture advantage.

Quality/preparation can change suitability, shelf life or species preference, but not create pay-to-win Bonding.
