# Character & Eco-Kin Relationship Rules

Status: **CANON SYSTEM GUARDRAIL**

## Relationship model

Humans and Eco-Kin interact through trust, history, care, conflict, goals, habitat, and repeated behavior. A bond is not a possession flag.

Each named human/Eco-Kin relationship should track:

- Trust
- Fear / stress
- Familiarity
- Care history
- Shared mission history
- Habitat safety
- Recent harm or protection
- Willingness to accompany
- Sanctuary preference
- Rival/faction pressure

## NPC/Eco-Kin examples

- **Kaelen + Sherlock Hound:** practical field partnership, rescue/search, manual Sanctuary work, mutual independence.
- **Liora + Eco-Kin:** research partnership; she must request observation/access rather than treating Eco-Kin as lab property.
- **Nurse Calla + patients:** medical consent/safety rules, Recovery Ward, Healing Incubator, release/rest options.
- **Mora + Eco-Kin:** no harvesting living bodies for crafting. Use naturally shed materials, purified resources, salvage, Runes, Rootsteel, Resonance Alloy, Spirit Glass, Memory Stone.
- **Commander Vex + captive Eco-Kin:** antagonist coercion system. Command Collars create rescue missions and moral/world consequences; the player dismantles them.
- **Ryn:** ideological rival, proof that personal power without Harmony can become self-destructive.
- **Nature:** communicates consent and world-state through Resonance rather than ordinary ownership mechanics.

## Player mechanics

The player can:

- Observe
- Protect
- Calm
- Kindle
- Bond
- Release
- Defer
- Treat
- Relocate
- Invite to Sanctuary
- Travel together
- Train cooperatively
- Enter real-time combat or approved Arena/EchoDeck simulations

The player cannot:

- Sell sentient Eco-Kin as property
- Force work
- Force evolution or mutation
- Use Eco-Kin as fuel, ammunition, ingredients, or disposable crafting inputs
- Convert a living individual into inventory data

## Sanctuary aptitude

“Sanctuary aptitude” means a preferred helpful behavior the Eco-Kin may voluntarily perform, such as scouting, garden care, water care, repair assistance, comfort, archive help, or defense. It is not a job assignment that overrides agency.
