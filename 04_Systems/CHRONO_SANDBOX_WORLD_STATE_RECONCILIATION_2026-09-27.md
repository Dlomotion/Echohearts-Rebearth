# ECHOHEARTS: REBEARTH — CHRONO-SANDBOX WORLD-STATE RECONCILIATION

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Status:** CANON-CORRECTED SYSTEM DIRECTION / IMPLEMENTATION PENDING  
**Source intake:** Section XXXI Tri-Core Environmental Matrix, boss/progression notes, save/UI loop, and repeated Chrono-Sandbox material.

## 1. Purpose

Preserve the strongest design idea from the Section XXXI packet — a living world whose ecology changes because of corruption and restoration — while removing copied franchise framing, absolute-stat exploits, sentient-partner ownership language, and unsupported production claims.

The result must connect to the existing Rebearth story, Blight ecology, Kindling, A.E.G.I.S., D.A.H.L.I.A., V/D/H/P, seasonal Eco-Kin cadence, and evidence-first UE5.8 production plan.

## 2. World-state foundation

Two broad world-state presentations may be retained:

- **Pristine / Recovering Rebearth** — regions whose ecology and Heart-Code are stable enough to sustain natural migration, restoration and community growth.
- **Fractured Rebearth** — regions destabilized by Blight, Iron Assembly interference, Chrono damage, Ancient Tech failure, ecological collapse or story events.

These are regional/world-state descriptors, not separate duplicate games or world saves by default.

Each region should expose authored state data such as:

- `RegionStateID`;
- Purity/restoration percentage;
- Blight family/severity;
- ecology health;
- migration pressure;
- resource stress;
- active anomaly source;
- sanctuary/outpost integrity;
- quest/season modifiers;
- restoration milestone state.

## 3. Three corruption families — original Echohearts rewrite

The pasted labels `Shallow Hallow`, `The Corruption`, and `The Cript` are not used as active production names. Their one-to-one outside-franchise framing is retired.

The underlying ecological functions are retained through original working Echohearts categories:

### A. Glassbloom Blight — false radiance / stasis

**Working status:** APPROVED-PENDING naming and art review.

Fantasy:

- radiant crystal overgrowth;
- beautiful but biologically flattening;
- suppresses individual ecological variation;
- water/ice/mineral surfaces become over-ordered and brittle.

Gameplay effect:

- temporary Harmony amplification can occur;
- Purity and agency deteriorate as severity rises;
- affected Eco-Kin may become unnaturally passive or synchronized;
- the condition is **recoverable through cleansing/restoration** unless a specific story beat establishes otherwise.

Do not set Harmony to an unconditional maximum or Purity to literal zero for every entity. Use authored severity curves, resistance, ecology traits and caps.

### B. Mawrot Blight — devouring organic collapse

**Working status:** APPROVED-PENDING naming and continuity review.

Fantasy:

- deep organic rot invading soil/stone seams;
- chasms, fungal-vein growth and structural decay;
- high-mass corrupted organisms and aggressive territorial behavior.

Gameplay effect:

- regional Density pressure may rise on corrupted enemies;
- structures in untreated zones suffer controlled degradation;
- extraction/resources become contaminated;
- restoration requires source removal, containment and habitat repair.

Do not apply a universal permanent 2% durability loss per minute. Decay must be bounded by material, severity, difficulty and maintenance systems.

### C. Bonewire Blight — ossuary/mechanical calcification

**Working status:** APPROVED-PENDING naming and art review.

Fantasy:

- pale calcified soil;
- bone-like mechanical growths and dead signal scaffolds;
- brittle structures carrying high-energy discharge;
- Iron Assembly/Necro-Strain intersections are possible where story-authorized.

Gameplay effect:

- armor-piercing or rigidity-breaking attacks may become more common;
- corrupted constructs trade toughness for brittleness/high Vibrance output;
- players can exploit fracture points rather than suffering blanket defense bypass.

No zone automatically ignores every defensive calculation.

## 4. Blight is a state, not a new species

A corrupted Eco-Kin remains the same underlying identity unless the canon explicitly establishes a distinct species.

Rules:

- corruption does not create a new Permanent Dex ID by itself;
- cleansed/purified/corrupted states belong in the Forms/State registry;
- player actions focus on protection, stabilization and restoration;
- no forced hive-mind state becomes an excuse to permanently erase Eco-Kin agency;
- seasonal variants may use one of these regional conditions without changing the parent identity.

## 5. Seasonal Eco-Kin connection

New Eco-Kin do not need to be implemented all at once.

Future sessions/seasons can introduce partners because:

- a restored biome supports a returning species;
- a Chrono-Rift reveals an Ancient Beast lineage;
- a migration route reopens;
- Glassbloom/Mawrot/Bonewire cleansing reveals a previously hidden ecology;
- Sky Reclamation opens a new habitat;
- an expansion introduces an off-world lineage.

The 125-ID Permanent Dex remains the current production identity authority. Availability changes by season; identity does not silently expand.

## 6. World-restoration loop

Canonical loop for a corrupted region:

**Detect → Survey → Protect → Contain → Cleanse Source → Restore Habitat → Reintroduce Ecology → Stabilize → Monitor**

This loop should feed:

- Kindling opportunities;
- regional traversal;
- Sanctuary resources;
- story choices;
- world events;
- boss/Sovereign encounters;
- seasonal migration;
- crafting and Ancient Tech repair;
- A.E.G.I.S. ecology records.

The strongest reward is visible world recovery, not just higher loot numbers.

## 7. Boss-progression correction

The pasted `Terraria-adapted` progression remains **REFERENCE-ONLY** as a direct template.

Transferable lessons that may survive:

- bosses should alter the arena/environment;
- phases should demand a new tactical response;
- traversal, ecology and team composition can matter;
- major victories can change world-state access;
- destructible spaces can create memorable encounter geometry.

Do **not** ship one-to-one equivalents of King Slime, Eye of Cthulhu, Eater of Worlds, Queen Bee, Skeletron, Wall of Flesh, The Twins, Destroyer, Skeletron Prime, Plantera, Golem, Duke Fishron, Cultist/Pillars or Moon Lord under replacement names with matching structure.

Existing Echohearts encounter names such as `Amorphous Core`, `Ocular-Vortex Nova`, `Hive-Queen Vespa`, and `Aether-Vortex Boundary` remain **design candidates requiring originality review**, not automatically locked bosses merely because a renamed comparison exists.

## 8. Replace “Hardmode” with an original world escalation contract

`Hardmode` is retired as active Echohearts terminology.

Working system name:

# **Fracture Escalation**

A major campaign victory may advance a world/region into a higher-threat state that introduces:

- stronger anomaly families;
- deeper Ancient Tech access;
- new restoration opportunities;
- altered migrations;
- new Sovereign encounters;
- advanced crafting/resource bands;
- Chrono-Rift instability;
- new seasonal story hooks.

Fracture Escalation must be authored and reversible/containable where story appropriate; it is not permission for uncontrolled permanent griefing of a shared server world.

## 9. Boss design rules for original Echohearts encounters

Every major boss/Sovereign fight should answer:

1. What ecology or story problem created this encounter?
2. What visible world-state does the boss affect?
3. What is the player protecting or restoring?
4. What changes between phases besides raw damage/HP?
5. How do Eco-Kin roles matter without forcing one exact species?
6. What traversal/environmental mechanic is unique to Echohearts?
7. What persists after victory?
8. What accessibility alternative exists for high-motion/visual mechanics?
9. What can be represented safely in multiplayer?
10. What evidence proves the encounter works in a packaged build?

## 10. V/D/H/P regional interaction

Regional corruption may modify V/D/H/P, but never through uncontrolled global absolutes.

Recommended model:

`EffectiveStat = SpeciesBaseline + InstanceGrowth + Kindling/Context + RegionalModifier + TemporaryCombatModifier`

Each regional modifier should include:

- min/max clamp;
- resistance/susceptibility tags;
- duration;
- cleanse/recovery behavior;
- UI telegraph;
- save/network policy.

Purity remains ecological/spiritual integrity and corruption pressure; it is not a morality score for the player or a biological “blood purity” mechanic.

## 11. Sanctuary/base connection

Base automation remains **Sanctuary Aptitudes / Partner Assist**, not forced factory labor.

Corruption-region recovery can create meaningful base tasks:

- water/soil purification;
- medical recovery;
- seed propagation;
- habitat reconstruction;
- material decontamination;
- sensor deployment;
- rescue logistics;
- Reclamation Cohort dispatch.

Elapsed-time production must be bounded by capacity, care, energy, habitat, fuel and persistence rules.

## 12. A.E.G.I.S. world-state interface

A.E.G.I.S. should expose the world system through a clear ecology dashboard:

- region restoration percentage;
- current Blight family/severity;
- active source/anomaly;
- protected habitats;
- migration indicators;
- weather/hazard state;
- local Sovereign warning;
- Chrono instability when applicable;
- recommended nonlethal field tools;
- accessibility-safe warnings.

D.A.H.L.I.A. may summarize and explain state changes but does not decide canon outcomes for the player.

## 13. Save/persistence relationship

Persistent world choices should store **authoritative region/world deltas**, not attempt to serialize the entire canonical species registry into every save.

Persistent examples:

- restoration milestone completed;
- Blight source removed;
- bridge/outpost rebuilt;
- bounded destructible checkpoint state;
- migration route opened;
- Sanctuary upgrade committed;
- Chrono record stabilized.

Temporary combat state should not leak into permanent world history unless an authored transaction commits it.

## 14. Current release scope

This system is larger than the funding vertical slice.

Vertical-slice implementation should prove only a narrow version:

- one biome/region;
- one corruption family;
- one restoration chain;
- one meaningful ecology change;
- one boss/Sovereign encounter;
- 4–6 polished Eco-Kin;
- save/reload of the changed regional state;
- packaged runtime evidence.

The remaining corruption families, major Chrono eras and seasonal Eco-Kin waves can expand later.

## 15. Verification boundary

This document does not prove:

- procedural global voxel infection;
- planet-scale destruction;
- seamless nested-city streaming;
- persistent shared-world Fracture Escalation;
- 125 active Eco-Kin simulation;
- raid-safe region propagation;
- completed boss AI;
- completed save/migration systems.

All of those remain implementation/R&D items behind the existing evidence gates.
