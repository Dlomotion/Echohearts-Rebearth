# Resonance Arena / Core-Bound Champions Legacy Reconciliation

Status: TACTICAL MODE PROPOSAL, NOT MAIN CAMPAIGN RULESET

Historical material proposed 4v4/5v5 turn-based combat, VIT/MGT/AGS/RSN/FORT/VEL stats, Rune-Matrix gear, mobile auto-farm, instant Mutation, Sovereign tournament ladders and a second title called `Core-Bound Champions`.

## Canon resolution

### Main campaign
The main Echohearts: Rebearth campaign remains **third-person real-time action combat** with the player active beside autonomous Eco-Kin.

### Resonance Arena
Turn-based or timeline-based combat is allowed only as an approved in-world competitive/simulation layer such as Resonance Arena, Harmony Circuit or EchoDeck.

### `Core-Bound Champions`
Do not create a second standalone game architecture by default. Salvage `Core-Bound Champions` as a possible Arena season, tournament circuit, title tier, ruleset, or event brand subject to naming/originality review.

## Stat migration
Historical VIT/MGT/AGS/RSN/FORT/VEL cannot replace the public core:
- Vibrance
- Density
- Harmony
- Purity

Arena-only derived values may exist behind the UI if they are deterministic derivatives of the authoritative model and documented in the Game Data Dictionary. Do not maintain a second independent stat owner.

## Element rule
The locked 12 elements remain:
Solar, Lunar, Hydro, Magma, Aero, Flora, Volt, Terra, Iron/Tech, Spirit, Chaos, Radiant.

Legacy labels such as Light, Shadow, Frost, Aether, Temporal, Void, Nature, Leaf, Flame, Tide, Metal or Neutral must be mapped case-by-case or retired. They do not expand the core element table automatically.

## Rune system
Resonance Runes remain viable as tactical equipment if they:
- produce readable build diversity;
- do not create pay-to-win power;
- use capped, testable modifiers;
- do not force evolution/growth;
- respect the same authoritative stats as the campaign.

Example Arena build identities may include Vanguard, Sentinel, Support, Control, Sustain and Disruptor, but those are tactical roles rather than new creature classes.

## Mutation / evolution correction
Historical instant `Mutate at 100% SYNC`, forced Mutation at Altars and repeated reversion for hidden stat gains are non-canon.

Use approved growth architecture instead:
- natural growth/evolution where biologically and narratively appropriate;
- Resonance Trait Infusion;
- Bio-Synergy Weaving;
- Primal Reversion;
- temporary Arena-safe resonance forms only when authored and consent-compatible.

## Sanctuary correction
Arena support systems may read Sanctuary upgrades but must not turn Sanctuary into a factory or produce creatures through summoning machines. Sanctuary remains a care/restoration/community hub.

## Cross-platform direction
Useful historical idea: management-heavy UI can be simplified for smaller screens while console/PC presents high-fidelity combat. However, cross-platform implementation must share authoritative progression and cannot give mobile auto-farm an unfair economy advantage.

## Tournament/story use
Apex Circuit/Sovereigns of the Spire can be salvaged as an Arena competition arc only if it does not duplicate existing Monolith trials, story Guardians or chapter progression. Completion should test restoration knowledge, team tactics and Kindling, not creature possession.

## Verification
NOT YET VERIFIED. No Arena combat implementation, stat migration, cross-platform synchronization or Rune balance is verified until actual repository/build/test evidence exists.
