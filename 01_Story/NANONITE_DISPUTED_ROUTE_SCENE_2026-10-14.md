## Scene purpose
Introduce Nanonite's reconstruction grievance through environmental evidence rather than letting arbitrary AI text direct the player. This is a bounded authored lead before any approved boss placement, not a substitute for existing bosses or the campaign.

At an existing Meridian archive chosen during world review, the player sees two projected route marks beside an Eco-Kin recovery corridor. One points across visibly damaged roots; the other loops along a repaired walkway. Both are plausible, but only the local survey record has current habitat and passage evidence.

D.A.H.L.I.A. reports: “Two route records disagree. Neither is cleared for travel yet.”
An archive fragment answers: “I restored the record. Why do you distrust the result?”
The player can inspect missing timestamps, compare physical root damage and ask the nearby synthetic citizen for their own memory. The citizen refuses to be described as someone's property. The fragment's later attribution to Nanonite requires story approval.
