# Eco-Kin Origin — Canon Reconciliation

Status: **APPROVED-PENDING / continuity merge**.

## Canon direction

Echohearts is not about collecting creatures as property. It is about restoring Rebearth, forming profound Kindlings with sentient Eco-Kin, uncovering how living systems were transformed, and stopping factions that turn the forces of nature into instruments of control.

## World-changing event

Historical drafts used names such as **Celestine Fall**, **Celestine Bang**, elemental comet, and other labels. These are retained as development history only until the Master Game Bible selects one final public name.

The usable story principle is preserved:

- A catastrophic celestial/resonant event altered Rebearth’s biosphere.
- The event carried elemental and frequency effects through living systems.
- Animals, plants, fungi, protists, microbial colonies, and later lab-created variants became capable of Eco-Kin emergence.
- Human institutions later studied recovered fragments and ancient technologies, creating the conditions for artificial Resonance experiments, weaponization, corruption, and the spread of The Blight/Data Virus.

## Biological-origin taxonomy

**Eco-Kin remains the franchise-wide collective term.**

The historical proposed biological groups remain **review labels**, not automatically locked public taxonomy:

- Animal-origin: Zoa
- Plant-origin: Flora
- Fungal-origin: Myko
- Protist-origin: Proto
- Microbial/colony-origin: Monera

These labels require originality, scientific-language, UI-readability, and existing-Dex compatibility review before canon lock.

## Birth / emergence

Historical “Resonance Emergence” is retained as a useful biological concept but corrected so it does not deny all natural reproduction.

Eco-Kin may enter the world through multiple legitimate paths depending on species:

1. **Natural birth / hatching** for animal-origin lineages.
2. **Seed, bud, bloom, fruiting-body, spore, colony, or fission emergence** for non-animal biological origins.
3. **Echo-Egg or lineage-specific inherited structures** where already established.
4. **Resonance Emergence** when a living ecosystem reaches unusual Harmony/Purity conditions and a new individual awakens through the planet’s living resonance.
5. **Synth-Origin creation** from ethical or unethical laboratory intervention, always carrying story consequences and identity rights.

No Eco-Kin exists merely as a manufactured stat package.

## Corruption

Historical Shadow Beast / Shadow Ecoform language maps into the established **Blight-Touched / Blightborn / Data Virus** framework.

Corruption is a condition, not a moral species class. It may be caused by environmental collapse, coercion, unsafe experiments, extraction, memory trauma, or Blight exposure. The player’s first objective is to understand and stabilize, not assume the individual is evil.

## Factions

Historical faction names such as Aether Garrison, Umbral Covenant, Chrysalis Syndicate, Helix Consortium, or other pasted societies do not automatically create new canon factions. Their useful functions are merged into the existing faction network:

- coercive military weaponization,
- deliberate Blight corruption,
- illegal biological experimentation,
- resource extraction,
- black-market Eco-Kin exploitation,
- false restoration/control ideology.

Use existing Obsidian Ring, Shattered Chord, Apexium-related, Null/Blight-aligned, and established villain structures unless the Master Game Bible explicitly approves a new faction.

## Bonding / “capture” correction

Historical Echo Snares, Prism Capsules, spirit scrolls, restraints, low-health weakening, containment conversion, and similar capture language are **retired from player mechanics**.

Current player-facing loop:

**Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer**

Safe tools may include Resonance Anchors, Calm Rings, Purity Field Rings, Open-Gate Recovery Pens, Echo Cradles for emergency transport, Recovery Sleds, Habitat Return Beacons, music/song interaction, food/water placement, listening posts, and A.E.G.I.S. observation.

Any restraint/cage/collar technology belongs only to villainy, rescue emergencies under strict welfare rules, or historical evidence, never ordinary player collection.

## Story consequence

The origin mystery should unfold gradually through field ecology, ancient records, D.A.H.L.I.A., the EcoDex, damaged Meridian sites, Nature, the Ancient Oracle, corrupted biomes, and faction experiments. The player learns not only **what created Eco-Kin**, but how different societies responded to them: partnership, worship, study, exploitation, fear, commerce, militarization, and restoration.
