# 1. Formal title

# **THE VOID WAR**

The Void War is Echohearts: Rebearth's final pre-U.N.I.T.Y. war.

It is not a copied alternate-dimension war, not an Eternalism/multiverse plot, and not a conflict built around remote-controlled bodies or hidden child operators.

In Echohearts, **Void** is a war-era term for a catastrophic condition in which living, ecological, social and Heartroot connections have been severed so completely that the resulting absence begins behaving like an expanding system.

The Void War is therefore the war over whether **connection itself can survive**.

# 2. Trigger: what the Summoning War breaks

During the Summoning War, multiple factions reopen ancient Callways, Riftways, Spire routes, Guardian channels and sealed Ancient Tech corridors at the same time.

Those systems were never designed to operate together under damaged Great-Collapse conditions.

The resulting overload creates a chain of **Hollow Fronts**: regions where Blight damage, Gloom pressure, damaged Memory Lattices and severed Heartroot routes synchronize into one spreading failure state.

Ebonmaw exploits those failures.

The Void War begins when separate Hollowing incidents stop behaving like local disasters and start coordinating across distance.

D.A.H.L.I.A. identifies the first impossible pattern:

> **The breaches are not communicating. They are becoming the same silence.**

# 3. The enemy is not emptiness alone

The Void War has three active threat layers.

## A. Hollow Hosts

Living beings, damaged Eco-Kin, abandoned constructs, ruins, battlefield systems and ecological nodes can become Hollow Hosts.

Some remain recoverable.

Some retain memory fragments or recognizable behavior.

Some are too structurally destroyed to restore and must be severed or destroyed.

Infection alone never proves moral guilt.

## B. Severance Engines

Old war infrastructure from the World War, Great War, Fracture Wars and Summoning War is repurposed into systems that isolate regions from the Heartroot network.

These machines can:

- cut migration corridors;
- jam A.E.G.I.S. ecology telemetry;
- isolate settlements from emergency routes;
- fracture Riftways;
- suppress regional Kindling communication;
- overwrite or blank damaged historical records;
- convert abandoned Nodes into Hollow relays.

## C. Ebonmaw's Hollow Front

Ebonmaw becomes the strategic intelligence behind the war.

It does not need to occupy every city.

Its strategy is to make each region believe it is alone, each faction believe betrayal is inevitable, and each survivor believe cooperation only creates vulnerability.

Its victory condition is not territorial conquest.

Its victory condition is **permanent disconnection**.

# 4. Nature's role

Nature becomes the central savior figure of the Void War because the Hollow attacks exactly what she exists to defend.

Her Living Accord capability can temporarily reconnect isolated systems, but she cannot simply erase the Void.

Every connection she restores creates strain across her own body and Heartroot-linked senses.

The Core-Binder learns the key limitation:

> **Nature can reopen a connection. She cannot make anyone choose to keep it.**

That prevents her from becoming a one-character solution.

Nature's destiny during the Void War is to learn how to distribute connection rather than carry every connection herself.

This lesson becomes the mechanical and emotional prerequisite for U.N.I.T.Y.

# 5. Void War campaign structure

## VOID-01 — The Silence Between Routes

After the Summoning War, major travel and communication paths begin failing without obvious physical destruction.

The Core-Binder investigates multiple regions and discovers that Heartroot connections are being severed from the inside.

Gameplay:

- rescue cut-off settlements;
- trace dead route segments;
- defend repair crews;
- identify recoverable Hollow Hosts;
- stop opportunistic factions from looting isolated communities;
- restore one regional connection before its ecology crosses a permanent collapse threshold.

## VOID-02 — Hollow Host

The player encounters the first major named Hollow Host who can still communicate intermittently.

The mission establishes the Void War's core moral rule: not every enemy-shaped target should be killed immediately.

A.E.G.I.S. must differentiate:

**Recoverable / Uncertain / Irrecoverable / Active Lethal Threat.**

The mission can end in rescue, containment, severance or lethal destruction depending on actual state and player execution.

## VOID-03 — The Broken Skyway

A major sky route collapses while refugees, faction convoys and Eco-Kin migration groups are crossing it.

The Core-Binder fights through a moving aerial battlefield while Nature grows living root-bridges and temporary traversal structures.

The player cannot save every route personally; allied faction outcomes from earlier acts determine which fronts receive support.

## VOID-04 — Archive of Missing Names

Chrono Archives begin losing references to real people and places—not because history is being rewritten, but because damaged records are being disconnected from every surviving index that points to them.

The Warden of Seconds and D.A.H.L.I.A. help the player recover evidence from independent sources.

This mission reinforces the established Chrono rule:

**repair memory; do not rewrite causality.**

The player's task is historical reconstruction, not timeline replacement.

## VOID-05 — Siege of the Living Root

Ebonmaw launches a coordinated attack on multiple Heartroot junctions.

Nature refuses to retreat into permanent protection because doing so would leave entire regions disconnected.

This becomes a large-scale real-time combat chapter:

- armed faction warfare;
- Eco-Kin partner combat;
- aerial assaults;
- underground root-defense routes;
- destructible war infrastructure;
- evacuation missions;
- Hollow Host rescue/destruction choices;
- moving Nature support objectives;
- multiple simultaneous fronts.

No hub has automatic plot immunity.

## VOID-06 — The World Stops Answering

Ebonmaw succeeds temporarily.

For a short period, major Heartroot regions stop responding to one another.

A.E.G.I.S. cannot provide a complete planetary picture.

D.A.H.L.I.A. loses remote telemetry.

Faction leaders receive contradictory information.

Eco-Kin migration behavior changes.

Weather systems destabilize.

The player must operate on local evidence and relationships rather than a perfect central map.

This is the Void War's lowest point.

## VOID-07 — Nature's Choice

Nature reaches the oldest surviving Heartroot chamber and discovers that she can force every disconnected route back online by centralizing the entire network through herself.

Doing so would save millions immediately—but would create the exact planetary command architecture every previous war tried to own.

Nature refuses the shortcut.

Instead, she chooses the harder path: reconnect regions one at a time and give each region independent control over whether it joins the emerging Living Accord.

This decision defines her destiny.

## VOID-08 — The Hollow Crown

Ebonmaw manifests its largest coordinated war-form through a network of Hollow Hosts and Severance Engines.

This is not Ebonmaw's final philosophical defeat, but it is the final military battle of the Void War.

The Core-Binder and Nature cannot win through damage alone.

The battle requires:

- destroying irrecoverable Severance Engines;
- rescuing recoverable Hosts;
- reopening independent regional links;
- keeping Nature mobile rather than trapped as a static power source;
- using allied factions earned through previous acts;
- protecting Heartroot junctions long enough for local communities to choose reconnection.

The Hollow Crown collapses when enough regions independently reconnect that Ebonmaw can no longer maintain one synchronized silence.

# 6. The Void War twist

The key revelation is that Ebonmaw did not create every weakness it exploits.

The World War, Great War, Fracture Wars and Summoning War created much of the infrastructure, fear and historical isolation that make the Hollow possible.

Ebonmaw's genius is **coordination of existing fractures**.

The enemy therefore cannot be permanently defeated by killing one being.

If Rebearth returns to the same political and ecological isolation afterward, another Hollow Front can form.

This directly creates the need for U.N.I.T.Y.

# 7. How the Void War changes U.N.I.T.Y.

Before the Void War, U.N.I.T.Y. is an idea.

After the Void War, it is a survival requirement.

The war proves five things:

1. centralized control is too dangerous;
2. total isolation is too fragile;
3. Nature cannot safely carry the whole world alone;
4. technology cannot replace trust;
5. connection must be distributed, voluntary and redundant.

That becomes the architecture of the final Living Accord.

The war continuum is therefore:

**WAR ON HUMANITY / WORLD WAR → THE GREAT WAR → SPACETIME CONFLICT / FRACTURE WARS → SUMMONING WAR → VOID WAR → FINAL U.N.I.T.Y. WAR**

# 8. U.N.I.T.Y. carry-forward states

Void War outcomes feed directly into the finale through state such as:

- number of restored Heartroot junctions;
- Hollow Hosts rescued;
- Hosts irreversibly lost;
- settlements abandoned or evacuated;
- factions that shared emergency infrastructure;
- factions that hoarded routes/resources;
- verified historical archives recovered;
- Severance Engines destroyed;
- local regions that voluntarily joined the Living Accord;
- Nature trust/strain state;
- Sanctuary and Sky-route damage;
- cosmic allies who answered Void War distress calls.

These are design-state requirements, not claims of an implemented runtime schema.

# 10. Final Void War thesis

Every earlier war fought over **who controls power**.

The Void War asks something worse:

> **What happens when nobody trusts connection enough to share power at all?**

Nature's answer becomes the foundation of U.N.I.T.Y.:

> **Connection is not surrender. A bond remains alive only when every side can still choose.**

The Void War ends when Rebearth survives the silence.

U.N.I.T.Y. begins when its people decide to answer one another again.
