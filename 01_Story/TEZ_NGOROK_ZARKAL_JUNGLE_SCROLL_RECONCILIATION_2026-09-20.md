## AI Mistake Patch / Continuity QA

Historical chat material drifted between treating **Tez**, **N’gorok** and **Zar’kal** as separate characters and one assistant sentence implying they should become a single secret Kin. That merge is rejected because the broader source material gives them distinct identities, roles and encounters.

Current reconciliation:

- **Leontez “Tez”** = hero / ally candidate, working title **The Sun-Forged Maverick**.
- **N’gorok** = lion-like fallen ruler / corrupted regional boss candidate.
- **Zar’kal** = crow-based Blight-Weaver antagonist candidate. The user's explicit correction that Zar’kal **is a crow** overrides the earlier jackal-like description.
- **Jungle Scroll** = hidden lore/discovery artifact that may reveal a secret Eco-Kin, lost route, ritual history or character truth. The exact secret Eco-Kin identity is **unresolved** and must not be fabricated as locked canon.
- Historical `Solar`, `Dark`, `Shadow`, `Fire` and similar labels must use the active Rebearth Essence canon when gameplay data is authored. Solar remains a celestial/cycle motif rather than a primary Essence.
- Historical **Lumina Wilds** remains a location candidate pending mapping into the current Rebearth world. It does not create a new continent.

## Leontez “Tez”

**Working role:** regional hero / recurring ally / playable-adjacent story character candidate  
**Working title:** The Sun-Forged Maverick  
**Status:** APPROVED-PENDING

### Identity direction

Tez should feel energetic, confident, warm and defiant without becoming a parody or a copy of an outside character.

Preserve from the source direction:
- distinctive dreadlocks;
- functional, expressive adventure clothing;
- sun-stone / solar-etched ornament possibilities;
- fluid parkour-influenced movement;
- playful confidence that can become serious when people or Eco-Kin are endangered;
- a strong visual sun/lion motif.

Do not literally turn Tez into a lion unless a later canon decision defines him as nonhuman. Lion imagery may appear through silhouette language, crest motifs, armor shapes, color blocking, faction heritage or an associated Eco-Kin.

### Essence / ability migration

Historical 'solar power' becomes an authored combination of:
- **Pyre** for heat/flame manifestations;
- **Aura** for protective/life-force projection where appropriate;
- **Solar** as a celestial/cycle tag or visual tradition, not a tenth Essence.

Final kit is pending character-combat design.

### Dialogue direction

Historical catchphrases are source material, not mandatory repeated barks. Keep the voice concise, confident and human. Avoid turning every line into a sun pun.

## N’gorok

**Working role:** fallen lion-like ruler / tragic boss / regional authority figure  
**Status:** APPROVED-PENDING

### Core story function

N’gorok once tried to protect his people/territory and accepted increasingly dangerous methods as fear of darkness, loss or collapse grew. Zar’kal exploited that fear. The result should connect to Echohearts' central theme: protection can become control when fear overrides consent and stewardship.

### Visual direction

- lion-inspired anatomy/identity if retained;
- regal but battle-worn presentation;
- damaged sun imagery rather than generic evil spikes everywhere;
- Blight/Gloom effects visibly layered over an original readable identity;
- black/crimson effects may mark corruption but do not become new Essences.

### Boss encounter salvage

Keep the useful arena principle from the old sun-dial concept:
- light/shade zones that change tactical safety;
- destructible/disabled environmental reflectors;
- escalating loss of safe space;
- an opportunity to weaken corruption or restore environmental controls rather than only depleting health.

Do **not** lock the historical 30% transformation number until encounter testing.

## Zar’kal — The Blight-Weaver

**Working role:** crow antagonist / ritual manipulator / architect of N’gorok's fall  
**Status:** APPROVED-PENDING

### Canon correction

Zar’kal is **crow-based**, not jackal-based.

### Visual direction

- avian/crow anatomy or crow-associated humanoid silhouette must be decided explicitly before final art;
- black sunstone / cracked relic motifs may remain;
- crimson/onyx garments or plumage accents are acceptable;
- mask, staff and ritual objects should be original to Echohearts and avoid generic occult clutter;
- visible Blight effects should remain distinct from the natural Shade Essence.

### Ability direction

Historical abilities translate into bounded systems:
- short-range Shade traversal / authored rift repositioning;
- Blight curses or field contamination;
- temporary disruption of Pyre/Aura techniques;
- ritual summons using already-established corrupted fauna/Eco-Kin encounter rules;
- environmental ritual nodes the player can disable, cleanse or redirect.

No unrestricted teleportation, permanent power nullification or resurrection without explicit story rules.

## Jungle Scroll

**Status:** CANON-COMPATIBLE MECHANIC / exact content APPROVED-PENDING

The Jungle Scroll should be a physical lore object, not a menu-only key.

Possible functions:
- layered map fragments visible only after environmental conditions are met;
- pictographic clues to a hidden habitat;
- a record of Tez/N’gorok/Zar’kal history;
- a route to an unidentified rare Eco-Kin;
- a warning about a Heartroot wound or Meridian site;
- a puzzle requiring observation of wildlife, sun angle, rainfall or ruins rather than a generic password.

The unidentified secret Eco-Kin must receive its own Dex intake before being named or promoted.

## World integration

The Tez/N’gorok/Zar’kal arc should attach to an existing or approved-pending Rebearth region rather than creating an isolated game world.

Recommended integration test:
- humid/jungle sub-biome connected to a Tree of Life root branch;
- old solar/Meridian observatory or sun-alignment infrastructure;
- community that remembers N’gorok before his fall;
- Zar’kal exploiting a Blight/Gloom fault;
- Jungle Scroll quest begins as exploration and culminates in a moral/territorial crisis.

`Lumina Wilds` remains a working regional/sub-biome name until `02_World` placement review approves or renames it.

## Story consequences

The arc should support at least three outcomes:
1. expose Zar’kal and stabilize N’gorok without erasing responsibility;
2. defeat/contain N’gorok while preserving evidence of manipulation and history;
3. fail or partially restore the regional root, producing a persistent altered world state.

A successful outcome does not reset the region to untouched perfection.
