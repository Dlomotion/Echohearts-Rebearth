# Shattered Frontier — Character Cast Canon Reconciliation

Status: **APPROVED-PENDING**. Integrated into the existing Rebearth story as an early/mid-campaign character arc, not as a replacement storyline.

## Player

### Frequency Tamer / Veridian Keeper
Historical draft title: **Core-Binder**.

The player begins as a stranded survivor/scavenger in the Vespera Basin near the Whispering Glade and grows into a restorer whose choices reshape Rebearth. “Core-Binder” may survive as an old Meridian-era title discovered in archives, but the live player-facing role remains Frequency Tamer / Veridian Keeper.

The player’s Resonance Pulse is an A.E.G.I.S.-mediated field technique used to scan, calm, stabilize, purify, and coordinate. It does not override Eco-Kin agency.

## Core allies

### Kaelen “Kale” — Frontier Scout
Merged identity: earlier “Kale” becomes the nickname for established **Kaelen**.

Role: survival guide, Sanctuary construction mentor, traversal teacher, skeptical of overreliance on Ancient Tech.

Partner: **Sherlock Hound**.

Story function: teaches the player that building a Sanctuary is not about ownership; it is about creating a place where people and Eco-Kin can choose to recover, work together, or leave.

### Liora — Resonance Engineer
Historical draft title: Aether-Engineer.

Role: engineer/researcher who survived the collapse of a Meridian chronometry research annex. She designs Purification Spire upgrades, safe field instruments, and clean energy systems.

Correction: “Aether” is not a core element. Her engineering discipline is **Resonance / Ancient Tech**. “Chronos Hub” is treated as a retired/legacy label pending world-map review.

## Sanctuary and world NPCs

### Elder Thorne — Restoration Mentor
Guides Growth Rites, safe Resonance adaptation, old-world ethics, and the historical record surrounding Nature.

Correction: “Mutation Rituals” becomes **Growth Rites / Resonance Adaptation**. Nothing forces transformation.

### Mora — Relic Smith
Operates the Solar Forge. Refines **Resonance Alloy, Rootsteel, Runes, Memory Stone, Spirit Glass, and purified materials** into field equipment.

Correction: “Echo-Steel” is a legacy nickname, not a new core material unless later approved.

### Collector Silas — Traveling Merchant / Information Broker
Appears during rare Lunar/Red-Veil nights and migration events. Trades legal field supplies, seeds, cosmetic relics, books, naturally shed materials, and safe purified resources.

Corrections: “Blood-Moon” becomes **Red Veil / Lunar Veil** terminology. “Void-Glass” maps to **Spirit Glass / Moon-Glass** unless a separate material is approved. Restoration Units (ERU) remain an economy proposal, not locked currency.

### Nurse Calla — Recovery Specialist
Works between the Sanctuary Recovery Ward, Healing Incubators, and Field Academy care facilities. Treats Blight-Touched Eco-Kin, fear injuries, Spirit Load, poisoning, memory wounds, and environmental trauma.

Correction: “Shadow-Bound” becomes **Blight-Touched / Memory-Wounded** in player-facing canon.

## Antagonists

### Echolord Vorlag, “The Void-Scholar”
The pasted “Void-Scholar” is merged into existing villain continuity as an alias/title connected to **Echolord Vorlag**, not a second unrelated grand antagonist.

Belief: the Circle of Life endlessly repeats pain, so choice should be replaced by a controlled world-reset system.

Goal: seize Nature’s resonance and Meridian control architecture to impose a false solution to suffering.

### Commander Vex — Industrial Enforcement Commander
Role: senior extraction commander tied to an industrial faction cell. “Iron-Syndicate” is treated as a field-cell/legacy label pending faction cleanup, with Obsidian Ring/Apexium/Shattered Chord relationships reconciled elsewhere.

Villain mechanic: **Command Collars** and coercive extraction harnesses are antagonist-only technologies used to enslave Eco-Kin and force mining or combat. Players disable, expose, and dismantle them. They never become a player progression tool.

### Ryn, the Blightmarked Challenger
Historical title retired: “Shadow-Stalker Ryn,” because Shadow Stalker is already used as a conflicting enemy label.

Ryn is a rival traveler who self-experimented with Blight exposure and Ancient Tech. Their body corruption is not Eco-Kin fusion. They challenge the player in Harmony Circuit / Resonance Arena encounters to prove power is stronger than trust.

## Sentinels

### Nature — Guardian of Life
Nature remains the franchise’s primary Guardian of Life. Nature communicates through Resonance, environmental responses, visions, and behavior more often than ordinary speech.

Nature may guide the player toward the first **Resonance Kit**, but does not become a collectible, summonable inventory object, or replacement starter.

### The Ancient Oracle — Rooted World Witness
A giant stationary Flora-aligned elder Eco-Kin/guardian-form rooted into an ancient Heartroot-linked site.

Function: its changing glow, leaf patterns, root-song, and A.E.G.I.S. interpretation reveal restoration routes, forgotten memories, migration warnings, and regional distress.

Correction: it does not literally replace the game’s map or quest log. It is an in-world information source that feeds the A.E.G.I.S./EcoDex interface.

## Story placement

This cast enters through the existing chapter grid:

1. Vespera Basin / Whispering Glade: Kaelen, Sherlock Hound, first Sanctuary work.
2. Echohearts Field Academy / Central Pavilion: Liora, Nurse Calla, historical records.
3. Industrial Edge: Commander Vex and coercive extraction technology.
4. Wider restoration arc: Mora, Silas, Elder Thorne, faction pressure.
5. Arena/Rival arc: Ryn.
6. Guardian/late-story revelations: Nature, Ancient Oracle, Vorlag/“Void-Scholar.”

Every appearance must connect to a mission trigger, system lesson, world-state consequence, relationship change, and persistent save flag.
