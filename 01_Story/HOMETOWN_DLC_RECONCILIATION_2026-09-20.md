## Preserved source idea
A later story expansion revisits the protagonist's hometown after it has been devastated by an enemy force. Survivors scatter, community infrastructure collapses, and the player must investigate what happened while helping people rebuild. The source ties this arc to emotional memory, farming/economic recovery, survivor quests and buried Ancient Tech history.

## AI Mistake Patch
- Do not create a brand-new villain faction solely because the historical text said `villainous team`. Map the perpetrators to an existing faction, antagonist network or approved expansion force after continuity review.
- Do not assume the hometown is on Rebearth, Earth or another world until the main story timeline and portal history establish the location.
- Do not use destruction only as spectacle. The arc must change survivors, settlements, economy, world state and the player's relationships.
- Historical breeding stations, Eco-Kin storage and forced worker automation are not current canon.
- Historical squad-of-five battle rules do not replace the active 8-roster / 3-active real-time campaign contract.
- Gold remains a historical economy candidate until the currency ledger confirms final use.

## Story value to preserve
1. **Return:** the player revisits a place tied to personal history.
2. **Loss:** familiar landmarks are damaged or gone.
3. **Investigation:** clues connect the attack to larger world history.
4. **Survivors:** named residents respond differently to displacement and rebuilding.
5. **Restoration:** farms, water, homes, routes and community spaces visibly recover.
6. **Choice:** rebuild in place, relocate some residents, or restore only what the community consents to restore.
7. **Memory:** repaired places retain scars rather than resetting to a perfect pre-attack state.

## Mission-chain candidate structure
### HT-01 — The Road Back
Trigger: story unlock and verified travel route.
Player action: reach the hometown, scan damage, locate survivors.
Visible delta: emergency safe zone established.

### HT-02 — Names on the Door
Player action: identify missing residents through evidence and witness accounts.
Visible delta: Havenlink registry and survivor board become active.

### HT-03 — Water Before Walls
Player action: restore clean water before cosmetic rebuilding.
Visible delta: sickness risk drops and residents begin returning.

### HT-04 — The Field Remembers
Player action: restore contaminated farmland through Living Soil systems.
Visible delta: first harvest and food security return.

### HT-05 — What They Came For
Player action: investigate the attacker motive and recover evidence without looting sacred/community property.
Visible delta: antagonist connection is revealed.

### HT-06 — Rebuild or Relocate
Player action: resolve resident/community preference through dialogue and material support.
Visible delta: settlement layout and population state branch.

### HT-07 — Hometown, Changed
Player action: defend the recovery effort and complete the community restoration decision.
Visible delta: permanent scarred-but-living final state.
