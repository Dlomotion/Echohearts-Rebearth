# Hometown DLC Reconciliation

Status: **STORY ARC PROPOSAL / APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT INTAKE**

## Core premise

Later in the Echohearts: Rebearth story, the player returns to a meaningful hometown/community space that has been devastated by an established villain network. The destruction is not a disposable spectacle. It creates survivors, displacement, grief, investigation, rebuilding pressure and a direct emotional link between the player’s past and Rebearth’s larger conflict.

## Continuity rules

- Do not create a disconnected “villainous team” if an existing faction or antagonist network can carry the attack.
- The event must connect to the Blight/Data Virus, Ancient Tech exploitation, the Circle conflict, or another already-established main-story pressure.
- The player’s response should include rescue, investigation, restoration and community rebuilding, not only revenge combat.
- Eco-Kin remain autonomous partners during evacuation, search, defense and reconstruction.
- The hometown is not restored to a perfect reset. Rebuilding preserves scars, memorials and changed relationships.

## DLC gameplay pillars

1. **Emergency return** — travel back after the crisis signal and assess damage.
2. **Rescue and Havenlink response** — locate survivors, injured Eco-Kin and missing residents.
3. **Evidence trail** — use A.E.G.I.S., local witnesses and environmental traces to identify the responsible faction/cell.
4. **Community stabilization** — water, food, shelter, medical support and defensive restoration.
5. **Rebuilding choices** — prioritize homes, farming, clinic/recovery, trade, Sanctuary support, memorials and infrastructure.
6. **Counter-operation** — confront the antagonist plan without turning the DLC into a simple revenge corridor.
7. **Persistent aftermath** — NPCs, services, local ecology and town visuals remain changed based on the player’s decisions.

## Farming / productivity connection

The hometown arc is a natural place to make the farming and Sanctuary systems emotionally meaningful.

Rebuilding can require:
- seeding and watering restored plots
- harvesting food for survivors
- sustainable wood/material recovery
- livestock care where the region supports it
- processing stations
- Community Supply Crates
- voluntary Eco-Kin helper assignments
- area-level upgrades that expand helper capacity

The player should feel that rebuilding the local economy is part of healing the community, not a detached minigame.

## Story consequences

Possible persistent outcomes:
- survivors relocate or return
- businesses reopen in different forms
- memorial spaces appear
- certain NPC relationships deepen or fracture
- local Eco-Kin behavior changes as habitat health improves
- new investigation leads connect back to the main antagonist network
- the hometown becomes a late-game support hub if adequately restored

## Mission proof requirements

Every Hometown DLC mission must specify:
- prerequisite
- trigger
- objectives
- taught/used system
- terminal completion proof
- visible world delta
- NPC/Eco-Kin consequence
- reward/unlock
- save flag
- ethical alternative where relevant
- failure/partial state
- valid successor

## Naming status

**“Hometown”** remains the working DLC/arc title until the actual settlement name and its location in the current Rebearth map are locked.
