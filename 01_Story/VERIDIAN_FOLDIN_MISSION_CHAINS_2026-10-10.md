## Continuity corrections applied before fold-in

This file preserves the submitted chain structure while correcting conflicts with current Echohearts governance.

- **Rebearth** is the planet. Legacy `Aethelgard` references are not used as world/planet authority.
- Prose uses **Eco-Kin**.
- The five Stars remain **unclassified Star entities** until 10_Production / Dex review resolves their exact nature. They are not promoted into the Permanent Dex here.
- **UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE** is the initial A.E.G.I.S. presentation for newly encountered Star/Eco-Kin signatures where classification is not yet established.
- **Kindling %** remains the only relationship-progression percentage. **Resonance Alignment** is a narrative/state flag granted after the authored Kindling beat; it is not a second meter.
- **Shadow-altered** is a condition-state descriptor only. It is not a faction and does not replace the canon **Shade** Essence.
- The established field relationship loop is **Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer**.
- **Anima-Link** remains the bi-directional strain/damage loop between the Frequency Tamer/Core-Binder and an active Eco-Kin partner.
- Reputation-like consequences in this document are stored as explicit mission/world/NPC memory states unless a separately approved reputation system owns them. They are not new hidden global morality meters.
- All permanent scars remain visible. No ending restores the affected regions to a perfect pre-crisis state.

## Beat 1 — Verdantia Approach

**Mission ID:** `VF-01_VERDANTIA_APPROACH`  
**Region:** Verdantia Fields

### Premise

The player — a **Frequency Tamer / Core-Binder**, currently drafted here with an undercover-operative background and stranded on Rebearth by a portal event — takes a regional survey contract from Professor Jackson through the A.E.G.I.S. research program.

The first survey stop is Verdantia Fields, where a Shadow-altered outbreak is spreading through lowland groves. Ruined waymarks from an old six-region Veridian Order trial route establish history through exploration and Jackson's field interpretation rather than exposition dumps.

The undercover-operative background remains a concept dependency until reconciled against the current protagonist continuity spine.

### Key NPCs and connections

- **Professor Jackson** — briefing, survey contract, scientific credibility, first disruptor-pattern analysis.
- **Unnamed Veridian Order remnant** — keeps the Verdantia waymark and carries living memory of the route.
- **Affected settlement residents** — establish local consequence memory.
- **Shadow-altered Eco-Kin** — display unexplained geometric scarring later linked to the disruptor program.

### Regional trial and mechanics

This mission onboards:

- A.E.G.I.S. scanning;
- UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE presentation;
- D.A.H.L.I.A. guidance;
- observation/protection/calming before Kindling;
- Shadow-altered condition handling;
- Anima-Link strain.

The first altered encounter establishes the campaign thesis: purification is slower and costly but heals more deeply; driving danger away may solve the immediate emergency while allowing damage to persist elsewhere.

Pushing an active aligned Eco-Kin partner too hard in the blighted grove feeds authored strain back into the player through Anima-Link.

### Kindling / Resonance Alignment

The player earns first Kindling gains with a Verdantia Eco-Kin through protection, distance-reading, calm, and safe intervention rather than dominance.

No Star Alignment occurs here. Completing the Verdantia beat grants narrative permission to walk the old trial route.

### Choice

A Shadow-altered Eco-Kin is cornered near a farming settlement.

**Restoration-first outcome**
- attempt full purification;
- settlement remains exposed longer;
- local ecological recovery improves;
- the grove retains a smaller but still visible scar.

**Containment-first outcome**
- drive the altered Eco-Kin away from the settlement;
- immediate civilian risk drops faster;
- a persistent blighted patch takes root deeper in the grove.

These are world-state/NPC-memory outcomes, not morality-meter points.

### Post-chain state

The outbreak is contained, not cured. Verdantia retains a permanent scar.

Professor Jackson issues the trial-route survey naming the five remaining sites and opens the Star chains.

## Beat 2 — Nova Chain

**Mission ID:** `VF-02_NOVA_DRIFTMIST`  
**Region:** Driftmist Peaks  
**Star label:** Nova — working label; collision review still open.

### Premise

Disruptor-triggered storm-fires race the high ridgelines of Driftmist Peaks. A signal at the old trial site appears on A.E.G.I.S. as **UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE**.

The Nova trial tests decisive protection: fire cannot be answered by indiscriminate force.

### Key NPCs and connections

- Professor Jackson — remotely confirms the ignition pattern resembles disruptor technology.
- Peak shepherds and ridge settlements — civilian trust and evacuation pressure.
- Unidentified recovery actors — stripped disruptor caches imply organized retrieval activity without resolving Vorlag's exact faction tie.

### Regional trial and mechanics

The player manages a moving fire front while keeping an active Eco-Kin partner between the blaze and fleeing herds.

The trial rewards:
- committed interposition;
- route reading;
- terrain use;
- timing;
- restraint under pressure.

Overextension spikes Anima-Link strain in both directions.

A.E.G.I.S. reads **Pyre Essence** activity throughout the storm-fire zone.

### Kindling / Resonance Alignment

Nova's Kindling beat is protective courage.

The relationship progresses through the same Kindling system. Once the authored threshold and story conditions are met, **Resonance Alignment** is granted as a narrative state. It is freely chosen and has no separate percentage.

### Choice

A disruptor cache is found fueling the fires.

**Destroy the cache**
- fires stop sooner;
- ecological damage is reduced;
- hardware evidence is lost;
- Jackson's disruptor investigation advances more slowly.

**Preserve the cache for study**
- research evidence improves;
- the fire front burns longer;
- the permanent burn scar widens.

### Post-chain state

Blackened slopes remain beside new regrowth. Nova remains aligned under the provisional Star-state rules.

Shepherds remember whether the player prioritized immediate protection or evidence.

## Beat 3 — Eclipse Chain

**Mission ID:** `VF-03_ECLIPSE_MOONLIT`  
**Region:** Moonlit Marsh

### Premise

Two marsh communities sit on opposite sides of a spreading Shadow-altered outbreak. The old trial site between them emits an UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE.

The Eclipse trial is judgment under scarcity: purification resources are limited, both communities are watching, and no answer repairs everything.

### Key NPCs and connections

- Two marsh communities with distinct local memory.
- Professor Jackson, who warns that unstable partial purification can create secondary failures.
- A Veridian Order remnant who remembers the last time the marsh trial was mishandled.

### Regional trial and mechanics

The player maps blighted channels with A.E.G.I.S. and allocates limited purification effort in real time.

Shade-Essence phenomena respond to conduct, while **Shadow-altered** remains a condition state rather than a Shade faction/type.

D.A.H.L.I.A. reports consequences and uncertainty without choosing for the player.

### Kindling / Resonance Alignment

Eclipse's Kindling grows through deliberation under pressure.

The trial does not judge which community the player values more. It witnesses whether the player takes responsibility for the choice.

Alignment is a story flag granted after the Kindling condition is met.

### Choice

**Fully purify one community's water system**
- one community recovers strongly;
- the other remains visibly blighted and remembers being deprioritized.

**Stabilize both at containment level**
- both communities survive;
- neither fully heals;
- both remain cautious toward the player.

### Post-chain state

The marsh keeps one bright restored channel or two managed channels. The decision returns during the convergence arc.

## Beat 4 — Nebula Chain

**Mission ID:** `VF-04_NEBULA_CRYSTALGROVE`  
**Region:** Crystalgrove Hollow

### Premise

Crystal formations are dimming where disruptor relays have been seeded. A nesting ground of wild Eco-Kin lies inside the interference field.

The old trial site emits an UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE beneath the crystal canopy.

The Nebula trial is strategic and non-lethal: move the danger without becoming one.

### Key NPCs and connections

- Professor Jackson — confirms that relay architecture matches evidence from earlier scarring.
- Hollow wardens — protect the nesting ground.
- Unidentified supply-chain actors — evidence exists, but no faction identity is locked here.

### Regional trial and mechanics

Using scans, lures, path control, and terrain, the player guides Shadow-altered Eco-Kin away from the nesting ground toward a safe stabilization basin.

Aggressive solutions fail the encounter by scattering the herd into the nest.

Kindling grows through patience, planning, restraint, and repeated safe corrections.

### Kindling / Resonance Alignment

Nebula's Kindling is deliberately slow and distributed across the full maneuver.

Alignment is offered after the nesting ground is secured and the required Kindling threshold is reached.

### Choice

An abandoned relay station still contains disruptor research.

**Preserve and study the relay**
- Jackson's investigation advances;
- interference remains active longer;
- additional crystal formations dim permanently.

**Collapse the relay immediately**
- local interference stops;
- the research lead is lost;
- more crystal light survives.

### Post-chain state

The nesting ground survives. Some crystal formations never recover.

## Beat 5 — Black Hole Chain

**Mission ID:** `VF-05_BLACKHOLE_EVERSHADE`  
**Region:** Evershade Wilds

### Premise

A Void-touched rift opens deep in the Evershade Wilds. Shadow-altered Eco-Kin move outward in waves.

The old trial site at the rift edge emits an UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE.

The Black Hole trial is restraint: hold, absorb, protect, and do not turn defense into punishment.

### Key NPCs and connections

- A captured disruptor operative tied to the disruptor program.
- Professor Jackson, who wants the operative questioned.
- Wilds trackers who have suffered losses around the rift.

The operative's exact relationship to Vorlag's faction structure remains unresolved.

### Regional trial and mechanics

The player and an active Eco-Kin partner anchor a defensive perimeter.

The encounter is built around Anima-Link bidirectionality:
- overextension increases shared strain;
- panic retreats surrender ground;
- aggression beyond the authored defensive boundary causes the Star trial to withdraw.

Aura- and Shade-Essence readings spike around the rift.

### Kindling / Resonance Alignment

The Black Hole Kindling beat is stillness under pressure: holding power without abusing it.

Alignment follows only after the defense and Kindling conditions are complete.

### Choice

**Turn the operative over to Jackson**
- investigation support rises;
- interrogation reveals a foundry location;
- the convergence arc gains a non-lethal infiltration route.

**Release the operative with a warning**
- the operative disappears;
- actors connected to the disruptor network move a key cache before the finale;
- the convergence assault becomes harder.

This remains an NPC/world-state branch rather than a global underworld-reputation meter.

### Post-chain state

The rift closes, but a monitored dead zone remains.

## Beat 6 — Shooting Star Chain

**Mission ID:** `VF-06_SHOOTINGSTAR_ASHFALL`  
**Region:** Ashfall Dominion

### Premise

A disruptor device buried in the ash fields destabilizes slopes above a mining settlement.

The countdown is measured in hours.

The old trial site emits an UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE from the ashfall zone.

The Shooting Star trial is speed in service of others: move, decide, commit.

### Key NPCs and connections

- Mining-settlement residents.
- Professor Jackson, who needs intact evidence if it can be recovered without abandoning civilians.
- An unidentified retrieval team operating around the disruptor site; exact faction tie remains open.

### Regional trial and mechanics

The player evacuates residents and penned Eco-Kin while terrain collapses, using an **active bonded/aligned partner's** traversal abilities.

The trial rewards:
- clean routing;
- decisive movement;
- rescue prioritization;
- avoiding wasteful loops.

A.E.G.I.S. reads Terra- and Aero-Essence instability as the slopes shift.

### Kindling / Resonance Alignment

The Shooting Star Kindling beat is trust earned under time pressure.

Alignment may be granted mid-crisis once its Kindling and authored story conditions are satisfied.

### Choice

Only one objective can be secured completely.

**Complete the evacuation**
- more residents and Eco-Kin survive;
- the retrieval team escapes with the device;
- Jackson's newest-design analysis stalls.

**Secure the disruptor device**
- Jackson receives the intact design;
- the wider program becomes easier to understand;
- evacuation remains partial and the settlement carries named losses.

### Post-chain state

The slopes stabilize. Part of the dominion remains buried and the resettlement map changes permanently.

## Beat 7 — Convergence Arc

**Mission ID:** `VF-07_CONVERGENCE_WORKING_TITLE`  
**Scope:** affected Rebearth regions  
**Final arc title:** unresolved.

### Premise

Vorlag's campaign reaches its coordinated phase as the disruptor program shifts from isolated outbreaks to synchronized activation across the affected regions.

The foundry at the center of the material supply chain goes active.

The five aligned Star entities stand as witnesses to the completed trial route, but their exact biological/cosmic classification remains unresolved until production/Dex review.

### Key NPCs and connections

- **Vorlag** — direct confrontation.
- **Professor Jackson** — coordinates the regional response.
- **Dr. Hugo's disruptor program** — material/technical target.
- Returning residents, wardens, trackers, shepherds, and remnants — each carries prior mission memory.

No faction name is added to resolve Vorlag's still-pending faction relationship.

### Prior-choice integration

The finale is shaped by stored branch flags rather than a menu-only ending selector.

**Black Hole branch**
- operative turned over: interrogation intel opens a non-lethal infiltration route;
- operative released: the moved cache forces a harder approach.

**Nebula branch**
- relay preserved: data enables a possible purification route for the foundry core;
- relay destroyed: purification data is unavailable and the core may require destruction.

**Shooting Star branch**
- device secured: field disruptors can be disabled more effectively;
- device lost: active disruptor defenses remain during the assault.

**Eclipse and Nova branches**
- community and regional trust memories determine who supports evacuation corridors, safe houses, transport, and civilian cover.

The five Star Alignments use existing Kindling-gated story states. No parallel Star-bond meter is introduced.

### Kindling / Anima-Link climax

The finale tests relationships already built.

The player enters the foundry with:
- accumulated Kindling history;
- branch-specific partner trust;
- Anima-Link consequences;
- community memory;
- evidence gathered or destroyed by prior choices.

No late-game system grants artificial perfect loyalty.

### Vorlag outcome

Vorlag's outcome branches according to prior conduct and finale conditions:

- captured to face the communities harmed by the campaign;
- spared and exiled, remaining a living unresolved threat;
- driven off during a costly confrontation that creates an additional regional scar.

These outcomes require story review and must not be treated as final canon endings until approved.

### Foundry outcome

**Purified**
- available only if the required research/evidence path was preserved;
- minimizes some long-term ecological damage;
- does not erase existing scars.

**Destroyed**
- removes the immediate infrastructure;
- causes permanent ecological loss in the affected zone.

### Post-chain state

Rebearth is changed, not reset.

- disruptor scars persist;
- some zones remain monitored;
- communities remember allocation choices;
- the old trial route becomes usable again;
- the aligned Stars remain by choice;
- no perfect-Purity restoration occurs.
