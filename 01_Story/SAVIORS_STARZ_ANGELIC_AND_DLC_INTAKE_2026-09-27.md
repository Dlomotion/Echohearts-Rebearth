**Authority:** current Six-Era Story Continuity Spine, Star Rewrite, Chrono-Rift/Ancient Tech continuity, and current terminology lock.

## 1. Expansion placement

The new cosmic material is preserved as a **post-foundation expansion lane**, not a replacement for Rebearth's main 20-chapter story.

Working expansion/DLC titles supplied by the creator:

- **Summoning Wars** — working DLC title;
- **Saviors of the Universe** — working expansion / crossover-scale story lane;
- **Ancient Tech Wars** — working DLC title;
- **STARZ*** — working umbrella/title mark for a youthful cosmic-savior lane, pending naming/trademark/originality review.

These connect most naturally after the campaign reaches **Star Rewrite**, through Riftways, off-world crises, Ancient Tech discoveries, and multigenerational/cosmic story arcs.

## 2. Heaven / Naveah twins

**Heaven** and **Naveah** remain preserved as the existing twin-character concept.

They are not automatically Eco-Kin and do not consume Permanent Dex IDs merely because they appear in cosmic/angelic content.

Their exact role—human/Kin, celestial-linked NPCs, Core-Binders, Saviors, or another story category—remains a character-registry decision.

## 3. Saviors of the Universe premise

Working story core:

A widening cosmic Darkness begins consuming worlds through fear, corruption, domination, and damaged spirit-memory. **Nature** responds by calling or awakening a new generation of Saviors who must locate other heroes across the universe.

The Saviors are not replacements for Eco-Kin partnership. They are a separate heroic/cosmic cast that may fight beside Eco-Kin, Kin, Bots, mages, knights, guardians, or other original Echohearts peoples.

The player-facing fantasy may include:

- finding non-sentient relics such as Crystals, Runes, Sigils and Banners;
- awakening or recruiting eligible heroes through story relationships rather than ownership;
- choosing long-term character traits / power branches;
- protecting worlds from corruption;
- saving Lost Souls and Lost Angels where the story establishes they retain agency;
- differentiating benevolent and hostile Fiends rather than treating all beings of one taxonomy as morally identical.

## 6. Character motivation intake

- **Mena and Althea**: sisters who operate through dreams and attack the spirit/psyche; use psychological/cosmic horror without removing player agency.
- **Garth**: antagonist centered on humiliation, bullying and reducing others' sense of worth.
- **Deep**: tied to a distant region/realm of extreme Darkness; exact person/place distinction requires registry review.
- **Lord Dred**: human tyrant who spreads power through fear, conquest and enslavement; he and his human collaborators should remain morally responsible individuals rather than proof that humanity itself is evil.
- **Lunaz**: former Savior whose fall can explore corruption, grief, temptation or ideological fracture rather than simplistic species morality.

## 9. Taxonomy routing

The creator's additional broad creature/person categories are routed as follows:

- **Kin** — humanlike peoples with nonstandard genetic/biological traits; maps to existing Humanoid-Kin/Kin character taxonomy, not an Eco-Kin synonym.
- **Rad / robot concept** — routes to existing Bots/construct taxonomy unless an original `Rad` faction/species definition is later approved.
- **Bugs** — routes through Bug-Kin / insect Eco-Kin body families where sentient/ecological, or ordinary hostile fauna where not Eco-Kin.
- **Agents** — occupational/faction role, not biological species by default.
- **Blood / Water / Spirit** — may become authored technique, environment, culture, Aura/Shade/Torrent interaction, or story tags; they do not replace the nine production Essences.
- **Humanoids** — route through Humanoid-Kin / NPC character systems.

## 10. Connection to existing lore

The Saviors/STARZ material should enter through Star Rewrite and distant Riftway content so it can coexist with:

- Rebearth's Six Eras;
- the Aurelian deep past;
- Ancient Tech Wars;
- the Iron Assembly;
- Chrono-Rift scars;
- Heaven/Naveah;
- the Ancient Vanguard;
- later cosmic civilizations;

It must not retroactively rewrite the cause of every existing Eco-Kin, faction, or historical event.

## 11. Theme

The expansion lane keeps the project's core emotional rule:

> power matters, but relationship, courage, healing, restoration, memory and love determine what survives.

`A savior from the dark` is preserved as a strong thematic phrase for this lane.
