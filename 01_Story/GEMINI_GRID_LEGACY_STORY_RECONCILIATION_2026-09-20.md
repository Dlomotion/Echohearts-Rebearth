## The Six Eras: reconciled meaning

The Gemini material describes six digital eras. Those names and beats are not allowed to overwrite the current master story timeline. They may instead function as a **Meridian archival six-era model** recovered by the player.

### Archive Era I — Meridian Investigation

A late Meridian research period studying consciousness persistence, world-memory systems, ancient technology and the planet's failing ecological balance.

**Kept:** dangerous experimentation, data collapse, archive evidence, moral warnings.  
**Removed:** literal deletion of physical Earth / Rebearth.

### Archive Era II — Void of Silence

The collapse aftermath as preserved in damaged records: missing telemetry, fragmented messages, corrupt memory blocks and silent networks.

**Kept:** horror, missing people, broken archives, incomplete truth.  
**Removed:** humanity drifting as disembodied `Ghost Data` in a pure digital void.

### Archive Era III — Rebearth Awakening

Nature and Eco-Kin reclaim damaged systems and ruins while surviving communities rebuild.

**Kept:** lush neon-natural contrast, re-emergence, archaeological mystery.  
**Corrected:** the player is not a species called `Rebearth`; Rebearth is the planet.

### Archive Era IV — Divide of the Echoes

A period of ideological conflict over how Ancient Tech should be used: restoration, preservation, control, abandonment or transcendence.

**Kept:** moral division and faction choice pressure.  
**Corrected:** this cannot replace the current antagonist/faction network.

### Archive Era V — Great Compression

Ancient archives, broken world-state records and Echo Rifts begin to overlap with present locations.

**Kept:** glitched environmental storytelling, layered ruins, memory bleed, unstable historical records.  
**Corrected:** the world is not simply `running out of server memory`.

### Archive Era VI — New Horizon

The current civilization must decide how Rebearth will live with Ancient Tech, the Circle of Life, Eco-Kin autonomy and the scars of the Blight.

**Kept:** final-era identity, irreversible choice, future-facing ending.  
**Corrected:** no perfect reboot, no deathless utopia, no universal reality rewrite.

## True Ending — Harmonic Resonance

**Status:** CANON DIRECTION / REQUIREMENTS NOT FULLY LOCKED

The Gemini version correctly identifies **Harmonic Resonance** as the highest-order reconciliation ending, but its outcome is corrected to fit current canon.

### Preserved intent

- The player refuses a false binary between total technological control and total destruction.
- Ancient Tech is not simply erased or allowed to dominate life.
- Eco-Kin, people and restored ecosystems retain agency.
- The ending requires unusually strong mercy / restoration / memory-preservation choices.
- Astryx and other world-scale threats are resolved through understanding, protection and system-level choice rather than raw conquest alone.

### Corrected outcome

Harmonic Resonance does **not** abolish death, erase grief, perfectly restore every life, or make reality infinitely editable. Instead, the player helps stabilize a living relationship between Rebearth's ecology, Ancient Tech, world-memory systems and surviving communities.

The world survives changed. Damaged places retain scars. Lost people remain remembered. Repaired systems remain fallible. Eco-Kin remain autonomous living beings, not digital applications or world-editing tools.

### Ending-slot correction

The Gemini source claims five specific secret endings, but the current project only preserves **five secret-variant slots** in addition to the four primary ending families. Their exact names and requirements are not to be fabricated until the ending matrix is approved.
