## Visual target
Historical source direction described a Nintendo-esque readable aesthetic blended with a cinematic Unreal Engine 5.2 / 8K / ray-traced presentation. For current production, preserve the useful intent without copying another franchise:

**Current original Echohearts target:**
- highly readable, appealing creature and character silhouettes
- expressive faces and body language
- premium cinematic materials and lighting
- biological surfaces integrated with crystalline, Resonance, Ancient-Tech, or STARZ* details only when lore supports them
- strong full-body readability before surface-detail complexity
- cinematic Unreal Engine 5.8 presentation target, with scalable production settings rather than assuming 8K/ray tracing as a gameplay requirement

## Do not copy
Do not reproduce another publisher's characters, proportions, costumes, logos, UI language, signature silhouettes, or recognizable franchise art direction. Inspiration is limited to transferable principles such as readability, charm, contrast, color grouping, animation appeal, and cinematic presentation.

## Mandatory art QA
Every character must pass:
- correct limb count
- coherent joints and weight distribution
- no duplicate appendages
- no accidental human torso on animal Eco-Kin
- readable silhouette at gameplay distance
- unique head / torso / limb / tail / wing language when relevant
- material hierarchy that reads without excessive bloom
- element readability without relying only on color
- VFX readability in combat and low-vision/high-contrast modes
- animation feasibility
- rig feasibility
- collision / traversal feasibility where applicable
- originality / outside-IP resemblance review
- consistency between concept, model, animations, and gameplay function

## Reference artwork status
Historical generated group images and cards are REFERENCE assets until the actual binaries are present in the project asset repository and pass anatomy, originality, naming, canon, and production QA.

## Player-experience test
A final character asset should answer:
1. Why will a player remember this character from one silhouette or behavior?
2. What makes this character feel alive rather than decorative?
3. How does this character respond to or change Rebearth?
