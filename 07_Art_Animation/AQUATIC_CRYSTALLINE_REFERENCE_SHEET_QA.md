## Visual Read
The group shares a recognizable family language:
- turquoise/cyan crystalline growths,
- coral-pink/gold accents,
- pale biological fur/skin/shell surfaces,
- luminous blue eyes/cores,
- marine/reef/shoreline cues,
- magical crystalline-water presentation.

This is useful as a regional material language, but each surviving species must still have a unique body plan and gameplay reason to exist.

## Collision Risks
The sheet currently shows strong shared palette/material motifs. Before canon lock, check whether designs are sufficiently separated by:
- silhouette,
- locomotion,
- body plan,
- head/ear/horn structure,
- tail structure,
- aquatic vs shoreline vs terrestrial ecology,
- combat/restoration role,
- scale,
- animation language.

## Shellara Resolution Task
Determine whether the two Shellara-like concepts are:
1. one species with seasonal/Regional Form,
2. juvenile/adult Growth Rite stages,
3. bonded/restored variants,
4. separate species requiring distinct names,
5. duplicate concepts that should be merged.

Do not keep both as separate species solely because two images exist.

## Anatomy QA
For every design promoted from this sheet:
- verify coherent limb count,
- verify joints and weight-bearing anatomy,
- verify paws/hooves/flippers/tails match locomotion,
- avoid decorative crystal growths that obscure animation joints,
- preserve readable face/eye direction,
- ensure water adaptation has biological or Resonance logic,
- remove generation artifacts and accidental merged appendages.

## Current Element Mapping Rule

Historical notes calling for Nintendo-esque aesthetics or UE5.2 are inspiration history only. Do not copy another franchise's recognizable design language. 8K/ray-traced presentation is a marketing/key-art target, not a guaranteed gameplay runtime requirement.
