© 2026 Into Deep Studios and Donta L. Owens. All rights reserved.

## Canon Identity
**Pupular**
Legacy source name: `Chaos Woof`

## Purpose
Translate the historical Chaos Woof artwork and edits into the official Eco-Kin art pipeline without making a second species or preserving incompatible naming/typing by accident.

## Visual Goals
- Instantly readable as an Echohearts Eco-Kin.
- Strong silhouette at thumbnail distance.
- Personality visible before VFX.
- Urban/creative energy may remain as a design influence if it survives canon review.
- Any Ancient Tech should look integrated, purposeful, and removable from the biological silhouette.
- Use the final approved twelve-element visual language once typing is locked.

## Required Character Sheet
1. Front view
2. Side view
3. Back view
4. Three-quarter hero view
5. Scale comparison with player
6. Calm expression/state
7. Curious/social state
8. Fear state
9. Defensive state
10. Rest/recovery state
11. Movement silhouette study
12. One world-interaction pose
13. One combat pose
14. One Sanctuary pose

## Animation Minimum
- idle A / idle B
- look/listen reaction
- walk / trot / run as anatomy requires
- stop/turn
- startled/fear response
- flee
- warning/defensive tell
- primary attack or ability tell
- hit reaction
- recovery / relaxed state
- social interaction
- Sanctuary interaction
- player-recognition reaction if story-approved

## QA Gates
- no accidental extra limbs or anatomy artifacts
- no outside-franchise logos or copied costume motifs
- name in public-facing concept art must read `Pupular`
- VFX do not hide silhouette or facial read
- element/readability must work without relying only on color
- accessibility pass for flashing/glitch effects if retained
- proportions remain consistent across sheet, 3D model, and animation
- behavior communicates agency, not pet-object presentation
