## Canon Identity
**LEONIX**

## Purpose
Translate the historical Leonix artwork and generated presentation card into the official Echohearts art pipeline while preserving the original silhouette and preventing invented card details from becoming canon by accident.

## Visual Continuity Baseline
Preserve unless formal art review changes it:
- celestial/prismatic lion-warrior identity
- white, blue-crystal, and gold construction
- enormous luminous wings
- radiant crown structure
- energy-bearing tail
- high-order guardian presence
- leonine physical power plus protective celestial energy

## Source Priority
1. Original Leonix artwork: primary visual reference.
2. Generated named character card: presentation/reference only.
3. Later model sheets must reconcile both against current canon and anatomy standards.

## VFX Rules
- STARZ* may inform particles and presentation but is not a core element.
- VFX must not hide the lion silhouette, eyes, paws, wing roots, or attack tells.
- Flash intensity, bloom, rapid strobing, and high-contrast particle bursts require accessibility alternatives.
- Radiant/light effects must also read through shape, timing, iconography, and motion, not color alone.

## Anatomy QA
- preserve a coherent lion-based skeletal read unless canon classification explicitly changes morphology
- no accidental extra limbs
- wings must have clear attachment and load-bearing logic
- tail must remain structurally consistent across views
- crown/horns must not change count or placement between frames
- paws/claws must be consistent across model sheet and animation
- armor/crystal plates must not intersect joints during full motion

## Generated-Card Errors To Exclude
Do not canonize:
- accidental 11.2 m / malformed size text
- infinite or nonsensical weight values
- unapproved Cosmic element
- unapproved Spirit element
- unapproved Humanoid-Kin classification
- any generated-stat or measurement label that was not explicitly approved

## QA Gates
- name is always `Leonix` in current public-facing art
- silhouette is recognizable at thumbnail distance
- lion identity remains readable without VFX
- wing silhouette remains distinct from existing guardians
- current twelve-element visual language is used once typing is locked
- armor does not overpower biological identity
- no outside-franchise logos, copied costumes, signatures, or recognizable protected design motifs
- animation tells are readable in combat
- VFX accessibility options defined
- proportions remain consistent from concept to model to animation
