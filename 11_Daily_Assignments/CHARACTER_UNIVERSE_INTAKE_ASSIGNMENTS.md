## Purpose
Ensure every Echohearts character, shown or not shown in the source chat, is reviewed through the existing project workflow without creating parallel registries or art pipelines.

## Assignment Track
### `EH-CHAR-001` Master Character Intake
Create a one-row-per-character intake list across Eco-Kin, humans, NPCs, villains, Guardians, Titans, Celestial Echoes, Humanoid-Kin, and approved variants. Record source, current name, aliases, class, canon status, art availability, and next action.

### `EH-CHAR-002` Identity Collision Audit
Find duplicate names, near-duplicates, reused body plans, repeated faction roles, redundant Legendary functions, and accidental outside-IP resemblance.

### `EH-CHAR-003` Visual Identity Sheet
For each active character, define front/side/back, scale, silhouette, materials, anatomy, expressions/behavior states, hero pose, and gameplay-distance readability.

### `EH-CHAR-004` Animation & Behavior Pass
Define locomotion, idle, social, fear, warning, defensive, injured, recovery, interaction, combat tells, and world-reaction states appropriate to the character.

### `EH-CHAR-005` Gameplay Purpose
Each character must have a distinct reason to exist in story, ecology, combat, Sanctuary, restoration, traversal, investigation, faction play, or world consequence.

### `EH-CHAR-006` Aquatic / Crystalline Review
Review Bloomtide, Seafoam Dews, Shellara, Coveclaw, Brightgleam, Mirlijaw, Abyssance, and the malformed Driftide/Seedlina source entry. Resolve the two Shellara concepts before assigning permanent IDs.

### `EH-CHAR-007` Art Recovery
Recover historical reference art only for active review targets. Do not mass-import every old generated image before canon review.

### `EH-CHAR-008` UE5.8 Feasibility
For approved characters, define model complexity, rig requirements, material/VFX needs, animation budget, collision, LOD/scalability, gameplay data, and performance risks.

### `EH-CHAR-009` Publication QA
Require anatomy, originality, naming, current-element mapping, stats, lore, agency, accessibility, and implementation status checks before publication.

## Visual target correction
Historical wording referenced a Nintendo-esque aesthetic and UE5.2. Preserve the transferable goals of charm/readability plus cinematic rendering, but current production uses an original Echohearts visual identity and Unreal Engine 5.8. Do not copy another franchise's characters or signature art language.
