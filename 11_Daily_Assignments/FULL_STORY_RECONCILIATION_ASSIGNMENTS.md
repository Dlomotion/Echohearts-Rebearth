## Assignments

### EH-STORY-001 — One-Page Lore Summary
Create a one-page newcomer summary using only current approved canon plus clearly labeled approved-pending concepts.

Acceptance:
- new reader understands Rebearth, Eco-Kin, Blight, A.E.G.I.S., Sanctuary, major conflict and player role,
- no unexplained legacy terminology,
- ends with the Heart Statement promise.

### EH-STORY-002 — Cinematic Opening
Reconcile the crash/arrival/Heartroot opening with Architect-memory material without spoiling the deeper reveal too early.

Acceptance:
- clear player goal within first minutes,
- emotional Eco-Kin beat,
- visible environmental threat,
- one mystery hook,
- no lore dump.

### EH-STORY-003 — Publisher Pitch Story
Write the narrative pitch for publishers/studios around player fantasy, differentiated mechanics, world scale and transmedia potential.

Acceptance:
- original positioning,
- no outside-franchise comparisons used as identity,
- clear why players return,
- clear solo/co-op/PvP boundaries,
- clear first playable slice.

### EH-STORY-004 — Full Act/Chapter Spine
Map the approved main campaign from opening through final Heal/Break/Balance-Transcend choice.

Acceptance:
- every act has region, emotional question, major Eco-Kin relationship, villain pressure, taught system, world delta and transition to next act,
- all major NPCs have a reason to enter and recur,
- no disconnected side arc.

### EH-STORY-005 — NPC Relationship Web
Build one relationship map connecting Professor Jackson, Imani, Mina, Toma, Nyla, Ari, Nico, Nurse Maribel, Elder Lillian, D.A.H.L.I.A., major villains, Guardians and key Eco-Kin.

Acceptance:
- each recurring NPC has desire, fear, conflict, ally/rival links, regional tie and gameplay function,
- NPC memory reacts to player choices.

### EH-STORY-006 — Eco-Kin Story Web
Connect starter, regional, Guardian, Titan and historical-batch Eco-Kin to ecology, quests, Sanctuary behavior and world state.

Acceptance:
- no Eco-Kin exists only as collectible filler,
- every canon Eco-Kin has ecological reason, behavior and at least one world/story hook.

### EH-STORY-007 — Side-Quest Consequence Audit
Review all major side arcs, including Everhour Haven, and attach each to main-story themes and persistent consequences.

Acceptance:
- prerequisite,
- trigger,
- objectives,
- taught system,
- outcome/world delta,
- save flag,
- successor or closure.

### EH-STORY-008 — Legacy Cosmic/Faction Reconciliation
Audit Great Compression, Astryx, STARZ*, Saviors, Lord Dred, Lost Souls, Deminions, Rad, Bugs, Agents, Blood-Water Spirits, Kinfolk, Humanoids, Null Choir/Hourglass Choir and related historical terminology.

For each: CANON, MERGED/RENAMED, APPROVED-PENDING, RETIRED/REFERENCE-ONLY, or REJECTED.

### EH-STORY-009 — Terrain/Landmark Continuity
Map the eight-major-terrain aspiration onto the current Heartreach region structure and define sub-biomes/landmarks needed to approach the larger terrain-variation target without inflating production scope.

### EH-STORY-010 — Anime/Graphic Novel Outline
Adapt the approved game story into a transmedia season/volume outline without changing game canon or creating contradictory endings.

### EH-STORY-011 — Final Manuscript Assembly
Only after the above reconciliation passes, assemble the publication-ready full story.

Acceptance:
- beginning-to-end chronology,
- consistent terminology,
- no accidental outside-IP references,
- side quests connected,
- NPC/Eco-Kin/ecosystem/landmark continuity,
- emotionally legible final choice and aftermath.
