## Purpose
Move the Resonance Kit, Resonance Shards and historical mutation packet through the existing Echohearts workflow without creating a parallel evolution system.

## Assignments
### EH-EK-RKIT-001 — Base Canon Lock
Confirm Resonance Kit permanent identity, classification, scale, body plan, current twelve-element treatment, Frequency Shift behavior and first-story placement.

### EH-EK-RKIT-002 — Shard Family Audit
Review Vineshard, Cinderstone/Ashpulse, Tide-Pearl/Boilreef, Static-Core/Oilstatic and Void-Glass/Voidglitch. Map each to approved biomes, current elements, source landmarks and acquisition rules.

### EH-EK-RKIT-003 — Branch Form Review
Review Sylva-Lynx, Flarevane, Aqua-Drift, Volt-Shifter and Umbra-Soul for unique silhouette, ecology, traversal, battle/support role, restoration use, Sanctuary behavior, art feasibility and animation needs.

### EH-EK-RKIT-004 — Growth Rite / Consent Rules
Define the authoritative proceed/defer/refuse/unstable states, specialist intervention, Harmony/Purity prerequisites, Discordance warnings and no-force rules.

### EH-EK-RKIT-005 — A.E.G.I.S. UI
Design the Shard-Arc screen with form knowledge, biome source, Harmony/Purity, compatibility, willingness and safety warnings. Include accessibility and reduced-motion states.

### EH-EK-RKIT-006 — Persistence & Multiplayer Contract
Define saved state, permanent EcoKinID continuity, branch/form state, co-op authority, idempotency, save-version migration and reconnect behavior. Keep NOT YET VERIFIED until tested in UE5.8.

### EH-EK-RKIT-007 — Legacy Line Triage
Review Iron-Spark/Ferrumwarden/Magnetarion Ward, Botan-Bug/Grove-Beetle/Canopyrex, Stone-Treader/Basalt-Crusher/Magmaweld Golem and Aether-Wisp/Aetherwing Seraphin/Aurelior Prime. Each entry must end as CANON, APPROVED-PENDING, MERGED/RENAMED, RETIRED/REFERENCE-ONLY or REJECTED.

### EH-EK-RKIT-008 — Playtest Question
Test whether players understand that Resonance Shards are place-memory catalysts and not forced-evolution consumables. Record confusion, excitement, favorite branch, perceived agency and whether each form feels like the same companion.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Strengthens at least one Heart Statement pillar.
- Does not create a thirteenth element.
- Does not duplicate Growth Rites/forms.
- No forced mutation, fusion or raw Blight crafting.
- Every branch has a distinct gameplay reason to exist.
- Eco-Kin agency is visible in UI and story.
- Form choice creates a world consequence, not only stat changes.
- UE5 implementation remains NOT YET VERIFIED without project evidence.
