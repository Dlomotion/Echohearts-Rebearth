## Assignment Queue

### EH-SURV-001 — Survival Pressure Contract
Define food, clean water, temperature, weather, shelter, medicine and hazard pressure without turning the campaign into constant meter maintenance.
Acceptance: each pressure creates a meaningful decision and has accessible mitigation.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-002 — Sanctuary Daily Condition Check
Design one readable daily summary covering weather, food/water, crops, resident wellbeing, Eco-Kin wellbeing, defenses, research and active threats.
Acceptance: player can understand Sanctuary priorities in under 30 seconds.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-003 — Eco-Kin Welfare and Refusal
Integrate fatigue, fear, sickness, injury, morale, habitat comfort and voluntary Sanctuary Aptitude participation.
Acceptance: overwork is mechanically worse than proper care; Eco-Kin can refuse unsuitable activity.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-004 — Seasonal Living Soil Farming
Map crops, soil conditions, water, Purity, weather and seasonal availability to the existing Living Soil system.
Acceptance: farming supports food, medicine, crafting and restoration without permanent-stat exploits.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-005 — Visible Restoration Delta
Choose one region and author a before/after restoration state affecting visuals, traversal, wildlife, NPCs, resources and quests.
Acceptance: the player can identify the restored state without opening a menu.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-006 — Ecology Simulation Rules
Define bounded predator/prey, vegetation, water quality, disease and migration relationships for one biome.
Acceptance: ecological problems have at least one non-kill solution and cannot spiral infinitely off-screen.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-007 — Research Progression Slice
Implement a paper design for Artifact → Scan → Decode → Prototype → Field Test → Stabilize → Manufacture.
Acceptance: one piece of Ancient Tech has a complete research path tied to a mission and world consequence.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-008 — Portal/Rift Traversal Physics
Design one authored momentum/gravity traversal challenge using the current Riftway architecture.
Acceptance: readable setup, accessibility alternative, no exploit loop, deterministic completion proof.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-009 — Horror / Cozy Pacing Pass
Define threat windows, safe windows, night danger, Gloom events and Sanctuary recovery pacing.
Acceptance: tension rises and falls intentionally; the game is not hostile 24/7.
Status: DESIGN / NOT YET VERIFIED.

### EH-SURV-010 — Hardcore Mode Decision
Review whether optional permadeath/hardcore belongs in Echohearts after save, accessibility and multiplayer systems are proven.
Acceptance: formal KEEP / REDESIGN / RETIRE decision with evidence.
Status: BACKLOG.

## Dependencies
Do not jump the current technical gates. Survival implementation must wait behind authorized UE5.8 build proof, command-buffer proof, ECO-API-001, BCT-001 and the first end-to-end build/craft transaction where relevant.

## Canon QA
Every survival feature must preserve:
- Eco-Kin agency
- Vibrance / Density / Harmony / Purity as public core stats
- twelve canonical elements
- real-time campaign combat
- Kindling rather than capture
- no creature trading or forced labor
- no raw Blight Husk crafting
- persistent world consequences
