# ART REPOST ROUTING + QA

**Scope:** Every Echohearts image reposted from an older chat, whether generated art, animal reference, character reference, UI mockup, environment concept, or screenshots of prior work.

© 2026 Into Deep Studios and Donta L. Owens. All rights reserved.

## Rule

A reposted image is evidence of prior project intent, not automatic permission to replace canon art.

Route each image through:

**Source identification → character/Eco-Kin match → canon comparison → originality/provenance review → anatomy review → silhouette review → material/VFX review → model/rig/animation feasibility → story/world connection → approval status.**

## Destinations

- Approved permanent Eco-Kin identity art → `03_EcoKin_Dex/<EcoKinID or Name>/Art/` when the production asset exists.
- Character/NPC concepts → `07_Art/Characters/` after identity review.
- Eco-Kin concepts awaiting identification → `03_EcoKin_Dex/Intake/` or the existing pending intake registry.
- World/environment/landmark concepts → `07_Art/Environment/` and cross-reference `02_World` / `05_Levels`.
- UI concepts → `06_UI_UX/`.
- Outside/reference compositions, derivative experiments, malformed generations, obsolete names, anatomy errors, duplicated identities → `99_Reference_Retired_Needs_Redesign/`.

## Mandatory Visual QA

- Verify the subject is attached to the correct character/Eco-Kin identity.
- Preserve locked skin tone, proportions, eye color, animal DNA, gear, and biome when those fields are approved.
- Animal Eco-Kin must keep correct quadruped/invertebrate/aquatic/avian body plans and believable locomotion.
- Reject extra limbs, missing limbs, fused digits, accidental human anatomy, floating artifacts, duplicate body parts, unreadable faces, and text errors.
- Ancient Tech should look integrated with or respectfully fitted to anatomy, not pasted on as generic armor.
- Corruption visuals must communicate injury/story state and cannot silently become an evolution or desirable cosmetic.
- A new image-generation variation never overwrites an approved identity by itself.

## Originality Firewall

- Do not copy outside characters, creatures, outfits, logos, weapons, proper nouns, signatures, poses, or franchise-specific visual language.
- Real-animal references can inform anatomy, locomotion, behavior, patterning, and ecology.
- Public game/movie/anime references remain research only. Extract principles, not protected identity.

## Story Connection Requirement

Before an image moves toward publication, record:

- Who/what it depicts.
- Where it exists on Rebearth.
- What chapter, quest, biome, faction, Sanctuary role, or restoration state it connects to.
- Which canonical element(s) and four resonance attributes matter.
- Whether the image is Active Canon Art, Working Concept, Anatomy Reference, World Reference, or Retired/Needs Redesign.

## Status Language

Use **APPROVED ART** only after canon and production approval. Use **WORKING CONCEPT** for plausible but unapproved art. Use **REFERENCE ONLY** for research/reference material. Use **RETIRED / NEEDS REDESIGN** for contradictions, copied-looking work, anatomy failures, or superseded versions.
