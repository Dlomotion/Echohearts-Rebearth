## AI Mistake Patch / Continuity QA

The visible reference cards use historical elemental labels and role words. Art labels must now follow the active nine-Essence canon:

- Water → Torrent
- Earth → Terra
- Air/Wind → Aero
- Life/Nature/Verdant → Flora
- Ice/Frost → Glaze where it is an actual Essence expression
- Harmony → public attribute / behavior tag
- Coastal, Sand, Guardian, Sentinel, Tracker, Explorer, Support, Excavator, Purifier and Herald → ecology/role tags

Do not preserve obsolete element badges simply because they are printed on a historical card.

## Visual-source status

The currently reposted board is sufficient to preserve **concept identity and broad silhouette direction**, but it is not sufficient evidence that ten separate clean, production-resolution source images have been recovered.

Therefore:
- do not claim a complete raw-art archive exists from this board alone;
- do not upscale thumbnails and call them master art;
- do not infer hidden anatomy from cropped/small images;
- recover original source images when available before model-sheet lock;
- keep each species as one identity, not a new species for every card variation.

## Species art QC targets

### Azurithor
Whale-shark anatomy first. Broad aquatic mass, readable fins/gills/tail, no unnecessary humanoid armor. Ocean-restoration function should appear through ecology and gear/markings only where justified.

### Vuyamba
Large sensory ears and desert-adapted body plan. Ear scale must still support plausible locomotion, collision and animation.

### Mubariki
Rainforest guardian with armor that reads as natural/ecological or Ancient-Tech-compatible adaptation rather than generic plate armor.

### Kelparc
Kelp armor remains flexible, waterlogged and anatomy-aware. Avoid rigid humanoid shoulder-pad logic on an animal body.

### Duneskimmer
Burrowing anatomy should explain excavation: forelimbs, claws, head wedge, body profile and sand shedding. 'Harvester' is an ecology role, not industrial machinery ownership.

### Riftspine
Mountain locomotion must remain believable despite quills/defensive structures. Preserve foot placement, center of mass and climbing clearance.

### Nzalaqua
Wetland/floodplain guardian. Body plan should communicate wading/swimming/soft-ground movement without becoming a generic water monster.

### Siltrex
River-delta body plan should visually support sediment movement and bank interaction. Avoid magical unlimited terraforming cues.

### Virelily
Amphibious/floating purifier. Plant structures can support phytoremediation identity, but the underlying animal/amphibious anatomy must remain readable.

### Frostwind Stagel
Stag-like alpine anatomy. Use Glaze/Aero visual language through frost accumulation, coat adaptation, breath, wind behavior and antler treatment without turning it into a generic ice crystal sculpture.

## Style rule

Use the wider Echohearts art direction:
- strong readable silhouettes;
- natural asymmetry and material wear;
- vibrant focal color against grounded environmental palettes;
- species-specific anatomy;
- limited Ancient-Tech treatment where story supports it;
- Blight, Resonant and Biomimetic variants kept visually distinct;
- no universal music/choir/rhythm iconography.

## Promotion requirements

Before a species receives FINAL ART status:
1. clean source art or approved new concept sheet exists;
2. name/EcoKinID matches Dex;
3. current Essence labels are correct;
4. anatomy pass completed;
5. silhouette does not duplicate another roster member;
6. originality/IP review completed;
7. biome/environment sheet exists;
8. material/color callouts exist;
9. variant boundaries are defined;
10. model/rig/animation requirements are recorded.
