## Visual QA checklist

- Correct limb count and joint direction
- No accidental human hands/feet on animal Eco-Kin
- No duplicated heads/tails/wings
- Functional armor/harness placement
- Readable face and eyes at gameplay camera distance
- Clear scale reference
- Distinct silhouette from existing roster
- Consistent Rebearth material language
- No copied logos, costumes, signatures, franchise symbols, or near-duplicate silhouettes
- Presentation text spelled correctly
- Naming matches current canon
- Background biome matches actual habitat
- Remove generator artifacts, duplicate silhouettes and stray scale-reference figures from final production art
- Preserve exact approved identity across future renders: face, species/body plan, signature armor/material zones, palette and defining silhouette

## Production target

High-end cinematic presentation is a rendering target, not a license to destroy gameplay readability. Model sheets must include front/side/back, neutral pose, scale, material breakdown, expression/behavior sheet, locomotion notes, and VFX-safe zones before 3D production.
