# Character Design Consistency + Mythic Art QA — 2026-09-20

**Project:** Echohearts: Rebearth / ECO-KIN’S
**Status:** CANON production-art QA rule + current-batch SOURCE intake

## Locked image-production rule

For established Eco-Kin, new art must follow the approved prior image/design record rather than inventing a different creature under the same name.

Default output rule:

- **one Eco-Kin per image**;
- full body visible unless a deliberate portrait/cinematic crop is requested;
- character name visible and spelled exactly;
- `Eco-Kin` label spelled exactly;
- coherent anatomy;
- approved body plan, colors, markings, materials, gear and elemental/resonance motifs preserved;
- no lineup/grid/card sheet unless the user explicitly requests a roster sheet;
- no accidental redesign for novelty;
- fix only actual errors unless a redesign is explicitly requested.

## Art-error retirement rules

Route a generation to `99_Reference_Retired_Needs_Redesign` when it contains any of the following:
- wrong Eco-Kin name;
- `Echo-Kin`, `Eko-Kin`, `Shadow Kin`, or another mistaken franchise label;
- extra/missing/fused limbs, digits, wings, horns or tails;
- wrong body plan;
- off-model face or proportions;
- wrong evolution stage;
- duplicated accessories;
- invented element family replacing current canon;
- direct imitation of another franchise/celebrity/protected character;
- roster-sheet output when an individual image was requested;
- unreadable or misspelled embedded text.

## Repitch consistency gate

Before an image is used in a publisher/company repitch, it must pass:

1. identity match against permanent name/EcoKinID;
2. approved source-image comparison;
3. anatomy/body-plan QA;
4. palette/material/Ancient-Tech consistency;
5. canonical element/iconography check;
6. originality/IP/cultural-context review;
7. exact embedded-text check;
8. biome/story compatibility;
9. crop/layout readability;
10. final art-manifest status.
