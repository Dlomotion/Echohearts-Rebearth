## Status vocabulary

- **CANON LOCK** — already established source-of-truth rule or identity.
- **APPROVED PENDING** — accepted direction that still needs naming, story, art, data, or implementation proof.
- **DRAFT / REFERENCE** — useful source material, biological reference, composition study, or historical concept that cannot overwrite canon.
- **REJECTED CONFLICT** — contradicts current canon, belongs to another project/brand, breaks identity continuity, or cannot be used directly without redesign/reframing.
