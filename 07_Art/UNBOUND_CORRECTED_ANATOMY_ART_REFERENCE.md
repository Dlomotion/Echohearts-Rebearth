# Unbound — Corrected Anatomy Art Reference

Status: ACTIVE PRODUCTION REFERENCE / FINAL ART STILL NOT YET VERIFIED

## Non-negotiable correction
The most recent corrected-foot direction controls future Unbound work. Unbound must have exactly two legs with readable lower-body anatomy.

Foot/leg QA:
- left and right legs clearly separate
- coherent hip-to-knee-to-ankle flow
- visible heel/plantar structure appropriate to the design
- claws/toes individually readable, not fused or melted
- no duplicated feet or hidden third limb
- weight distribution plausible for standing, walking, turning, landing and combat
- armor follows anatomy instead of disguising errors

## Preserve the established design
Do not replace the character while correcting anatomy. Preserve:
- horned mask/helmet silhouette
- liberation/broken-chain motif
- cyan, violet and gold resonance language
- layered feather/fur/energy mantle
- spectral Spirit Form concept
- raw, mystical, weathered guardian presence

## Production views
Required before model lock:
1. neutral front
2. neutral side
3. neutral rear
4. feet close-up from front/side/three-quarter
5. walk contact pose
6. run/push-off pose
7. landing pose
8. Chain Breaker action silhouette
9. Wind Walk movement silhouette
10. Spirit Form overlay separated from physical mesh

## Originality and visual-quality rule
Aim for an original Echohearts silhouette with premium biological/relic materials. Historical references to another studio's aesthetic are readability research only and are not a style-copy instruction.

## Failure routing
Any generation with extra legs, fused toes, broken ankles, floating chains that read as limbs, malformed hands/feet, duplicated armor plates or inconsistent character identity goes to `99_Reference_Retired_Needs_Redesign`.
