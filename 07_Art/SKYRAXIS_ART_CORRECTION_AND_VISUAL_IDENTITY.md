## Make the Design Better

The final design should be **raw, authentic, dangerous, specific, and unlike a basic fantasy monster**.

Echohearts visual logic:

- anatomy first
- ecology second
- story function third
- resonance/Ancient-Tech detail fourth
- decorative spectacle last

The creature should still look like Skyraxis if every glow effect is turned off.

### Biological / Ecological Design Questions

Before final art approval, answer:

- What does Skyraxis eat?
- Where does it sleep or nest?
- How does it survive extreme wind shear?
- How do its feet grip rock, ruin, bark, or Ancient-Tech surfaces?
- How does it steer in crosswinds?
- What body features change in storms?
- What physical signs show age, injury, rank, season, or migration history?
- Why would smaller aerial Eco-Kin follow or avoid it?

## Shape Language

Recommended direction:

- powerful chest designed for sustained flight or gliding
- narrow, pressure-cut leading edges on wings or aerodynamic structures
- two strong legs with clear ankle/heel/claw structure
- body mass distributed for landing impact
- tail or rear stabilizer with a functional flight role
- asymmetrical wear, scars, torn membrane edges, feather wear, mineral abrasion, or wind-polished surfaces where appropriate

Avoid:

- generic dragon head + bird wings + random crystals
- giant shoulder armor with no anatomical attachment
- floating metal plates with no gameplay/lore function
- overdesigned crown structures that obscure expression
- excessive glow that erases form readability

## Surface / Material Direction

Use materials that imply a creature that has lived in Rebearth’s sky for years:

- weathered keratin / horn
- real feather, membrane, fur, scale, or mixed biological texture based on final DNA decision
- subtle resonance mineralization growing along stress-bearing anatomy
- wind-burnished edges
- rain streaking and storm residue
- scars that glow only when resonance is active

Ancient-Tech integration should be symbiotic or environmental, not costume armor pasted onto the body.

## Color and VFX

Aero should read through motion and atmospheric behavior, not simply blue coloration.

Use:

- pressure ripples
- dust displacement
- cloud shear
- contrail fragments
- localized refraction
- riftwind distortion
- restrained resonance highlights

Accessibility:

- critical combat tells must remain readable without bloom
- no attack should rely only on hue
- warning poses, audio and motion timing must communicate intent
