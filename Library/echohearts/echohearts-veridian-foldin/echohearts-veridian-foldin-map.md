## 2. Connection web — one story, how everyone knows each other

- **The player**, a Frequency Tamer (undercover operative stranded on Rebearth by a portal event — locked), takes a regional survey contract from **Professor Jackson** of the A.E.G.I.S. research program.
- Jackson was formed in the tradition of **the Veridian Order**, an old Frequency Tamer lineage whose six-region trial route once kept Aethelgard's ecosystems in balance.
- **Vorlag** was the Order's champion. He broke from it to dominate EcoKin by force, building a war faction supplied by **Dr. Hugo's disruptor program** — the present-tense engine of Shadow-altered outbreaks across the regions.
- The **five Star EcoKin** are bound to the Order's old trial sites. Each regional mission chain ends at a trial that can only be completed through Kindling; each Star, once in Resonance Alignment, becomes a living witness against Vorlag's methods.
- The ongoing story is therefore one story: the player's restoration survey → the Order's collapse → Vorlag's war → the five Star chains → a convergence finale. **Endings restore imperfectly** (locked — scars remain, no perfect-Purity reset).
