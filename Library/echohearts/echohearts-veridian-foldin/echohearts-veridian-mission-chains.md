# Echohearts: Rebearth — Veridian Fold-In Mission Chains

**Status:** CONCEPT — routes to 01_Story / 02_World; nothing enters 00_Canon_Lock without review.
**Basis:** the reconciliation map (`echohearts-veridian-foldin-map.md`), Tay's 2026-10-05 fold-in ruling. This document structures the pasted material into missions — it does not rewrite story prose, rename anything the map preserved, or resolve the map's flagged items (Stars' exact nature, convergence arc naming, Vorlag's faction tie, Celestine Bang placement, the Nova name collision — all still pending 10_Production / Dex intake).

**Chain order:** the map lists the Stars as Nova, Eclipse, Nebula, Black Hole, Shooting Star. Region assignments below keep every preserved region name verbatim; Eclipse, Nebula, Black Hole, and Shooting Star sit in the regions the source material already paired them with, and Nova takes Driftmist Peaks (the remaining preserved region). Verdantia Fields hosts the approach chain.

**Standing rules applied to every beat:** every EcoKin encounter begins as **UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE** on A.E.G.I.S. scans; progression runs on **Kindling %** (no parallel system); Shadow is a condition to be purified, never a faction to be exterminated; endings restore imperfectly — scars remain.

### Beat 1 — Verdantia Approach (Verdantia Fields)

**Premise.** The player — a Frequency Tamer, an undercover operative stranded on Rebearth by a portal event — takes a regional survey contract from Professor Jackson of the A.E.G.I.S. research program. The survey's first stop is Verdantia Fields, where a Shadow-altered outbreak is spreading through the lowland groves. The Veridian Order enters here as history: ruined waymarks of its old six-region trial route, explained by Jackson, not by exposition dumps.

**Key NPCs and connections.** Professor Jackson (briefing, contract-giver, research-credibility thread); an unnamed Veridian Order remnant keeping the Verdantia waymark (living history, not a quest dispenser); first field signs of the disruptor program — geometric scarring on altered EcoKin that Jackson cannot yet explain.

**Regional trial and mechanics.** Survey mechanics onboarding: A.E.G.I.S. scanning, UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE discovery protocol, D.A.H.L.I.A. guidance. First Shadow-altered encounter tutorializes the thesis choice: purification is slow and costly but heals; driving the altered off is fast but leaves ecological damage. Anima-Link basics surface here — pushing an aligned partner too hard in the blighted grove feeds strain back to the Tamer.

**Kindling / Resonance Alignment beat.** First Kindling % gains with a Verdantia EcoKin through protection and calm rather than combat dominance. No Alignment with a Star here — this beat earns the player the right to walk the old trial route.

**Moral choice and lasting consequence.** A Shadow-altered EcoKin is cornered near a farming settlement: attempt full purification (restoration–Purity; slow, the settlement stays exposed longer, ecological reputation rises) or drive it off and let the wilds absorb it (industry–control; fast, settlement trust rises, a blighted patch takes root in the grove permanently). Either way the settlement remembers who chose what.

**Post-chain state.** Outbreak contained, not cured. One grove remains visibly blighted — a permanent scar on the Verdantia map. Jackson issues the Aethelgard survey naming the five old trial sites, opening the Star chains.

### Beat 2 — Nova Chain (Driftmist Peaks)

**Premise.** Disruptor-triggered storm-fires are racing the high ridgelines of Driftmist Peaks, and something at the old trial site is broadcasting on A.E.G.I.S. as UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE. The Nova Star EcoKin holds the ridgeline trial: fire cannot be fought with force here — only with decisive protection.

**Key NPCs and connections.** Jackson (remote analysis of the ignition pattern — it matches disruptor tech, tightening the Hugo thread); peak shepherds and ridge settlements (citizen trust at stake); Vorlag's presence is felt only as aftermath: stripped disruptor caches in the burn zone.

**Regional trial and mechanics.** A moving-front trial: the player must keep an aligned partner between the fire line and fleeing EcoKin herds, using terrain and timing rather than attacks. Bold, committed interpositions raise Kindling; hesitation or overextension spikes Anima-Link strain both ways. Pyre-Essence behavior is foregrounded through A.E.G.I.S. readings.

**Kindling / Resonance Alignment beat.** Nova's Kindling grows through protective courage — standing in the fire's path for others, never through destruction. Alignment is offered after the ridgeline holds: a Resonance Alignment, freely chosen, never taken.

**Moral choice and lasting consequence.** A disruptor cache is found fueling the fires: destroy it now (fires stop sooner; ecological damage minimized; the hardware evidence is lost, research credibility stalls) or preserve it for Jackson's team (research credibility rises and the Hugo thread advances, but the fires burn longer and the burn scar widens). The peaks keep whichever scar was chosen.

**Post-chain state.** The ridgeline holds but the burns remain — blackened slopes beside already-greening regrowth. Nova travels in Resonance Alignment. Shepherds remember whether the Tamer chose their herds or the evidence.

### Beat 3 — Eclipse Chain (Moonlit Marsh)

**Premise.** Two marsh communities sit on opposite sides of a spreading Shadow-altered outbreak, and the old trial site between them is broadcasting UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE. The Eclipse Star EcoKin's trial is one of judgment under scarcity: purification resources are limited, and both communities are watching.

**Key NPCs and connections.** The two marsh communities (citizen trust splits here); Jackson (warns that partial purification can destabilize — a research-credibility stake); a Veridian Order remnant who kept the marsh waymark and remembers the last time this choice was made badly.

**Regional trial and mechanics.** A dual-pressure trial: blighted channels must be mapped via A.E.G.I.S., and the player's decisions allocate limited purification effort across both communities in real time. Shade-Essence phenomena in the marsh respond to the player's conduct — aggression deepens the blight's hold; calm, measured work loosens it. D.A.H.L.I.A. tracks the shifting balance without prescribing an answer.

**Kindling / Resonance Alignment beat.** Eclipse's Kindling responds to deliberation held under pressure — the player must choose without the luxury of certainty. Alignment follows the trial regardless of which community was favored; the Star witnesses the choice, it does not grade it.

**Moral choice and lasting consequence.** Fully purify one community's waters (restoration–Purity; one community thrives and trusts the player deeply, the other remains blighted and remembers being left) or stabilize both at containment level (industry–control; both survive but neither heals, both communities stay wary). The marsh map permanently reflects the decision.

**Post-chain state.** The marsh is changed, not saved: one bright channel or two managed ones, and a community that will bring this choice up again in the convergence arc.

### Beat 4 — Nebula Chain (Crystalgrove Hollow)

**Premise.** The hollow's crystal formations are dimming where disruptor relays were seeded, and a nesting ground of wild EcoKin sits in the relays' interference field. The old trial site broadcasts UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE from beneath the crystal canopy. The Nebula Star EcoKin's trial is strategic and non-lethal: move the danger without becoming one.

**Key NPCs and connections.** Jackson (the relay design matches the Verdantia scarring — the disruptor program is now a confirmed pattern); hollow wardens protecting the nesting ground; no Vorlag presence, only his supply chain.

**Regional trial and mechanics.** A manipulation trial: using A.E.G.I.S. scans, lures, and terrain, the player herds Shadow-altered EcoKin away from the nesting ground and into a containment basin for later purification. No attack-driven solution works — aggression scatters the herd into the nest. Kindling grows through patience, planning, and restraint; rushed play collapses the attempt and must be re-approached.

**Kindling / Resonance Alignment beat.** Nebula's Kindling is the slowest of the five — it is earned across the whole maneuver, not in a single moment. Alignment is offered once the nesting ground is clear: the Star chose the player for how the problem was solved.

**Moral choice and lasting consequence.** An abandoned relay station holds intact disruptor research data: seize and study it (research credibility rises sharply and the Hugo thread advances; the site's interference lingers while study continues, dimming more crystal) or collapse the site immediately (the dimming stops now; the data — and the lead — is buried). The hollow's light level is permanently set by this call.

**Post-chain state.** The nesting ground survives; some crystal formations never regain their light. Wardens record the Tamer as either the one who protected the nest or the one who traded its light for answers.

### Beat 5 — Black Hole Chain (Evershade Wilds)

**Premise.** A Void-touched rift has opened deep in the Evershade Wilds, and Shadow-altered EcoKin are pouring outward in waves. The old trial site at the rift's edge broadcasts UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE. The Black Hole Star EcoKin's trial is restraint: hold the line, absorb, protect — never strike first.

**Key NPCs and connections.** A captured disruptor operative — one of Hugo's people, the first living link in the supply chain; Jackson (wants the operative for interrogation); wilds trackers who have lost kin to the rift.

**Regional trial and mechanics.** A defensive trial built on the Anima-Link's bidirectionality: the player and their aligned partner anchor a perimeter while waves press in. Every overextension feeds strain back into the Tamer; every panicked retreat costs ground. The trial is failed by aggression, not by damage — the Star withdraws its trial if the player turns the defense into an assault. Aura- and Shade-Essence readings spike around the rift throughout.

**Kindling / Resonance Alignment beat.** The Black Hole Star's Kindling is earned in stillness under pressure — the player proves they can hold power without using it. Alignment follows a completed defense: a bond founded on restraint.

**Moral choice and lasting consequence.** The captured operative: hand them to Jackson (research/faction standing rises; Jackson extracts the foundry's location — a concrete advantage in the convergence arc) or release them with a warning (underworld reputation rises; the operative vanishes, and Vorlag's people move a key cache before the finale — a concrete complication in the convergence arc). Both choices are remembered by name.

**Post-chain state.** The rift is sealed but the scar remains — a monitored dead zone the wilds route around. Trackers mark the Tamer as either the shield that held or the one who let a killer walk.

### Beat 6 — Shooting Star Chain (Ashfall Dominion)

**Premise.** A disruptor device buried in the dominion's ash fields is destabilizing the slopes above a mining settlement, and the countdown is measured in hours, not days. The old trial site broadcasts UNKNOWN ECOLOGICAL SIGNATURE from the ashfall zone. The Shooting Star EcoKin's trial is speed in service of others: move, decide, commit.

**Key NPCs and connections.** The mining settlement (evacuation is the immediate stake); Jackson (needs the device intact if possible — it is the program's newest design); a Vorlag retrieval team already moving on the site.

**Regional trial and mechanics.** A time-critical trial: evacuate the settlement's people and its penned EcoKin while the slopes fail, using a released partner's mobility across collapsing ground. The trial rewards decisive routing — every wasted circuit costs lives; every clean run builds Kindling. Terra- and Aero-Essence instability is readable on A.E.G.I.S. as the slopes shift.

**Kindling / Resonance Alignment beat.** The Shooting Star's Kindling is earned at speed — trust built in seconds, through committed action rather than deliberation. Alignment is offered mid-crisis, while the ash is still falling: the Star chooses the Tamer who kept moving for others.

**Moral choice and lasting consequence.** The settlement's people or the device — there is only time to secure one fully: complete the evacuation (citizen trust and EcoKin trust rise; the retrieval team escapes with the device, and Jackson's analysis stalls) or secure the device for Jackson (research credibility rises and the program's newest design is exposed; the evacuation is partial, and the settlement buries its dead remembering the choice). The dominion's resettlement map carries the outcome.

**Post-chain state.** The slopes stabilize; part of the dominion stays buried. Survivors rebuild on ground that remembers what was prioritized.

### Beat 7 — Convergence Arc (Aethelgard-wide)

**Premise.** Vorlag's campaign peaks: the disruptor program moves from seeding outbreaks to a coordinated ignition across Aethelgard, and the foundry at the heart of the supply chain goes active. All five Star EcoKin, now in Resonance Alignment, stand as living witnesses against Vorlag's methods — the trial route completed becomes the war's answer.

**Key NPCs and connections.** Vorlag (rogue Frequency Tamer; direct confrontation for the first time); Professor Jackson (coordinates the Aethelgard-wide response); Dr. Hugo's program as the material target (the foundry); every community, warden, tracker, and remnant met across the six prior beats returns with their memory of the player's choices intact.

**Mechanics and how prior choices feed the finale.** The finale's reachable paths are set by the chains, not by a menu:
- Black Hole chain: Jackson's interrogation intel (if the operative was handed over) opens a non-lethal infiltration route; if the operative was released, the moved cache forces a harder assault approach.
- Nebula chain: studied relay data (if preserved) enables purification of the foundry's core rather than its destruction; if the site was collapsed, the core must be destroyed, with permanent ecological cost.
- Shooting Star chain: the secured device (if recovered) can shut down field disruptors during the assault; if lost, the assault faces active disruptor defenses.
- Eclipse and Nova chains: the communities' trust determines who answers the call when the finale needs evacuation corridors and civilian cover — and who refuses.
- The five Star EcoKin fight as aligned partners under standard Binding/Release and Anima-Link rules; no new combat system is introduced.

**Kindling / Resonance Alignment beat.** The arc's emotional spine is the five Alignments holding under the worst pressure of the story — Kindling tested, not granted. Whatever the player built across the chains is what they bring into the foundry.

**Moral choice and lasting consequence.** Vorlag's fate branches on accumulated conduct: captured (faces the communities he harmed — citizen trust and faction standing shift), spared and exiled (underworld reputation shifts; he remains a living loose end), or driven off at cost (a community pays the price of the final confrontation). The foundry's end — purified or destroyed — permanently sets Aethelgard's ecological baseline.

**Post-chain state.** Aethelgard is changed, not saved: disruptor scars persist in every region, some blighted zones remain under watch, the Veridian Order's trial route is walked again by new Tamers. The Stars remain in Resonance Alignment — they chose to stay. No perfect-Purity reset; the world remembers everything, including the player.
