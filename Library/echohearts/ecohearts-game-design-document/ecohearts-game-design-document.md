# Ecohearts — Game Design Document

**Saviors of the Universe**

An EcoKin action RPG where players summon EcoKin via the A.E.G.I.S. to defend the universe against Lord Dred and the forces of darkness.

## 1. High concept

- **Genre**: EcoKin action RPG with base building, breeding/fusion, and multiplayer
- **Core fantasy**: You are a Savior of the Universe, chosen by Nature and granted power by the Stars [REVIEW: player frame]
- **Core loop**: Explore -> Encounter -> Battle -> Binding -> Raise / Growth Rite / Concord Fusion -> Build base -> Push deeper into dark territory
- **Inspirations in the concept**: Real-time elemental combat, homeland building, guild warfare, and deep creature collection

### Design pillars

- **Summon and fight together**: You never watch from the sidelines — Frequency Tamer and EcoKin share the battlefield
- **Every creature has a life**: EcoKin work, rest, eat, breed, mature, and are reborn — not just battle units
- **Light against the dark**: Nature's Saviors push back Lord Dred's fear-spreading darkness, planet by planet
- **Your world, your base**: Homeland building and EcoKin labor make progress visible and personal
- **Deep for pros, open for all**: Branching Growth Rites, runes, and four-attribute depth reward mastery

### Pitch

In deep darkness lies evil and corruption. Lord Dred spread his evil by forcing worlds to fear him and destroyed planets, until his forces reached Earth — vowing to control and enslave humans in a dark void. In response, Nature created the Saviors of the Universe to destroy all evil across the universe.

Saviors are humans who summon warriors, mages, knights, and fiends through the A.E.G.I.S. Their Stars grant them powers, and the traits and powers they gain are chosen by the player as they progress.

## 6. EcoKin — the creatures

### Collection

- **Over 1,000 EcoKin** across world elements (Water, Fire, Wind, Light, Dark, and more) [REVIEW: count vs 125-ID Dex authority; elements vs nine Essences — 10_Production]
- **Eco-Dex (Codex)**: Version-exclusive EcoKin drive trading between players to complete the codex
- **18 elemental types** [REVIEW: vs nine Essences]: Combat advantage is a high-stakes elemental matchup system

### Party structure

- **Active release**: 3 EcoKin from your A.E.G.I.S. in battle
- **Party carry**: 8 EcoKin at a time
- **Storage**: The rest live in a digital PC system and the A.E.G.I.S.
- **Roles**: Attacker, Supporter, Healer, Defender — each with a different fighting style
- **Squad format (large-scale)**: Squads of five — typically two tanks front, three attackers back, using elemental counters (Water, Fire, Earth, Electric)

### Binding

- **A.E.G.I.S.**: Binds EcoKin with varying success rates
- **Binding traps**: Built from different materials
- **Unsuccessful Bindings**: Yield Echotraces for research — EcoKin are never consumed
- **Shrine bosses**: Can enter Binding

### Maturation and Concord Fusion

- **Growth Rites**: Structured maturation into stronger forms — no level resets
- **Catalyst rites**: Catalyst items gate Growth Rites alongside Kindling and ecology accomplishments
- **Branching paths**: Many EcoKin have branching maturation paths
- **Breeding**: Breed two EcoKin — offspring inherit powerful Passive Skills or emerge as new hybrid lineages
- **Concord Fusion (registered pairs only)**: Combine two EcoKin into a single, much more powerful entity — only certain registered pairs; not legendaries, gods, angels, or demon lords
- **Death cycle** [REVIEW: conflicts with Growth Rites and the no-sacrifice rule]: A fallen EcoKin leaves behind an Eco-Egg to restart the cycle with better stats
- **Degeneration** [REVIEW: level-reset conflicts with Growth Rites]: Revert to a lower level to increase Max Level and stats

### Deep systems

- **Runes**: Deep rune system customizing Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity
- **Echoprint identity and four-attribute tuning**: Competitive-grade individuality for professional play
- **Work Suitability**: Mining, watering, handiwork — assign EcoKin to automate farming, resource gathering, and factory lines at base
- **Sanity and energy**: Manage beds and food at the homestead

## 7. Core gameplay — the Frequency Tamer fantasy

You directly control a Frequency Tamer who fights alongside a team of three EcoKin. This is not turn-based menu combat.

- **Real-time action combat**: Manual dodging, dodge rolls, combos, and timed skill usage
- **Weapons**: Swords, nunchuks, bows, spears — up to assault rifles and rocket launchers
- **18-element system** [REVIEW: vs nine Essences]: High-stakes elemental rock-paper-scissors (Water, Fire, Wind, Light, Dark, Earth, Electric, Grass, Poison, Ice, Metal, and more)
- **Summon objects**: Find Crystals, Runes, Sigils, and Banners in the world to gain Saviors to summon and fight
- **Crafting tables**: Create unique Saviors
- **Star powers**: Your Star grants powers; you pick character traits and powers as you progress down the line
- **Level up to Divine**: Top-end progression rank

> **Design note:** The combat pitch mixes real-time action (dodge rolls, combos) with automated squad battles and a card system in the arena. Decide which mode is the "core" combat and which are sub-modes, so the game has one clear identity.

### Battle formats

- **Open-world action**: One-on-one live battle factions, turn-based to live-battle spectrum across the world
- **Arena tactics**: A Yu-Gi-Oh-type card game layer — players use a Card system during active fights to trigger tactical spells and skills
- **Squad battles**: Automated but strategic — five EcoKin, two tanks in front, three attackers back
- **Visual Novel / Tactical RPG hybrid beats**: Choices determine which characters live or die, and how EcoKin mature and what their stats are

## 8. Binding and progression

- **A.E.G.I.S. Binding**: Varying success rates per species
- **Binding traps**: Built from different materials found in the world
- **Echotrace consolation**: Even unsuccessful Bindings provide Echotraces for research — never consumed; the grind always pays something
- **Catalyst items**: Item-gated Growth Rites alongside structured maturation
- **Branching Growth Rites**: Many species have multiple paths
- **Degeneration** [REVIEW: level-reset conflicts with Growth Rites]: Revert to a lower level to increase Max Level and stats — deep menu planning
- **Competitive depth**: The four-attribute matrix (Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity) and Echoprint identity for professional-grade team building
- **Runes**: Customize Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity, and more; farming high-tier runes (e.g., in the Treasure Keep) is the core endgame grind
- **Eco-Egg rebirth** [REVIEW: conflicts with Growth Rites / no-sacrifice rule]: A fallen EcoKin leaves an egg to restart the cycle with better stats
- **Version exclusives**: Force trading between players to complete the Eco-Dex (Codex)
- **Cleaning the ecosystem**: A stated progression theme — your work restores the planet, viewable via the A.E.G.I.S.

## 9. EcoKin life systems

### Work and base labor

- **Work Suitability traits**: Mining, watering, handiwork, and more (Kindling, Lumbering in reference architectures)
- **Automation**: Assign EcoKin to farm, gather resources, and run factory assembly lines at base
- **Management via the A.E.G.I.S.**: Send EcoKin out to gather materials across the land and manage them remotely

### Wellbeing

- **Sanity and energy**: Provide enough beds and food
- **Homestead facilities**: Production buildings, hospitals, and a hatchery

### Breeding and Concord Fusion

- **Nursery breeding**: Two EcoKin produce offspring inheriting powerful Passive Skills or entirely new hybrid lineages
- **Concord Fusion (registered pairs only)**: Combine two EcoKin into one much more powerful entity — only certain registered pairs
- **Hard rule**: Never legendaries, gods, angels, or demon lords

> **Design note:** Breeding (offspring) and fusion (combined entity) do similar jobs. Consider making breeding about inheritance and hybrids, and fusion about one-time power spikes — or merge them into one system.

### Evolution strains

**Class 1 — Hyper-Adaptive (8 forms)**: Unstable genetic strands that morph in real time under weather anomalies or DNA fusion.

_Exemplar — Helmut / Rookey lineage:_

- **Base (Mundane)**: Balanced neutral attributes; dense shell for burrowing and resource collection
- **Mountain Woods (Golden)** — Earth / Primal: Stealth extraction at the Russet Highlands Rock Altar; golden-brown bark plating with iron ore
- **Thunderstorm** — Electric / Air: High-voltage lightning sectors; jagged conductive spikes
- **Snowfield / Permafrost** — Ice / Solid: Snowy weather clusters; insulated crystalline layer, crowd-control blunt modifiers
- **Prismana (Prismana Pawney variant)** — Dark / Prismatic: Regional completion at the Nurture Branch; glass-like weapon ridges, dark combat aura
- **Volcanic**: Heat-resistant volcanic glass scaling
- **Deep Trench**: High-pressure hydrostatic armor, deep-sea bioluminescence
- **Abyssal / Nightmare**: Corrupted shadow matter, seasonal launch challenges

**Class 2 — Tri-Tier (6 forms)**: Lineages that diverge permanently early based on nutrition or habitat.

_Exemplar — Wisptis / Ignitis lineage:_

- **Path A — Terrestrial**: Wisptis (Lumen, cloaking energy entity) -> Ignitis (Gamma, mid-tier mobility) -> Apex Ignitis (Nova [REVIEW: name collision with Nova Star], heavy physical DPS)
- **Path B — Forest / Blight**: Forest Wisptis -> Forest Ignitis (Grass / Dark; spawns in the Mistwoods at (-1023, 1371) after a baseline Ignitis lands finishing blows on six wild Forest Wisptises) -> Blighted Apex Ignitis (support / poison, spore contamination fields)

### Weather synchronization

When an extreme weather layer activates over a map block, the spawner suppresses standard arrays and forces hyper-adaptive variants to materialize. At the base tech tables, players can splice genetic remnants (Night Stones, Vein Essence) to force Growth Rite pathways without standard maturation.

## 10. Homeland base and Sky Island

### Homeland (player base on land)

- **Rebuild and decorate**: Farm crops, build decorations, manage the base alongside aligned EcoKin
- **Social hub**: Access multiplayer mode at base; portals to other zones; fast travel [REVIEW: fast-travel rule] to the Arena
- **Crafting**: Crafting tables create unique Saviors; place Banners, work with Crystals, Runes, and Sigils
- **Facilities**: Production buildings, hospitals, hatchery
- **NPC invitations**: Invite NPCs to the home base — but not to Sky Island

### Sky Island

- **Intel hub**: Gather information across the world and bring it back to the Dr.; review research results together
- **Planet health**: Check how the planet is doing via the A.E.G.I.S.

> **Design note:** Sky Island excludes invited NPCs while Homeland welcomes them — a nice lore beat (research sanctum vs. home). Make sure players feel the reason in-story, or it reads as an arbitrary restriction.

## 12. Multiplayer and competitive

- **Co-op**: 4-player co-op exploration; dedicated servers up to 32 players for building and EcoKin trading
- **Arena PvP**: Live battle factions; ranked by "Kills" and total "Power"

- **Egg Heist (PvEvP)**: Teams steal rare eggs while fending off players and wild EcoKin

## 14. Codex, map, and save architecture

### Master Codex / Eco-Dex

- **Species records**: Species ID, unlocked-form bitmask (bit 0 = Base, bit 1 = Golden, bit 2 = Thunderstorm), total encounters, total Bindings
- **Reference scale**: Paldeck (~300 entries, work-suitability matrix), Terraria Bestiary (~550, kills-to-reveal), Summoners War Monster Book (1,200+, awakening split), Digimon Field Guide (~350, branching lineage, memory cost)
- **Target**: 1,000+ EcoKin with regional variants [REVIEW: scope vs 125-ID Permanent Dex authority]

### World Atlas

- **Zone database**: Biomes and sanctums with transit nodes [REVIEW: fast travel], POIs (merchants, altars, quest triggers), and hardcoded grid boundaries that transition UI, camera, and audio between biomes
- **Dynamic spawners**: Weather and time matrix overlays the weighted spawn pools

### Cross-platform save sync (UE 5.8, C++)

- **Unified serialization payloads**: Save header (account ID, slot, timestamp, last platform — PC / Console / Mobile, game version) plus the codex ledger, player location / rotation, and level, serialized to compact binary
- **Conflict resolution**: Deterministic timestamp hierarchy — newer save wins — merged with a codex-ledger merge: form bitmasks OR together, encounter / Binding counts take the max, so offline unlocks are never lost
- **Save-point pipeline**: Local trigger -> compressed local binary write -> async API POST to backend -> server handshake (version + timestamp check, HTTP 200) sets the "Cloud Save Synchronized" marker
- **Sync**: Mobile and console share one profile; sync mobile to console; cross-platform save sync is a core pillar

> **Design note:** The save blueprint resolves conflicts purely by timestamp. If a player makes big progress offline on mobile while a stale cloud save is newer, the merge protects the codex but not world state. Consider a per-system merge policy before locking this in.
