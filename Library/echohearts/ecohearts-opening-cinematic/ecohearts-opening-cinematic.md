# ECHOHEARTS: REBEARTH — Opening Cinematic Narration
**Finalized terminology pass — 2026-10-05**
**Status:** CONCEPT (script) — routes to 01_Story / 07_Art; nothing enters 00_Canon_Lock without review.

---

**[BLACK. A heartbeat. Slow. Then a second heartbeat answering it.]**

**AURALIS** *(ancient, neither young nor old, neither one voice nor many)*:
"Before you were born, the world broke. Not in war alone — in forgetting."

**[Images: forests, oceans, the cities of old Earth. Then the Fracture — landmasses splitting, islands rising into the cloud seas, rivers falling forever through the sky.]**

"They called life a resource. They measured it. Divided it. Owned it. And when it would not obey… they tried to rebuild it in their own image."

**[Laboratories. Geometric scars. Bioluminescent veins. Synth-Origin forms waking in glass.]**

"But life does not ask permission to be alive."

**[Montage — the five Orders of Sentience: Zoa herds thundering across the plains; Flora forests breathing light back into dead soil; Myko networks glowing beneath the caverns; Proto tides reshaping themselves; Monera colonies blooming where nothing should survive.]**

"From the wild, they woke on their own — **Zoa, Flora, Myko, Proto, Monera**. Five orders of sentience. From the laboratories, others woke too — marked **Synth-Origin**, scarred and brilliant, asking a question no scientist could answer: *does being made instead of born make you less alive?*"

**[A creature writhes as its Echoheart fractures — light draining, form twisting.]**

"And some… fractured. Not evil. *Wounded.* Shadow is not a kind of creature. It is what happens when an Echoheart breaks — and the world carries its scars."

**[AURALIS, the Tree of Life — vast beyond measure, roots running through forest, ruin, ocean, and sky.]**

"I am Auralis. I remember when the first root found the first stone. Four rhythms hold the world together — **Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity** — and I remember every promise the world made. And every one it broke."

**[A figure falls through a portal of green-gold light — the player.]**

"You did not come here to conquer. You came because something in you heard the Resonance… and answered."

**[A wrist bracer ignites — A.E.G.I.S.]**

"This is **A.E.G.I.S.** It will not cage them. You do not *catch* an EcoKin. You earn its trust. **Observe. Protect. Calm. Kindle.** What is bound in **Resonance Alignment** stays only because it *chooses* to stay. Those who learn this are called **Frequency Tamers**."

**[Montage — restoration: rivers clearing, migration routes reopening, a desert remaining a desert but alive; predators hunting; storms passing; wild EcoKin remaining wild.]**

"You will not make Rebearth perfect. Perfection is another kind of death. You will make it *livable* — for humans, for EcoKin, for the wild things, for the machines that dream, for everything the old world threw away."

**[A distorted symbol flickers. A whisper, almost kind:]**
*"Someone must control it all."*

**AURALIS:**
"Something older than your fear is waiting for you to believe that. Do not."

**[Final image: the Tree against sunrise over the fractured islands. A small fairy light — Finn — spirals up into the sky.]**

"They are not your property. They are not your tools. They are your kin — **family through the living world**."

"Rebearth is not asking for a savior."

"It is asking for someone willing to *practice* love."

### Terminology notes for production
- **EcoKin** (never "Eco-Kins"); the five Orders named on first appearance.
- **Synth-Origin** used for lab-created; the "made vs born" question stated, not answered.
- **Shadow** described as fracture/condition, never as a faction or species.
- **Binding / Resonance Alignment** replace capture/ownership language; **Release** replaces deployment.
- **Kindling** named as the bond; **A.E.G.I.S.** named as the device.
- Four attributes (**Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity**) spoken once, plainly.
- **Frequency Tamer** named as the earned title.
- Ending promise is **livable, not perfect** — thematically locked.
- "Celestine Bang" intentionally **not** used (pending review); no event name spoken.
- "Keeper" intentionally **not** used (pending reconciliation with Frequency Tamer / Core-Binder).
