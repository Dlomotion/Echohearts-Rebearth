## 1. HIGH CONCEPT & PILLARS

**One line:** An open-world monster-taming action RPG where you don't collect creatures — you earn their trust, fight beside them, and heal a living planet together. **(CONCEPT)**

**Project thesis:** *Echohearts is not simply about collecting creatures. It is a sweeping struggle to restore Rebearth, form profound Kindlings with sentient Eco-Kin, uncover how humanity tampered with the fragments that first transformed life, and prevent Shadow Beasts and competing secret societies from turning the living forces of nature into instruments of conquest. The player's strength comes not merely from possessing powerful Eco-Kin, but from understanding, healing, training, protecting, and resonating with them.* **(CONCEPT, reconciled)**

**The eight USPs** — what makes Echohearts unique: **(CONCEPT)**
1. **The Bond Works Both Ways** — the Anima-Link is bidirectional: power is shared, and so is strain. Hurting your bonded Eco-Kin hurts you.
2. **You Do Not Capture Trust With RNG** — there are no random capture rolls. An Eco-Kin *chooses* to stay through Kindling, or it doesn't.
3. **Restoration Is Character Progression** — restoring a wetland changes which Eco-Kin return, which routes open, which Growth Rites become available. "Restoration is progression."
4. **Canonical World Consequences** — one-time Event Sovereigns happen once per save with permanent consequences.
5. **The Player Fights Too** — you are a Core-Binder in the field, not a bystander giving orders from safety.
6. **Every Eco-Kin Has Ecological Purpose** — each creature has a real role in the food web, not just a battle stat line.
7. **A Sanctuary Instead of a Factory** — Homeland is built on earned trust, not optimized production of creatures.
8. **One Franchise Can Expand Without Diluting the Core** — Summoning Wars, Saviors of the Universe / STARZ, and Ancient Tech Wars continue a predetermined world chronology.

**The five ideas to protect at all costs:** **(CONCEPT)**
1. An Eco-Kin can choose not to join you — winning an encounter ≠ ownership.
2. Your relationship changes gameplay — Kindling, Anima-Link stability, behavior, and growth make the same species feel different depending on how you treated it.
3. The planet remembers — saving or neglecting a biome changes routes, populations, resources, and encounters.
4. Growth tells a story — a Mutation should make the player ask "why did mine become this?", answered by where it lived, what it survived, who it bonded with, what weather it experienced.
5. Letting go can be progression — releasing an Eco-Kin into a restored habitat; months later it remembers you, its offspring inhabit the area, the ecosystem changed.

**Genre:** monster-taming action RPG / ecological restoration / sanctuary building / real-time combat. Third-person open world. **(CONCEPT)**

## 2. STORY

### 2.1 The world before the player — CANON premise elements
- **Planet: Rebearth** — a sentient planet with a living-memory system. **(CANON)**
- The **Grand Fracture** was caused by accumulated ecological strain, interference, and unstable experiments — not by one civilization. **(CONCEPT)**
- Eco-Kin, Sanctuaries, Sanctums, and portal technology **predate humanity**: resonance communication, biological architecture, restoration systems, Anima-Link precursors, atmospheric stabilization, and Eco-Kin partnership traditions all existed before the First Crossing Era, when humans arrived through an unstable portal. The portal network is called the **Echo-Warp Gates**. **(CONCEPT)**
- The **Blight** spreads through broken ecological networks; the **Hollowing** is its spreading end-state. The **Virus** is the fear→control cycle plus a false-prophecy alteration of the truth ("Many hearts become one resonance" corrupted into "One sovereign shall command the four"). **(CONCEPT)**
- **War on Humanity** is the locked historical conflict (Era 3). "The War on Life" survives only as an in-fiction survivor quote: *"the forests remembered it as the War on Life."* **(CANON: War on Humanity locked; the quote framing is CONCEPT)**

### 2.2 The Sovereign Resonance Arc — CONCEPT (reconciled)
- The **Heartroot Resonance Array** is an ecological immune response — not a weapon.
- The **four Resonance Keys** are the four attributes: Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity.
- **Sovereign Resonance** is synchronization, not a fifth element.
- The **Wardens of Seconds** guard temporal pressure points.
- **D.A.H.L.I.A.** is the intelligence inside A.E.G.I.S.; the mystery of who built her deepest code is an open story thread.
- **Nine Essences** (Flora, Torrent, Pyre, Terra, Aero, Glaze, Voltic, Aura, Shade) are the operational element system pending a verified Elemental Matrix.
- Endings **restore imperfectly** — there is no perfect-world reset.

### 2.3 The war continuum — CONCEPT
World War / War on Humanity → The Great War → Spacetime Conflict / Fracture Wars → The Summoning War → The Void War → The Final U.N.I.T.Y. War.
- **U.N.I.T.Y. = "United Network of Individuals, Tribes & Yonderworlds"** — principle: *"We are stronger together without surrendering who we are."* The opposing force weaponizes disconnection.
- The opposing force across eras: secret societies and war factions seeking Eco-Kin for warfare and power; the Hunter's Concord (control/weaponization), the Hollow Choir (Eco-Kin sovereignty), the Skybound Council (political dominance), the Chorus Remnants (restoration).

### 2.4 Nature's arc — CANON + CONCEPT
- **Nature is the Legendary Humanoid-Kin** — Guardian of Life, protector of the Circle of Life, origin in the Heart of Rebearth's Sacred Forests. Deep melanin skin, flowing locs, wings as layered leaf-petals, living-vine armor. Abilities: Life Burst, Growth Shield, Nature's Embrace, **Ancient Bloom** (ultimate — purifies corruption, restores ecosystems). Crafting: Sacred Bark, Luminous Pollen, Sunlight Essence, Ancient Forest Crystals. *"She does not rule Rebearth. She keeps it alive."* **(CANON: identity and never-capturable status. CONCEPT: ability/crafting details.)**
- Nature is **never captured, never an object, collectible, weapon, monarch, or prize**. She has agency; the player must prove she can trust their decisions; her agency matters to the ending. **(CANON)**
- Nature undergoes **conditional Mutations only** — never ordinary evolution. (Her spec's "Seedling → Verdant → Prime Guardian" path conflicts with this and needs reconciliation — flagged, not resolved.)
- Mother Verdalis was proposed as a replacement and is **rejected** — Nature is the Legendary Guardian.

### 2.5 Campaign shape — CONCEPT
- 8-act / 20-chapter spine. Campaign loop: Explore → Observe → Gather → Survive → Bond → Build → Battle → Restore → Research → Unlock → Expand.
- The Circle of Life mission: the Tree of Life as world-organ; Circle Nodes as regional sites; Circle Verdict endings (Restore / Rewrite / Shatter — *"No clean endings. Only ecological truth."*); post-game *"The Circle Never Ends"* — a maintenance loop where ecosystems get harder, not enemies.
- **Event Sovereigns** — major one-time world encounters, once per save/world-state with permanent consequences, replayable only via higher-difficulty Chrono Archive simulations that don't rewrite canon: Morrowmire Colossus, Vesperwing Parallax, Crownspore Regent, Riftback Leviathan, Hushglass Warden, Basalith Warden.
- The hometown devastation/rebuilding arc is a later-game/DLC concept.
- **Open decision:** the manuscript/PR #6 arc's "War on Life" vs locked "War on Humanity" (resolved as in-fiction quote above, pending Tay's final call).

### 3.2 The four cultures — CONCEPT
The Rooted (First Grove), The Ascendant (Sky Haven), The Hollowed (Deep Echo), The Unbroken (Iron Exile) — each with visual identity, signature Eco-Kin bond, and role in the world. ### 3.3 Homeland & Sky Haven — CONCEPT
- **Homeland** is the personalized ecological Sanctuary and progression hub: grow crops, restore habitats, craft equipment, research discoveries, heal companions, construct facilities, manage resources, invite eligible NPCs, assign willing Eco-Kin by natural ability and consent (aquatic Kin help water systems, burrowing Kin help soil), decorate living spaces, build specialized zones, prepare expeditions. ### 3.4 Living ecosystem — CONCEPT
A statistical background model (Plant Biomass, Water Availability, trophic populations) drives trophic-cascade journal events, real-animal drives, and Eco-Kin vs Eco-Kin encounters — most encounters don't end in death. Creatures follow food chains, dominant-species hierarchies, and authentic life cycles; organic territorial fights and predation happen independent of the player. Design bar: *"Rebearth feels alive even when the player does nothing."* Purity bands (0–20 severe corruption … 81–100 resonant) drive visible environmental transformation.

## 5. SYSTEMS

### 5.1 Anima-Link — CANON
The bond between a Frequency Tamer and an Eco-Kin is **bidirectional**: power is shared, and so is strain. Severely injuring or overextending a bonded Eco-Kin causes the Tamer stamina loss, physical stress, weakened coordination; reckless player behavior destabilizes linked Eco-Kin. Transformations carry synchronization costs (high Vibrance/Density forms carry high Anima-Link strain). Core combat question: *"How far are you willing to push someone who trusts you?"*

### 5.2 Kindling — CANON (mechanism), CONCEPT (details)
Kindling is the trust relationship (trust %), replacing the XP-grind feel. Growth Rites are gated by it. The **Kindling Question** — whether close human/Eco-Kin bonds alter wild behavior — is deliberate ethical tension with no single correct answer. Care Consequence, Purification, and Restoration Farming are preserved systems. The Resonance Counter Window merges parry + Eco-Kin command into one timing window (three skill levels). **(CONCEPT)**

### 5.3 A.E.G.I.S. & D.A.H.L.I.A. — CONCEPT
- **A.E.G.I.S.** is the player's device/system: it scans environmental conditions, behavior, and ecological relationships; validates and communicates — it never magically creates biological truth, cannot determine trust/consent, and cannot contradict server/world-state facts. Six-view UI. Field methods: spoor analysis, non-invasive observation, population sampling, acoustic monitoring. **(CONCEPT)**
- **D.A.H.L.I.A.** = "Data-Archive Heuristic for Living Intelligence Alignment" — the Sky Archive intelligence, Dex initializer, Forms Registry validator, and canon librarian. The mystery of who built her deepest code is an open story thread. **(CONCEPT)**
- A.E.G.I.S. command-buffer hardening (VS-AZ-02: command ownership → server request → server validation → authoritative mutation → replicated result → client presentation, with rate limiting + rejection telemetry) is a **SYSTEM CONTRACT — NOT YET VERIFIED**.

### 5.4 Combat — CONCEPT (NOT YET VERIFIED)
- Real-time third-person combat; the player fights personally as a Core-Binder. Field roster of 8, 3 active companions. **(CONCEPT)**
- Authoritative battle core (PR #9 / Issue #8): server-authoritative command validation, deterministic integer damage, disconnect/reconnect snapshots, server-side reward gate. Standalone C++17 harness compiled with warnings-as-errors, tests passed — **NOT YET VERIFIED** at the Unreal level (no packaged 5.8 build, no real RPC/session replication, no two-client consistency). Issue #8 stays open.
- Online server/Arena battles may switch to server-authoritative turn-based combat, then return to the real-time hub. **(CONCEPT)**
- Weapon damage formula (CONCEPT): Final = Base × Weakpoint × (1+Elemental) × Resonance Match (1.5 / 1.0 / 0.5). Weapons support restoration, never creature-killing.
- Six playable combat DISCIPLINES as loadouts, not classes (CONCEPT): Vanguard, Stalker, Channeler, Conductor, Trapper, Frequency Tamer.

### 5.5 Restoration as progression — CONCEPT
Restoration feeds progression directly: a restored wetland changes which Eco-Kin return; a repaired corridor unlocks traversal; a cleaned region changes Growth Rite eligibility. Visible restoration arc: corrupted location → purified river → vegetation → Eco-Kin populations → farmable soil → settlement → economy → research → Echo-Warp Gate reconnects. Sanctuary defense, Restoration Altars, Restoration Blooms, Great Bloom ceremonies. Artifacts each force a choice (restore / study / craft / trade / seal / corrupt / return) affecting the Circle Balance Gauge.

### 5.6 Multiplayer direction — CONCEPT
Single-player + optional 4-player co-op exploration first. Larger dedicated-server worlds, Arenas, server-vs-server, and high-population systems are **deferred** — not required to prove the first game. Team-Up Resource Expeditions with Eco-Kin roles (Digger/Forager/Scout/Guard/Carrier). Anti-exploit rules; ecological consequences (overharvesting damages habitats, lowers yields/Purity).

### 5.7 Player paths — CONCEPT
14 player paths (Tamer → … → Poacher) with multi-dimensional reputation and a Circle Balance Gauge. Needs a systems design pass.
