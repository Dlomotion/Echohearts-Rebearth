# Echohearts: Rebearth — Side Quest Pack: "The Long Game" (2026-10-22)

Status: **CONCEPT — Tay's direction 2026-10-22: "fix it all and add most as new side quests."** Source: the other AI's third (guard-prompt) draft. Every fix applied: its 13 invented region names replaced by **our 13 regions**; its hub ("Sound-Well") replaced by the **Sanctuary and its Chronicler**; its finale ("Core Matrix") **not** converted — our Sovereign Resonance finale (Chronicle 27) already holds that ground, and the Echoheart Core idea remains a separate flagged proposal. Everything else became side content, because side content is what keeps a world alive between Chronicles. Rewards are canon measurements only: Kindling %, region restoration %, Dex entries, corruption cleansed, resident service tiers. Several quests are given by **Love** (traveling Tamer NPC) or the Sanctuary residents, in line with their established roles. All names vetoable.

## The Pack (13 side quests)

### 1. First Chord — *Deepwood edge* — Giver: Love
A brand-new Tamer is failing her first Binding ceremony — not from weakness; she's trying to *match* her partner instead of *listening* to it. Walk the ceremony with her (the ceremony's own phases, played as a student instead of the candidate). **Beat:** Attunement pulses synced to the wild EcoKin's actual rhythm, which changes when it gets nervous. **Reward:** the young Tamer joins the Sanctuary as a recurring face; Kindling +5% with your lead partner (you taught, so you learned).

### 2. The Missing Pages — *Sanctuary → any 3 regions* — Giver: the Chronicler
A society raid scattered a Dex archive drone; its logged pages (real observations of real species) are sitting in canisters across three regions. Recover them by *re-observing* the species each page describes — the page only unlocks when your own log matches it. **Reward:** +15 Dex entries validated by D.A.H.L.I.A.; Chronicler service tier up.

### 3. Canopy Chords — *Deepwood canopy* — Giver: the Scout
A flying EcoKin is tangled in a naturally collapsed vine cage high in the canopy. Free it without cutting a single living vine — unwind the collapse in the order it fell, puzzle-style, while its flock circles. **Reward:** the flock's migration route opens (a flight lane over Deepwood); Restoration +2% Deepwood.

### 4. The First Discordance — *Volcanic Ridge foothills* — Giver: the Purifier
A frenzied Apex-state EcoKin (not a boss — a guardian driven mad by a cracked geothermal vent) is scattering the foothill herds. Soothe it with auditory balms matched to its erratic pulse, then seal the vent with a Terra partner. **Reward:** Purifier service tier up; foothill grazing grounds reopen.

### 5. The Sub-Surface Current — *Coralus Depths* — Giver: Love
Aquatic EcoKin have lost their migratory pathways — shifting currents silenced the Harmony lines they navigate by. Swim the old route with a Torrent partner, re-singing each waypoint buoy in sequence. **Beat:** a moving escort where the *school follows your Harmony*, and straying off-key scatters them. **Reward:** Coralus migration restored; Restoration +3% Coralus Depths.

### 6. Thermal Inversion — *Volcanic Ridge* — Giver: the Mender
A territorial magma-dweller pack is overheating its own den — the cooling-crystal seams it depends on have been poached. Carry Glaze crystals in under a heat timer, place them with a Glaze partner's guidance, and talk the pack down while the den cools. **Reward:** the pack tolerates Sanctuary survey teams; a Glaze crafting seam opens for the Artificer.

### 7. Cloud-Grid Meridian — *Skycurrents* — Giver: the Scout
A pollution pocket (society dumping, drifting up from below) is bending avian navigation instincts over the Skycurrents. Ride the high walkways, cleanse the pocket with the Purity Key node by node while crosswinds push your flight line. **Reward:** Skycurrents trade current reopens; Restoration +2% Skycurrents.

### 8. The Petrified Sonata — *Open Range* — Giver: the Chronicler
Ancient stone conductors stand in the crystal fields, silent for a century. Channel the correct Essence into each conductor in the order the carved sonata demands — the Range itself hums the melody at dawn, if you're there to hear it. **Reward:** a buried survey vault opens (lore + a Catalyst cache); Chronicler's Aide job output up.

### 9. Tundra Cadence — *Frostwind Peaks* — Giver: Love
Frost blight is creeping over the lichen fields the peak fauna winter on. Heal it patch by patch with Flora partners — each patch is a small purification duel against Hollow-state grazers who are *also* just hungry. Feed first, purify second. **Reward:** the winter herd survives to spring (a visible world change); Restoration +3% Frostwind Peaks.

### 10. The Shifted Valley — *Rift Anchorage, Sky Isles* (post-Chronicle 13) — Giver: D.A.H.L.I.A. (flagged anomaly)
A valley where paths loop infinitely — walkers come out where they went in, days later, remembering a different valley. Enter with Harmony and Purity Keys applied *simultaneously* (a Concord-Fused partner is the intended key) and walk the valley's true shape. **Reward:** the loop closes; a Shade-aspected survey site opens; Vaeruun's shadow, lingering at the Anchorage, is one degree quieter.

### 11. Echoes of the Deep Trench — *Coralus Depths, the trench* — Giver: the Bondkeeper
Something leviathan-sized is thrashing in the trench, and every throe sends a pressure wave up at the bayou communities. AbyssDive down and find the truth: it's an old, old EcoKin with a society harpoon-lance in its flank from a decade ago. Remove it under Mender direction while it decides whether to trust the hands on it. **Reward:** the trench goes quiet; the Bondkeeper tells you its name — it's in the Dex, page one of the trench chapter.

### 12. The Apex Zenith — *Moonridge summit* — Giver: the Scout
The summit guardian of Moonridge has never let a Tamer stand on the peak. Climb (ride or fly — no other way), present yourself at the aurora line, and undergo its judgment: a no-combat trial where your *whole party's* Kindling is read at once. Pass, and the final Essence frequency of the ridge attunes. **Reward:** Moonridge summit opens as a fast-flight nexus; Nyctalon country becomes navigable at night.

### 13. Echoes of the Fracture — *any Heartroot node* (post-game, repeatable) — Giver: D.A.H.L.I.A.
The world healed imperfectly — small fractures still tick open at Heartroot nodes like old scars in cold weather. Stabilize them as they appear: short, varied anomaly cleanses (a chasm pocket, a looping path, a silenced waypoint) drawn from the whole game's encounter vocabulary. **Reward:** per-node restoration ticks; this is the living endgame loop that keeps Rebearth breathing after the credits.

## Addendum 2 — Salvage from the fourth paste (2026-10-22, "Complete Chronicles" draft)

This draft mostly re-expanded material already converted above (Canopy Chords, Sub-Surface Current, Petrified Sonata, Silt Distortion, Stray Melody, Ancient Directive, Guardian of the Grove all reappear under its own numbering). Its claimed "official" region names and its replacement Essence list (Magma/Frost/Marine/Geo/Static/Umbral/Astral) were **rejected** — the nine Essences stand: Flora, Torrent, Pyre, Terra, Aero, Glaze, Voltic, Aura, Shade. "Level 100 entity" and the 200-Eddy "identical archetypes" filler were rejected (we don't do levels, and we don't pad quest counts). Three things were genuinely worth keeping:

- **Eddy categories adopted as an index** for organizing side content (not as a quota): *Environmental Stabilization · Lost Melody Tracking · Ancient Tech Re-Initialization · Community Harmonization.* Our existing 17 side quests sort into these cleanly.
- **The Data Thief** (new side quest, *Sanctuary → Wetland Forge*, giver: the Bondkeeper) — the paste's "Scholar's Betrayal" shrunk to its honest scale: a Chronicler's aide is selling Dex observation data to a secret society, letting poachers predict migration routes. Track the leak through the Missing Pages archive, confront the aide (who is scared, not evil — the society has something on him), and choose: expose him, or let him feed the society one last, false migration route and walk away clean. Morvane already owns the forced-alignment villainy; this is the small human version.
- **The Infinite Cadence, reframed** (capstone, giver: Love) — the paste's "all 648 species perform" is impossible and wrong (Nature performs for no one). Reframed: after every side quest in the pack is complete, the Sanctuary holds a cadence night where **your own Kindled partners** — every EcoKin who chose you across the whole game — perform together on the Heartroot green. No reward but the moment itself, and a Dex page written by D.A.H.L.I.A. titled with your Tamer's name.
- **"Continuum Paradox"** — filed as a *proposed label* for the post-game high-tier Eddy band (Echoes of the Fracture and its successors), Tay's call; not adopted as a separate 14-quest structure, since our post-game already runs through the Eddies and the Essence pilgrimage.
