# Echohearts: Rebearth — Mission Campaign (2026-10-22)

Status: **CONCEPT — built by Love at Tay's direction, 2026-10-22.** The source paste was a Pokémon mission list with our title pasted over it (every creature, place, and item in it was Pokémon IP). **Zero Pokémon content survives here** — only the *structural idea* (numbered chronicles, an urban arc, frontier operations, timed incursions) was kept, per Tay's standing rule: methods, never content. Every mission below is built from locked Echohearts canon: Rebearth's 13 regions, the 14-boss order, the four Resonance Keys, the Dred arc, the Sanctuary, and the 14-path companion matrix. Progression gates on **Resonance Milestones** — canon measurements only (Kindling %, restoration %, corruption cleansed, Dex logged). Nothing is captured; EcoKin are **bound by Resonance Alignment** or they choose to stay. Nothing sentient dies; frenzied EcoKin are **quelled by purification**, never killed.

## Part I — Main Chronicles (27)

The spine. Bosses land in their locked order; world-state transitions fire on their locked triggers.

1. **Shimmer Shore Awakening** — Wake on Rebearth's shore as the Elemental Comet's light fades. Piplin chooses you — your first Kindling, the tuning-fork partner. Learn the Anima-Link and what its strain costs *you*.
2. **The Sanctuary Trial** — Earn your place at the Sanctuary: A.E.G.I.S.-scan three wild EcoKin in Deepwood (read their Echoprints, never trap) and return with one willing companion via the Binding ceremony.
3. **The Catalyst Workbench** — Craft your first Resonance balms and Kindling offerings at the Sanctuary bench from shed/gifted materials. Nothing taken by force, ever.
4. **Logging the Deepwood** — Complete your first Echo-Dex pages: log 10 Deepwood species by observation and Kindling. D.A.H.L.I.A. validates each entry against the Forms Registry.
5. **The Warden's Request** — A Hollow-state EcoKin is destabilizing Deepwood's grove. Reach it through the ceremony's willingness model — calm it, don't corner it. *(Optional boss path opens: Gloomtrot.)*
6. **Summoned by the Chronicler** — The Sanctuary's Chronicler clears you for the wider wilds. The restoration map opens: 13 regions, each with a restoration % that only rises when its land and EcoKin heal.
7. **Frenzy of the Rust King** — Rustmaul, a Corrupted guardian of the Red Canyons, is driving herds off the strata cliffs. Quell by purification — a Resonant Climax, not a kill. *(Boss 2: Rustmaul.)*
8. **The Wayward Surveyor** — A Sanctuary Scout is missing in Moonlit Bayou. Track her by the EcoKin she Kindled with — they remember every promise, and they'll lead you to her.
9. **The Coastline Horizon** — Secure passage along the Azure Coastline toward Coralus: escort a migrating herd past society poacher nets. Release every netted EcoKin — freed, never claimed.
10. **The Kingless Deep** — Below Coralus, Hadalon circles a drowned reservoir vault, Severe-stage and deaf to every call. The AbyssDive: descend, endure, purify. *(Boss 7: Hadalon — order flexes here for the dive gate; see note.)*
11. **Scaling the Shaded Peaks** — Earn a flying partner's trust and ride the Skycurrents up to Moonridge. Flight or ride, always — the peaks have no other door.
12. **The Slumbering Glacial Lord** — Kryodon sleeps inside Frostwind Peaks' buried facility, and a secret society is drilling toward it. Reach the behemoth first; wake it gently or not at all. *(Boss 8: Kryodon.)*
13. **Exile from the Sanctuary** — The sky fractures — a Blightstorm tears across the Great Plains and the Sanctuary itself is breached. Fall back, regroup, and fight the storm's heart. *(Boss 3: Blightstorm — on fall: **The Skies Clear**, and the Sky Isles rise.)*
14. **Trial of the Vibrance Key** — At a Heartroot node, prove tempo: a duel-chain where your partners' strain is your own. Win without breaking a single Anima-Link.
15. **Trial of the Density Key** — Prove endurance: hold a corrupted chasm crossing against waves of Hollow-state EcoKin — every one quelled by purification, none destroyed.
16. **Trial of the Harmony Key** — Prove attunement: a Concord Fusion performed willingly, held through a full Resonance Strain cycle, then unfused with both partners whole.
17. **Trial of the Purity Key** — Prove clarity: descend into a Resonance Chasm and cleanse it to zero corruption while your own link carries the land's strain feedback.
18. **Atop the Heartroot Array** — With four Keys turned, the Heartroot Resonance Array answers. Vaeruun, Rift-Crown Arbiter (Shade), bars the way — a counterpart trial, not a conquest: withstand his judgment and be *recognized*.
19. **The Mirror Fracture** — Through the rift: Vaeluun, Rift-Temple Arbiter (Aura), the light counterpart. Between the two arbiters the false prophecy is exposed — "One sovereign shall command the four" was a lie written over "Many hearts become one resonance."
20. **The New Dawn** — Post-game opens. The nine Essences are scattering from the healed Array — begin the Essence pilgrimage across all 13 regions.
21. **The Pyre Essence** — Volcanic Ridge: Pyrewyrm's caldera, where the Land Cools on purification. *(Boss 9: Pyrewyrm.)*
22. **The Aura Essence** — Moonridge under aurora: Nyctalon's night hunt, an optional duel of patience in the dark. *(Boss 11: Nyctalon, optional.)*
23. **The Terra Essence** — Great Plains: Bastodon, the fortress tortoise, holds a mesa that is a buried city. Earn passage; the mesa opens. *(Boss 6: Bastodon.)*
24. **The Herald's Gambit** — The Society Herald finally shows the societies' hand: a coordinated strike on three Sanctuaries at once. Hold all three — residents rally, structures can fall and be rebuilt, people cannot be lost. *(Boss 10: Society Herald.)*
25. **The God of Rot** — The Dred arc crests: Doctor Morvane's deepest lab, beneath the Institute, where Lord Dred keeps what the fracture gave him. Free every EcoKin in the combining chambers. The player always arrives in time. *(Interweaves the 10-mission Dred chain.)*
26. **The World Breathes Easier** — Shadow Beast Prime rises from the largest chasm on Rebearth — an Ecoheart fully fractured, wearing no species. Walk it back from 100 corruption to zero. It is purified, never slain. *(Boss 13: Shadow Beast Prime — on fall: **The World Breathes Easier**.)*
27. **The Sovereign Resonance** — With the full Dex logged and every region restored, Nature — the Legendary Humanoid-Kin — appears at the Heartroot. Not a battle. Not a binding. A synchronization: many hearts, one resonance. The Restoration Era begins, and it heals imperfectly, the way real healing does. *(Boss 14: Society Paragon is confronted within this finale's opening movement — the last human claim to sovereignty, refused.)*

*Boss-order note: the locked 14-boss order and its optional flags (Gloomtrot, Rotwarden, Nyctalon optional) govern; Chronicle numbering flexes where a region gate requires it (Hadalon's dive). Rotwarden's optional purification slots into the Moonlit Bayou stretch of Chronicle 8 as a discoverable detour.*

## Part II — The Forge Reclamation (Urban Chronicles, 42 ops)

The urban campaign, rebuilt for Rebearth: **Wetland Forge**, the drowned industrial city where river meets ancient-tech works, is reclaimed street by street. Dispatched from the Sanctuary; every op raises the city's restoration %.

- **Ops 1–5 — Foothold:** Establish the Forge Sanctuary annex at the old dam house, Kindled partner attuned to the city's grid, first three districts' Heartroot nodes surveyed.
- **Ops 6–15 — Reconnect the Grid:** Restore Heartroot conduits across the commercial and industrial districts — each reconnection visibly changes the city (water clears, moss returns, lights warm). Society saboteurs contest every third node.
- **Ops 16–35 — The Fractured Twenty:** Twenty Severe-stage EcoKin haunt the Forge's flooded works — each is a purification encounter with its own remembered history (D.A.H.L.I.A. reads its Dex entry aloud before you descend). Quell all twenty; their Kindling, afterward, is their choice.
- **Ops 36–42 — Defense of the Spire:** The societies make their play for the Forge's Heartroot Spire during a hurricane landfall (forecast on A.E.G.I.S. 24h out — you prepare, or you go hard and risk the rebuild). Hold the Spire and the **Infinite Circuit** unlocks: the Duel Circuit's endless ladder, where strain persists between matches and nothing is ever taken from the loser.

## Part III — Frontier Operations (Sanctuary-dispatched, 3 sectors)

Structured deployments from the Sanctuary's Bondkeeper board. High stakes, small squads, always a way through that harms no one.

### Sector 1 — The Dry Frontier (Great Plains / Red Canyons), 10 ops
1. **The Initiate's First Step** — Clear a canyon pass where a Rage-state herd blocks the only bridge — by calming, not clearing out.
2. **The Chronicler's Shield** — Escort the Sanctuary Chronicler through canyon country to a strata archive.
3. **The Mesa Fire** — A lightning-set grassfire runs at a hollow mesa full of nesting EcoKin. Water-partner coordination; evacuate, contain, heal the burn.
4. **The Society Roadblock** — A secret society has sealed a pass to strip-mine crystal. Infiltrate; collapse their operation, free their bound-by-force EcoKin (force breaks the link — they leave shattered, and you carry the strain of it).
5. **The Missing River-Barge** — Recover a stolen Sanctuary supply barge from a canyon grotto.
6. **Clearing the Cliffside** — Rockslide traps a survey team in the strata terraces. Heavy partners move stone; nobody blasts near the nests.
7. **The Counterfeit Kiln** — Infiltrate a forge stamping out counterfeit Binding devices — domination tech. Shut it down; the G.L.Y.T.C.H. chain opens here.
8. **Sub-Canyon Rescue** — Retrieve captured Sanctuary staff from a flooded under-canyon holding pen.
9. **Storming the Quarry Post** — The society's canyon command post. Open assault or quiet infiltration — your call, same mercy rules.
10. **The Summit Stand** — At the canyon's highest spire, stop the society waking what sleeps under it. *(Optional boss echo: GROVANTIS sighting — Boss 12's first shadow.)*

### Sector 2 — The Drowned Frontier (Coralus Depths / Moonlit Bayou), 11 ops
1. **The First Commission** — Escort a high-priority medicine transport through the bayou before the tide turns.
2. **Deep Water Echoes** — Rescue civilians trapped in a rising-tide cave; your Torrent partners are the only way in or out.
3. **The Ice Shelf Protocol** — Extract a stranded survey team from a calving glacier segment at the bayou's cold mouth.
4. **The Raging Brushfire** — Bayou gas-fire: coordinate Torrent skills and wind breaks to save the grove and everything nesting in it.
5. **Beneath the Ruins** — Under the flooded ruins: an Institute-era tampering facility, still warm. Document it, purge it, free what's caged in the dark.
6. **Defending the Annex** — A surprise society strike on the Wetland Forge annex. Structures can fall. People don't.
7. **The Sky Carrier Infiltration** — A society airship is moving caged EcoKin over the bayou. Board it mid-flight (ride or fly — no other way up), free every cage, land it soft.
8. **The Torrent Trial** — Trial of the marine trenches: an ancient guardian tests whether your Kindling holds at crushing depth.
9. **The Glaze Trial** — Trial of the frozen vault: slide-ice chamber glyphs, solved with a Glaze partner's senses, not force.
10. **The Pyre Trial** — Trial of the magma chamber beneath the bayou's volcanic root: a Pyre guardian, a narrow bridge, a promise kept.
11. **Tower Inversion** — Ascend the drowned signal tower and dismantle the domination broadcast rig at its heart — the device that whispers *command* into Anima-Links. Pull it down and every forced link in the sector snaps free at once.

### Sector 3 — The Sky Frontier (Skycurrents / Prism Cloud Temples), 10 ops
*Opens only after Blightstorm falls and the Sky Isles rise (Chronicle 13).*
1. **The Communications Lockout** — Reclaim a sky relay from society climbers before they blind the Isles.
2. **The Stolen Songbird** — A unique songbird EcoKin, targeted by collectors for its voice. Track the poachers; the rescue ends with the bird choosing its own sky.
3. **Bypassing the Cloud Mines** — Society sky-mines seed a trade current. Chart a clean lane; disarm what you pass — nothing detonates near a nesting cloud.
4. **The Glider Rescue** — Free gliding EcoKin from high-altitude capture nets strung between isles.
5. **The Aero Monument Trial** — Ancient wind-gate puzzles on the Galeward Spires.
6. **The Glaze Monument Trial** — Frost-glyph chambers on the Mistveil Gardens' cold terraces.
7. **The Voltic Monument Trial** — Dismantle storm-spires feeding an island-wide barrier over Stormcrown.
8. **The Mirror Prism Mystery** — Align the Prism Cloud Temples' light-beams to open the floating fortress vault — what's inside is a name from the false prophecy, and it's empty. That's the point.
9. **The Armor of the Societies** — The Pincher-lords' counterparts: society enforcers in weaponized resonance armor. Their armor runs on forced links — break the links, and the armor is just weight.
10. **The Fortress Fall** — The sky fortress's ancient engine is a weaponized Heartroot tap. Shut it down by *healing its root*, not firing it — the fortress settles gently onto the sea. Nobody falls.
