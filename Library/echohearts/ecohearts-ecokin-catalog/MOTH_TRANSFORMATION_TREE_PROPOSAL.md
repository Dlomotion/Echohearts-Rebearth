## How to read the chain

A moth begins on one of three branches. Each **Stage** is a Growth Rite (gated by Biological Maturity, Kindling %, Ecology accomplishments, Catalyst items) — the moth matures, it does not reset. The **Sun branch** (Sun Tapestry Eco-type) runs Emberatlas → Chrismoth; the **Moon branch** (Lunar Current Eco-type) runs Lunamoth → Emperor Moth. Both branches converge on **Solunara**, the unified Stage-3 apex — "Harmony of Light and Night, Keeper of Eternal Balance." The **Prism branch** (Prismavex, Emperyx) runs parallel as prismatic kin. **Chrysalix** is the guardian of transformations, not a stage. **Chrisma Moth** is Chrismoth's seasonal Winter Form.
