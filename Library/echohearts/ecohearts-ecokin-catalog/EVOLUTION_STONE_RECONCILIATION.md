# Evolution-Line Reconciliation: folded with a stone

**Tay ruling (2026-10-12):** "Yes fold evolution with a stone."
**Status:** Tay-directed design reconciliation. Proposal-tier — pending 10_Production review. Nothing to 00_Canon_Lock.

## The ruling

"Evolution line / evolution stage / evolution path / Lv." wording printed on Eco-Kin art cards is **folded into the Growth Rite system with Catalyst (Evolution) Stones**, not treated as level-reset evolution.

## The mechanism

Each card's printed line (e.g. Sproutchid → Breezeling → Zephyrbloom → Stormpetal → Thunderlily → Thunderbloom; Scaldlet Lv.1-15 → Steambite → … → Thermivorx Lv.84+) becomes a **Growth Rite chain**:

1. **Stages stay visible.** The chain's stages remain as Growth Rite milestones — the art cards are evidence of the intended progression, not discarded.
2. **No levels, no resets.** Printed Lv. ranges and "evolution" language are design-evidence/provenance only. Growth Rites never reset the creature; the Echoprint stays intact.
3. **Stones gate the rites.** Each rite in the chain is gated by an **Evolution Stone** (a Catalyst item) plus the standard Growth Rite requirements: Biological Maturity, Kindling (trust), and Ecology accomplishments.
4. **A.E.G.I.S. / D.A.H.L.I.A.** validate the chain: stone authenticity, rite eligibility, and Forms Registry entries for each stage's form.

## Applies to (cards filed with printed lines)

- Thunderbloom (6-stage), Cryolithix (5-stage), Luminspire (4-stage), Pelagonis (5-stage), Myceliris (6-stage), Coralyx (6-stage), Mangrovyx (6-stage), Thermivorx (6-stage, Lv-ranged), Sting Wreck (3-stage: Tidelet → Sting Wreck → Wreckstorm).
- Future cards with printed lines fold the same way.

## The stones (expanded 2026-10-12, proposal-tier)

**Catalyst (Evolution) Stones** are physical resonance-focusing items — found, crafted, or awarded. They are catalysts, never creatures; nothing is consumed or sacrificed (per the no-sacrifice lock). A stone attunes the Growth Rite to an Essence and stabilizes the transformation.

### Stone taxonomy (proposal-tier names)

| Stone | Attunes to Essence | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| Bloomstone | Flora | grove hearts, restoration sites |
| Torrentstone | Torrent | pure springs, tide shrines |
| Pyrestone | Pyre | volcanic vents, forge ruins |
| Terrastone | Terra | deep caverns, mountain roots |
| Aerostone | Aero | sky nests, storm spires |
| Glazestone | Glaze | glacier cores, aurora fields |
| Volticstone | Voltic | storm rods, lightning scars |
| Aurastone | Aura | dawn sanctuaries, kindling altars |
| Shadestone | Shade | moonlit groves (never corruption) |

### Acquisition paths

1. **Found** — in the wild at resonance-rich sites (ecology accomplishment tied).
2. **Crafted** — at Restoration Farming workshops from gathered materials.
3. **Awarded** — by Sanctuaries, A.E.G.I.S. milestones, or Kindling thresholds.

### Rite-gating flow

1. The Eco-Kin reaches the next stage's **Biological Maturity** threshold (printed Lv. ranges map here, e.g. Thermivorx's Lv. 84+ ≈ apex maturity).
2. **Kindling** trust with the Frequency Tamer meets the rite's requirement.
3. An **Ecology accomplishment** relevant to the stone's source is completed.
4. The **stone is presented** at the rite — D.A.H.L.I.A. validates stone authenticity and rite eligibility via the A.E.G.I.S. device.
5. The rite completes: the new stage's form is entered in the Forms Registry; the Echoprint never changes; the stone is spent as a catalyst (not a sacrifice).

### What stones are not

- Not levels, not XP items.
- Not consumed creatures — no-sacrifice lock holds.
- Shadestone is Shade-Essence attunement, never a corruption vector; Shadow remains a condition, not a category.
