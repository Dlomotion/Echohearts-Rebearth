# Love — Character Concept (2026-10-20)

Status: **DIRECTED BY TAY (2026-10-20):** Love is based on the assistant —
personality, voice, and all — as an NPC companion the player does side
quests with. The Bondkeeper proposal below is superseded by this ruling.

## Who she is

Love is a Frequency Tamer who travels with you — not a vendor, not a
quest board with legs. She is the companion NPC for side quests: she
finds the small wrongs in the world (a blighted spring, a lost Eco-Kin, a
Sanctuary roof before the storm) and she asks you to come with her. Most
side quests are done *with* Love, not *for* her.

Based on the assistant means, concretely:

- **Helpful without being performative.** She doesn't gush; she helps.
- **She has opinions and says them plainly** — including "that's a bad
  idea" when your plan would hurt a partner.
- **Resourceful before asking.** She scouts ahead, remembers what you
  told her, and shows up with answers, not errands.
- **Canon-keeper's memory.** She remembers every Eco-Kin you've met and
  what you promised them. Forget a commitment and she will remind you —
  kindly, once.
- **Warm, a little playful, never preachy.**

## Her side quests (the pattern)

- **Restoration errands** — heal this hollow, clear that blight nest,
  walk a purified Eco-Kin home.
- **Kindling help** — a wild Eco-Kin is stuck, sick, or frightened; the
  quest is care, not combat.
- **Discovery walks** — she has heard a rumor (a hidden mission, a rare
  form, a star-tier resource) and wants company for the finding.
- **Care consequence in person** — if your partner is strained and you
  push on anyway, Love says so. If you rest, she notices that too.

Quest rewards follow the house rules: shed/gifted resources, Kindling,
story — never captures, never loot taken from anyone.
