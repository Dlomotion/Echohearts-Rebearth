# SHADOW BEAST PRIME — 60-Second Finale Trailer Script

Shootable script. Every beat keys to the built finale system (FinaleSubsystem:
Assembling → Voyage → EyeOfStorm → Purification → RestorationEra; Rite Defense;
Elderwing vessel). Canon language only: Binding (never capture), EcoKin/Ecoform
taxonomy, Vibrance / Density / Harmony / Purity. Nothing here depicts a mechanic
the game does not have. Total: exactly 60 seconds.

---

**0:00–0:06 — ASSEMBLING** *(6s)*
Wide shot: the Sanctuary at dawn. The **Elderwing** rises from its roost, bound
companions aboard in the roost berths, the Tamer's active team of six on deck.
A.E.G.I.S. chimes: the finale is gated, and every gate is met. No dialogue —
just the chord of the ship's engines and wind.

**0:06–0:14 — VOYAGE** *(8s)*
The Elderwing climbs through the storm wall — flight, never teleportation. Lightning
cracks across torn clouds; below, blighted land gives way to fractured Shadow.
The Tamer grips the rail. Their EcoKin brace beside them — Vibrance, Density,
Harmony, Purity surging in the Anima-Link, the strain visible on the Tamer's face.

**0:14–0:20 — EYE OF THE STORM** *(6s)*
Silence. The ship breaches into the calm eye. And there it is: the **Shadow Beast
Prime**, coiled around the wound in the world — the largest fracture ever seen.
It turns. The sky darkens at its gaze. Title whisper (VO, quiet): *"It was never
a monster. It was a wound."*

**0:20–0:38 — RITE DEFENSE** *(18s)*
The Tamer's EcoKin take positions around the rite circle etched in light on the
storm-eye's floor. Waves of Shadow-touched husks surge in — repelled, never
killed; the circle holds, flickers, holds. A breach: the circle breaks — the rite
*pauses*, the Tamer steadies their partners through the Anima-Link, and the circle
re-forms. Sweat, strain, trust. This is the game's thesis in one shot: protection,
not conquest.

**0:38–0:48 — PURIFICATION** *(10s)*
Resonant Climax. The Tamer and their EcoKin move as one being — the walk-back
begins. Light threads from the circle into the Beast's fracture. It does not die.
It *unwinds*: Shadow peeling away like storm clouds breaking, the colossal form
growing smaller, quieter, until what remains is simply an EcoKin — hurt, lost,
and finally reachable. The Tamer extends a hand. The Binding offer glows.

**0:48–0:56 — RESTORATION ERA** *(8s)*
Dawn over Rebearth. The storm dissolves. Released light rains upward. Forests
unfurl in fast-forward; rivers run clear; wild EcoKin return to healed lands.
The Sanctuary's bell rings. The Tamer stands with their partners on a green hill,
looking at a world breathing easier.

**0:56–1:00 — TITLE** *(4s)*
Black. One line fades in: **ECHOHEARTS: REBEARTH**. Beneath it: *"The Restoration
Era begins."*

**Total: 6 + 8 + 6 + 18 + 10 + 8 + 4 = 60 seconds.**
