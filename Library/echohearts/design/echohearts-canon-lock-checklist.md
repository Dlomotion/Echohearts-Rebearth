# Echohearts: Rebearth — Canon Lock Checklist

Living document. Every lock below is one of Tay's own rulings, quoted with its date.
New rulings extend this list; nothing here is ever rewritten or relaxed. Pasted
material (mostly ChatGPT) is scored against this checklist as reconciliation
evidence — never auto-canon.

## Binding ceremony tuning (ruled 2026-10-22 — Tay: "Keep all 9 as built")
The Binding ceremony (Approach → Attunement → The Offer → Alignment) carries
these tuning values as Tay's locks. Every value below is the as-built default he
approved; each stays reversible by his later ruling and is attributed to him.
1. **Phase order and the 4-second Approach beat stand** — Approach → Attunement →
   The Offer → Alignment, with the 4-second approach pause before anything is asked.
2. **3 synced heartbeat pulses complete Attunement.**
3. **The willingness model stands** — willingness = encounter state base (Calm .70 /
   Hollow .50 / Alpha .45 / Rage .30 / Blight .20) + attunement + remembered
   kindness − corruption; the EcoKin aligns at .55 or above. Never random.
4. **Purification is offered mid-ceremony at corruption 40** — cleanse and
   continue, rather than abandoning the ceremony.
5. **Strain costs stand** — 2 per attunement attempt, 8 to purify, 12 to align;
   strain is held (frozen) during The Offer so the decision is never rushed.
6. **A successful Alignment seeds Kindling at 12–25%.**
7. **The 24-hour dignity timer stands** — after a decline, no re-offer for 24
   hours. A decline withdraws with dignity; no punishment.
8. **NeverBindableIDs stands** — Nature is the canonical never-bindable entry;
   a never-bindable EcoKin is refused gracefully.
9. **The ceremony defers while a duel is running** — the offer waits; it never
   interrupts a duel.

## Identity & taxonomy
1. **EcoKin = the creature. Ecoform = a form/state it takes on.** (2026-10-05)
   Base, Growth Rite, Mutation, Resonance Strain, Shimmer Form — all Ecoforms attach
   to the base EcoKin; the Dex entry never splits.
2. **Five Orders of Sentience: Zoa, Flora, Myko, Proto, Monera.** (2026-10-05)
3. **The planet is Rebearth. The player is the Frequency Tamer / Core-Binder.**
   (2026-10-05) "Tamer of Beasts" and "Savior of the Universe" framings are retired.
4. **Lab-created variants are Synth-Origin** — geometric scars, bioluminescent
   veins, echo instability; stronger and more dangerous. (2026-10-05)

## Attributes & essences
5. **The four attributes are Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity.** Never ATK/HP/SPD/DEF,
   never int-mapped generics. (2026-10-05)
6. **Nine essences: Flora, Torrent, Pyre, Terra, Aero, Glaze, Voltic, Aura, Shade.**
   (2026-10-05) **Shadow is a CONDITION, not an element or category** — Echoheart
   fracture from forced domination, DNA over-tampering, environmental collapse, or
   weaponization.

## Bonding & progression
7. **Binding, Resonance Alignment, Release.** Never capture, trapping, catching,
   ownership, or containers. "A companion is a relationship, not a container."
   (2026-10-05)
8. **Growth Rites replace evolution.** No level-reset evolutions. Gated by Biological
   Maturity, Kindling (trust %), Ecology accomplishments, and Catalysts. (2026-10-05)
9. **Echoprint is immutable** — an approved appearance is never silently replaced.
   **Shimmer Form** is the universal ascension and preserves identity. (2026-10-05)
10. **Anima-Link is bidirectional** — EcoKin strain/damage feeds back as stamina/health
    consequences on the Tamer. (2026-10-05)
11. **No sacrifice of sentient partners, ever.** Duplicates yield research/resonance,
    never consumed. Willing Release of a duplicate yields shed/gifted bodily resources
    for crafting (Monster Hunter's *method* — never its content). Release ≠ sacrifice.
    (2026-10-05; revised 2026-10-14)
12. **No killing.** Beaten wild EcoKin yield and withdraw — never killed. (2026-10-19)
13. **First partner: Piplin** (Flora healer) is every Tamer's first Kindling; all
    further partners come through the 14-path companion-acquisition matrix.
    (2026-10-12, Tay's Starter Ruling — Option C Hybrid)

## States, corruption & travel
14. **Rage / Alpha / Blight / Hollow are encounter STATES, never species.**
    **Omega is a Humanoid-Kin classification**, never captured. Nature is the
    exception: never Alpha/Omega/capturable. (2026-10-05)
15. **Corruption stages: Clean → Tainted → Corrupted → Severe → Fractured.**
    Form shifts at 50, reverts below 30; 100 = Echoheart fracture into Shadow.
    (2026-10-05)
16. **Travel is flight or ride — never teleportation.** (2026-10-05)

## World & production rules
17. **No new Bibles, Dexes, currencies, or governance structures.** New material routes
    into the existing 00_Canon_Lock → 11_Publication workflow only. (2026-10-05)
18. **Study other games' methods; never use their content, names, creatures, maps,
    code, fiction, or visual identities.** Original-only IP. (standing)
19. **Runtime target locked: Unreal Engine 5.8.3.** (2026-10-15)

## Standing paste-triage behavior
Every pasted draft Tay shares is scored against this checklist before anything else:
- Each violation names the lock number above and quotes the lock it breaks.
- Salvageable *methods* are separated from hard violations (a good method from a
  paste can be rebuilt in our systems; a violation cannot).
- Anything no lock answers is flagged as an **open ruling for Tay**, never scored
  either way.
- A repeated paste gets pointed to its earlier verdict, not re-reviewed from scratch.
- Past triage reports are kept on file; this checklist is re-read fresh each time so
  new rulings apply immediately.
