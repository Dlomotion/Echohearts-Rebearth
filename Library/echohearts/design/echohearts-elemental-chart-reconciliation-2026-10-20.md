# The 12-Element Chart × The Nine Essences — Reconciliation (2026-10-20)

Status: PROPOSAL for Tay's approval. Built because Tay said "create the 12
elemental chart and 9 essence and expand on everything."

## The ruling structure

Both stay. They stop competing because they do different jobs:

- **The nine Essences are the canon system** (LOCKED): Flora, Torrent, Pyre,
  Terra, Aero, Glaze, Voltic, Aura, Shade. Combat, forms, and canon math run
  on these. Nothing in the chart overrides them.
- **The 12 chart types become A.E.G.I.S. field tags** — the labels D.A.H.L.I.A.
  shows Tamers in the field for quick reading. Tags are a *vocabulary layer*
  over the Essences, many-to-one. A tag is never a tenth essence.

## The chart's gap

The 12-chart has **no ice tag** — Glaze has no chart counterpart. Proposal:
where the chart is silent, A.E.G.I.S. falls back to the Essence name itself
(Glaze). No 13th tag is invented.

## Strengths & weaknesses

The chart's strength/weakness wheel folds into the existing nine-Essence
interaction work (the same reconciliation queue as the old 18-element RPS).
No new combat math is created here; tag-level matchups resolve to their
Essence mapping first.
