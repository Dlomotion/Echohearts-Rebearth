# The Seven Beasts — Beast-Tier EcoKin Group (Inspiration-Driven Revision)
**Status:** CONCEPT — proposed group; routes to 03_EcoKin_Dex (intake). Nothing enters 00_Canon_Lock without review.
**Compiled:** 2026-10-05, per Tay's directive: the seven must be beasts, not basic creatures.
**Revision note:** Redesigned using Tay's uploaded inspiration images (2026-10-05). GROVANTIS adopted directly from Tay's own artwork as the Flora beast.

## Design direction (from the inspiration images)
- **The GROVANTIS bar:** every beast fuses fauna + element + landscape the way GROVANTIS fuses lion, flora, and crystal — lush, painterly, grand scale, luminous accents, readable powerful silhouette. This is the visual benchmark for all seven.
- **True anatomy always:** every beast reads as a real animal first (eagle, owl, monitor lizard, tortoise, leviathan, behemoth, lion) with the elemental fusion layered on — never a generic monster blob.
- **Presence over stats:** each beast is a region-defining event. Encountering one changes the weather, the soundscape, the behavior of every wild EcoKin nearby.
- **Original designs only:** the Palmon screenshots were used for *presentation* inspiration only (see below). No creature, name, or design from Palmon: Survival is reproduced here.

## The Seven

### 1. Fulgurix — the Storm That Hunts
- **Order:** Zoa | **Essence:** Voltic / Aero
- **Concept art:** `art/media-generation-seven-beasts-fulgurix-0-40f02f53-2035-41da-9677-07743193d0a8.webp`
- **Body plan:** True thunderbird raptor — broad eagle anatomy, primary feathers shaped like forking lightning, voltic energy tracing the wing edges, dense talons built for striking from storm cloud.
- **Presence:** Its arrival drops barometric pressure; A.E.G.I.S. scans static out. Valleys darken under its wingspan.
- **Temperament:** Proud, territorial; judges intent from altitude before ever descending.
- **Habitat:** Storm spires and high ridgelines; rides supercell fronts.
- **Kindling route:** Only through protecting its nesting grounds during storms — never through combat dominance. It tests Tamers by forcing them to shelter its fledgling zones while it watches.
- **Signature:** *Skyrender Dive* — a lightning-wreathed stoop that shatters corrupted storm-cells; never turned on the healthy wild.
- **Ecoforms:** Base storm-raptor Ecoform; charged-plumage Growth Rite Ecoform after sustained Kindling; fracture-scarred variant recorded as a condition state, never a separate identity.

### 2. Hadalon — the Trench Sovereign
- **Order:** Proto (colony intelligence expressed as one vast body) | **Essence:** Torrent / Shade
- **Concept art:** `art/media-generation-seven-beasts-hadalon-0-f39673ae-5986-4d06-9d69-b8f9dff49303.webp`
- **Body plan:** True abyssal leviathan — whalefall anatomy, translucent pressure-skin, bioluminescent lure-rows along the jaw; unmistakably a deep-sea animal, not a serpent cliché.
- **Presence:** Its surfacing displaces harbors; migration routes bend around its feeding grounds.
- **Temperament:** Ancient, patient; speaks in infrasound pulses D.A.H.L.I.A. can translate but never fully parse.
- **Habitat:** Hadal trenches and polar tidegates; rises only when trench ecology destabilizes.
- **Kindling route:** Acoustic reciprocity — answering its pulses honestly across repeated encounters — plus cleansing the trench runoff poisoning its feeding grounds.
- **Signature:** *Pressure Psalm* — deep resonance that stills corrupted currents and steadies allied EcoKin in heavy water.
- **Ecoforms:** Base leviathan Ecoform; luminous deep-rite Ecoform in trench darkness; old Blight-scar tissue recorded as condition history.

### 3. Pyrewyrm — the Furnace Below
- **Order:** Zoa | **Essence:** Pyre / Terra
- **Concept art:** `art/media-generation-seven-beasts-pyrewyrm-0-b04bdbed-4ea7-4fd5-bb43-82766e45fba0.webp`
- **Body plan:** True magma wyrm — elongated monitor-lizard anatomy, thick obsidian scales with molten heat seams between plates, no wings; moves like liquid stone through lava tubes.
- **Presence:** Volcanic fields go quiet when it moves; its tunnels become the region's arteries.
- **Temperament:** Slow, deliberate, fiercely protective of geothermal nurseries where young fire-adapted EcoKin gestate.
- **Habitat:** Volcanic ridges, lava tubes, scorched calderas.
- **Kindling route:** Through heat-work — venting pressure safely, shielding nurseries during eruptions — never by provoking it to surface.
- **Signature:** *Caldera Breath* — controlled thermal venting that purifies Blight-fused rock and opens sealed geothermal chambers.
- **Ecoforms:** Base wyrm Ecoform; ash-plated mature Ecoform; heat-fracture scarring noted as condition, never a separate beast.

### 4. Kryodon — the White Silence
- **Order:** Zoa | **Essence:** Glaze
- **Concept art:** `art/media-generation-seven-beasts-kryodon-0-68c621a4-cf0c-415d-802e-630a7bd381af.webp`
- **Body plan:** True glacial behemoth — mammoth-grade quadruped, dense underwool beneath ice-rimed guard plates, forward-curving tusks used for carving shelter, not goring.
- **Presence:** Blizzards ease in its wake; herds of cold-adapted EcoKin travel in its wind-shadow.
- **Temperament:** Gentle, mournful; remembers every lost calf of every species it has sheltered.
- **Habitat:** Frost rookeries, permafrost basins, glacier marches.
- **Kindling route:** Through winter stewardship — feeding stranded herds, holding migration lines open — measured over seasons, not encounters.
- **Signature:** *Stillwhite* — a cold-calm field that slows corrupted motion and lets injured EcoKin rest without freezing.
- **Ecoforms:** Base behemoth Ecoform; deep-winter Ecoform with full ice mantle; old poaching scars carried visibly, healed but never erased.

### 5. GROVANTIS — the Verdant Throne
- **Order:** Flora | **Essence:** Flora / Terra
- **Source art:** Tay's own artwork (`workspace/user/files/a220848b-55c8-4ed6-9277-6f11ac7be3ca-1_all_11722_26_p7jw.jpg`) — adopted as the visual anchor for this beast.
- **Concept art:** `art/media-generation-seven-beasts-grovantis-0-61c129ae-750d-4e2e-9699-2d61c7e1191b.webp` (catalog copy: `../ecohearts-ecokin-catalog/EK_ART_Grovantis_v1.webp`) — CONCEPT art only, not approved canon art, same tier as the other six.
- **Body plan:** True lion-form beast fused with living wood, leaf, blossom, and green crystal — bark-textured musculature, crystal spines, flowering crown; unmistakably plant, unmistakably a beast. This is the benchmark the other six are art-directed against.
- **Presence:** Forests rearrange around its resting sites; clearings bloom in its footprints months later.
- **Temperament:** Wrathful toward exploiters, tender toward seedlings; judges Tamers by how they treat the smallest green things.
- **Habitat:** Deep canopy interiors, veilbark forests, restoration-critical woodlands.
- **Kindling route:** Through restoration labor — replanting, defending nurseries, refusing shortcuts — under its silent observation.
- **Signature:** *Thornwake* — a blooming surge that seals Blight-wounds in living wood and raises sanctuary groves.
- **Ecoforms:** Base lion-beast Ecoform; flowering war-form Ecoform when its groves are threatened; burn-scars from the old wars carried as history.

### 6. Nyctalon — the Eye Between Dark
- **Order:** Zoa | **Essence:** Shade
- **Concept art:** `art/media-generation-seven-beasts-nyctalon-0-27786865-6b3c-458e-8355-dc116b484267.webp`
- **Body plan:** True nocturnal apex raptor — great-horned-owl anatomy at titan scale, silent-flight feather fringes, star-speckled indigo plumage, deep amber eyes; no demonic styling.
- **Presence:** Night goes watchful where it hunts; corrupted EcoKin freeze rather than flee.
- **Temperament:** Inscrutable, precise; intervenes only at the exact moment of need — never early, never late.
- **Habitat:** Umbral wilds, moonwell sanctuaries, light-starved caverns.
- **Kindling route:** Through restraint — declining easy kills, protecting the vulnerable in darkness — proven across many nights.
- **Signature:** *Gloaming* — a darkness that clarifies: allies see truly, while Shadow-fractured EcoKin are revealed without being harmed.
- **Ecoforms:** Base raptor Ecoform; moonlit hunt Ecoform; one blinded eye from an old fracture, kept — it navigates by resonance now.

### 7. Bastodon — the Walking Bulwark
- **Order:** Zoa | **Essence:** Terra
- **Concept art:** `art/media-generation-seven-beasts-bastodon-0-141c489d-5880-4557-b6a0-89838971f653.webp`
- **Body plan:** True fortress tortoise — ankylosaur-grade quadruped, fused carapace plates rising like natural battlements, columnar legs, moss and wildflowers colonizing its shell; a living stronghold with an animal's heart.
- **Presence:** Settlements are founded in its migratory shadow; its resting hollows become sanctuaries.
- **Temperament:** Immovable, patient; decides slowly and never reverses a judgment of trust.
- **Habitat:** Shattered frontiers, deep-hearth basins, amberlog woodlands.
- **Kindling route:** Through endurance alongside it — standing watch through long nights, refusing to abandon its slow pace for faster gains.
- **Signature:** *Bulwark March* — a seismic advance that shatters corrupted fortifications and shields everything behind it.
- **Ecoforms:** Base bulwark Ecoform; moss-crowned elder Ecoform; siege-cracks from the Resource Wars worn openly.
