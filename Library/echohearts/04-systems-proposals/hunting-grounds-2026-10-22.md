# The Hunting Grounds — Sport of the Stalk (2026-10-22)

Status: **CONCEPT — Tay's direction 2026-10-22: "I want a hunt… like hunters do in real life as a sport."**

## The one adaptation, stated plainly

Real hunting's sport is the *stalk* — reading sign, working the wind, patience, terrain, and the discipline to get close without being known. Echohearts keeps **all of that**. The shot is a **resonance tag**: an A.E.G.I.S. close-range scan (with a Snap photograph) taken inside the quarry's optimal band, undetected. The EcoKin walks away. The trophy is the tag record and the photograph — scored exactly the way real hunting scores a harvest. Lethal hunting is not adapted: it would break Tay's own locks (a companion is a relationship; nothing sentient is killed; the thesis is restoration). The sport here is *harder* than a rifle, not softer — a bullet needs one moment of stillness; a tag needs you inside thirty meters, downwind, unseen, long enough for the scan to sing.

## The sport

**The Grounds.** Licensed hunting reserves in five regions, each with its own quarry, terrain language, and Lodge:
- **Great Plains** — long grass and wind discipline; herds spotted from ridges, stalked through draws. Signature quarry: the great plains grazers.
- **Deepwood** — dense cover, sound carries; rub trees, beds, and trails. Signature quarry: Bramblecub kin and the old antlered ones.
- **Frostwind Peaks** — mountain stalks; altitude, white cover, glassing from spurs. Signature quarry: peak-climbers that vanish into snow glare.
- **Moonlit Bayou** — water stalks from skiff and mudflat; calls and decoy chords. Signature quarry: the bayou gliders.
- **Red Canyons** — extreme range spotting; the stalk is a route problem across broken stone. Signature quarry: canyon sprinters.

**The hunt loop.** (1) **Read sign** — tracks, scat, rubs, beds, calls; A.E.G.I.S. ages the sign and reads direction, but never draws you a line to the animal. (2) **Plan the stalk** — wind (shown on grass, water, and your partner's coat), noise surface, cover lines, sun. (3) **The approach** — movement noise, silhouette, and scent cone are all simulated; a spooked quarry bolts and the hunt is *over* — no chasing a blown stalk, that's the ethics and the difficulty in one rule. (4) **The tag** — inside the optimal band (closer is worth more, too close risks the spook), hold the scan through one full breath cycle while your partner holds position. (5) **The score** — the Lodge scores every tag like a harvest card: distance, undetected %, wind discipline, sign-reading accuracy, time. A perfect tag on a wary old quarry is the sport's summit.

**Seasons & the Hunt Circuit.** Grounds rotate by the forecast weather system — migration and rut seasons move quarry between grounds and change behavior. Scored hunts run as a **Hunt Circuit** alongside the Duel Circuit: seasonal leaderboards, Lodge-hosted exam hunts, and team stalks (two hunters, one tag, both partners working cover).

**The Hunters.** The Lodge keeps the sport's code — **the Hunter's Terms**: never harm; never press a wounded, sick, or corrupted quarry (you stand off and call the Purifier — a corrupted quarry converts the hunt into a purification escort, scored separately and honored higher); a blown stalk ends the hunt; what the quarry sheds — antler, feather, scale, at rubs and beds — may be gathered as gifted material under the standing Release-resource economy. Nothing is ever taken from the animal itself. Lodge ranks run Tracker → Stalker → Windreader → Huntmaster. A **Huntmaster** NPC runs each Lodge (name proposal — Tay's call).

**What a clean tag earns.** Ecology data for the Chronicler (a tagged quarry's Dex entry deepens: range, habits, sign), Lodge trophies (the Snap print on the Lodge wall), shed/gifted crafting material found along the stalk, and the thing hunters actually talk about: **the quarry remembers you.** A cleanly tagged EcoKin has met a human at its most vulnerable range and been left in peace — tagged individuals gain a standing Kindling hook, and some return later to offer Binding on their own initiative. The best hunters in Rebearth are the Tamers the wild trusts most. That is not a softening of the sport; it is the sport's whole reputation economy.

## The Hunt chain (10 missions — the missing missions)

1. **First Sign** *(Deepwood Lodge)* — the Huntmaster teaches sign: age three trails, call the species, direction, and hour before you're trusted with a tag lens.
2. **Wind & Patience** — a full stalk on a plains grazer with the wind shifting mid-approach; the tutorial is failing honestly and re-planning, not a scripted win.
3. **The Long Glass** *(Red Canyons)* — spotting and identification at extreme range; three similar species, one legal tag, misidentification ends the day.
4. **Close Enough** — your first scored tag inside the optimal band, undetected. The Lodge records it; your name goes on the board.
5. **Night Sign** *(Moonlit Bayou)* — a nocturnal stalk by call and water-sound; the bayou at night, scored on sign-reading alone.
6. **The White Stag Problem** *(Frostwind)* — a famous old quarry every hunter in the region has failed on for years; it's not faster than you, it's *smarter* — it pattern-reads hunters. Beating it requires breaking your own habits. (Species name is a Lodge nickname, not a Dex entry.)
7. **Poachers in the Grounds** — the mirror: a society crew is running a *lethal* hunt camp inside the Deepwood Grounds — cages, snares, a buyer waiting. Shut it down: free the caged, break the snare lines, and escort the crew out under the Terms — they leave alive and unarmed, and the Lodge board posts their names under a different kind of trophy.
8. **The Rut** *(Great Plains, seasonal)* — herd movement season; scored team stalks during the migration, with herd guardians actively testing every hunter in the grass.
9. **What the Storm Left** — a post-hurricane grounds survey (weather tie-in): sign washed clean, quarry displaced — a tracking hunt across a changed map, doubling as a restoration survey for the Scout.
10. **The Huntmaster's Exam** — one tag, one day, the Grounds' wariest quarry, no A.E.G.I.S. sign-aging assist. Pass and the Lodge grants the Huntmaster rank — and the old quarry, if you tagged it clean, sometimes comes to the Lodge fence line in later seasons. Looking. Deciding. (Binding offers remain the EcoKin's choice, always.)

## Expansion (2026-10-22, later) — Field Studies: the Colossal Encounters, in detail

Tay's direction: "Continue and expand on it, give detailed descriptions." The colossal-encounter draft he pasted was Monster Hunter's *method* — which Tay approved as method on 2026-10-14 — wearing invented region names and unapproved tool names. Below, the method is kept and everything else is ours: our regions, our vocabulary (purification, Resonance Alignment, shed/gifted materials), and the Hunter's Terms governing every encounter. **A colossal encounter is a hunt that becomes a rescue:** the quarry is frenzied — by corruption, by a clogged vent, by pain — and the "fight" is surviving its territory long enough to vent the frenzy at its source. Nothing is slain. Encounter titles below are Lodge field names; species entries route to the Dex as proposals, never silent canon.

**Frenzy vents (the break-point mechanic, in canon terms).** A frenzied colossal carries its distress visibly: corruption-crusted horns, vents clogged with crystal growth, cracked armor plates humming at the wrong pitch. Targeting a vent with the matching Resonance Key and venting it — cracking the crust, clearing the vent, shattering the false plate — releases the pressure safely. Each vent cleared lowers the frenzy one stage (Fractured → Severe → Corrupted → Tainted → Clean). When the last vent clears, the creature is exhausted, not defeated — and the finish is the ceremony, not a blow: a full Resonance Alignment offered at colossal scale, heartbeat-synced through your whole party's partners. If it accepts, the study completes and the Dex entry opens. If it declines, it leaves in peace, and the Lodge records the study as complete anyway — you did your part.

**Field tools (PROPOSALS — Tay's call).** The pasted draft named an Acoustic Projector, Resonance Lances, and Soothe-Balm Launchers. In canon terms these are A.E.G.I.S. field attachments, and the names stay proposals until Tay rules: the **Projector** (ranged Key delivery — a Vibrance chord needs range against a flying quarry), the **Lance** (a focused Density/Harmony applicator for vent work at mid-range), and **balm canisters** (Purity applications thrown or launched, extending the balm language already in the world). No weapons exist behind these names: every tool delivers a Key, a balm, or a chord — never damage. Gear crafted from shed/gifted materials follows the standing economy: hazard-rated **field gear** (heat, cold, pressure, and spore ratings — proposal name "Resonance Exosuit" not adopted) that lets a hunter survive deeper Grounds, and Projector/Lance upgrades that widen frequency range and shorten chord charge.

### Study 1 — The Glacial Avalanche Titan *(Frostwind Peaks)*
**Habitat & sign.** A heavy-plated quadruped colossus whose migration path crosses three avalanche chutes. Sign: fresh slide scars, plate-shed fragments in the snowmelt (Glacial Shell Flakes — gifted material), and a silence the local EcoKin keep around its route.
**Engagement.** Its footsteps shed avalanches off the chutes — the terrain is the attack. You work the stalk *between* slides, reading which chute its weight is loading. When it charges, it commits: a plated avalanche with legs, unstoppable and unsteerable.
**Vents.** Both frost-armored forelegs carry corruption-crusted plate seams. Density Key strikes crack the frozen seams (never the leg) — each foreleg vented halves its charge power, because it can finally *feel* the ground properly again.
**The finish.** Vent both forelegs and its frenzy breaks into exhaustion in a steaming crater of its own melt. The Alignment is offered standing in that crater. Shed yield: shell flakes and a molted plate section, left in its tracks like a signature.

### Study 2 — The Frost-Bite Stalker *(Frostwind Peaks, night)*
**Habitat & sign.** A camouflaged airborne mammal that hunts the night grounds in absolute silence — no tracks in fresh snow (it lands only on rock), no calls. Sign is *negative*: a hunting route is a corridor where the small EcoKin have gone quiet in sequence.
**Engagement.** This study inverts the hunt: it stalks *you*. The engagement is audio discipline — its only tell is the half-second hush as its wingbeats stop before a glide-strike. Partners with hearing-linked Ecoforms earn their keep here.
**Vents.** It carries a single frenzy source: a crystal growth at the throat that's been *tuning it wrong*, driving it to hunt compulsively. A Vibrance flare (Projector, proposal) breaks its cloak mid-swoop, opening one clean window per pass to work the throat vent with Harmony.
**The finish.** When the growth shatters, the sound it makes is the first call it has given in years — the study completes when it answers your partner's call back. Shed yield: cloak-down feathers, gifted at the roost it returns to.

### Study 3 — The Dunescar Leviathan *(Red Canyons)*
**Habitat & sign.** A colossal segmented burrower moving through the canyon-floor sands. Sign: glassed sand ridges along its runs (heat-fused, Density-distorted), whirlpool pits where it feeds, and shed segment rings at the pit rims.
**Engagement.** Its breaches create sand vortexes that drag anything on open ground toward the pit. High rock is life: the stalk is a ledge-to-ledge route problem, timed to its breathing rhythm below the sand.
**Vents.** It has no armor to break — the vent is the breach itself. When it surfaces to feed, a Harmony Chord delivered into the open maw disrupts the burrowing rhythm and forces a full surface — the only moment its underside frenzy-crust (three crystal-clogged segments) is reachable for Purity work.
**The finish.** Three segments cleansed across three breaches, and the last surface is voluntary: it rises, holds, and lets the ceremony happen in the open. Shed yield: segment rings and glassed-sand lenses, left at the final pit.

### Study 4 — The Sun-Forge Biped *(Volcanic Ridge)*
**Habitat & sign.** A fast theropod-style colossus whose tail spikes superheat and launch volcanic debris. Sign: scorched spike-castings along its territory edge (Molten Horn Castings — gifted material), and impact craters still warm.
**Engagement.** Speed is the whole problem: tail-swipes arc debris in patterns, and it repositions faster than any other colossal in the studies. The stalk is short — the engagement is a dance at mid-range, reading the tail's heat shimmer for the launch tell.
**Vents.** The tail spikes themselves. A Torrent balm douses a heated spike-set, making the *false growth* (corruption crystal, not living spike) brittle; one heavy Density application shatters the growth clean. Three spike-sets carry growth; the living spikes are never touched, and A.E.G.I.S. paints the difference so the player always knows which is which.
**The finish.** With its tail honest again, its frenzy drains with the heat. It cools standing in its own crater field, and the Alignment offer happens at the temperature a partner can stand beside. Shed yield: horn castings and cooled spike sheaths.

### Study 5 — The Thorn-Weaver *(Deepwood canopy)*
**Habitat & sign.** An ancient spider-like EcoKin whose frequency-dampening webs span the canopy, muting the forest's resonance for a full kilometer — and trapping smaller wildlife in the bargain. Sign: the quiet itself, then web anchors humming flat, then the trapped.
**Engagement.** Vertical: the study is fought on the web-bridges and canopy lines, freeing trapped EcoKin as you go (each freed animal is a witness, and the Weaver notices). Its toxic vine spray patterns the bridges into a moving maze.
**Vents.** The structural anchors first — Aero applications sever anchor lines (the web falls; the Weaver descends to ground it can no longer dampen) — then the true source: a corrupted web-spinner, pulsing the dampening frequency. Purity Key, close range, while it decides whether you are a threat or the first help it has had in a century.
**The finish.** The spinner cleansed, the Weaver's first act is to re-string one bridge — *resonant* this time, tuned to the forest instead of against it. The Deepwood's sound comes back over about a minute of gameplay. That minute is the reward. Shed yield: resonant silk, gathered from the new bridge with its blessing.

### How the studies sit in the game
Each study is a Lodge-posted Field-Study Chronicle (Briefing → Tracking → Engagement → Vents → the Soothing), repeatable at higher frenzy starts for Hunt Circuit scoring. Studies gate on canon measurements — Lodge rank, regional restoration, Dex depth — and weave into the compendium: the Sun-Forge Biped's false growth is Morvane-pattern crystal (Dred-arc evidence), the Thorn-Weaver's forest borders Chronicle-era Deepwood, and every study's completion visibly changes its region (slides settle, the corridor sings again, the canopy re-tunes). The pasted draft's region names (Permafrost Shelf, Dust-Vein Steppes, Verdant Lattice) and its Sound-Well board were not used — the Lodge and our 13 regions carry all of it.
