## The frame

The Restoration Era is the main story: heal Rebearth, bind companions, uncover what humanity did to the fragments. **Titan Awakening is what comes after** — the post-Restoration loop for players who finished the story and want the world itself as the next challenge.

Titans are dormant world-scale Eco-Kin — mountain-spine, abyssal-trench, storm-system scale. They are **not collectible**. A Titan is never Bound, never owned, never put in a container; a companion is a relationship, and a Titan is a *place* that woke up. Awakening one changes the region permanently: weather patterns shift, new Growth Rite grounds open, corrupted zones begin purifying. This is the thesis at world scale — restoration you can stand inside.

## The awakening rite — four stages

Each Titan wakes through a multi-stage rite keyed to the **four Resonance Keys** (Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity — the Sovereign Resonance Arc's additive canon). No stage can be skipped; no stage can be bought.

1. **Stirring (Discovery).** A.E.G.I.S. forecast systems — the same front-tracking logic as the weather proposal — detect the Titan's resonance signature: a storm that never moves, a trench singing at 4 Hz, a mountain warm in winter. Tamers investigate through Discovery hunts. This is detective work, not a boss door.
2. **Approach (Consent).** The Binding ceremony's language scales up: the Titan is *invited*, never pulled. A forced awakening fractures the Echoheart — mechanically, that's the Shadow condition (a condition, never a category): a Titan woken by domination wakes **Shadowed**, and the raid becomes a purification instead of a partnership. The choice is the content.
3. **The Rite (Multi-Tamer).** Three movements, each gated by one or two Resonance Keys:
   - *Vibrance + Density:* the proving — combat phases against the Titan's Warden-forms (Rage/Alpha/Blight/Hollow encounter states apply at this scale; a Blighted Titan phase is the Shadow-condition warning).
   - *Harmony:* the attunement — participating Tamers must synchronize Kindling rhythms; Anima-Link strain feeds back into every participant, so the raid has a real shared stamina economy. Mismanaged strain staggers the whole rite.
   - *Purity:* the offering — each Tamer contributes shed/gifted bodily resources from willingly released duplicates (the revised release economy, never sacrifice). The Titan reads what the region's Tamers have given back to the world.
4. **Aftermath (Living change).** An awakened Titan is a permanent regional system: new weather, new rites, new wild encounters, a sanctuary that defends itself (Sanctuary defense, retained). A Shadowed Titan, once purified, leaves a *scar* that becomes future content — endings heal imperfectly, never a perfect Purity reset.

## Cataclysm-class raid structure

- **Scale:** multi-Tamer (the duel circuit's party/raid scaffolding extends here). Solo Tamers can Stir and Approach; the Rite itself requires a band.
- **Difficulty comes from coordination, not numbers:** Anima-Link strain is the raid's shared resource. This is the anti-cliché — the boss doesn't just hit harder, it tests whether the band can hold resonance together.
- **Rewards:** never the Titan. Rewards are regional: new Growth Rite grounds, Catalyst items, star-tier resources (Yellow→Red), Titan-marked gear patterns for the Sovereign Forge. The Titan *remembers* the Tamers who woke it — a reputation line, not a possession.
- **Failure:** a failed rite doesn't kill the Titan (nothing is killed — Shadow Beasts are walked back/purified, per the trailer script). Failure deepens dormancy; the region's forecast goes quiet for a season. The next band tries differently.
