# PvP — Care Consequence & the Arena (2026-10-20)

Status: CONCEPT proposal, Tay's veto pending. How Echohearts' care systems
survive contact with competition — the arena must never reward treating a
partner as ammunition.

## Care consequence in the arena

- **Anima-Link is live in duels.** Strain your partner takes feeds back to
  you, exactly as in the wild. A Tamer who red-lines a partner every match
  feels it in their own stamina — the bidirectional rule has no arena
  exception.
- **Strain persists between matches.** A partner who yielded at high strain
  enters the next duel tired (CompanionCare conditions apply: weary,
  shaken). Rest, the Infirmary, and Campfire Care are between-round play,
  not menus to skip.
- **Kindling gates the Climax.** The Resonant Climax (purification finish)
  is available only at healthy trust — you cannot finisher-move with a
  partner who doesn't believe in you. Care is a competitive stat.

## Arena rules

- **Yield is always legal, always free.** Either side may yield at any
  moment; the match ends, nobody loses anything but the match.
- **Nothing is taken from the loser.** No loot, no resources, no partners.
  Circuit standing moves; possessions don't.
- **No Binding in the arena.** An opponent's partner is never on the
  table, in any mode, under any wager.
- **Purification only.** Finishers purify and end the contest; there are
  no fatalities, in PvP as everywhere in Rebearth.
- **The echo rule (Option A).** Fighting a rival packet never harms the
  real partner it echoes — the snapshot absorbs the duel, the living
  EcoKin never knows.

## Why this is the design

Every other creature game makes competition the place where the fiction
goes to die. Rebearth's thesis is the opposite: the better you care, the
stronger you fight. The arena is where that thesis gets proven in public.
