# Boss Progression — The World's Trials, In Order (2026-10-16)

**Status:** CONCEPT + code (`Source/Echohearts/Public|Private/Progression/`) · IMPLEMENTED / NOT COMPILED / NOT TESTED

## The idea (Terraria's method, Echohearts' soul)
In Terraria, bosses form an ordered progression — some optional, some required — and defeating them changes the world itself. Here, fourteen bosses already in the game gate the story, and threshold victories change Rebearth: skies clear, the land cools, the blight recedes, new regions open. A boss may only be challenged once every **required** boss before it has fallen. Optional bosses never block anything — they reward the curious.

## The eras of the world
Dawn of Restoration → **Skies Clear** → **Land Cools** → **World Breathes Easier** → **Restoration Era**. Each era is a promise the restoration is working — and each is visible in the world, not just a flag.

## What this means in play
You always know the next trial and the next horizon. Beat the Blackened Sky and you can fly the storm belt. Cool the Ashfall Spires and the fire-scarred regions start healing. Bring down the Fractured Apex and the whole world exhales. And through it all, the societies are watching — until they stop watching and start coming.
