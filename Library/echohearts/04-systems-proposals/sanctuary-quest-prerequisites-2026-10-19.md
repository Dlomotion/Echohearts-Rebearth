# Sanctuary Quest Prerequisites — Earned Trust, Earned Services (2026-10-19)

**Status:** CONCEPT. All names vetoable. Routes to 04_Systems. No new Bibles, Dexes, or structures.

## The idea
Residents arrive when the Sanctuary proves itself (existing rule: nobody comes to a Sanctuary that isn't healing). The quest-prerequisite layer is the second half of that promise: **arrival is only the beginning.** Each resident offers a quest chain. Completing it unlocks their deeper services and their expansion tiers. Nothing is bought with currency — everything is earned through restoration, trust, and rites.

## Prerequisite vocabulary (canon only)
Quests gate on things the game already measures — never on new currencies or invented stats:
- **Kindling %** (trust with a specific partner or across all partners)
- **Growth Rite** completion (per partner)
- **Dex identities** registered (count)
- **Region restoration %** (per region)
- **Boss progression** (the 14-boss order; world eras: Skies Clear, Land Cools, World Breathes Easier, Restoration Era)
- **Corruption walked back** (specific Corruption forms purified; blight cleansed)
- **Ecology accomplishments** (the existing accomplishment system)

## The six chains

### Mender — "Hands That Remember"
*Arrives: your first Growth Rite.*
1. **Tend the Wounded** — Ease Link Strain for three different partners in one day. *Prereq: 2 partners with Kindling ≥ 40%.* → Unlocks: Heal Companion II.
2. **The Long Night** — Keep a partner above 50% stamina through a full Blight-zone crossing. *Prereq: quest 1; any region ≥ 30% restored.* → Unlocks: Mass Ease (party-wide strain relief).
3. **What the Link Carries** — Witness a partner's strain feedback and choose rest over pushing (a scripted story beat, not a fail state). *Prereq: quest 2; Anima-Link attunement tier 2.* → Unlocks: Mender's expansion tier (field hospital; residents recover faster).

### Artificer — "What the Shed Leaves Behind"
*Arrives: your first shed/gifted resources change hands.*
1. **First Forging** — Craft three items from shed/gifted resources only. *Prereq: 100 units of any resource (first star).* → Unlocks: Concord Catalyst forging.
2. **Star by Star** — Reach Silver star tier in two resources. *Prereq: quest 1.* → Unlocks: Harmonic Accord armor crafting (one piece per completion thereafter).
3. **The Sovereign's Tools** — Forge a Concord Catalyst and use it in a Sovereign Concord rite. *Prereq: quest 2; Concord Fusion unlocked.* → Unlocks: Artificer's expansion tier (master forge; Gold+ star recipes).

### Chronicler — "The Memory of Rebearth"
*Arrives: 25 Dex identities registered.*
1. **Lineage** — Register 50 Dex identities. *Prereq: arrival.* → Unlocks: D.A.H.L.I.A. deep consults (form registry access).
2. **The First Stories** — Complete the opening region's ecology accomplishments. *Prereq: quest 1.* → Unlocks: Lineage Records (view any partner's full history).
3. **What We Did** — Recover a tampering-site record (ties to the DNA-tampering storyline). *Prereq: quest 2; boss 10 (Society Herald) fallen — the societies move openly.* → Unlocks: Chronicler's expansion tier (archive hall; canon-lore codex).

### Bondkeeper — "The Shape of Trust"
*Arrives: any partner reaches 80% Kindling.*
1. **Eighty Percent** — Raise a second partner to 80% Kindling. *Prereq: arrival.* → Unlocks: Kindling Guidance II (trust-gain counsel).
2. **The Hard Conversation** — Resolve a partner's Hollow-adjacent strain through care, not items (a Bondkeeper-guided story beat). *Prereq: quest 1.* → Unlocks: Care Remedy recipes.
3. **Kindling Beyond Measure** — Reach 100% Kindling with any partner. *Prereq: quest 2.* → Unlocks: Bondkeeper's expansion tier (sanctuary grove; all partners gain trust faster while home).

### Purifier — "Walk Them Back"
*Arrives: your first Corruption form walked back.*
1. **Three Walked Back** — Purify three of the twelve Corruption forms. *Prereq: arrival.* → Unlocks: Purification Aid II.
2. **Cleanse the Blight** — Cleanse blight taint from a full territory. *Prereq: quest 1; region ≥ 50% restored.* → Unlocks: Blight-warding for the Sanctuary.
3. **The Thirteenth** — Face the truth that one Corruption form cannot be walked back by force (story beat; sets up Shadow Beast Prime). *Prereq: quest 2; boss 12 (GROVANTIS) fallen.* → Unlocks: Purifier's expansion tier (purification spire; region-wide blight resistance).

### Scout — "Maps of the Healing World"
*Arrives: any region reaches 50% restoration.*
1. **Eyes Up** — Mark three corrupted territories. *Prereq: arrival.* → Unlocks: Region Intel II (spawn/state forecasts).
2. **Flight Paths** — Chart a safe flight route through a cleared sky. *Prereq: quest 1; boss 3 (Blightstorm) fallen — Skies Clear.* → Unlocks: flight-route fast travel (ride/flight only — never teleportation).
3. **Beyond the Map** — Scout the edge of a sealed region. *Prereq: quest 2; boss 13 (Shadow Beast Prime) fallen — World Breathes Easier.* → Unlocks: Scout's expansion tier (sky-watch; early warning of Society movements).

## Rules the layer obeys
- **No quest fails for combat losses.** Quests are about care, restoration, and trust — a lost duel never fails a quest, it just delays it.
- **Prerequisites are visible, never hidden.** The game always shows what a quest needs and how close you are.
- **Eras gate, never grind.** The biggest gates are story moments (boss falls, regions heal), not repetition counts.
- **Everything reversible.** Quest rewards unlock services; they never lock content away permanently.

## What this means in play
Your Sanctuary fills with people because the world is healing — and then those people ask things of you that make you better at healing it. The loop tightens: restore → arrive → quest → deepen → restore further.
