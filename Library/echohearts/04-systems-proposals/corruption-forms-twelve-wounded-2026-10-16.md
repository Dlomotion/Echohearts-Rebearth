## The loop
1. **Wounded place** → wild ones living there accumulate corruption (blight zones, domination ruins, tampering labs, weapon caches).
2. **At 50**, the Corruption form manifests — stats redistribute toward force, away from harmony. Essence expression slides toward Shade.
3. **Encounters** — the tamer meets them around the map: fight, avoid, or purify. Purifying the creature is the first step to healing its territory (restoration loop).
4. **Purification** — care, restoration work, A.E.G.I.S. rites walk corruption back down; the form falls away and the original stats return exactly.
5. **At 100** — Echoheart fracture. The Shadow condition takes hold. Never killed, never a new species: lost, until someone brings them back through a major rite.

## Tamer-side rules
- A bonded partner left in a blight zone corrupts like anything else — neglect has teeth (ties to Kindling/Care Consequence).
- Forcing commands through a strained link feeds corruption. The bond keeps score.
- A fractured partner cannot be summoned until purified — the call would go somewhere that isn't them anymore.
