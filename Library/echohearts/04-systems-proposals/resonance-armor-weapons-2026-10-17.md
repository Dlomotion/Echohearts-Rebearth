# Resonance Armor & Weapon Classes (2026-10-17)

**Status:** CONCEPT proposal → `04_Systems` (reversible — Tay vetoes everything below)
**Frame:** Harmonic vs Discordant. Everything in this universe revolves around Frequency; the Tamer's gear is no exception.

## The Harmonic Accord (Resonance armor set)

A four-piece armor set worn by the Frequency Tamer, each piece attuned to one of the four Resonance Keys — Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity. The armor does not generate power; it *conducts* the Tamer's own frequency and steadies the Anima-Link. It is Harmonic-aligned by construction.

### The four pieces
1. **Pulseweave Vest** (Vibrance) — Woven from shed Brightfilament. Essence abilities strike harder, but every strike pays slightly more link strain. Power has a price, and the vest never hides it.
2. **Densplate Guard** (Density) — Plate grown from shed Carapace shards. Blunts the feedback the Anima-Link carries back into the Tamer — EcoKin damage still reaches you (the link is bidirectional, always), but the edge is taken off.
3. **Chordmail Weave** (Harmony) — Interlaced harmonic filament. Link strain accumulates more slowly, and Kindling trust gains from care come slightly faster. The quiet piece; Tamers who rush never appreciate it until they need it.
4. **Clearward Helm** (Purity) — Set with a cleansed Glaze lens. Resists corruption buildup when walking blighted ground and strengthens purification rites. The helm hums faintly near tainted zones — a warning, not a weapon.

### Set bonus — Sovereign Resonance
Wearing all four pieces at once attunes them into brief synchronization: for a short window after a perfectly-timed ability (Harmonic Momentum at full), strain costs drop to nearly nothing. It is synchronization, not a fifth element — the four Keys agreeing with each other.

### Crafting
Forged by the Artificer resident from **shed or gifted** bodily resources only — never from a harmed partner. Each piece requires resources tied to its Key (Brightfilament for Vibrance, Carapace for Density, etc.).

### Discordant mirror (proposal)
Discordant Tamers — those who force domination — wear a corrupted mirror called the **Dissonance Rig**. It inverts every Accord effect: more power, no strain warning, and it *feeds* on the wearer's link until it frays. Players cannot craft it; it exists so you can recognize what the Accord is *not*.

## Weapon classes (4, original)

Wielded by the Frequency Tamer alongside their deployed EcoKin. All weapons pay Anima-Link strain per strike — the armory is an extension of the bond, not a replacement for it.

1. **Chordblades** (twin short blades) — Fast, close, rhythmic. Each alternating strike lands slightly faster than the last, rewarding sustained tempo. Builds Harmonic Momentum faster than any other class. For Tamers who fight *with* the pulse.
2. **Pulselance** (long spear) — Reach and precision. Thrusts can be charged; a fully-charged thrust released exactly on the link pulse pierces through adds to reach the true target. For Tamers who wait for the right heartbeat.
3. **Echocaster** (wrist-mounted projector) — Ranged frequency bolts. Fires in short chains — and chains across *essences*: a Torrent bolt followed by a Voltic bolt triggers an Overcharge Flash that staggers. The natural partner of the Essence Chaining system.
4. **Denshammer** (two-handed maul) — Slow, devastating, honest. Every swing costs heavy strain and shakes the arena; fully-charged slams create shockwaves that knock back corrupted adds. For Tamers who end arguments.

### Crafting & growth
Weapons are forged and reforged by the Artificer from shed resources. Reforging re-rolls a weapon's resonance stats — never its identity. A Chordblade remains a Chordblade; the Echoprint rule holds for gear as it does for Kin: nothing you love is silently replaced.
