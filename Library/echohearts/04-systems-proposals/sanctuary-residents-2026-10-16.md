# Sanctuary Residents — Rebearth's People, Coming Home (2026-10-16)

**Status:** CONCEPT + code (`Source/Echohearts/Public|Private/Sanctuary/`) · IMPLEMENTED / NOT COMPILED / NOT TESTED

## The idea (Terraria's method, Echohearts' soul)
In Terraria, friendly NPCs move into player-built houses when achievement conditions are met, then sell items and offer services. Here, the people of Rebearth move into **your Sanctuary** as restoration milestones are reached. Nobody comes to a Sanctuary that isn't healing — every arrival is earned.

## The six founding residents

| Resident | Arrives when | Services |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Mender** | Your first Growth Rite is completed | Heal Companion, Ease Link Strain |
| **Artificer** | Your first shed/gifted resources change hands | Craft from Shed Resources, Forge Concord Catalyst |
| **Chronicler** | 25 Dex identities registered | Consult D.A.H.L.I.A., Read Lineage Record |
| **Bondkeeper** | Any partner reaches 80% trust | Kindling Guidance, Care Remedy |
| **Purifier** | Your first Corruption form is walked back | Aid Purification, Cleanse Blight Taint |
| **Scout** | Any region reaches 50% restoration | Region Intel, Mark Corrupted Territory |

## Sanctuary defense
When the Sanctuary is threatened, residents defend it — themselves and the tamer beside them. Their defense grows as restoration % rises: a Sanctuary that has healed more of the world is harder to break. (This is where the Society War arc bites — when the secret societies move openly, your people stand with you.)

## What this means in play
Restore the world and it fills your home with people. Each one opens a service no machine can give you: the Mender's hands, the Artificer's forge, the Chronicler's memory, the Bondkeeper's counsel, the Purifier's courage, the Scout's maps. And when trouble comes, they don't hide — they stand.
