# Concord Fusion Progression — Growth Rites, Mutation & DNA Paths (2026-10-16)

**Status:** CONCEPT + code (`Source/Echohearts/Public|Private/Fusion/`) · IMPLEMENTED / NOT COMPILED / NOT TESTED

## The hard rule
Concord Fusion is allowed **only for registered pairs**, and it **never kills or consumes the parents**. Both parents enter dormant resonance inside the combined being. Unfusing wakes them exactly as they were — same Echoprints, same trust, same selves. The fused EcoKin is a stable combined identity with its own Dex ID (allocated above the wild range, starting at 100001) and its own lineage record. Unfused records are archived, never deleted.

## Growth Rite path: three stages
A combined being must prove its two halves can live as one before it grows.

1. **Attunement** — the parents' rhythms learn each other's tempo. Requires: both parents biologically mature, the fusion's own Kindling (trust) at 40%+, 1 ecology accomplishment, a Concord Catalyst.
2. **Harmonization** — two natures, one stride. Requires: fusion trust 65%+, 3 ecology accomplishments, a Concord Catalyst.
3. **Sovereign Concord** — the combined being stands on its own: a sovereign self, not a sum. Requires: fusion trust 85%+, 5 ecology accomplishments, a Concord Catalyst, plus D.A.H.L.I.A. validation if DNA stability is below 60.

## Mutation path
The fusion inherits mutation nodes from **each** parent, plus **one unique fusion node** that exists only because these two became one. Every trigger is conditional — never random. Fusions with Nature lineage get the strictest gate: every mutation demands its explicit condition, every time, no exceptions.

## DNA path
Every fusion carries a lineage record: both parents' Dex IDs and Echoprints, inherited essence expressions (nine Essences only), and a **DNA stability score** (0–100). Each Synth-Origin parent costs 25 stability — lab-born lines carry echo instability. Below 60, the fusion may not attempt Growth Rites until **D.A.H.L.I.A.** reviews and validates the lineage. Validation steadies the line; it never rewrites it.

## What this means in play
Find two partners willing to attempt Concord. Register the pair. Fuse them with a Concord Catalyst. Raise the new being's trust like any partner — it is someone, not a tool. Walk it through Attunement, Harmonization, and Sovereign Concord. If the line ever needs to end, unfuse: both parents come home.
