# The Sky Isles — What Rose With the Healing (2026-10-19)

**Status:** CONCEPT — Tay's canon call ("create the Sky isles," 2026-10-19). All names vetoable. Routes to 02_World + 04_Systems. No new Bibles, Dexes, or structures.

## Canon: what they are
The Sky Isles are a floating archipelago above Rebearth — stone, soil, forest, and water held aloft by **resonance lift**: dense flora-crystal formations in the isles' bedrock resonate with the planet's restored regions, and the harmony literally holds them up. This is the thesis made geography: **the isles rose as Rebearth healed.** Old stories say they slept underground during the worst of the blight; as restoration spreads, more of them surface into the sky. The archipelago is still growing — it is a visible promise, not a finished place.

## Reaching them (canon travel rules hold)
- **Gate:** the isles become reachable after **Blightstorm (boss 3)** falls — *Skies Clear*, flight routes open. Before that, the corrupted sky is impassable.
- **How:** flight or ride, never teleportation. You need a flying partner with the Kindling and stamina for the ascent — the climb itself is a rite of trust.
- **First arrival:** scripted story beat. Your partner strains against the updraft; the Anima-Link carries it; the isles catch you. Nobody teleports into wonder.

## Skyhold — the sky base (vetoable name)
**Skyhold** is your sanctuary annex in the clouds — not a replacement for your ground Sanctuary, an extension of it. Residents rotate up by quest: the Artificer keeps a sky-forge here, the Scout keeps the sky-watch, the Mender a cloud-garden for recovering partners. Skyhold has its own defense (it matters when the societies move openly — the sky is the last place they look, until it isn't).

Skyhold is where the **crafting table tiers** live (see below). It is also where flying partners rest: Kindling gains are quickened for partners stabled here — the sky rewards those who belong to it.

## The crafting table tiers (our names, vetoable)
Three tiers, one per depth of mastery. Every recipe uses **shed/gifted resources only** — the no-sacrifice rule reaches the sky. Tiers gate on **resource star tiers** (Yellow→Bronze→Silver→Gold→Platinum→Purple→Red), not on currency.

| Tier | Name (vetoable) | Unlocks | Gated by |
|------|-----------------|---------|----------|
| 1 | **Warden's Bench** | Basic gear: travel harnesses, link-charms, strain salves | Reach the Sky Isles; Artificer quest 1 done |
| 2 | **Chordwork Atelier** | Harmonic Accord armor pieces; weapon frames (Chordblades, Pulselance, Echocaster, Denshammer) | Silver star in 2+ resources; Artificer quest 2 done |
| 3 | **Sovereign Forge** | Sovereign Resonance set bonuses; Concord Catalysts; purification instruments | Gold star in 2+ resources; Artificer quest 3 done; Skyhold restoration ≥ 50% |

**Crafting rules:**
- No recipe consumes a sentient partner or anything taken by force. Willing Release yields shed/gifted resources; the forge only takes what was given.
- Star tiers are per-resource: every 100 units grants a star. Higher tiers don't replace lower ones — a Bronze recipe stays Bronze forever.
- Masterwork: crafting at max Kindling with the partner whose shed resource you're using grants a **Resonant** quality tag — small, never game-breaking, always personal.

## What this means in play
Beat the storm, earn the sky. The isles are the game's second horizon — a place that exists because you healed the world below it, with a forge that turns what your partners freely gave into the gear that carries you further. And when the societies come for the ground, you'll already have somewhere above it.
