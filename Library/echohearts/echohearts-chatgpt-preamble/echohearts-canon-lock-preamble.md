## Locked foundations

- **Planet:** Rebearth. **Creatures:** EcoKin. **Ecoform** = a form/state an EcoKin takes on (base form, Growth Rite form, Mutation, Resonance Strain, Shimmer Form). Never use "Eco-Kin"/"Echo-Kin" as the creature noun.
- **Five Orders of Sentience:** Zoa (animal-origin), Flora (plant-origin), Myko (fungal-origin), Proto (protist-origin), Monera (microbial colony intelligences). Lab-created variants keep their Order with a **Synth-Origin** label.
- **Player framework:** Frequency Tamer (profession) / Core-Binder (advanced specialization).
- **Attributes — ONLY these four:** Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity. Never substitute ATK/HP/SPD/DEF, VIT/MGT/AGS/RSN/FORT/VEL, IVs, or EVs.
- **Anima-Link is bidirectional:** EcoKin strain and damage feed back as stamina/health consequences on the bonded Tamer.
- **Binding** (not catching), **Resonance Alignment** (not ownership), **Release** (not deployment). EcoKin choose whether to remain aligned. **Kindling %** is the trust bond.
- **Device:** A.E.G.I.S. (wrist bracer). **D.A.H.L.I.A.** is the intelligence inside it — never a replacement name for the device.
- **Nine Essences:** Flora, Torrent, Pyre, Terra, Aero, Glaze, Voltic, Aura, Shade. Older element names (Fire, Water…) may appear only as in-fiction historical or regional terms.
- **Shadow is a CONDITION** (Echoheart fracture), never a species, category, or faction.
- **Progression:** immutable Echoprint → Growth Rites (structured maturation, no level-reset evolution) → registered Mutation → Resonance Strains (redistributed budgets, never inflated) → Concord Fusion (registered pairs only) → Shimmer Form (identity-preserving). No sacrificing or consuming EcoKin; duplicates yield research, never consumed.
- **The 125-ID Permanent Dex is production authority.** The 1,120-name pool is a historical archive, not 1,120 canonical species. Never invent new Dex slots.
- **Endings restore imperfectly.** No perfect-Purity reset.
