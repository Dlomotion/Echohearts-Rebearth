## Filing paths (verified costs, 2026-10-08)

### Path A — Statutory conversion (recommended in the research note)
1. **EOA 205 — Statement of Conversion**, filing fee **$100** (expedited $200).
   Submit in duplicate. Payment by cashier's check, certified check, money order,
   or Illinois attorney's/CPA's check — **no personal checks**.
2. **NFP 102.10 — Articles of Incorporation (not-for-profit)**, filed *together*
   with the EOA 205: fee **$50** (10 business days) or **$75** (24-hour service).
3. Illinois conversions governed by the Entity Omnibus Act (805 ILCS 215/1102).
4. **Minimum 3 directors** (805 ILCS 105/108.10) — need not be Illinois residents;
   3–7 may be listed on the Articles.
5. Charitable-purpose wording and IRS dissolution clause must live in the Articles
   (paper NFP 102.10 accommodates provisions the online flow can't). Purpose must
   be genuinely charitable — **not** "raise money to develop Echohearts" (private-
   benefit problem).

**Path A total state fees: $150** ($100 + $50), or $175 with 24-hour Articles.

### Path B — Fresh incorporation + wind down the LLC
File NFP 102.10 alone ($50/$75) as a brand-new nonprofit corporation, then
dissolve or repurpose the LLC separately. Simpler paperwork per filing, but two
entities to manage during transition and the LLC's history doesn't carry over.
