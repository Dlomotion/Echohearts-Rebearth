## Canon triage: keep vs. violation

**KEPT (methods, adapted to our rules):**
- Vertical strata progression (surface → deep core) — method, fine.
- Core-Extractor upgrades 1×1 → 3×3 → 5×5 — fine.
- Echo-Resonance Radar — fine, reads as A.E.G.I.S. scanner flavor.
- Heart-Drive class slots (Vanguard / Neural Streamer) — fine as a gear system.
- Echo Meter as a recharging resource — fine. Note: this is NOT Anima-Link
  strain; it's a separate tool-resource for this game mode.
- Three crafting terminals — fine as proposals.
- Mineable background walls revealing starfields/power grids — fine.
- Thrusters for low gravity — fine: travel is flight or ride.

**REFUSED (canon locks, with grounds):**
- **Short-range teleports — refused.** Lock: travel is flight or ride, NEVER
  teleportation. No exceptions for abilities.
- **Phasing through thin walls — refused.** Teleport-adjacent; same lock.
  Redesign instead: Echo-Shielding becomes a directional barrier plus brief
  immunity to *damage* (not movement through walls).
- **The blueprint has no Eco-Kin in it — flagged.** The thesis is relationships
  with sentient Eco-Kin, not replacing them with gear. Fix: Eco-Kin nests live
  in the strata, the radar can find them, and befriended Eco-Kin grant the
  Heart-Drive attunements — the systems tie together instead of competing.

## Starter gear — stats & recipes

All stats use the four attributes: Vibrance / Density / Harmony / Purity.
Tier: surface materials (Obsidian, Amethyst, Data-Root). All crafted at the
Resonance Crucible.

- **Core-Extractor Mk.I** — 1×1 mining field. Vibrance 8 (dig speed),
  Density 12 (tool health). Recipe: 6 Obsidian + 4 Data-Root.
- **Echo-Resonance Radar (basic)** — Harmony 8 (scan clarity; reveals
  valuables through 1 wall layer). Recipe: 4 Amethyst + 4 Data-Root.
- **Rootweave Suit** — Density 6, Harmony 4, Purity 6. Recipe: 8 Data-Root.
- **Vanguard Drive (Rustic)** — tanky class attunement. Density +10,
  Vibrance +4. Recipe: 8 Obsidian + 6 Data-Root.
- **Streamer Drive (Rustic)** — long-range class attunement. Vibrance +8,
  Harmony +6. Recipe: 6 Amethyst + 6 Data-Root.

Upgrade path: Mk.II (3×3) needs Echo-Stone from the Chrono-Fractures —
which is exactly what the first boss is blocking.

## First boss: Gnashtail, the Tainted Burrower (name vetoable)

A burrowing Eco-Kin fractured by Void Decay seeping up from the Absolute
Void. It nests in the Chrono-Fractures and its corruption field blocks
descent. The fight ends in purification — never a kill.

- **Phase 1 — Dust Lines.** Burrow charges telegraphed by dust lines on the
  surface. Echo-Shielding (barrier) or a sidestep beats them.
- **Phase 2 — Glitch Echoes (50%).** The fracture deepens: time-glitch
  echoes of its charges linger and detonate late. Radar marks the real one.
- **Phase 3 — The Shudder (25%).** It stops attacking and shudders — the
  fracture is visible. Swap the extractor for the Purification Pulse (radar
  overcharge) and hold the circle while it resists. This is a rite, not an
  execution.

**Loot (shed/gifted — the no-kill rule holds):**
- Shed Echo-Stone ×12 — feeds the star-tier resource track.
- Tainted Slag ×8 — crafting material, purifies into building blocks.
- **Vanguard Drive (True)** — gifted by the healed Gnashtail, not taken.
  Density +18, Vibrance +8, and it attunes the Core-Extractor Mk.II
  schematic (3×3 field).
- Radar schematic: Echo-Stone attunement — see through 3 wall layers.
- The Chrono-Fractures descent unlocks.

After the fight, Gnashtail becomes a nest-ally: its burrow is a safe
rest point, and befriending it is the blueprint's missing Eco-Kin beat,
answered.
