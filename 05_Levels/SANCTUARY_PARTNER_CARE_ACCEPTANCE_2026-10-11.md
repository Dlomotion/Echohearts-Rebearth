## Playable beats

1. In an existing approved Sanctuary test space, the player invites an eligible existing Eco-Kin to Garden Care. The partner may accept, decline or defer. Show the response before reserving workstation/materials; never penalize refusal or reduce Kindling as an obedience score.
2. An accepted partner tends a planted bed with species-appropriate movement. A.E.G.I.S. shows the approved task and current availability from typed authoritative state; no raw inventory map is exposed to the widget.
3. An authored care threshold is reached. Pause the task safely and release its reservation. Anima-Link strain consequences remain bidirectional under existing rules; this scenario adds no invented numerical damage or fatigue formula. The player can rest alongside the partner, supply approved care, or postpone the work.
4. Resume only after current eligibility and willingness are rechecked. Resume continues the recorded transaction stage; it cannot duplicate inputs, output, rewards or Training credit.
5. The bed's restoration feedback is observable, but task repetition does not grind Training. The eventual Stage 07 milestone requires the established functioning-sanctuary story conditions and authorized unlock transaction.

## Acceptance cases

- Declined invitation leaves costs, task slot and Kindling unchanged.
- Two players cannot reserve the same task/partner concurrently; server resolves ownership and eligibility.
- Care pause releases the workstation once, keeps approved recovery state, and produces no phantom completion.
- Retry/reconnect cannot duplicate material deductions, output, cue playback or Training credit.
- Reload preserves care/strain and rechecks consent; offline time alone does not invent recovery completion.
- Destroyed/unloaded partner or workstation invalidates the invitation/reservation safely.
- Missing producer state remains unavailable, distinct from valid zero strain; UI rebinding after travel removes old subscriptions.
- Muting audio/reduced motion/controller-only navigation still communicates every decision and consequence.
