# Theater Network Reconciliation

Status: **APPROVED-PENDING**
Implementation: **NOT YET VERIFIED**

## Canon role

**Theaters** are authored or system-assisted challenge spaces tied to restoration, investigation, rescue, environmental stabilization, combat and world-state change. They must belong to the current Rebearth campaign and cannot become a disconnected grind layer.

Main campaign play remains **real-time third-person action**. Tactical grid/turn-based combat belongs only to approved Resonance Arena / Harmony Circuit / EchoDeck simulation contexts.

## Required Theater fields

Every Theater must define:

- TheaterID
- approved region / landmark
- prerequisite and entrance trigger
- portal/route relationship
- unique biome identity
- primary world wound or threat
- player actions
- puzzle or traversal mechanic
- combat encounter plan
- restoration/rescue objective
- boss or mini-boss logic when needed
- ethical alternative where relevant
- completion proof
- visible Rebearth delta
- Eco-Kin/NPC/faction consequence
- reward/unlock
- save flags
- failure/partial state
- successor mission
- intended danger/level tier

## System corrections from historical drafts

- **Vibrance, Density, Harmony and Purity** remain the public gameplay-stat language.
- Historical Hertz/Velocity, VIT/RSN/SYNC and similar stat packs are internal/reference-only unless separately approved for Arena simulation.
- Remove `Sanctuary Worker` as an Eco-Kin role. Use **Sanctuary Aptitude / Partner Assist** with consent, care, refusal and fatigue rules.
- Do not place sentient Eco-Kin in generic storage. Use Kindling Haven, Bond Registry, habitat/care spaces and approved party-management abstractions.
- Do not award extracted `Core Shards` or other harvested sentient parts. Use approved non-sentient rewards such as Resonance Shards, Echo-Dew, Purity Resin, purified materials, relics, research records, cosmetics or route unlocks.
- Mutation/evolution cannot be forced as a dungeon reward. Growth Rites/adaptations require compatibility, willingness, Harmony, Purity and story/system rules.
- Bosses need mechanics, counterplay, world consequence and restoration choices rather than serving only as health walls.

## Portal puzzle rule

Portal puzzles may support route discovery, boss weak-point access, split-path encounters, timed rescue, escape and secret exploration. They must never allow an unvalidated client teleport or create impossible save/reload states.

## QA gates

Every Theater must be checked for soft locks, portal destination failures, collision/pathing, puzzle readability, boss phase clarity, save/reload recovery, accessibility, controller navigation, co-op state, performance and world-state correctness.
