# Shattered Frontier — First Sanctuary Mission

Status: **APPROVED-PENDING**

Working mission ID: `SF_01_FIRST_SANCTUARY`

## Placement

Early Vespera Basin, after the player survives the Whispering Glade opening and before the Sanctuary becomes a permanent hub.

## Purpose

Introduce Kaelen “Kale,” Sherlock Hound, Sanctuary philosophy, voluntary Eco-Kin cooperation, first construction/restoration loop, and the difference between shelter and ownership.

## Trigger

The player follows Sherlock Hound’s warning bark to a damaged clearing where an old restoration site has been half-buried by Blight-scarred soil and a collapsed listening post.

## Opening scene

Kaelen is trying to lift a broken timber frame while Sherlock Hound circles the perimeter, repeatedly stopping at one contaminated patch of soil.

**KAELEN**
“You can stack walls anywhere. That don’t make it a Sanctuary.”

Sherlock growls softly at the poisoned ground, then looks toward the player.

**KAELEN**
“He’s telling you before I do. Something under that dirt ain’t right.”

A.E.G.I.S. identifies a damaged Heartroot feeder line and a frightened wild Eco-Kin hiding beyond the clearing.

## Objectives

1. Scan the clearing with A.E.G.I.S.
2. Follow Sherlock Hound’s trail markers without entering the contaminated soil.
3. Locate clean water and safe soil.
4. Clear debris from the old Listening Post.
5. Build a temporary **Heartroot Shelter** foundation.
6. Place a **Purity Well** intake away from the contaminated patch.
7. Approach the frightened Eco-Kin using the established calm/space rules.
8. Choose **Bond / Release / Defer / Treat** when the Calm Window opens.
9. Restore the Listening Post.
10. Sit at the new Sanctuary fire with Kaelen and record the first Sanctuary EcoDex note.

## Visible world change

The clearing changes from gray Blight dust and unstable debris to a modest functioning refuge: clean water begins flowing, insects and small wild Eco-Kin cautiously return, the Listening Post emits a gentle pulse, and new safe paths appear on the A.E.G.I.S. map.

## Failure / partial state

The mission cannot permanently fail, but careless play may frighten the wild Eco-Kin away, contaminate the first water placement, or force a rebuild. Kaelen comments on the player’s choices and the Eco-Kin may return later if trust conditions improve.
