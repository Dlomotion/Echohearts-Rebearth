## Playable beat
The newly bonded partner stands beside the player. A.E.G.I.S. illuminates a single link on the hip-mounted Eco-Device; D.A.H.L.I.A. asks the player to check how the partner is feeling before making a call.

The player offers rest. The partner leaves along an authored safe transition, and the field indicator becomes empty. No charge or strain is added by recall. The player then sends one invitation through the Anima-Link, sees its projected cost and waits for an answer.

The partner may approach if willing and safe. If it has withdrawn to heal, the call stays unanswered with a clear care explanation. The lesson completes through demonstrating a valid request and respecting its outcome, not forcing a successful arrival or repeatedly draining a device.

| Outcome | Player feedback | State/cost |
|---|---|---|
| Successful invitation | One link brightens; partner arrives at approved safe point | One active partner, successful-call charge/strain only |
| Healing withdrawal | “Your partner needs recovery time.” | No spawn, no cost, no override |
| Low trust | “The link is not ready for this call.” | No forced answer; care/story relationship path |
| Expiry during safety check | “The call expired. Check the arrival space before retrying.” | Ticket consumed; no actor, charge or strain |
| Ceremony protected | “Let the ceremony finish.” | No forced recall/swap/interruption |
| Free recall | Link opens to resting state, with gentle lowering tone | Zero new charge/strain; no refund |
| Later ordered swap | First partner rests before the next answers | At most one active; refusal afterward leaves the field empty |

Swap is a later practice variant requiring a second existing bonded partner; do not invent an extra bond or roster entry to run this first trial.
