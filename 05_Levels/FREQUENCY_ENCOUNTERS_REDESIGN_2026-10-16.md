# Frequency Encounter Redesign — Eleven Provisional Anomaly Slots
**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** REFERENCE-ONLY source intake → PROPOSED ORIGINAL ENCOUNTERS; not canon approvals  
**Do not modify:** 125-ID Permanent Eco-Kin Dex, existing sovereign identities, six-era/eight-act story arc, nature of Blight/Data Virus, established endgame.

## Encounter architecture
These are **eleven numbered provisional design slots** derived from general input intentions (multi-phase threats, aerial tracking, submerged relics, station defense, territorial wildlife, major pressure disruptions). Their visual silhouettes, targets, mechanical twists, causes, counterplay and resolution have been **independently reworked** for Rebearth. Avoid one-to-one timelines, compositions, transformations, attack choreography, archetypes or names from external source material.

**Common runtime contract:** `EncounterID`, `RegionGate`, `StoryGate`, `WorldStateGate`, `BCT_EncounterSeed`, `PhaseState`, `TelegraphAsset`, `ThreatBudget`, `EnvironmentResolutionObjective`, `Pause/ReconnectRecovery`, `ResolutionFlags`, `NonCombatAlternative`, `RewardProvenance`.

- Main campaign uses **real-time third-person action**, not auto-scroll screen borders or projectile patterns depending on a 2D camera viewport.
- Phase transitions use readable warning signals and condition-based authored objectives. Any health threshold is an **internal gameplay threshold**, not a replacement player stat. Do not grant untelegraphed instant kills, invulnerable permanent pursuit objects or unbounded spawns.
- Threat responses tie to **Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity**, relevant existing Essence profiles, gear and Anima-Link strain. No new elemental resistance axis.
- Each encounter has **protect / recover / halt / retreat** options compatible with ecology. No forced capture of Sovereign Eco-Kin.
- All names below are working names, not releases; approvals depend on duplication screening against existing bosses, factions, Nature and E.C.O. Sentinel continuity.

## 01 — Siltwake Aggregate (working)
**Environment:** a failed mineral-filtration plant where mudglass clusters swell under high pressure. **Shape:** irregular layered silt plates and elastic suspended sediment, unlike a round amorphous gel. **Core mechanic:** strikes detach *environmental pressure gaskets*, changing which pumps flood; the player must re-seat three gaskets before destabilization. At high distortion the aggregate migrates along a visible drainage network, **never teleports to the player's exact capsule**. **Counterplay:** scan fluid color, divert pressure, use Terra/Torrent isolation. **Outcome:** water-treatment route restored; no forced organism slaughter.

## 02 — Nightglass Cartographer (working)
**Environment:** a suspended Meridian coastal scan station that treats people as occluding mapping noise. **Shape:** articulated planar mapping fan with six folding reflectors, no giant eyeball or exposed mouth. **Core mechanic:** it creates a moving *negative-space navigation puzzle* by projecting safe and unsafe survey sectors across cliff platforms. Phase two disconnects its map slices; players can rotate relay reflectors to shut down tracking. **Counterplay:** move behind coastal stone and retune a buoy. **Outcome:** archived route map authenticated.

## 03 — Crimson Archive Choir (working)
**Environment:** a sealed damaged Blight-stressed archive. **Shape:** layered memory spools around a central sealed recorder, not a living brain. **Core mechanic:** interactive voice/memory evidence resolves the archive's false target identity; in combat mode, three rotating signal tapes must be silenced while avoiding impulse waves. When one tape breaks, the others reorganize their sound field; there are **no illusionary player duplicates or teleport-charge copies**. **Counterplay:** Harmony listening puzzle with A.E.G.I.S. transcript verification. **Outcome:** region outbreak cause revealed.

## 04 — Amberwing Assembly (working)
**Environment:** subterranean Amber Wilds-like caverns pending map authority; broken resource extractors interfere with pollinator migration. **Shape:** a segmented automated sorter with detached luminous wing panels, not a sentient queen. **Core mechanic:** players reprogram conveyor directions while sorter arms sweep three altitude bands; defensive motes have capped population and retreat when food corridors reopen. **Counterplay:** read amber airflow currents, disable extractor output. **Outcome:** pollinator flyway returns; never coerces Eco-Kin into defense units.

## 05 — Memory Gate Clamp (working)
**Environment:** ancient Archive Dungeon candidate, pending story clearance. **Shape:** a suspended locking lattice with articulated anchor prongs, not an animate skull with two hands. **Core mechanic:** five anchor clamps alternately bind **structural floor tiles**, telegraphed with UV floor maps; players may release tiles in any order. Final phase is a rotating beam-channel puzzle through the loosened lock. **Counterplay:** legible lane warning, safe ledges, environmental power-cut alternative. **Outcome:** library can be accessed without deleting its recordkeeping system.

## 06 — Watershed Sentinance (working)
**Environment:** foundational Heartroot-linked deep pumping corridor. **Shape:** immense stationary horizontal relay canopy spanning a subterranean reservoir, not a scrolling vertical wall chasing a bridge runner. **Core mechanic:** re-route pressure through three independent culverts while movable barriers establish current vectors. The encounter pressure climbs with unresolved flooded sectors; no source-NPC sacrifice or extraction ritual. **Counterplay:** safe draining channels and cut-off valves. **Outcome:** gates open one **local region access flag**; it cannot silently change the whole campaign into a new "Awakening" act.

## 07 — Parallax Survey Pair (working)
**Environment:** exposed offshore antenna-causeway. **Shape:** two asymmetric survey platforms linked through vulnerable line-of-sight fiber routes. **Core mechanic:** Unit Tide projects broad, *slow* current maps; Unit Wind creates rapidly moving safe gaps in suspended rigging. Their behaviors change according to which cable junction the player repairs, **not a mirrored twin blaster/jaw/laser 40% transformation**. Phase two: isolated units can be safely powered down or lured into a mutual calibration. **Counterplay:** repair conductive grounding and sever a synchronization error. **Outcome:** predictable shipping forecasts return.

## 08 — Substrate Borer Array (working)
**Environment:** buried geothermal exchange and cracked tunnel grid. **Shape:** modular drilling frame on fixed rail struts, not a freely tunneling segmented predator with lasers on every segment. **Core mechanic:** track acoustic drilling rhythm to predict cave-in zones and escort trapped civilians; modules retract when three geothermal valves are cooled. A broken module becomes salvage scrap **not an autonomous attacking clone**. **Counterplay:** Terra cooling braces, audible telegraph. **Outcome:** reopened safe passage and vent integrity.

## 09 — Rootwake Tangle (working)
**Environment:** diseased mangrove/reef root connection. **Shape:** large Flora scaffold formed from entangled dead infrastructure and living root tissue. **Core mechanic:** the player discovers Blight pressure imbalance and gradually restores three root-salinities while dodging **terrain growth** (not projectile spam/grappling chase). Phase two branches shift from dangerous sweeping to protective bridge creation as Purity increases. **Counterplay:** water flow and shade regulation. **Outcome:** nursery habitat restored; ethical care takes priority to species classification.

## 10 — Spectrum Calibration Field (working)
**Environment:** storm-damaged atmospheric observatory. **Shape:** distributed refractive panels and light membranes, **not a winged humanoid radiant deity**. **Core mechanic:** frequency-scanning of six translucent weather panes reveals safe corridors; changing light direction rotates one sector at a time. **Daylight behavior:** stronger exposure and faster panes but capped damage, explicit audio/text telegraphs, no instant lifecycle override. **Counterplay:** real-time shelter and prism calibration. **Outcome:** atmospheric storm mitigation network restored.

## 11 — Foldwake Relay Heart (working)
**Environment:** late campaign Meridian star-navigation relay, only with main story approval. **Shape:** off-axis ring scaffold exposing a four-channel energy junction, not a cosmic humanoid with forehead/hand relics. **Core mechanic:** stabilize **Vibrance, Density, Harmony, Purity** distribution to prevent space-folding hazard corridors from intersecting inhabited lanes; destroying nodes without routing power worsens the world-state outcome. Phase two requires repairing the damaged core before containment shuts down. **Counterplay:** lane-reading, timed cooling and coordinated network reset. **Outcome:** **local** space-folding incident controlled; explicitly **not** a substitute for the canon final boss or final ending requirements.

### Boss reward principles
Unique rewards may be **research evidence, relief supply, purified recovered materials, new route access, workshop components, community trust and documented repairs**. Never claim specific numeric drop probabilities or Essence resistance coefficients before approved tuning tables. If random rewards are used: deterministic server-authoritative roll seed, anti-duplication transaction ID, odds disclosure where appropriate and QA statistical tests.
