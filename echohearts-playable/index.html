1. Mine & Chop

Every block can be broken. Grass, dirt, stone, coal & gold ore drop resources. Trees drop wood + apples.

🏠

2. Build Shelter

Place blocks to make walls, platforms and a safe house. Monsters can't pass through solid blocks.

🌙

3. Night = Danger

Days are calm. At night zombies, eyes & mischievous imps spawn faster and hit harder. Torches of daylight return at dawn.

👾

4. Fight Mischief

Slimes bounce, imps dash & steal coins, eyes fly. Defeat them for gel, coins, hearts & apples.

👁️

5. Boss Fight

The Echo Devourer charges, hovers and spawns minions at 50% HP. Dodge its dash, strike when it pauses. Win = Echo Heart!
