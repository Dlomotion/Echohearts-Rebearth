## Source Scope
This intake preserves the connected story-system thread spanning:
- The First Blight Siege
- Neon Sea

- Clockspire of Aevoryn
- Sanctuary growth
- farming, combat, Eco-Kin bonding, world restoration and visible consequence

## Core Story Spine Preserved
The source material establishes a strong design principle: story, combat, farming, Sanctuary building, Eco-Kin bonding, resource gathering and world restoration must not feel like separate game modes. Each major quest should teach a system and permanently alter Rebearth.

### Act 1 Candidate: The First Blight Siege
Historical beats preserved for review:
- Piglot bond and first restored Sanctuary plot
- corrupted Dandelion crisis
- Elder Lillian's warning that restored life attracts Blight pressure
- first Sanctuary defense
- first Purity Tower pressure event
- first player-facing proof that combat exists to restore, not simply destroy

Candidate boss identity under review:
- Dreadlion Bloom

Historical choice structure preserved:
- careful cleansing
- faster destruction with ecosystem cost
- partner-first protection with infrastructure cost

Required current-canon patch:
- Eco-Kin may assist Sanctuary defense voluntarily; they are not garrison inventory or forced workers.
- Farming uses current public stats and approved Living Soil rules, not a conflicting hidden stat stack unless explicitly approved.
- Any Dandelion recruit/bond result must use Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond/Release/Defer.

### Act 2 Candidate: Neon Sea
Historical beats preserved for review:
- poisoned/oil-static sea routes
- reef restoration and underwater traversal
- SeaShock, SeaShield, Jelly Jellyfish and Orcanize story roles
- Vance as an extraction-minded antagonist who treats Rebearth as inventory
- Dredge-Titan extraction machine boss
- victory visibly restores coral routes, migration, farming, machine reliability and the Global Purity Map

Current-canon alignment:
- Neon Sea / Azuredeep already exists in active world canon.
- underwater travel must follow current swim → aquatic partner → pressure-safe route progression.
- Vance remains a proposal until villain continuity review confirms identity and faction placement.
- Demon-type wording in machine corruption is legacy/proposal language pending taxonomy review.

### Act 3 Candidate: First Migration / Seasonal Pulse
Preserve the idea that restored regions change later through:
- migration
- weather
- Purity state
- time-of-day
- season
- story flags

This should reinforce existing Rebirth Seasonal Clock, wild Eco-Kin behavior and world-memory systems rather than creating a duplicate global simulation.

### Starvault Spires Setup
Preserve as historical proposal:
- migration beacon / constellation-city concept
- moon-glass bridges
- observatories
- migration memory
- Alien-Kin refugee history

`Starvault Spires` remains proposal terminology until world/canon review decides whether to approve, rename or merge it into current sky/world structure.

### Act 4 Candidate: Clockspire of Aevoryn
This is strongly compatible with current canon because Aevoryn is already the locked Time Guardian / landmark identity.

Preserve:
- biome memories
- puzzle/combat/rest floors
- world-memory restoration
- milestone rewards tied to time/memory understanding
- Aevoryn lore leading toward final consequences

Patch required:
- avoid endless-tower/grind framing unless it serves story.
- rewards should not trivialize world consequence or produce unrestricted rewind exploits.
- Tamer branches such as Blightbreaker/Lifewarden/Gatewalker remain proposals until current progression-tree review.

## Heart Statement Test
Every surviving mission in this intake must prove:
1. This world needs me.
2. These Eco-Kin are alive.
3. My choices changed Rebearth.
