## The Heart Test
Every major feature should strengthen at least one of these feelings:
1. **This world needs me.**
2. **These Eco-Kin are alive.**
3. **My choices changed Rebearth.**

## Pillar 1: This World Needs Me
Players should see ecological wounds, threatened communities, unstable weather, Blight pressure, damaged Sanctuaries, and unresolved history. Objectives must create visible consequences, not abstract checklist progress.

## Pillar 2: These Eco-Kin Are Alive
Eco-Kin need distinct silhouettes, cries, habitats, movement, fear responses, social behavior, rest behavior, combat roles, and memory. Some attack, flee, hide, warn, protect young, observe, or refuse contact.

## Pillar 3: My Choices Changed Rebearth
Major decisions alter world state, NPC trust, Eco-Kin behavior, faction pressure, Sanctuary growth, environmental recovery, and later mission options. Recovery should be staged and visibly persistent.

## Core Gameplay Loop
Explore → Observe/Track → Protect/Fight/Calm → Restore → Bond/Release/Defer → Build → Learn → Return to a changed world.

## Quality Questions For Every Feature
- What emotion is the player meant to feel?
- What new decision does this create?
- What visible proof remains afterward?
- How does an Eco-Kin react?
- How does an NPC or faction react?
- What does the world remember?
- Is the system understandable within seconds?
- Is this distinctly Echohearts rather than borrowed structure?
