# Echohearts: Rebearth — Player Experience Pillars

© 2026 Into Deep Studios and Donta L. Owens. All rights reserved.

## Heart Statement
Every major system, mission, Eco-Kin, region, UI feature, mini-game and visual change should support at least one of these feelings:

1. **This world needs me.**
2. **These Eco-Kin are alive.**
3. **My choices changed Rebearth.**

## Pillar 1: The World Needs Me
The player should repeatedly see that intervention matters.

Examples:
- damaged habitats recover after restoration,
- NPC routes reopen,
- Eco-Kin return to safe areas,
- Sanctuary improvements visibly help surrounding communities,
- storms, Blightfronts and faction pressure create meaningful problems rather than random noise.

## Pillar 2: Eco-Kin Are Alive
Eco-Kin should never feel like inventory entries with animation attached.

Requirements:
- distinct body plans and silhouettes,
- calm, fear and defensive states,
- habitat-specific behavior,
- flee/warn/hide/watch/protect-young/approach behavior where appropriate,
- memory of rescue, harm, release and trust,
- refusal states,
- voluntary Sanctuary/Havenlink behavior,
- independent story significance.

## Pillar 3: My Choices Changed Rebearth
Choices need persistent evidence.

Examples:
- visible world-state changes,
- changed NPC dialogue,
- Eco-Kin trust/fear memory,
- mission successor variation,
- different Sanctuary conditions,
- altered faction pressure,
- Timeline Map and Archive updates,
- final Heal/Break/Balance consequences.

## Supporting Experience Pillars

### Cozy-Crazy Contrast
Safe spaces should feel warm, readable and restorative. Wild spaces should feel beautiful, unstable and consequential.

### Restoration Over Extraction
Exploration rewards understanding, care and repair. Resource gathering must respect Living Crust and habitat rules.

### Learn Through Play
A.E.G.I.S., EcoDex, Academy training, tracking and mini-games should teach systems through interaction rather than walls of exposition.

### Player Clarity
The player should understand what they can do next, why it matters and what changed because of it.

### Ethical Power
The player becomes powerful through knowledge, trust and restoration, not through ownership override or forced fusion.

## Mini-Game Experience Rule
Mini-games are approved only when they make the core game stronger. They should teach or deepen scanning, Kindling, purification, farming, care, crafting, traversal, memory reconstruction, seasonal ecology, EchoDeck or Arena simulation. Their rewards and persistence must connect back to the main game world.

## Quality Gate
Before approving any feature, ask:
- Does it make Rebearth feel more alive?
- Does it strengthen Eco-Kin agency?
- Does it create a visible player consequence?
- Is the player-facing feedback understandable?
- Is this original to Echohearts rather than copied identity?
- Is its verification status honest?
