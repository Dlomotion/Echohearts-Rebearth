## World Scale Direction
The source material asks for eight major terrain families while supporting close to one hundred local terrain/biome variations. This remains a world-design target, not a claim that 100 unique production biomes are implemented.

The currently authoritative major-region structure remains the existing Rebearth/Heartreach map. Local sub-biomes, microclimates, ruins, caves, wetlands, urban districts, sky routes, coastlines and seasonal states can provide the broader variation without creating duplicate continents or disconnected map taxonomies.

## Player Identity and Build Diversity
The final story/game structure must support players who identify differently:
- attacker/frontline,
- support/healer/restoration,
- builder/architect,
- engineer/logistics,
- scout/tracker,
- caretaker/Havenlink,
- tactical/Harmony Circuit,
- explorer/researcher.

Player uniqueness should come from Eco-Kin relationships, equipment/loadout choices, Sanctuary specialization, field tools, skill priorities, cosmetics and world decisions, not from pay-to-win rarity or forced creature exploitation.

## Item Customization/Rarity Direction
Items can vary by rarity and specialization, but rarity must not erase player skill or ecological logic. Strong item identity should come from:
- biome source,
- crafted material history,
- restoration provenance,
- modifiers that support different playstyles,
- visual customization,
- attachment/charm slots,
- safe upgrade paths,
- unique lore and discovery context.

## Everhour Haven Side-Quest Intake
Historical premise:
A picturesque elder community is built above a Chrono-Rift. Residents have survived timeline breaks, Name Loss events and failed Echo Rifts. The Hourglass Choir, linked to the Null Choir and a Star-Cast entity called the Chronovore, steals remaining years and stores them in time seeds to accelerate a larger cosmic audit.

### Required Reconciliation
- Connect the community to current Heartreach geography.
- Define whether Chrono-Rift maps to Aevoryn/time-memory canon.
- Resolve Astryx against current villain hierarchy.
- Ensure elders are characters with agency, not quest props.
- Give the arc a visible world consequence and persistent save flag.
- Tie rewards to memory/restoration rather than raw power inflation.
