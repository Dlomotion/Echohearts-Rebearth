👹 HEARTBREAKER BOSS

EchoHearts Rebirth

Terraria-style rules: 100 HP (each ❤ = 20 HP), 100 Mana, damage numbers, crits, knockback, and invincibility frames after a hit.
Move with WASD / Arrow Keys. Shoot with Space / click, use E for Echo Burst.
💛 Hearts heal 20 HP  •  ★ Stars restore mana  •  🌙 Night spawns stronger monsters
👹 Every 3 levels: “Heartbreaker has awoken!” — defeat it to be reborn with +20 Max HP.

On mobile: drag to move, tap Echo Pulse to shoot.

Enemies

  • 😈 Mischievous Imp — 20 HP, 12 contact damage — fast and darting. Drops hearts/stars like in Terraria.
  • 👾 Void Monster — 60 HP, 20 contact damage, 2 defense — chases you, stronger at night. +25 pts.
  • 👹 Heartbreaker Boss — 600+ HP, 35 contact damage, 6 defense — awakes every 3 levels, shoots void orbs, spawns imps, enrages below 25% HP. Defeat it for +200 pts, +20 Max HP, +1 defense.
  • ◆ Void Orb — 15 damage — boss shot. You get brief invincibility frames after any hit, so you won't take damage every frame.
  • Weapons: Echo Pulse — 22 damage, 10% crit, strong knockback. Echo Burst (E) — 55 damage, costs 30 mana.
