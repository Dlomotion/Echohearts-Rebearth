## Why This Is Reference-Only
The later continuity pass established **LEONIX** as a Light-aligned lion-vanguard baseline and made the original Leonix artwork the primary visual reference. Cosmic, Spirit, Humanoid-Kin, exact measurements, rarity, title, origin, and generated abilities remain unapproved until current canon review resolves them.

## Reuse Rule
Individual ideas may return only through Leonix's active canon review. Any recovered concept must:
- map to the current twelve-element system,
- preserve Leonix's lion-vanguard identity,
- avoid overlap with Nature, Sol-Spectra, Luna-Spectra, Aevoryn, or other Guardian roles,
- preserve Eco-Kin agency,
- avoid outside-IP resemblance,
- pass gameplay, art, animation, ecology, and accessibility review.
