## System Conflicts To Avoid
1. Forced experimentation.
2. Forced mutation/evolution.
3. Player/Eco-Kin fusion.
4. Raw Blight materials used directly in crafting or growth.
5. Eco-Kin treated as passive infrastructure or power batteries.
6. Homogeneous three-stage evolution applied to every Eco-Kin regardless of ecology.
7. New element taxonomies created from old draft labels.
8. Mutation success framed as a pure stat check without agency.
9. Discordance rewarded as a power shortcut.
10. Absolute immunity or perfect resistance generated by a form.

## Salvageable Design Intent
- biomes influence forms,
- environmental pressure can trigger adaptation,
- Resonance Shards carry place-specific identity,
- Restoration Altars can support Growth Rites,
- Harmony and Purity should matter,
- branch forms should alter traversal and restoration,
- villains can contrast the Keeper by forcing unstable change,
- Discordance can be a meaningful story warning state.

## Current Recommendation
Use **Shard-Arc Adaptation** as the active player-facing framing unless later canon review approves another name. Preserve `Resonance Mutation` as historical terminology in archive/lore only where it improves continuity.
