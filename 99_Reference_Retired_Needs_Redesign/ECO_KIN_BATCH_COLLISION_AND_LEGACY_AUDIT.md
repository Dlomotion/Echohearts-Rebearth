**ACTIVE QA / SOURCE CLEANUP**

The historical batch archive contains strong creature ideas but also repeated names, repeated ecological roles, legacy element labels, inconsistent numbering, and old progression language. This file prevents those historical generations from silently becoming duplicate canon.

Examples include:
- **Veripaw** appears in more than one historical batch.
- **Verithorn** appears in multiple historical batches.

- **Shadowfin** appears repeatedly.
- **Glacifin** appears repeatedly.

## Numbering Problems
Historical headings use batch ranges such as `026–035`, `036–045`, and later ranges into the 500s. The sequence contains visible gaps, overlaps, and repeated range labels. Therefore:
- batch numbers are archival sequence markers only,
- they are not permanent EcoDex IDs,
- they cannot be used as save-data identity,
- permanent EcoKinID/EchoprintID is assigned only after canon review.

Current canonical elements remain:

Migration must be deliberate. Old labels may become status families, VFX themes, biome descriptors, materials, sub-affinities, Arena traits, or be mapped to one or two current elements where justified.

## Legacy Progression Terms
The archive repeatedly uses:
`Chil-Kin → Mega Kin → Ultimate Kin`

This is not automatically active canon. Review against current Growth Rite/form rules. Do not preserve any implementation that implies forced transformation, forced mutation, DNA overwrite, fusion, or level-only evolution when ecology/Bond/story requirements are more appropriate.

## Naming Saturation Flags
The archive heavily reuses certain prefixes/themes:
- `Veri- / Verid-`
- `Solar- / Sol-`
- `Shadow- / Umbra- / Night-`
- `Glaci- / Frost-`
- `Aero- / Sky-`
- `Mire- / Toxi- / Veno-`
- `Crag- / Stone- / Quake-`
- `Lumi- / Lumin-`

This creates franchise-readability and memory problems if too many final species share the same construction. Naming QA should keep only the strongest examples and diversify phonetics, cultural texture, habitat logic, and species identity.

## Ecology Duplication Flags
Repeated roles visible in the archive include:
- luminous/healing pollinators,
- solar legendary guardians,
- shadow/abyss ambush predators,
- glacial mammoth/tank creatures,
- volcanic bruisers,
- coral/reef defenders,
- Sky-Island aerial strikers,
- toxic-swamp controllers,
- Badlands stone tanks,
- Veridian restoration fauna.

These are useful ecosystem categories, but each approved Eco-Kin needs a distinct behavior loop, body plan, ecological function, combat identity, and story reason.

## Legendary Inflation Rule
Many historical batches end with a Legendary/solar/celestial creature. That pattern should not carry forward automatically. Legendary, Guardian, Titan, Celestial Echo, Mythic, or high-order status must be scarce and story-earned.

## Ancient Tech Rule
Historical batches sometimes attach Ancient Tech or Ancient-DNA progression broadly. Current cleanup rule:
- ordinary wildlife is biological first,
- Ancient Tech appears only when habitat, story, ancestry, or Meridian interaction justifies it,
- Ancient Tech never becomes a generic armor layer pasted onto every Eco-Kin,
- DNA language must not imply forced genetic manipulation.

## Collision Resolution Procedure
For every duplicate or near-duplicate:
1. Compare source images and written descriptions.
2. Identify strongest silhouette and body plan.
3. Compare ecology and gameplay roles.
4. Check overlap with already locked Eco-Kin.
5. Keep, merge, rename, or retire.
6. Map to current elements and growth rules.
7. Assign permanent EcoKinID/EchoprintID only after approval.
8. Log the decision and reason.
