This record prevents older generated design material from silently overwriting current canon.

## Retired or redesign-required material

### Outside-game references
References to Calamity, Redemption, Thorium, Palworld-style elements, Pokémon-style naming, Digimon's Vaccine/Data/Virus associations, Chrome Digizoid, Virus Busters, Nightmare Soldiers and similar outside-franchise language are research/reference only. They cannot enter Echohearts public canon.

### Element systems
Historical Neutral / Fire / Water / Electric / Grass / Ice / Fighting / Poison / Ground / Flying / Psychic / Bug / Rock / Ghost / Dragon / Dark / Steel / Fairy / Holy-Light charts are not current canon.

Current elements are:
Solar, Lunar, Hydro, Magma, Aero, Flora, Volt, Terra, Iron/Tech, Spirit, Chaos, Radiant.

### Capture
Retired:
- weakening-to-capture
- cages
- foothold restraints
- spheres/balls/capsules
- guaranteed master capture
- Digital Matrix Capsule storage
- eco-traps that force ownership

Current field relationship loop:
Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer.

### Trading
Retired:
- trading Eco-Kin as commodities
- selling sentient partners
- trading forced genetics

Allowed economic exchange includes approved materials, food, cosmetics, crafted gear, Sanctuary items, naturally shed resources and EchoDeck content.

### Forced work
Retired:
- worker-beast framing
- mandatory farm/factory assignment
- living battery role
- mining until exhaustion

Current rule:
Eco-Kin may voluntarily assist through Sanctuary Aptitudes. Fatigue, fear, trust and refusal matter.

### Breeding and genetics
Historical selective-breeding optimization and mutation-for-productivity are not current canon.

Possible future compatible systems must respect:
- species biology
- agency
- welfare
- no forced mating
- no identity overwrite
- no forced mutation

### Main turn-based combat
Retired from main campaign.
Real-time action is authoritative for the overworld campaign. Turn-based/tactical play is restricted to approved Arena/EchoDeck/simulation contexts.

### Mandatory permadeath
Not locked.
May be explored as optional hardcore mode only after accessibility and save-design review.

### 24/7 monster pressure
Redesign required.
Persistent danger is useful, but safe recovery periods are necessary for pacing, Sanctuary attachment, social play and contrast.

### Nuclear Pillar / duplicate world manager
Do not implement as a second world-state authority. Salvage any useful stability/fallout concepts into the existing world-state architecture.
