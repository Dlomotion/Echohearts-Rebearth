## Source Thread Integration
This record absorbs the earlier Leonix naming, artwork, generated character-card, and continuation thread into the existing Echohearts: Rebearth workflow. It does not create a new Dex, Game Bible, character system, or parallel canon.

## Locked Identity
**LEONIX** is the current canonical name.

The following are current continuity anchors from the development thread:
- celestial/prismatic lion warrior presentation
- white, blue-crystal, and gold construction
- enormous luminous wings
- radiant crown structure
- energy-bearing tail
- high-order / legendary presentation quality
- frontline guardian combat fantasy combining leonine physical force with protective celestial energy

## Provisional / Review-Required Material
The historical generated card and early naming response introduced details that are not automatically canon:
- title: `The Celestial Wing King`
- classification: `Celestial Humanoid-Kin`
- elements: `Light • Cosmic • Spirit`
- affinity: `STARZ* Resonance`
- rarity: `Mythic Guardian`
- sky-temple / floating-kingdom habitat wording
- origin from a collision between starlight and an Echoheart Core
- signature abilities: Starfall Judgment, Prism Wing Ascension, Radiant Claw Nova, Crown of Eternity
- invented size/weight figures from generated presentation cards

The source thread explicitly rejects the accidental measurements and infinite-weight style values. `Cosmic`, `Spirit`, and `Celestial Humanoid-Kin` remain provisional unless they survive current project-wide review.

## Current Element Mapping Review
The active project uses the twelve core elements:
`Solar, Lunar, Hydro, Magma, Aero, Flora, Volt, Terra, Iron/Tech, Spirit, Chaos, Radiant`.

The historical phrase **Light-aligned** should therefore be reviewed as a likely presentation/identity mapping toward **Radiant**, but that mapping is not considered final until the Leonix element and combat audit is completed.

STARZ* remains presentation/VFX language, not a separate core element.

## Player-Experience Target
Leonix must support the Heart Statement:
1. **This world needs me.** Leonix must protect or restore something meaningful in Rebearth rather than exist only as spectacle.
2. **These Eco-Kin are alive.** He needs autonomous behavior, judgment, warning states, social preferences, trust rules, and non-combat presence.
3. **My choices changed Rebearth.** Leonix's response, location, protection state, or story consequence should react to player choices.

## Agency Rules
If Leonix remains a Guardian-class or equivalent high-order Eco-Kin:
- he cannot be owned, traded, stored, or forced into service,
- he cannot be treated as a guaranteed mount,
- he cannot be bonded through capture mechanics,
- any alliance must come through story, trust, restoration, and consent.

## Art Direction Requirements
- preserve the original artwork's core silhouette before redesigning details
- lion identity must remain readable before armor/VFX
- prismatic wing shapes must remain distinct and animation-feasible
- crown structure must not obscure facial readability
- white / blue-crystal / gold palette remains the continuity baseline unless art review proves a stronger canon-consistent solution
- no accidental extra limbs, wing duplication, or anatomy artifacts
- avoid outside-franchise logos, costumes, signatures, or recognizable protected character designs
- use current twelve-element readability standards once final typing is locked
