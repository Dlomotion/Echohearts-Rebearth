## Source Identity
The Resonance Kit is a small fox-like Eco-Kin with starry, unshaped resonance fur and the core ability **Frequency Shift**. It exists as an adaptive Eco-Kin whose environmental path is shaped by Resonance Shards found across dangerous biomes.

The historical design packet also describes a broader Resonance Mutation framework, Abyssal Biome shard sources, Restoration Altars, Great Bloom triggers, and multiple branch forms. These ideas are preserved here for structured canon review rather than copied directly into runtime.

## Current Canon-Safe Interpretation
The Resonance Kit is not treated as a forced-mutation subject. Its branching development is framed as **Shard-Arc Adaptation**, a willing growth response that depends on:
- Eco-Kin agency and consent,
- sufficient Harmony,
- sufficient Purity,
- a compatible restored biome,
- a valid Resonance Shard,
- A.E.G.I.S. stability checks,
- and no raw Blight contamination.

`Null-Aether` is preserved as a historical descriptor for its unshaped resonance state, not as a new thirteenth gameplay element unless separately approved.

## Canon Rules
1. No forced DNA splicing.
2. No involuntary evolution.
3. No player/Eco-Kin fusion.
4. No raw Blight Husk or unpurified corruption used as a growth ingredient.
5. No branch becomes mandatory because of level alone.
6. A failed or unstable adaptation pauses rather than harming or deleting the Eco-Kin.
7. Discordance may exist as a dangerous story state, never as a reward for abuse.
8. Every approved form must preserve recognizable identity from the base Resonance Kit.
9. Every approved branch needs a distinct world function, traversal function, combat/support role, and visible restoration consequence.

## Player Experience Reason
The Resonance Kit should make players feel that exploration changes a living companion without erasing who that companion already is. Shards become memories of places restored, not consumable force-evolution tokens.
