## Player-Experience Target
Pupular should help prove the Heart Statement:
1. **This world needs me.** Her ecology or story must solve a real Rebearth problem rather than exist only as a mascot.
2. **These Eco-Kin are alive.** She needs autonomous behavior, preferences, fear/defensive states, idle behavior, social reactions, and player-memory hooks.
3. **My choices changed Rebearth.** Her district/biome, quests, or social behavior must visibly change based on player actions.
