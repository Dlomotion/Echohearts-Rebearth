## What The Archive Contains
The supplied archive includes many generated Eco-Kin discovery batches, image links, names, habitats, rarity labels, combat/ecology roles, and older evolution language. Historical ranges extend from early entries such as 026–035 into later ranges in the 500s, but the numbering contains gaps, overlaps, duplicate names, and repeated concept families. Historical batch numbers are therefore **not authoritative EcoDex IDs**.

Representative preserved concepts include entries such as:
- Duskfin, Terrabolt, Lumoray, Vineclaw, Cryovulp, Fernbeetle, Searhound, Bubbleep, Galegriff, Mirewisp
- Tidalash, Graviklaw, Pyrofawn, Zephyloon, Toxigulp, Solarisq, Glaciram, Sandstrider, Umbraeel, Luminomoth
- Coralvine, Ironhide, Thermopyre, Galestorm, Mossbeast, Nightlurk, Glacifin, Duneslash, Oceanus, Emberpup
- Veridrench, Quaketusk, Solwisp, Voidmorph, Tidalcrab, Venomite, Aerowyrm, Lavasaur, Crystallux, Rootstalker
- many later Veridian, Solar, Shadow, Glacial, Volcanic, Coral, Sky-Island, Swamp, Badlands, Ancient-Grove, Abyssal, and Sun-Ruins lines

These names and descriptions are preserved for review, not automatically canonized.

## Intake Rule
Every historical Eco-Kin must pass this sequence before promotion:

`source recovery → duplicate/collision check → naming audit → body-plan/anatomy audit → current element mapping → habitat/ecology mapping → gameplay-role check → growth/forms review → agency/ethics check → art-source check → originality/IP check → EcoKinID/EchoprintID assignment → approval status`

## Approval States
Each historical entry receives exactly one current status:
- **CANON**: fully approved and reconciled.
- **APPROVED-PENDING**: concept accepted but requires data/art/implementation completion.
- **MERGED / RENAMED**: useful material folded into another approved Eco-Kin.
- **RETIRED / REFERENCE-ONLY**: preserved for inspiration/history but not active roster.
- **REJECTED**: conflicts with canon, duplicates another design, has unsafe provenance, or cannot be repaired economically.

## Canon Mapping Requirements
Historical typing must be translated into the current twelve-element system only:
- Solar
- Lunar
- Hydro
- Magma
- Aero
- Flora
- Volt
- Terra
- Iron/Tech
- Spirit
- Chaos
- Radiant

Old labels such as Light, Dark, Water, Fire, Wind, Plant, Poison, Ice, Cosmic, Ghost, Fairy, Steel, Rock, Nature, Crystal, Gravity, and similar terms are not automatically core elements. They may survive as visual descriptors, habitats, status families, ability themes, sub-affinities, materials, Arena traits, or be mapped to current elements after review.

## Growth / Evolution Migration
The archive repeatedly uses historical progression such as:
`Chil-Kin → Mega Kin → Ultimate Kin`

That is **legacy proposal language**. It must be reviewed against current Echohearts growth rules, including Natural, Regional, Restored, Discordance, STARZ*, Ancient Tech, Inheritance, and current Growth Rite logic. No historical line may force evolution, fusion, DNA overwrite, or mutation.

## Agency Rule
No historical batch concept can introduce:
- capture balls/spheres/capsules,
- harm-to-capture,
- cages as bonding,
- forced labor,
- forced evolution,
- sale/trade of sentient Eco-Kin as commodities,
- extracted sentient cores,
- player/Eco-Kin fusion.

Approved encounter logic remains:
`Observe → Protect → Calm → Kindle → Bond / Release / Defer`

## Roster Quality Rule
Before approving any historical entry, confirm it adds a reason to exist in Rebearth:
- unique silhouette/body plan,
- unique ecology or behavior,
- unique habitat function,
- useful gameplay role without duplicating another Eco-Kin,
- readable emotional states,
- connection to restoration/world response,
- viable rig/animation/VFX/audio scope,
- story or EcoDex value.

## Art Asset State
Historical generated image links in the archive are source references only. They are not treated as approved production art until the actual binary files are recovered into the project asset location and pass:
- anatomy QA,
- artifact cleanup,
- originality/IP review,
- naming consistency,
- silhouette/element readability,
- accessibility/VFX review,
- canon approval.
