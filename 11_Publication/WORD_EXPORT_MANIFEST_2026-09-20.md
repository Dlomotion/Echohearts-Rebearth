## Publication Gate

A file moves from working draft to public-ready only when:

- names match `00_Canon_Lock` and the canon glossary
- story timeline aligns with the Master Game Bible
- all outside/reference material has been removed or transformed into original Echohearts content
- Eco-Kin agency and anatomy rules are preserved
- technical claims are evidence-based
- art rights/provenance are reviewed
- spelling, continuity, chapter flow, captions, tables, and layout pass QA
- the version is frozen and recorded in the change ledger
