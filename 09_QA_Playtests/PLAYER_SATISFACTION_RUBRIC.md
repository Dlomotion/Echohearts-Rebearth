## Current Review Baseline
- World and environment visuals: 3/5
- Main character presentation: 3/5
- Human NPC presentation: 3/5
- Animation: 4/5
- Buttons and UI interactions: 3/5

These are design-review baselines, not player-test evidence.

## Required Questions Per Review
### World / Environment
- Can a region be identified from one screenshot?
- Does weather or ecology change gameplay, not just visuals?
- Does restoration leave visible before/after proof?

### Main Character
- Is the Architect/Tamer emotionally understandable?
- Is power limited enough to preserve tension?
- Do choices have visible consequences?

### Human NPCs
- Does each key NPC have a distinct silhouette, role, voice, and routine?
- Do NPCs remember player actions?
- Do NPCs feel like people rather than mission dispensers?

### Animation
- Do Eco-Kin visibly communicate calm, fear, and defense?
- Are interactions readable at gameplay distance?
- Do large and small Kin move differently?

### UI / Controls
- Can players find critical actions quickly?
- Are rescue actions as easy to access as combat actions?
- Is text readable and terminology understandable?
